Phrasal verbs

General info

A phrasal verb is a verb + a small word (up, off, on, out, back, down). The meaning is often different from the words alone: give up = stop trying.

We use them for

  • Daily actions: get up, sit down, put on, take off.
  • Everyday English: pick up, turn off, look for, find out.
  • Natural, informal speech: give up, come back, work out.

Words that go with them

up, down, on, off, out, back, in, away, over, after, for.

Russian has verbs with prefixes (встать, выключить). Uzbek has compound verbs (o'chirib qo'ymoq). English puts a separate word after the verb.

Фразовый глагол — это глагол + маленькое слово (up, off, on, out, back, down). Значение часто отличается от значений слов по отдельности: give up = перестать пытаться.

Когда используем

  • Повседневные действия: get up, sit down, put on, take off.
  • Обычный английский: pick up, turn off, look for, find out.
  • Естественная неформальная речь: give up, come back, work out.

Слова-подсказки

up, down, on, off, out, back, in, away, over, after, for.

В русском глаголы с приставками (встать, выключить). В узбекском составные глаголы (o'chirib qo'ymoq). В английском отдельное слово после глагола.

Phrasal verb — bu fe'l + kichik so'z (up, off, on, out, back, down). Ma'nosi ko'pincha so'zlarning alohida ma'nosidan farq qiladi: give up = urinishni to'xtatish.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Kundalik harakatlar: get up, sit down, put on, take off.
  • Oddiy inglizcha: pick up, turn off, look for, find out.
  • Tabiiy norasmiy nutq: give up, come back, work out.

Yordamchi so'zlar

up, down, on, off, out, back, in, away, over, after, for.

Rus tilida old qo'shimchali fe'llar (встать, выключить). O'zbek tilida qo'shma fe'llar (o'chirib qo'ymoq). Inglizchada fe'ldan keyin alohida so'z keladi.

  1. Phrasal verb = verb + particle. The meaning is often not the sum of the words: give up = stop trying.
  2. No object: some phrasal verbs never take an object: I woke up. Please sit down. He came back.
  3. Separable: a noun object can go in the middle or after: turn off the light / turn the light off. A pronoun must go in the middle: turn it off. Not: turn off it.
  4. Inseparable: the object always comes after the particle: look after the children, look after them. Look for my keys, look for them.
  5. More than one meaning: take off = remove clothes or leave the ground (a plane).
  6. Learn in chunks: learn the whole phrase with an object: turn off the light, look for my keys. Check a dictionary to see if the verb is separable.
  1. Фразовый глагол = глагол + частица. Значение часто не равно сумме слов: give up = перестать пытаться.
  2. Без дополнения: некоторые фразовые глаголы не имеют дополнения: I woke up. Please sit down. He came back.
  3. Разделяемые: дополнение-существительное может стоять посередине или после: turn off the light / turn the light off. Местоимение обязательно посередине: turn it off. Не: turn off it.
  4. Неразделяемые: дополнение всегда после частицы: look after the children, look after them. Look for my keys, look for them.
  5. Несколько значений: take off = снять одежду или взлететь (самолёт).
  6. Учи блоками: учи фразу целиком с дополнением: turn off the light, look for my keys. Проверяй по словарю, разделяемый ли глагол.
  1. Phrasal verb = fe'l + yuklama. Ma'no ko'pincha so'zlar yig'indisiga teng emas: give up = urinishni to'xtatish.
  2. To'ldiruvchisiz: ba'zi phrasal verblar to'ldiruvchi olmaydi: I woke up. Please sit down. He came back.
  3. Ajraladigan: ot-to'ldiruvchi o'rtada yoki keyin turishi mumkin: turn off the light / turn the light off. Olmosh albatta o'rtada: turn it off. turn off it emas.
  4. Ajralmaydigan: to'ldiruvchi doim yuklamadan keyin: look after the children, look after them. Look for my keys, look for them.
  5. Bir nechta ma'no: take off = kiyimni yechish yoki uchib ketish (samolyot).
  6. Birikma sifatida o'rganing: iborani to'ldiruvchi bilan o'rganing: turn off the light, look for my keys. Fe'l ajraladimi, lug'atdan tekshiring.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is there an object? No → verb + particle only: wake up, sit down.
  2. Is the object a pronoun (it, him, them)? Yes → separable verbs: put it in the middle: turn it off. Inseparable verbs: after the particle: look after them.
  3. Do I know the meaning? Do not translate word by word. Learn the whole phrase.

Phrasal verb + gerund

He gave up smoking. (not: gave up to smoke) She keeps on trying. They carried on working.

Common mistakes

  • Please turn off it. Please turn it off.
  • I'm looking my keys for. I'm looking for my keys.
  • I woke up him at seven. I woke him up at seven.
  • He gave up to smoke. He gave up smoking.
Pronoun rule: separable verb + pronoun = pronoun goes in the middle. Always: turn it off, pick it up, put them on.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Есть дополнение? Нет → только глагол + частица: wake up, sit down.
  2. Дополнение — местоимение (it, him, them)? Да → разделяемые глаголы: местоимение посередине: turn it off. Неразделяемые: после частицы: look after them.
  3. Я знаю значение? Не переводи слово за словом. Учи фразу целиком.

Фразовый глагол + герундий

He gave up smoking. (не: gave up to smoke) She keeps on trying. They carried on working.

Частые ошибки

  • Please turn off it. Please turn it off.
  • I'm looking my keys for. I'm looking for my keys.
  • I woke up him at seven. I woke him up at seven.
  • He gave up to smoke. He gave up smoking.
Правило местоимения: разделяемый глагол + местоимение = местоимение посередине. Всегда: turn it off, pick it up, put them on.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. To'ldiruvchi bormi? Yo'q → faqat fe'l + yuklama: wake up, sit down.
  2. To'ldiruvchi olmoshmi (it, him, them)? Ha → ajraladigan fe'llar: olmosh o'rtada: turn it off. Ajralmaydigan fe'llar: yuklamadan keyin: look after them.
  3. Ma'nosini bilamanmi? So'zma-so'z tarjima qilmang. Iborani butunlay o'rganing.

Phrasal verb + gerundiy

He gave up smoking. (gave up to smoke emas) She keeps on trying. They carried on working.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • Please turn off it. Please turn it off.
  • I'm looking my keys for. I'm looking for my keys.
  • I woke up him at seven. I woke him up at seven.
  • He gave up to smoke. He gave up smoking.
Olmosh qoidasi: ajraladigan fe'l + olmosh = olmosh o'rtada. Doim: turn it off, pick it up, put them on.

Real-life use

Phrasal verbs are everywhere in everyday English, in speech and in messages.

Daily routine

"I get up at seven, put on my coat and take the bus. I turn off my phone in class."

Travel and plans

"The flight took off late. I'll pick you up at the airport."

Asking for help

"Could you turn the music down? Can you look after my bag?"

IELTS Speaking: talking about habits

"I look after my little brother, and I gave up playing computer games to focus on my studies."

Examples and drill

No object

I woke up at seven. Please sit down. He came back late.

Separable

Turn off the light. / Turn the light off. / Turn it off. Put on your coat. / Put it on.

Inseparable

She looks after her brother. I'm looking for my keys. Look after them.

Two meanings

The plane took off. (left the ground) / Please take off your shoes. (remove)
