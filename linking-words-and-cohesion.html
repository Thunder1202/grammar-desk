Linking words and cohesion

General info

Linking words (linkers) connect ideas inside and between sentences. Cohesion means the text flows logically, so the reader can follow it. At B2 we choose the right linker for each function.

We use them for

  • Adding: moreover, in addition, as well as.
  • Contrasting: however, although, despite, whereas.
  • Giving reasons and results: because, due to, therefore, as a result.
  • Purpose and order: in order to, so that, firstly, finally.

Words that go with them

function, clause, noun, -ing, comma, new sentence.

Russian: однако, хотя, поэтому, чтобы. Uzbek: biroq, garchi, shuning uchun, ... uchun. English linkers need different patterns: although + clause, despite + noun.

Связующие слова соединяют идеи внутри предложения и между предложениями. Связность означает, что текст течёт логично, и читателю легко следить. На уровне B2 мы выбираем нужное слово по функции.

Когда используем

  • Добавление: moreover, in addition, as well as.
  • Противопоставление: however, although, despite, whereas.
  • Причина и результат: because, due to, therefore, as a result.
  • Цель и порядок: in order to, so that, firstly, finally.

Слова-подсказки

функция, клауза, существительное, -ing, запятая, новое предложение.

Русский: однако, хотя, поэтому, чтобы. Узбекский: biroq, garchi, shuning uchun, ... uchun. В английском у связок разные модели: although + предложение, despite + существительное.

Bog'lovchi so'zlar g'oyalarni gap ichida va gaplar orasida bog'laydi. Bog'lanish matn mantiqiy oqishini, o'quvchi kuzatib borishi oson bo'lishini anglatadi. B2 da biz har bir vazifa uchun to'g'ri bog'lovchini tanlaymiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Qo'shish: moreover, in addition, as well as.
  • Qarama-qarshilik: however, although, despite, whereas.
  • Sabab va natija: because, due to, therefore, as a result.
  • Maqsad va tartib: in order to, so that, firstly, finally.

Yordamchi so'zlar

vazifa, gap, ot, -ing, vergul, yangi gap.

Rus tili: однако, хотя, поэтому, чтобы. O'zbek tili: biroq, garchi, shuning uchun, ... uchun. Inglizchada bog'lovchilarning qoliplari har xil: although + gap, despite + ot.

Teacher's rule

FunctionLinkers and patternExample
additionmoreover, in addition, furthermore, as well asHe is smart. Moreover, he works hard.
contrastalthough / even though / whereas + clause; despite / in spite of + noun or -ing; howeverAlthough it rained, we went out. Despite the rain, we went out.
causebecause / since / as + clause; because of / due to + nounWe stayed home because it rained. / because of the rain.
resultso, therefore, as a result, consequentlyIt rained. As a result, we stayed home.
purposeto, in order to, so as to + verb; so that + clauseI left early so that I wouldn't be late.
sequencefirstly, then, after that, finallyFirstly, ... Finally, ...
examplefor example, for instance, such asFruit, such as apples and pears, is healthy.
  1. Choose by function: add, contrast, give a reason, give a result, give a purpose, show order, give an example.
  2. Clause or noun? although, even though, because, since, while + clause (subject + verb). despite, in spite of, because of, due to + noun or -ing: Despite the rain / Despite being tired.
  3. however, therefore, moreover, as a result usually start a new sentence (or follow a semicolon) and take a comma: It rained. However, we went out.
  4. but, so, and join two clauses in one sentence: I was tired, but I kept working. Never use although and but together.
  5. Purpose: to / in order to / so as to + verb. so that + clause: I left early so that I wouldn't be late.
  6. Cohesion: besides linkers, use pronouns and reference words (it, this, that, such) to avoid repeating nouns, and put new information at the end of the sentence.
  1. Выбирай по функции: добавить, противопоставить, дать причину, результат, цель, показать порядок, привести пример.
  2. Предложение или существительное? although, even though, because, since, while + предложение (подлежащее + глагол). despite, in spite of, because of, due to + существительное или -ing: Despite the rain / Despite being tired.
  3. however, therefore, moreover, as a result обычно начинают новое предложение (или идут после точки с запятой) и требуют запятой: It rained. However, we went out.
  4. but, so, and соединяют два предложения в одно: I was tired, but I kept working. Although и but вместе не используют.
  5. Цель: to / in order to / so as to + глагол. so that + предложение: I left early so that I wouldn't be late.
  6. Связность: кроме связок, используй местоимения и слова-ссылки (it, this, that, such), чтобы не повторять существительные, и ставь новую информацию в конец предложения.
  1. Vazifaga qarab tanlang: qo'shish, qarama-qarshi qo'yish, sabab, natija, maqsad, tartib, misol keltirish.
  2. Gapmi yoki otmi? although, even though, because, since, while + gap (ega + fe'l). despite, in spite of, because of, due to + ot yoki -ing: Despite the rain / Despite being tired.
  3. however, therefore, moreover, as a result odatda yangi gapni boshlaydi (yoki nuqtali verguldan keyin keladi) va vergul oladi: It rained. However, we went out.
  4. but, so, and ikki gapni bitta gapda bog'laydi: I was tired, but I kept working. Although va but ni birga ishlatmang.
  5. Maqsad: to / in order to / so as to + fe'l. so that + gap: I left early so that I wouldn't be late.
  6. Bog'lanish: bog'lovchilardan tashqari, otlarni takrorlamaslik uchun olmoshlar va ishora so'zlaridan (it, this, that, such) foydalaning, yangi ma'lumotni gap oxiriga qo'ying.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. What is the function? Contrast, reason, result, purpose, example, order, addition.
  2. What comes after the linker? A full clause → although, because, so that. A noun or -ing → despite, because of, due to.
  3. Where does it go? New sentence → however, therefore, moreover + comma. Same sentence → but, so, and, although.

Same idea, three ways

It rained, but we went out. / Although it rained, we went out. / Despite the rain, we went out.

Common mistakes

  • Although it rained, but we went out. Although it rained, we went out.
  • Despite it rained, we went out. Despite the rain, we went out. / Although it rained, we went out.
  • I stayed home because of it rained. I stayed home because it rained. / because of the rain.
  • It rained, however we went out. It rained. However, we went out.
One idea needs one linker: although OR but, not both.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Какая функция? Противопоставление, причина, результат, цель, пример, порядок, добавление.
  2. Что идёт после связки? Целое предложение → although, because, so that. Существительное или -ing → despite, because of, due to.
  3. Где она стоит? Новое предложение → however, therefore, moreover + запятая. То же предложение → but, so, and, although.

Одна идея, три способа

It rained, but we went out. / Although it rained, we went out. / Despite the rain, we went out.

Частые ошибки

  • Although it rained, but we went out. Although it rained, we went out.
  • Despite it rained, we went out. Despite the rain, we went out. / Although it rained, we went out.
  • I stayed home because of it rained. I stayed home because it rained. / because of the rain.
  • It rained, however we went out. It rained. However, we went out.
Одной идее нужна одна связка: although ИЛИ but, а не обе.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Vazifasi nima? Qarama-qarshilik, sabab, natija, maqsad, misol, tartib, qo'shish.
  2. Bog'lovchidan keyin nima keladi? To'liq gap → although, because, so that. Ot yoki -ing → despite, because of, due to.
  3. U qayerda turadi? Yangi gap → however, therefore, moreover + vergul. Bir gap ichida → but, so, and, although.

Real-life use

Linkers make emails, essays and speaking sound organised and natural.

Giving reasons

"I was late because of the traffic. We stayed home because it rained."

Comparing opinions

"Some people enjoy cities, whereas others prefer villages."

Emails and essays

"Firstly, the price is low. Moreover, the quality is high. However, delivery is slow. In conclusion, it is a good choice."

IELTS Speaking and Writing

"Although online learning is flexible, it can feel lonely. As a result, many students prefer a mix of both."

English only

Examples and drill

Addition

He is smart. Moreover, he works hard. She speaks French as well as Spanish.

Contrast

Although it rained, we went out. Despite the rain, we went out. He is rich, whereas his brother is poor.

Cause and result

We stayed home because of the rain. It rained. As a result, we stayed home.

Purpose

I study in order to pass. I left early so that I wouldn't be late.

Order and example

Firstly, ... Then, ... Finally, ... Fruit, such as apples and pears, is healthy.
