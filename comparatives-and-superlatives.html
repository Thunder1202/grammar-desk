Comparatives and superlatives

General info

We use comparatives to compare two things or people, and superlatives to say which one is number one in a group.

We use them for

  • Comparing two: My brother is taller than me.
  • The top or bottom of a group: Tashkent is the biggest city in Uzbekistan.
  • Saying things are equal: This phone is as good as that one.

Words that go with them

than, the, as ... as, much, a bit, a lot, in, of.

Russian and Uzbek add an ending or a word (выше, самый высокий / balandroq, eng baland). English does it too: -er / -est, or more / most.

Сравнительная степень нужна, чтобы сравнить двух людей или предметы. Превосходная степень показывает, кто или что номер один в группе.

Когда используем

  • Сравнение двух: My brother is taller than me.
  • Лучший или худший в группе: Tashkent is the biggest city in Uzbekistan.
  • Равенство: This phone is as good as that one.

Слова-подсказки

than, the, as ... as, much, a bit, a lot, in, of.

В русском и узбекском тоже есть окончания и слова (выше, самый высокий / balandroq, eng baland). В английском: -er / -est или more / most.

Qiyoslash darajasi ikki odam yoki narsani solishtirish uchun ishlatiladi. Orttirma daraja guruhda kim yoki nima birinchi ekanini bildiradi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Ikkitasini solishtirish: My brother is taller than me.
  • Guruhdagi eng yaxshi yoki eng yomon: Tashkent is the biggest city in Uzbekistan.
  • Tenglik: This phone is as good as that one.

Yordamchi so'zlar

than, the, as ... as, much, a bit, a lot, in, of.

O'zbek tilida ham qo'shimcha va so'zlar bor (balandroq, eng baland). Inglizchada: -er / -est yoki more / most.

Teacher's rule

AdjectiveComparativeSuperlative
short: talltallerthe tallest
ends in -e: nicenicerthe nicest
1 vowel + 1 consonant: bigbiggerthe biggest
consonant + y: easyeasierthe easiest
long: expensivemore expensivethe most expensive
irregular: good / bad / farbetter / worse / furtherthe best / the worst / the furthest
  1. Short adjectives (1 syllable): add -er / -est. Spelling: nice → nicer, big → bigger, easy → easier.
  2. Long adjectives (3+ syllables): more / the most + adjective. Two-syllable adjectives ending in -y take -ier / -iest.
  3. Than: comparative + than: She is older than me.
  4. The: always use the before a superlative: the oldest.
  5. Group: superlative + in (a place or group) or of (a number): the best in the class, the best of the three.
  6. Equal: as + adjective + as. Not equal: not as ... as.
  1. Короткие прилагательные (1 слог): добавляем -er / -est. Правописание: nice → nicer, big → bigger, easy → easier.
  2. Длинные прилагательные (3+ слога): more / the most + прилагательное. Двусложные на -y: -ier / -iest.
  3. Than: сравнительная + than: She is older than me.
  4. The: перед превосходной всегда the: the oldest.
  5. Группа: превосходная + in (место или группа) или of (число): the best in the class, the best of the three.
  6. Равенство: as + прилагательное + as. Неравенство: not as ... as.
  1. Qisqa sifatlar (1 bo'g'in): -er / -est qo'shamiz. Imlo: nice → nicer, big → bigger, easy → easier.
  2. Uzun sifatlar (3+ bo'g'in): more / the most + sifat. -y bilan tugaydigan ikki bo'g'inli sifatlar: -ier / -iest.
  3. Than: qiyoslash + than: She is older than me.
  4. The: orttirma darajadan oldin doim the: the oldest.
  5. Guruh: orttirma + in (joy yoki guruh) yoki of (son): the best in the class, the best of the three.
  6. Tenglik: as + sifat + as. Teng emas: not as ... as.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Comparing two or choosing the top of a group? Two → comparative + than. Group → the + superlative.
  2. Is the adjective short or long? Short → -er / -est. Long → more / most.
  3. Is it irregular? good, bad, far change completely.

Common mistakes

  • She is more tall than me. She is taller than me.
  • He is the more fast. He is the fastest.
  • This is more good. This is better.
  • It is the most big city. It is the biggest city.
Never use -er and more together. One only.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Сравниваю двух или выбираю лучшего в группе? Двух → сравнительная + than. Группа → the + превосходная.
  2. Прилагательное короткое или длинное? Короткое → -er / -est. Длинное → more / most.
  3. Оно неправильное? good, bad, far меняются полностью.

Частые ошибки

  • She is more tall than me. She is taller than me.
  • He is the more fast. He is the fastest.
  • This is more good. This is better.
  • It is the most big city. It is the biggest city.
Никогда не ставьте -er и more вместе. Только одно.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Ikkitasini solishtiryapmanmi yoki guruhdan eng yaxshisini tanlayapmanmi? Ikkita → qiyoslash + than. Guruh → the + orttirma.
  2. Sifat qisqami yoki uzunmi? Qisqa → -er / -est. Uzun → more / most.
  3. U noto'g'rimi? good, bad, far butunlay o'zgaradi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She is more tall than me. She is taller than me.
  • He is the more fast. He is the fastest.
  • This is more good. This is better.
  • It is the most big city. It is the biggest city.
-er va more ni hech qachon birga ishlatmang. Faqat bittasi.

Real-life use

You compare things every day when you choose, shop and talk about people.

Shopping

"This one is cheaper, but that one is better quality."

Choosing

"Which is the most comfortable hotel in the city?"

Talking about people

"My sister is younger than me, but she is more serious."

IELTS Speaking: describing a place

"It is the most beautiful place I have ever visited. It is much quieter than the city."

English only

Examples and drill

Comparatives

My brother is taller than me. This test is easier than the last one.

Superlatives

Tashkent is the biggest city in Uzbekistan. It is the most interesting museum in town.

Equal

This phone is as good as that one. English is not as difficult as I thought.

Irregular

Good, better, the best. Bad, worse, the worst.

Drill

1. Tom is (tall) ___ than Ben.
taller
2. This bag is (expensive) ___ than that one.
more expensive
3. She is the (young) ___ in the family.
youngest
4. My phone is (good) ___ than yours.
better
5. It is the (big) ___ city in the country.
biggest
6. Today is (hot) ___ than yesterday.
hotter
7. Correct it: He is more taller than me.
He is taller than me.
