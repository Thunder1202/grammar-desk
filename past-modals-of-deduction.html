Past modals of deduction

General info

Past modals of deduction help us guess about the past from the evidence we see now. The modal shows how sure we are.

We use them for

  • Sure it happened: The window is open. Someone must have broken in.
  • Sure it did not happen: She can't have forgotten. I reminded her yesterday.
  • Possible, not sure: He isn't here. He might have missed the bus.

Words that go with them

must have, can't have, couldn't have, might have, may have, could have, might not have.

Russian: наверное, он ушёл / не мог он забыть. Uzbek: ketgan bo'lsa kerak / unutgan bo'lishi mumkin emas. English uses modal + have + participle.

Модальные глаголы предположения о прошлом помогают строить догадки о прошлом по признакам, которые видим сейчас. Модальный показывает степень уверенности.

Когда используем

  • Уверены, что это было: The window is open. Someone must have broken in.
  • Уверены, что этого не было: She can't have forgotten. I reminded her yesterday.
  • Возможно, не уверены: He isn't here. He might have missed the bus.

Слова-подсказки

must have, can't have, couldn't have, might have, may have, could have, might not have.

Русский: наверное, он ушёл / не мог он забыть. Узбекский: ketgan bo'lsa kerak / unutgan bo'lishi mumkin emas. В английском модальный + have + третья форма.

O'tmish uchun taxmin modallari hozir ko'rayotgan belgilarga qarab o'tmish haqida taxmin qilishga yordam beradi. Modal qanchalik ishonchimiz borligini ko'rsatadi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Bo'lganiga ishonamiz: The window is open. Someone must have broken in.
  • Bo'lmaganiga ishonamiz: She can't have forgotten. I reminded her yesterday.
  • Mumkin, ishonchimiz yo'q: He isn't here. He might have missed the bus.

Yordamchi so'zlar

must have, can't have, couldn't have, might have, may have, could have, might not have.

Rus tili: наверное, он ушёл / не мог он забыть. O'zbek tili: ketgan bo'lsa kerak / unutgan bo'lishi mumkin emas. Inglizchada modal + have + uchinchi shakl.

Teacher's rule

  1. Use: a guess about a past situation, made from evidence we have now.
  2. must have + participle = sure it happened: The lights were on. They must have been at home.
  3. can't / couldn't have + participle = sure it did not happen: She can't have seen us. Never use mustn't have.
  4. may / might / could have = possible. Negative: might not have / may not have = possible it did not happen. Do not use couldn't have for that (couldn't = impossible).
  5. Continuous: modal + have been + -ing for an action in progress: She must have been sleeping.
  6. Say have, not of: must have, might have, could have. Never must of.
  1. Употребление: догадка о прошлой ситуации по признакам, которые есть сейчас.
  2. must have + третья форма = уверены, что это было: The lights were on. They must have been at home.
  3. can't / couldn't have + третья форма = уверены, что этого не было: She can't have seen us. Никогда не mustn't have.
  4. may / might / could have = возможно. Отрицание: might not have / may not have = возможно, этого не было. Для этого не используй couldn't have (couldn't = невозможно).
  5. Continuous: модальный + have been + -ing для действия в процессе: She must have been sleeping.
  6. Говори have, а не of: must have, might have, could have. Никогда must of.
  1. Qo'llanishi: hozirgi belgilarga asoslangan o'tmishdagi holat haqida taxmin.
  2. must have + uchinchi shakl = bo'lganiga ishonamiz: The lights were on. They must have been at home.
  3. can't / couldn't have + uchinchi shakl = bo'lmaganiga ishonamiz: She can't have seen us. Hech qachon mustn't have emas.
  4. may / might / could have = mumkin. Inkor: might not have / may not have = bo'lmagan bo'lishi mumkin. Buning uchun couldn't have ishlatmang (couldn't = mumkin emas).
  5. Continuous: modal + have been + -ing davom etgan ish uchun: She must have been sleeping.
  6. have deng, of emas: must have, might have, could have. Hech qachon must of emas.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. How sure am I about the past? Very sure it happened → must have. Very sure it did not → can't have. Not sure → might / may / could have.
  2. Was the action in progress? Yes → modal + have been + -ing.
  3. Is it possible it did NOT happen? Yes → might not have. Impossible → couldn't have.

Compare

He might not have heard us. (maybe he didn't) / He couldn't have heard us. (impossible: he was too far away)

Common mistakes

  • He mustn't have seen us. He can't have seen us.
  • She must of left early. She must have left early.
  • He might didn't hear us. He might not have heard us.
  • They must have sleeping. They must have been sleeping.
The opposite of must have is can't have, not mustn't have.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Насколько я уверен насчёт прошлого? Очень уверен, что было → must have. Очень уверен, что не было → can't have. Не уверен → might / may / could have.
  2. Действие было в процессе? Да → модальный + have been + -ing.
  3. Возможно, этого НЕ было? Да → might not have. Невозможно → couldn't have.

Сравни

He might not have heard us. (может быть, не услышал) / He couldn't have heard us. (невозможно: был слишком далеко)

Частые ошибки

  • He mustn't have seen us. He can't have seen us.
  • She must of left early. She must have left early.
  • He might didn't hear us. He might not have heard us.
  • They must have sleeping. They must have been sleeping.
Противоположность must have — can't have, а не mustn't have.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. O'tmish haqida qanchalik ishonchim bor? Bo'lganiga juda ishonaman → must have. Bo'lmaganiga juda ishonaman → can't have. Ishonchim yo'q → might / may / could have.
  2. Ish davom etayotgan edimi? Ha → modal + have been + -ing.
  3. Bo'lmagan bo'lishi mumkinmi? Ha → might not have. Mumkin emas → couldn't have.

Solishtiring

He might not have heard us. (balki eshitmagandir) / He couldn't have heard us. (mumkin emas: juda uzoqda edi)

Real-life use

We use past deduction when we look at evidence and guess what happened.

Looking at clues

"The window is open and the room is a mess. Someone must have broken in."

When someone is late

"He isn't here yet. He must have missed the bus, or he might have forgotten."

Rejecting an idea

"She can't have forgotten. I reminded her yesterday."

IELTS Speaking: describing a photo

"The man looks tired. He must have been working all night, or he might have travelled a long way."

Examples and drill

Sure it happened

She must have left early. They must have been at home.

Sure it did not happen

Possible

He might have forgotten. She may have missed the bus. It could have been a mistake.

Possible it did not

He might not have heard us.

In progress

They must have been sleeping. She might have been working.
