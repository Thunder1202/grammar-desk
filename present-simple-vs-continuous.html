Present Simple vs Continuous

General info

Both tenses talk about the present, but they show it in different ways. Present Simple is the general picture: habits, facts and permanent situations. Present Continuous is a snapshot: what is happening now, around now, or only for a short time.

Present Simple

  • Habits and routines: I get up at 7.
  • Facts and permanent situations: She lives in Samarkand.

Present Continuous

  • Happening now: She is cooking dinner.
  • Temporary situations: She is staying with her aunt this week.
Think of a film and a photo. Present Simple is the whole film (every day). Present Continuous is one photo (right now).

Оба времени говорят о настоящем, но по-разному. Present Simple — общая картина: привычки, факты и постоянные ситуации. Present Continuous — моментальный снимок: что происходит сейчас, в данный период или временно.

Present Simple

  • Привычки и распорядок: I get up at 7.
  • Факты и постоянные ситуации: She lives in Samarkand.

Present Continuous

  • Происходит сейчас: She is cooking dinner.
  • Временные ситуации: She is staying with her aunt this week.
Представь фильм и фотографию. Present Simple — весь фильм (каждый день). Present Continuous — один кадр (прямо сейчас).

Ikkala zamon ham hozirgi vaqt haqida, lekin turlicha ifodalaydi. Present Simple — umumiy manzara: odatlar, faktlar va doimiy holatlar. Present Continuous — lahzalik surat: hozir, shu davrda yoki vaqtincha bo'layotgan ish.

Present Simple

  • Odatlar va kun tartibi: I get up at 7.
  • Faktlar va doimiy holatlar: She lives in Samarkand.

Present Continuous

  • Hozir bo'layotgan ish: She is cooking dinner.
  • Vaqtinchalik holatlar: She is staying with her aunt this week.
Kino va suratni tasavvur qiling. Present Simple — butun kino (har kuni). Present Continuous — bitta surat (aynan hozir).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Can you see it happening right now? Yes → Present Continuous.
  2. Is it true in general, or does it happen every day? Yes → Present Simple.
  3. Is the verb (action word) know, like, want or need? Yes → Present Simple, always.

Common mistakes

  • I am going to school every day. I go to school every day.
  • She works now. She is working now.
  • I am liking coffee. I like coffee.
  • Do you working? Are you working? / Do you work?
Every day = Simple. Right now = Continuous. Look at the time word first.

In plain words

Think of a photo and a calendar. A photo shows this moment. A calendar shows what happens again and again. English has one form for each.

  • I drink tea every morning. Calendar: this happens every day.
  • I am drinking tea now. Photo: this is happening at this moment.
  • He works in a bank, but today he is working at home. Normal job, then something different only for today.

Ask yourself: is this my normal life, or only this moment?

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Видишь ли ты действие прямо сейчас? Да → Present Continuous.
  2. Это верно вообще или происходит каждый день? Да → Present Simple.
  3. Глагол (это слово-действие) know, like, want или need? Да → Present Simple, всегда.

Частые ошибки

  • I am going to school every day. I go to school every day.
  • She works now. She is working now.
  • I am liking coffee. I like coffee.
  • Do you working? Are you working? / Do you work?
Каждый день = Simple. Прямо сейчас = Continuous. Сначала посмотри на слово-подсказку.

Простыми словами

Представьте фотографию и календарь. Фото показывает этот момент. Календарь показывает то, что повторяется снова и снова. В английском для каждого своя форма.

  • I drink tea every morning. Я пью чай каждое утро. Календарь: так каждый день.
  • I am drinking tea now. Я пью чай сейчас. Фото: это происходит в данный момент.
  • He works in a bank, but today he is working at home. Он работает в банке, но сегодня работает дома. Обычная работа, а сегодня иначе.

Спросите себя: это моя обычная жизнь или только этот момент?

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Ishni aynan hozir ko'ryapsizmi? Ha → Present Continuous.
  2. Bu umuman to'g'rimi yoki har kuni bo'ladimi? Ha → Present Simple.
  3. Fe'l (harakat so'zi) know, like, want yoki needmi? Ha → Present Simple, doim.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I am going to school every day. I go to school every day.
  • She works now. She is working now.
  • I am liking coffee. I like coffee.
  • Do you working? Are you working? / Do you work?
Har kuni = Simple. Aynan hozir = Continuous. Avval yordamchi so'zga qarang.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Fotosurat va kalendarni tasavvur qiling. Fotosurat shu lahzani ko'rsatadi. Kalendar qayta-qayta bo'ladigan narsani ko'rsatadi. Ingliz tilida har biri uchun alohida shakl bor.

  • I drink tea every morning. Men har kuni ertalab choy ichaman. Kalendar: bu har kuni bo'ladi.
  • I am drinking tea now. Men hozir choy ichyapman. Fotosurat: bu aynan hozir bo'lyapti.
  • He works in a bank, but today he is working at home. U bankda ishlaydi, lekin bugun uyda ishlayapti. Doimiy ishi bor, bugun esa boshqacha.

O'zingizdan so'rang: bu oddiy hayotimmi yoki faqat shu lahzami?

Real-life use

When people talk, they use both tenses all the time.

Friendly chat

"What do you do?" "I'm a teacher." "And what are you doing today?" "I'm preparing lessons."

A change in your day

"I usually take the bus, but today I'm walking."

Job interviews

"I work in sales. This month I'm leading a new project."

Weather talk

"It rains a lot here, but it isn't raining today."

IELTS Speaking

"I usually study in the evening. Right now I'm preparing for my exam."
