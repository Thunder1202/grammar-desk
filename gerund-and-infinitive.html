Gerund and infinitive

General info

At B1 we go deeper into verb patterns. After a verb we use the gerund (-ing), the infinitive (to + verb), an object + infinitive, or a base verb. Some verbs even change meaning.

We use them for

  • Gerund after some verbs: She suggested going out.
  • Infinitive after others: He managed to finish.
  • Object + infinitive: She told me to wait.
  • Make / let + base verb: He made me laugh. Let me help.

Words that go with them

suggest, avoid, decide, manage, tell, allow, make, let, stop, remember, try.

Russian and Uzbek use the infinitive after almost every verb (я посоветовал ему подождать / unga kutishni maslahat berdim). English needs you to learn each verb pattern.

На уровне B1 мы глубже изучаем глагольные модели. После глагола идёт герундий (-ing), инфинитив (to + глагол), дополнение + инфинитив или основная форма. Некоторые глаголы даже меняют значение.

Когда используем

  • Герундий после одних глаголов: She suggested going out.
  • Инфинитив после других: He managed to finish.
  • Дополнение + инфинитив: She told me to wait.
  • Make / let + основная форма: He made me laugh. Let me help.

Слова-подсказки

suggest, avoid, decide, manage, tell, allow, make, let, stop, remember, try.

В русском и узбекском после почти любого глагола инфинитив (я посоветовал ему подождать / unga kutishni maslahat berdim). В английском нужно учить модель каждого глагола.

B1 darajasida fe'l qoliplarini chuqurroq o'rganamiz. Fe'ldan keyin gerundiy (-ing), infinitiv (to + fe'l), to'ldiruvchi + infinitiv yoki asosiy shakl keladi. Ba'zi fe'llar ma'nosini ham o'zgartiradi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin gerundiy: She suggested going out.
  • Boshqalaridan keyin infinitiv: He managed to finish.
  • To'ldiruvchi + infinitiv: She told me to wait.
  • Make / let + asosiy shakl: He made me laugh. Let me help.

Yordamchi so'zlar

suggest, avoid, decide, manage, tell, allow, make, let, stop, remember, try.

Rus va o'zbek tilida deyarli har bir fe'ldan keyin infinitiv (я посоветовал ему подождать / unga kutishni maslahat berdim). Inglizchada har bir fe'lning qolipini o'rganish kerak.

  1. Gerund after enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep, miss, finish, give up: She suggested going out.
  2. Infinitive after decide, hope, manage, afford, refuse, promise, seem, agree, plan: He managed to finish.
  3. Verb + object + to after want, ask, tell, allow, advise, invite, expect: She told me to wait.
  4. make and let + object + base verb (no to): He made me wait. They let us go. In the passive, make takes to: I was made to wait.
  5. Meaning changes: stop doing = quit, stop to do = pause in order to. remember doing = recall, remember to do = not forget a task. try doing = experiment, try to do = attempt.
  6. After a preposition always -ing: interested in learning, afraid of making mistakes. After many adjectives we use to: happy to help.
  1. Герундий после enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep, miss, finish, give up: She suggested going out.
  2. Инфинитив после decide, hope, manage, afford, refuse, promise, seem, agree, plan: He managed to finish.
  3. Глагол + дополнение + to после want, ask, tell, allow, advise, invite, expect: She told me to wait.
  4. make и let + дополнение + основная форма (без to): He made me wait. They let us go. В пассиве make берёт to: I was made to wait.
  5. Значение меняется: stop doing = бросить, stop to do = остановиться, чтобы. remember doing = помнить, remember to do = не забыть сделать. try doing = попробовать как эксперимент, try to do = пытаться.
  6. После предлога всегда -ing: interested in learning, afraid of making mistakes. После многих прилагательных to: happy to help.
  1. Gerundiy enjoy, avoid, mind, suggest, consider, keep, miss, finish, give up dan keyin: She suggested going out.
  2. Infinitiv decide, hope, manage, afford, refuse, promise, seem, agree, plan dan keyin: He managed to finish.
  3. Fe'l + to'ldiruvchi + to want, ask, tell, allow, advise, invite, expect dan keyin: She told me to wait.
  4. make va let + to'ldiruvchi + asosiy shakl (to siz): He made me wait. They let us go. Majhul nisbatda make to oladi: I was made to wait.
  5. Ma'no o'zgaradi: stop doing = tashlash, stop to do = ... uchun to'xtash. remember doing = eslash, remember to do = unutmaslik. try doing = tajriba qilib ko'rish, try to do = urinish.
  6. Predlogdan keyin doim -ing: interested in learning, afraid of making mistakes. Ko'p sifatlardan keyin to: happy to help.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is there a preposition before the verb? Yes → -ing.
  2. Is there an object (me, him, us) after the first verb? Yes → want / tell / ask + object + to. Make / let + object + base verb.
  3. Is the first verb stop, remember, try or forget? Check the meaning before you choose -ing or to.

Stop: two meanings

I stopped smoking. (I quit.) / I stopped to smoke. (I paused in order to smoke.)

Common mistakes

  • I suggest to go home. I suggest going home.
  • She made me to wait. She made me wait.
  • He decided going abroad. He decided to go abroad.
  • I stopped to smoke last year. (I quit.) I stopped smoking last year.
Verbs of wanting and planning take to. Verbs of liking, avoiding and finishing take -ing. Learn the exceptions as chunks.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Перед глаголом стоит предлог? Да → -ing.
  2. После первого глагола есть дополнение (me, him, us)? Да → want / tell / ask + дополнение + to. Make / let + дополнение + основная форма.
  3. Первый глагол — stop, remember, try или forget? Проверь значение, прежде чем выбрать -ing или to.

Stop: два значения

I stopped smoking. (Бросил.) / I stopped to smoke. (Остановился, чтобы покурить.)

Частые ошибки

  • I suggest to go home. I suggest going home.
  • She made me to wait. She made me wait.
  • He decided going abroad. He decided to go abroad.
  • I stopped to smoke last year. (I quit.) I stopped smoking last year.
Желание и планы → to. Нравиться, избегать, заканчивать → -ing. Исключения учи как готовые блоки.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Fe'l oldida predlog bormi? Ha → -ing.
  2. Birinchi fe'ldan keyin to'ldiruvchi (me, him, us) bormi? Ha → want / tell / ask + to'ldiruvchi + to. Make / let + to'ldiruvchi + asosiy shakl.
  3. Birinchi fe'l stop, remember, try yoki forget mi? -ing yoki to ni tanlashdan oldin ma'noni tekshiring.

Stop: ikki ma'no

I stopped smoking. (Tashladim.) / I stopped to smoke. (Chekish uchun to'xtadim.)

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I suggest to go home. I suggest going home.
  • She made me to wait. She made me wait.
  • He decided going abroad. He decided to go abroad.
  • I stopped to smoke last year. (I quit.) I stopped smoking last year.
Xohlash va rejalar → to. Yoqtirish, qochish, tugatish → -ing. Istisnolarni tayyor birikma sifatida o'rganing.

Real-life use

These patterns appear in suggestions, instructions, rules and advice.

Suggestions

"I suggest meeting at six. Why not consider taking the earlier train?"

Instructions and rules

"The teacher told us to open our books. My parents don't let me stay out late."

Advice

"The doctor advised him to sleep more. Remember to drink water."

IELTS Speaking: plans and interests

"I'm planning to study abroad, and I'm interested in learning more about business. My teacher encouraged me to take the test."

English only

Examples and drill

Gerund

She suggested going out. I avoid driving at night. Do you mind waiting?

Infinitive

He managed to finish. They refused to help. I can't afford to buy it.

Object + to

She told me to wait. They allowed us to leave.

Make and let

He made me laugh. Let me help you.

Meaning changes

I stopped smoking. I stopped to smoke. I remember locking the door. Remember to lock the door.

Drill

1. I enjoy ___ (cook).
cooking
2. She decided ___ (leave).
to leave
3. He told me ___ (wait).
to wait
4. The teacher made us ___ (repeat).
repeat
5. Choose: I stopped (to buy / buying) coffee on the way to work.
to buy
6. Choose: Remember (to lock / locking) the door.
to lock
7. Correct it: She made me to wait.
She made me wait.
