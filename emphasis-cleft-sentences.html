Emphasis: cleft sentences

General info

A cleft sentence splits one idea into two parts so that one piece gets all the attention. English likes the most important information at the end of a sentence, and clefts help us put it there.

Normal and cleft

Tom broke the window. → It was Tom who broke the window. → What Tom broke was the window.

We use them to

  • Correct a wrong idea: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it.
  • Stress something new or surprising: What surprised me was the price.
  • Organise a talk or an essay: The reason why sales fell is that prices rose.
  • Sound clear and strong: All I want is some peace.

Words that go with them

it was ... that / who, what ... is, all ... is, the reason why, the thing that.

In Russian and Uzbek we move words or use voice stress (это Том разбил окно / oynani Tom sindirdi). English often adds a frame, like It was ... that, to show the focus.

Расщеплённое предложение делит одну мысль на две части, чтобы вся важность пришлась на один элемент. В английском важная информация любит стоять в конце предложения, и такие конструкции помогают её туда поставить.

Обычное и расщеплённое

Tom broke the window. → It was Tom who broke the window. → What Tom broke was the window.

Зачем используем

  • Поправить неверную мысль: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it.
  • Выделить новое или неожиданное: What surprised me was the price.
  • Структурировать выступление или эссе: The reason why sales fell is that prices rose.
  • Звучать ясно и сильно: All I want is some peace.

Слова-подсказки

it was ... that / who, what ... is, all ... is, the reason why, the thing that.

В русском и узбекском мы переставляем слова или выделяем голосом (это Том разбил окно / oynani Tom sindirdi). В английском часто добавляют рамку, например It was ... that, чтобы показать акцент.

Bo'lingan gap (cleft) bitta fikrni ikki qismga bo'ladi, shunda bitta bo'lak butun e'tiborni oladi. Inglizchada eng muhim ma'lumot gap oxirida turishi yoqadi, cleft buni qilishga yordam beradi.

Oddiy va cleft

Tom broke the window. → It was Tom who broke the window. → What Tom broke was the window.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Noto'g'ri fikrni tuzatish: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it.
  • Yangi yoki kutilmagan narsaga urg'u: What surprised me was the price.
  • Nutq yoki esseni tartibga solish: The reason why sales fell is that prices rose.
  • Aniq va kuchli eshitilish: All I want is some peace.

Yordamchi so'zlar

it was ... that / who, what ... is, all ... is, the reason why, the thing that.

Rus va o'zbek tilida so'zlarni almashtiramiz yoki ovoz bilan ajratamiz (это Том разбил окно / oynani Tom sindirdi). Inglizchada urg'uni ko'rsatish uchun ko'pincha It was ... that kabi ramka qo'shiladi.

What Tom did yesterday was break the window.
  1. Two parts: a first part that sets up the topic, and the focus after be. The new or important word comes after be, at the end of the idea.
  2. it-cleft: It + be + focus + that / who clause. Use who (or that) for people. Use that for things, places, times and reasons: It was Tom who ... It was in Paris that ... Be follows the time of the story: It was ... / It is ...
  3. What can be the focus: the subject, the object, or a place, time or reason. For an action (the verb) use a wh-cleft: What Tom did was break the window.
  4. wh-cleft: What + clause + be + focus: What I need is a break. After What ... did, the verb can come with or without to: What she did was call / to call the police.
  5. Reversed wh-cleft: focus + be + what clause: A break is what I need. This puts the focus at the very start.
  6. all-cleft means "only": All I want is some peace. (= The only thing I want is some peace.)
  7. Agreement: in a wh-cleft be agrees with the focus: What I need is time. What I need are answers. In an it-cleft, be stays singular: It was the students who complained.
  8. Correcting and contrasting: add not ...: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it. In speech, stress the focus word. Do not use a cleft in every sentence: one strong cleft in a paragraph is enough.
  1. Две части: первая часть задаёт тему, а акцент стоит после be. Новое или важное слово идёт после be, в конце мысли.
  2. it-cleft: It + be + акцент + that / who. Who (или that) для людей. That для предметов, мест, времени и причин: It was Tom who ... It was in Paris that ... Be соответствует времени рассказа: It was ... / It is ...
  3. Что может быть акцентом: подлежащее, дополнение, место, время или причина. Для действия (глагола) используй wh-cleft: What Tom did was break the window.
  4. wh-cleft: What + часть + be + акцент: What I need is a break. После What ... did глагол идёт с to или без: What she did was call / to call the police.
  5. Обратный wh-cleft: акцент + be + what-часть: A break is what I need. Акцент стоит в самом начале.
  6. all-cleft значит «только»: All I want is some peace. (= The only thing I want is some peace.)
  7. Согласование: в wh-cleft be согласуется с акцентом: What I need is time. What I need are answers. В it-cleft be остаётся в единственном числе: It was the students who complained.
  8. Поправка и контраст: добавь not ...: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it. В речи выдели акцент голосом. Не используй cleft в каждом предложении: одного сильного на абзац достаточно.
  1. Ikki qism: birinchi qism mavzuni belgilaydi, urg'u esa be dan keyin keladi. Yangi yoki muhim so'z be dan keyin, fikr oxirida turadi.
  2. it-cleft: It + be + urg'u + that / who gap. Odamlar uchun who (yoki that). Narsa, joy, vaqt va sabab uchun that: It was Tom who ... It was in Paris that ... Be hikoya vaqtiga mos: It was ... / It is ...
  3. Urg'u nima bo'lishi mumkin: ega, to'ldiruvchi, joy, vaqt yoki sabab. Harakat (fe'l) uchun wh-cleft ishlating: What Tom did was break the window.
  4. wh-cleft: What + gap + be + urg'u: What I need is a break. What ... did dan keyin fe'l to bilan yoki to siz keladi: What she did was call / to call the police.
  5. Teskari wh-cleft: urg'u + be + what gap: A break is what I need. Urg'u eng boshda turadi.
  6. all-cleft «faqat» degani: All I want is some peace. (= The only thing I want is some peace.)
  7. Moslashuv: wh-cleft da be urg'uga moslashadi: What I need is time. What I need are answers. it-cleft da be birlikda qoladi: It was the students who complained.
  8. Tuzatish va qarama-qarshilik: not ... qo'shing: It was Tom, not Anna, who broke it. Nutqda urg'u so'zini ovoz bilan ajrating. Har gapda cleft ishlatmang: bir xatboshida bittasi yetarli.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. What do I want to stress? A person, thing, time, place or reason → it-cleft. An action or a whole idea → wh-cleft.
  2. Do I mean "only"? Yes → all-cleft: All I want is ...
  3. Am I correcting someone? Yes → add not ...: It was Tom, not Anna, who ...

Build it in 3 steps

Tom broke the window yesterday. 1) Choose the focus: Tom. 2) Start with It was. 3) Put the focus after be and join the rest with who / that. → It was Tom who broke the window yesterday.

Why it works

The listener hears the setup first and waits. The focus arrives at the end of that part, so it feels important. That is why the same fact sounds stronger as a cleft.

Common mistakes

  • It was Tom which broke the window. It was Tom who (or that) broke the window.
  • It was yesterday when Tom broke it. It was yesterday that Tom broke it.
  • What I need it is a break. What I need is a break.
  • It were the students who complained. It was the students who complained.
The linking word is that (or who for people). Never which, when or where after It was + focus.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Что я хочу выделить? Человека, предмет, время, место или причину → it-cleft. Действие или всю мысль → wh-cleft.
  2. Я имею в виду «только»? Да → all-cleft: All I want is ...
  3. Я поправляю кого-то? Да → добавь not ...: It was Tom, not Anna, who ...

Строим за 3 шага

Tom broke the window yesterday. 1) Выбери акцент: Tom. 2) Начни с It was. 3) Поставь акцент после be и присоедини остальное через who / that. → It was Tom who broke the window yesterday.

Почему это работает

Слушатель сначала слышит подготовку и ждёт. Акцент приходит в конце этой части, и он кажется важным. Поэтому тот же факт звучит сильнее.

Частые ошибки

  • It was Tom which broke the window. It was Tom who (or that) broke the window.
  • It was yesterday when Tom broke it. It was yesterday that Tom broke it.
  • What I need it is a break. What I need is a break.
  • It were the students who complained. It was the students who complained.
Связка — that (или who для людей). После It was + акцент никогда не which, when и where.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Nimani ta'kidlamoqchiman? Odam, narsa, vaqt, joy yoki sabab → it-cleft. Harakat yoki butun fikr → wh-cleft.
  2. «Faqat» demoqchimanmi? Ha → all-cleft: All I want is ...
  3. Kimnidir tuzatyapmanmi? Ha → not ... qo'shing: It was Tom, not Anna, who ...

3 qadamda yasash

Tom broke the window yesterday. 1) Urg'uni tanlang: Tom. 2) It was bilan boshlang. 3) Urg'uni be dan keyin qo'ying va qolganini who / that bilan ulang. → It was Tom who broke the window yesterday.

Nima uchun ishlaydi

Tinglovchi avval tayyorgarlikni eshitadi va kutadi. Urg'u shu qism oxirida keladi va muhim tuyuladi. Shuning uchun bir xil fakt kuchliroq eshitiladi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • It was Tom which broke the window. It was Tom who (or that) broke the window.
  • It was yesterday when Tom broke it. It was yesterday that Tom broke it.
  • What I need it is a break. What I need is a break.
  • It were the students who complained. It was the students who complained.
Bog'lovchi that (odamlar uchun who). It was + urg'u dan keyin hech qachon which, when va where emas.

Real-life use

Clefts help us correct, complain, present results and sound natural in interviews and essays.

Correcting someone

"No, it was Anna who called, not Tom. It wasn't the price that bothered me, it was the service."

Complaining and reacting

"What annoys me is the noise. What I can't stand is waiting."

Presenting results

"What we found was surprising. The reason why sales fell is that prices rose."

Job interviews

"What I enjoy most is working with people. It is teamwork that makes me productive."

IELTS Speaking and Writing

"What I like most about my city is the people. It is education that changes lives."

Examples and drill

it-clefts

It was Tom who broke the window. It was the price that surprised me. It was in 2019 that she moved. It is not money that matters.

wh-clefts

What I need is a break. What he did was call the police. What surprised me was how quiet it was.

Reversed and all-clefts

A break is what I need. All I want is some peace. All she did was smile.

The reason, the place, the thing

The reason why I called is that I need help. The place where we met was Paris. The thing that worries me is the cost.

Emphasis with do

I do like it! She did call, but nobody answered.
