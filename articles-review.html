Articles review

General info

English has three choices before a noun: a / an, the, or no article. The choice shows whether the listener knows which one we mean.

We use them for

  • a / an: one of many, first mention, jobs: I saw a dog. She is a teacher.
  • the: both people know which one: The dog was big. The sun is hot.
  • No article: general plurals, uncountables, languages, meals, sports: I like music. We speak English.

Words that go with them

a / an, the, zero article, first mention, second mention, general, specific, unique.

Russian and Uzbek have no articles. Ask: does the listener know which one? Yes → the. No → a / an. General → no article.

В английском перед существительным три варианта: a / an, the или без артикля. Выбор показывает, знает ли собеседник, о каком именно предмете речь.

Когда используем

  • a / an: один из многих, первое упоминание, профессии: I saw a dog. She is a teacher.
  • the: обоим понятно, какой именно: The dog was big. The sun is hot.
  • Без артикля: общее множественное и неисчисляемое, языки, приёмы пищи, спорт: I like music. We speak English.

Слова-подсказки

a / an, the, нулевой артикль, первое упоминание, второе упоминание, общее, конкретное, единственный.

В русском и узбекском артиклей нет. Спроси себя: слушатель знает, какой именно? Да → the. Нет → a / an. Общее → без артикля.

Inglizchada otdan oldin uch variant bor: a / an, the yoki artiklsiz. Tanlov tinglovchi aynan qaysi narsa haqida gapirayotganimizni bilishini ko'rsatadi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • a / an: ko'plardan biri, birinchi eslash, kasblar: I saw a dog. She is a teacher.
  • the: ikkalasi ham qaysi ekanini biladi: The dog was big. The sun is hot.
  • Artiklsiz: umumiy ko'plik va sanalmaydigan otlar, tillar, ovqatlar, sport: I like music. We speak English.

Yordamchi so'zlar

a / an, the, nol artikl, birinchi eslash, ikkinchi eslash, umumiy, aniq, yagona.

Rus va o'zbek tilida artikl yo'q. O'zingizdan so'rang: tinglovchi qaysi ekanini biladimi? Ha → the. Yo'q → a / an. Umumiy → artiklsiz.

Teacher's rule

ArticleUseExample
a / anfirst mention, one of many, jobsI saw a dog. She is a nurse.
theknown, second mention, unique, superlative, ordinalThe dog was big. The best day. The first lesson.
no articlegeneral plurals and uncountables, languages, meals, sportsI like ∅ music. We had ∅ lunch.
  1. a / an = one, not specific, first mention, jobs: I saw a car. She's a nurse. Use an before a vowel sound: an apple, an hour.
  2. the = both know which one: second mention (I saw a car. The car was red.), one in the world (the sun), superlatives (the best), ordinals (the first).
  3. No article for general plural and uncountable nouns: I love dogs. Music is important.
  4. No article for languages, meals, sports and most countries, cities and streets: I speak Russian. We had lunch. He plays football. She lives in Tashkent.
  5. the with some places and things: the UK, the USA, the Alps, the Nile, the Black Sea. Also instruments: play the piano.
  6. Fixed expressions have no article: go to school / work / bed, in hospital, by bus, at university. Compare: the school (the building).
  1. a / an = один, не конкретный, первое упоминание, профессии: I saw a car. She's a nurse. Перед гласным звуком an: an apple, an hour.
  2. the = обоим понятно, какой: второе упоминание (I saw a car. The car was red.), единственный в мире (the sun), превосходная степень (the best), порядковые (the first).
  3. Без артикля для общего множественного и неисчисляемого: I love dogs. Music is important.
  4. Без артикля для языков, приёмов пищи, спорта и большинства стран, городов и улиц: I speak Russian. We had lunch. He plays football. She lives in Tashkent.
  5. the с некоторыми названиями: the UK, the USA, the Alps, the Nile, the Black Sea. Также музыкальные инструменты: play the piano.
  6. Устойчивые выражения без артикля: go to school / work / bed, in hospital, by bus, at university. Сравни: the school (здание).
  1. a / an = bitta, aniq emas, birinchi eslash, kasblar: I saw a car. She's a nurse. Unli tovush oldidan an: an apple, an hour.
  2. the = ikkalasi ham qaysi ekanini biladi: ikkinchi eslash (I saw a car. The car was red.), dunyoda yagona (the sun), orttirma daraja (the best), tartib son (the first).
  3. Artiklsiz umumiy ko'plik va sanalmaydigan otlar uchun: I love dogs. Music is important.
  4. Artiklsiz tillar, ovqatlar, sport va ko'pchilik mamlakat, shahar va ko'chalar uchun: I speak Russian. We had lunch. He plays football. She lives in Tashkent.
  5. the ba'zi joy nomlari bilan: the UK, the USA, the Alps, the Nile, the Black Sea. Shuningdek cholg'u asboblari: play the piano.
  6. Qotib qolgan iboralar artiklsiz: go to school / work / bed, in hospital, by bus, at university. Solishtiring: the school (bino).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Does the listener know which one? Yes → the. No → a / an (singular) or no article (plural).
  2. Am I talking about things in general? Yes → no article: I like music.
  3. Is it a language, meal, sport or normal country or city? Yes → no article.

First and second mention

I saw a man and a dog. The man was tall, and the dog was black.

Common mistakes

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I saw a dog. A dog was big. I saw a dog. The dog was big.
  • He plays the football. He plays football.
  • I live in the Tashkent. I live in Tashkent.
Singular countable nouns always need a word before them: a, the, my, this. Not one alone.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Слушатель знает, какой именно? Да → the. Нет → a / an (единственное число) или без артикля (множественное).
  2. Я говорю обо всём в целом? Да → без артикля: I like music.
  3. Это язык, приём пищи, спорт или обычная страна или город? Да → без артикля.

Первое и второе упоминание

I saw a man and a dog. The man was tall, and the dog was black.

Частые ошибки

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I saw a dog. A dog was big. I saw a dog. The dog was big.
  • He plays the football. He plays football.
  • I live in the Tashkent. I live in Tashkent.
Перед исчисляемым существительным в единственном числе всегда нужно слово: a, the, my, this. Одно оно не стоит.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Tinglovchi qaysi ekanini biladimi? Ha → the. Yo'q → a / an (birlik) yoki artiklsiz (ko'plik).
  2. Umuman haqida gapiryapmanmi? Ha → artiklsiz: I like music.
  3. Bu til, ovqat, sport yoki oddiy mamlakat yoki shahar mi? Ha → artiklsiz.

Birinchi va ikkinchi eslash

I saw a man and a dog. The man was tall, and the dog was black.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I saw a dog. A dog was big. I saw a dog. The dog was big.
  • He plays the football. He plays football.
  • I live in the Tashkent. I live in Tashkent.
Birlikdagi sanaladigan ot oldida doim so'z bo'lishi kerak: a, the, my, this. Yolg'iz turmaydi.

Real-life use

Articles appear in almost every sentence we say or write.

Telling what you saw

"I saw a man outside. The man was carrying a big box."

Job and work

"I'm a tutor. I work at a school, and I go to work by bus."

Talking about hobbies and places

"I play the guitar and I love music. We speak English in class."

IELTS Speaking: describing your hometown

"I live in a small town. The best thing about it is the park, and the weather is great in spring."
