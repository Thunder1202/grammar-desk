C1/C2

Advanced. 10 topics. Choose one.

  • Inversion (full)Open topicWe cover: negative openers, so / such, place openers, Had / Should / Were. You will be able to: write and speak in a formal, emphatic style.Разбираем: отрицательные начала, so / such, начала с местом, Had / Should / Were. Вы сможете: писать и говорить в формальном, выразительном стиле.Ko'ramiz: inkor boshlanishlar, so / such, joy boshlanishlari, Had / Should / Were. Siz qila olasiz: rasmiy va ta'sirchan uslubda yozish va gapirish.
  • Emphasis: cleft sentencesOpen topicWe cover: It was ... that, What I need is, The thing that. You will be able to: put the focus on the part of the message you choose.Разбираем: It was ... that, What I need is, The thing that. Вы сможете: выделять нужную часть сообщения.Ko'ramiz: It was ... that, What I need is, The thing that. Siz qila olasiz: xabarning kerakli qismiga urg'u berish.
  • NominalisationOpen topicWe cover: turning verbs and adjectives into nouns. You will be able to: write denser, more formal academic sentences.Разбираем: превращение глаголов и прилагательных в существительные. Вы сможете: писать более плотные формальные академические предложения.Ko'ramiz: fe'l va sifatlarni otga aylantirish. Siz qila olasiz: zichroq, rasmiyroq akademik gaplar yozish.
  • Participle clausesOpen topicWe cover: -ing and -ed clauses for time, reason and result. You will be able to: combine ideas in shorter, more elegant sentences.Разбираем: обороты с -ing и -ed для времени, причины и результата. Вы сможете: соединять идеи в более коротких и изящных предложениях.Ko'ramiz: vaqt, sabab va natija uchun -ing va -ed gaplar. Siz qila olasiz: g'oyalarni qisqaroq va chiroyliroq gaplarda birlashtirish.
  • Subjunctive and unreal pastOpen topicWe cover: suggest that he go, It's time you left, as if / as though. You will be able to: express demands, advice and unreal situations formally.Разбираем: suggest that he go, It's time you left, as if / as though. Вы сможете: формально выражать требования, советы и нереальные ситуации.Ko'ramiz: suggest that he go, It's time you left, as if / as though. Siz qila olasiz: talab, maslahat va real bo'lmagan holatlarni rasmiy ifodalash.
  • Advanced passivesOpen topicWe cover: reporting passives, passive infinitives and gerunds, get-passive. You will be able to: report opinions and facts in a formal, impersonal way.Разбираем: безличные пассивные конструкции, пассивный инфинитив и герундий, get-passive. Вы сможете: формально и безлично передавать мнения и факты.Ko'ramiz: shaxssiz majhul qoliplar, majhul infinitiv va gerundiy, get-passive. Siz qila olasiz: fikr va faktlarni rasmiy, shaxssiz tarzda yetkazish.
  • Alternatives to ifOpen topicWe cover: unless, provided that, as long as, otherwise, supposing. You will be able to: express conditions in more varied and precise ways.Разбираем: unless, provided that, as long as, otherwise, supposing. Вы сможете: выражать условия разнообразнее и точнее.Ko'ramiz: unless, provided that, as long as, otherwise, supposing. Siz qila olasiz: shartlarni xilma-xil va aniqroq ifodalash.
  • Register and styleOpen topicWe cover: formal, neutral and informal language. You will be able to: choose words and grammar to suit the reader and situation.Разбираем: формальный, нейтральный и неформальный язык. Вы сможете: выбирать слова и грамматику под читателя и ситуацию.Ko'ramiz: rasmiy, neytral va norasmiy til. Siz qila olasiz: o'quvchi va vaziyatga mos so'z va grammatikani tanlash.
  • Cohesive devices for writingOpen topicWe cover: reference, substitution, ellipsis, linking phrases. You will be able to: make a long text clear, connected and easy to follow.Разбираем: ссылки, замена, пропуск слов, связующие фразы. Вы сможете: делать длинный текст ясным, связным и лёгким для чтения.Ko'ramiz: ishora, almashtirish, so'z tushirish, bog'lovchi iboralar. Siz qila olasiz: uzun matnni aniq, bog'langan va o'qishga qulay qilish.
  • Ellipsis and substitutionOpen topicWe cover: leaving out words and using so, not, do, one. You will be able to: avoid repetition and sound more natural.Разбираем: пропуск слов и замена через so, not, do, one. Вы сможете: избегать повторов и звучать естественнее.Ko'ramiz: so'zlarni tushirish va so, not, do, one bilan almashtirish. Siz qila olasiz: takrordan qochish va tabiiyroq gapirish.
