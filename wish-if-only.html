Wish / If only

General info

Wish and if only express regrets and unreal wishes: we want a situation to be different from reality. If only is stronger and more emotional.

We use them for

  • Present regret: I wish I had a car. (I don't have one.)
  • Past regret: I wish I had studied harder. (I didn't.)
  • Annoyance, wanting change: I wish you would stop talking.

Words that go with them

wish, if only, were, had + participle, would, could, hope.

Russian: жаль, что у меня нет машины / если бы только... Uzbek: kashki mashinam bo'lsa edi. English moves the verb one step back: wish I had, wish I had studied.

Wish и if only выражают сожаления и нереальные желания: мы хотим, чтобы ситуация отличалась от реальности. If only сильнее и эмоциональнее.

Когда используем

  • Сожаление о настоящем: I wish I had a car. (У меня нет.)
  • Сожаление о прошлом: I wish I had studied harder. (Я не учился.)
  • Раздражение, желание перемен: I wish you would stop talking.

Слова-подсказки

wish, if only, were, had + третья форма, would, could, hope.

Русский: жаль, что у меня нет машины / если бы только... Узбекский: kashki mashinam bo'lsa edi. В английском глагол сдвигается на шаг назад: wish I had, wish I had studied.

Wish va if only afsus va real bo'lmagan istaklarni bildiradi: biz vaziyat haqiqatdan boshqacha bo'lishini xohlaymiz. If only kuchliroq va hissiyotliroq.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Hozirgi afsus: I wish I had a car. (Mashinam yo'q.)
  • O'tmishdagi afsus: I wish I had studied harder. (O'qimadim.)
  • G'azab, o'zgarish istagi: I wish you would stop talking.

Yordamchi so'zlar

wish, if only, were, had + uchinchi shakl, would, could, hope.

Rus tili: жаль, что у меня нет машины / если бы только... O'zbek tili: kashki mashinam bo'lsa edi. Inglizchada fe'l bir qadam orqaga suriladi: wish I had, wish I had studied.

Teacher's rule

SituationFormExample
present regretwish + past simpleI wish I had a car.
past regretwish + past perfectI wish I had studied harder.
annoyance / changewish + wouldI wish you would stop talking.
abilitywish + couldI wish I could swim.
strongerIf only + same formsIf only I had more time!
  1. wish / if only + past simple = we want a present situation to be different: I wish I lived near the sea.
  2. were is used for all persons: I wish I were taller. I wish she were here. (Was is common in informal speech.)
  3. wish / if only + past perfect = regret about the past: I wish I had listened. If only I hadn't said that.
  4. wish + would = a complaint or a wish for someone or something else to change: I wish it would stop raining. I wish you would help. Not for yourself: not "I wish I would".
  5. wish + could = a wish for ability: I wish I could swim.
  6. hope or wish? hope = real possibility: I hope you feel better. wish = unreal: I wish I felt better.

  2. were для всех лиц: I wish I were taller. I wish she were here. (В разговорной речи часто was.)
  3. wish / if only + past perfect = сожаление о прошлом: I wish I had listened. If only I hadn't said that.
  4. wish + would = жалоба или желание, чтобы изменился кто-то или что-то другое: I wish it would stop raining. I wish you would help. Не про себя: не «I wish I would».
  5. wish + could = желание уметь: I wish I could swim.
  6. hope или wish? hope = реальная возможность: I hope you feel better. wish = нереально: I wish I felt better.
  1. wish / if only + past simple = hozirgi holat boshqacha bo'lishini istaymiz: I wish I lived near the sea.
  2. were barcha shaxslar uchun: I wish I were taller. I wish she were here. (Og'zaki nutqda was ham keng tarqalgan.)
  3. wish / if only + past perfect = o'tmish haqida afsus: I wish I had listened. If only I hadn't said that.
  4. wish + would = shikoyat yoki boshqa odam yoki narsa o'zgarishini istash: I wish it would stop raining. I wish you would help. O'zingiz uchun emas: «I wish I would» emas.
  5. wish + could = qobiliyat istagi: I wish I could swim.
  6. hope yoki wish? hope = real imkoniyat: I hope you feel better. wish = real emas: I wish I felt better.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it about now or the past? Now → past simple. Past → past perfect.
  2. Do I want someone or something else to change? Yes → wish + would.
  3. Is it really possible? Yes → hope. No, it is unreal → wish.

Fact and wish

Fact: I don't have a car. → I wish I had a car. / Fact: I didn't study. → I wish I had studied.

Common mistakes

  • I wish I would have a car. I wish I had a car.
  • I wish I have studied harder. I wish I had studied harder.
  • I wish I would be taller. I wish I were taller.
  • I wish it stops raining. I wish it would stop raining.
The verb after wish moves one step back, like in the Second and Third Conditional.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Про сейчас или про прошлое? Сейчас → past simple. Прошлое → past perfect.
  2. Хочу, чтобы изменился кто-то или что-то другое? Да → wish + would.
  3. Это действительно возможно? Да → hope. Нет, нереально → wish.

Факт и желание

Факт: I don't have a car. → I wish I had a car. / Факт: I didn't study. → I wish I had studied.

Частые ошибки

  • I wish I would have a car. I wish I had a car.
  • I wish I have studied harder. I wish I had studied harder.
  • I wish I would be taller. I wish I were taller.
  • I wish it stops raining. I wish it would stop raining.
Глагол после wish сдвигается на шаг назад, как в Second и Third Conditional.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Hozir haqidami yoki o'tmish haqidami? Hozir → past simple. O'tmish → past perfect.
  2. Boshqa odam yoki narsa o'zgarishini istayapmanmi? Ha → wish + would.
  3. Bu haqiqatan mumkinmi? Ha → hope. Yo'q, real emas → wish.

Fakt va istak

Fakt: I don't have a car. → I wish I had a car. / Fakt: I didn't study. → I wish I had studied.

Real-life use

We use wish and if only to talk about regrets, dreams and things that annoy us.

Dreams about now

"I wish I lived closer to work. I wish I had more free time."

Regrets

"I wish I had learned to drive earlier. If only I hadn't said that."

Complaints

"I wish you would turn the music down. I wish it would stop raining."

IELTS Speaking: talking about travel

"I wish I could travel more. If only I had more free time, I would visit different countries."
