There is / There are

General info

There is / There are tells the listener that something exists or is present in a place. We use it to introduce something new, usually with a place word. There is goes with one thing or an uncountable noun. There are goes with more than one.

We use it for

  • Saying what exists: There is a park in my city.
  • Describing rooms and places: There are two windows in the room.
  • Giving quantity: There are five students in the class.
  • Introducing new information: There is a problem with the printer.
Uzbek says bor (Xonada stol bor) and Russian says есть. English uses there is / there are, and the verb agrees with the noun that comes after it.

There is / There are сообщает, что что-то существует или находится в каком-то месте. Мы используем это, чтобы ввести новую информацию, обычно с указанием места. There is — с одним предметом или неисчисляемым существительным. There are — с несколькими.

Когда используем

  • Сказать, что что-то есть: There is a park in my city.
  • Описать комнату или место: There are two windows in the room.
  • Назвать количество: There are five students in the class.
  • Ввести новую информацию: There is a problem with the printer.
В русском говорят «есть» (В комнате есть стол). В английском нужно there is / there are, и глагол согласуется с существительным после него.

There is / There are biror narsa mavjudligini yoki biror joyda borligini bildiradi. U yangi ma'lumot berish uchun, odatda joy bilan ishlatiladi. There is — bitta narsa yoki sanalmaydigan ot bilan. There are — bir nechta narsa bilan.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Borligini aytish: There is a park in my city.
  • Xona yoki joyni tasvirlash: There are two windows in the room.
  • Miqdorni aytish: There are five students in the class.
  • Yangi ma'lumot berish: There is a problem with the printer.
O'zbek tilida bor (Xonada stol bor). Inglizchada there is / there are kerak va fe'l undan keyingi ot bilan moslashadi.

Teacher's rule

one / uncountablemore than one
+There is a book.There are two books.
−There isn't a book.There aren't any books.
?Is there a book?Are there any books?
  1. Choose by the noun: is with one thing or an uncountable noun (There is milk), are with plurals.
  2. Short form: There's a bank here. We do not write "there're".
  3. Negative: isn't / aren't. With plurals use any: There aren't any chairs.
  4. Questions: Is there...? Are there...? Short answers: Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. / Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
  5. With a list: the verb agrees with the first noun: There is a pen and two books.

Useful place words

in, on, under, next to, between, behind, in front of.

  1. Выбор по существительному: is с одним предметом или неисчисляемым (There is milk), are со множественным.
  2. Краткая форма: There's a bank here. «there're» на письме не пишем.
  3. Отрицание: isn't / aren't. Во мн. числе с any: There aren't any chairs.
  4. Вопросы: Is there...? Are there...? Краткие ответы: Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. / Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
  5. Со списком: глагол согласуется с первым существительным: There is a pen and two books.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it one thing, or many? One or uncountable → there is. Many → there are.
  2. Negative or question? isn't / aren't, or Is there / Are there at the start.
  3. Which small word? a with one thing. any with plurals in negatives and questions.

Common mistakes

  • There is two cats. There are two cats.
  • Have a park in my city. There is a park in my city.
  • There are a table. There is a table.
  • Is there any books? Are there any books?
The noun after the verb decides is or are. Look at it first.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Предмет один или несколько? Один или неисчисляемое → there is. Несколько → there are.
  2. Отрицание или вопрос? isn't / aren't, или Is there / Are there в начале.
  3. Какое маленькое слово? a с одним предметом. any со мн. числом в отрицаниях и вопросах.

Частые ошибки

  • There is two cats. There are two cats.
  • Have a park in my city. There is a park in my city.
  • There are a table. There is a table.
  • Is there any books? Are there any books?
Существительное после глагола решает: is или are. Сначала посмотри на него.

Real-life use

You need this structure whenever you describe places or ask whether something exists.

Describing your home

"There are two bedrooms and a big kitchen. There's a balcony too."

Asking in a hotel or a shop

"Is there a bank near here?" "Excuse me, is there Wi-Fi?"

Giving directions

"There's a pharmacy next to the metro."

Restaurants

"Are there any vegetarian dishes?"

Describing a photo (IELTS Speaking)

"In the picture there are three people, and there is a dog next to them."
