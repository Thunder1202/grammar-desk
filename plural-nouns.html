Plural nouns

General info

A singular noun names one thing. A plural noun names more than one. Most nouns make the plural by adding -s.

Spelling rules

  • Most nouns: + -s → book / books, pen / pens.
  • Ending in -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x: + -es → bus / buses, box / boxes, watch / watches.
  • Consonant + y: change to -ies → city / cities, baby / babies.
  • Vowel + y: + -s → day / days, boy / boys.
  • Ending in -f / -fe: often → -ves → knife / knives, leaf / leaves.

Irregular plurals

  • man / men, woman / women, child / children, person / people, foot / feet, tooth / teeth, mouse / mice.
  • No change: sheep / sheep, fish / fish, deer / deer.
A few very common nouns do not follow the -s rule: child → children, person → people. Learn them by heart.

Единственное число называет один предмет. Множественное число — больше одного. У большинства существительных множественное образуется добавлением -s.

Правила написания

  • Большинство: + -s → book / books, pen / pens.
  • На -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x: + -es → bus / buses, box / boxes, watch / watches.
  • Согласная + y: меняем на -ies → city / cities, baby / babies.
  • Гласная + y: + -s → day / days, boy / boys.
  • На -f / -fe: часто → -ves → knife / knives, leaf / leaves.

Неправильные формы

  • man / men, woman / women, child / children, person / people, foot / feet, tooth / teeth, mouse / mice.
  • Без изменений: sheep / sheep, fish / fish, deer / deer.
Несколько очень частых слов не подчиняются правилу -s: child → children, person → people. Учите их наизусть.

Birlik bitta narsani bildiradi. Ko'plik birdan ko'pni bildiradi. Ko'p otlarda ko'plik -s qo'shish bilan yasaladi.

Yozish qoidalari

  • Ko'p otlar: + -s → book / books, pen / pens.
  • -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x bilan tugasa: + -es → bus / buses, box / boxes, watch / watches.
  • Undosh + y: -ies ga o'zgaradi → city / cities, baby / babies.
  • Unli + y: + -s → day / days, boy / boys.
  • -f / -fe bilan tugasa: ko'pincha → -ves → knife / knives, leaf / leaves.

Noto'g'ri ko'plik shakllari

  • man / men, woman / women, child / children, person / people, foot / feet, tooth / teeth, mouse / mice.
  • O'zgarmaydi: sheep / sheep, fish / fish, deer / deer.
Bir necha juda ko'p ishlatiladigan ot -s qoidasiga bo'ysunmaydi: child → children, person → people. Ularni yodlab oling.

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. How many? One → singular. More than one → plural.
  2. How does the word end? Check the last letters and use the matching rule.

Common mistakes

  • three boxs three boxes
  • two citys two cities
  • five childs five children
  • many moneys a lot of money
Words like money, water, rice, bread have no plural form. They are uncountable.

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Сколько? Один → единственное. Больше одного → множественное.
  2. На что оканчивается слово? Проверь последние буквы и примени правило.

Частые ошибки

  • three boxs three boxes
  • two citys two cities
  • five childs five children
  • many moneys a lot of money
Слова money, water, rice, bread не имеют формы множественного числа. Они неисчисляемые.

O'zingizga ikkita savol bering

  1. Nechta? Bitta → birlik. Birdan ko'p → ko'plik.
  2. So'z qanday tugaydi? Oxirgi harflarga qarab mos qoidani qo'llang.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • three boxs three boxes
  • two citys two cities
  • five childs five children
  • many moneys a lot of money
money, water, rice, bread kabi so'zlarning ko'plik shakli yo'q. Ular sanalmaydi.

Real-life use

You need plural nouns to talk about more than one thing — every day.

Shopping list

"We need two loaves of bread, three tomatoes and some boxes of eggs."

Family

"I have two brothers and three sisters. My parents have many children."

School

"There are thirty students and six classes in our school."

Describing your city

"There are nice parks, but there aren't many buses."

Talking about money

"I don't have much money, but these shoes are cheap."
