Advanced articles and determiners

General info

At B2 we use articles and determiners (every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, few, little) to talk about groups, amounts and generalisations precisely.

We use them for

  • General statements: Tigers are endangered. The tiger is endangered.
  • Groups of people: the rich, the elderly, the French.
  • Amounts and choices: every student, both of them, a few friends, another coffee.

Words that go with them

the + adjective, every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, other, few, little.

Russian and Uzbek have no articles, but they do have words like каждый, весь, оба / har bir, butun, ikkala. The difficulty in English is the verb and noun form after them.

На уровне B2 мы используем артикли и определители (every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, few, little), чтобы точно говорить о группах, количестве и обобщениях.

Когда используем

  • Общие утверждения: Tigers are endangered. The tiger is endangered.
  • Группы людей: the rich, the elderly, the French.
  • Количество и выбор: every student, both of them, a few friends, another coffee.

Слова-подсказки

the + прилагательное, every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, other, few, little.

В русском и узбекском артиклей нет, но есть слова каждый, весь, оба / har bir, butun, ikkala. В английском сложность в форме глагола и существительного после них.

B2 darajasida artikllar va aniqlovchilar (every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, few, little) yordamida guruhlar, miqdorlar va umumlashmalar haqida aniq gapiramiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Umumiy fikrlar: Tigers are endangered. The tiger is endangered.
  • Odamlar guruhlari: the rich, the elderly, the French.
  • Miqdor va tanlov: every student, both of them, a few friends, another coffee.

Yordamchi so'zlar

the + sifat, every, each, all, whole, both, either, neither, another, other, few, little.

Rus va o'zbek tilida artikl yo'q, lekin каждый, весь, оба / har bir, butun, ikkala so'zlari bor. Inglizchada qiyinchilik ulardan keyingi fe'l va ot shaklida.

the + nationalitya nationality as a groupThe French love cheese.

all / wholeall + plural; the whole + singularall the students / the whole class

  1. General statements: plural without article (Tigers are endangered), the + singular (The tiger is endangered, formal), a / an + singular (A tiger is dangerous).
  2. the + adjective = a group of people, with a plural verb: The rich are not always happy. the + nationality: the French, the British, the Japanese.
  3. every + singular noun + singular verb: Every student has a book. each = one by one: Each student got a prize. Also: each other.
  4. all + plural or uncountable: all students, all the time. whole + the / a + singular: the whole day = all day. both / either / neither are for two things: Neither of them is perfect.
  5. another = one more (another coffee). other + plural (other people). the other = the remaining one: One is a doctor; the other is a teacher.
  6. few / little = not enough (negative). a few / a little = some, enough (positive). few + countable, little + uncountable.
  1. Общие утверждения: множественное без артикля (Tigers are endangered), the + единственное (The tiger is endangered, формально), a / an + единственное (A tiger is dangerous).
  2. the + прилагательное = группа людей, глагол во множественном числе: The rich are not always happy. the + национальность: the French, the British, the Japanese.
  3. every + существительное в ед. числе + глагол в ед. числе: Every student has a book. each = по одному: Each student got a prize. Также: each other.
  4. all + множественное или неисчисляемое: all students, all the time. whole + the / a + ед. число: the whole day = all day. both / either / neither — про два предмета: Neither of them is perfect.
  5. another = ещё один (another coffee). other + множественное (other people). the other = оставшийся: One is a doctor; the other is a teacher.
  6. few / little = не хватает (отрицательно). a few / a little = немного, достаточно (положительно). few + исчисляемое, little + неисчисляемое.
  1. Umumiy fikrlar: artiklsiz ko'plik (Tigers are endangered), the + birlik (The tiger is endangered, rasmiy), a / an + birlik (A tiger is dangerous).
  2. the + sifat = odamlar guruhi, fe'l ko'plikda: The rich are not always happy. the + millat: the French, the British, the Japanese.
  3. every + birlikdagi ot + birlikdagi fe'l: Every student has a book. each = birma-bir: Each student got a prize. Shuningdek: each other.
  4. all + ko'plik yoki sanalmaydigan: all students, all the time. whole + the / a + birlik: the whole day = all day. both / either / neither ikkita narsa uchun: Neither of them is perfect.
  5. another = yana bittasi (another coffee). other + ko'plik (other people). the other = qolgani: One is a doctor; the other is a teacher.
  6. few / little = yetarli emas (salbiy). a few / a little = ozgina, yetarli (ijobiy). few + sanaladigan, little + sanalmaydigan.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Am I talking about a whole group or kind? Use plural without article, or the + adjective for people.
  2. One by one or all together? each = one by one. every = all as a group. Both take a singular noun and verb.
  3. Is the amount enough or not enough? a few / a little = enough. few / little = not enough.

Few or a few?

I have a few friends. (I'm fine.) / I have few friends. (I feel lonely.)

Common mistakes

  • The rich is always happy. The rich are not always happy.
  • Every students passed. Every student passed.
  • I studied all the day. I studied all day. / I studied the whole day.
  • I have a little friends. I have a few friends.
every, each and neither take a singular verb: Every student has a book. Neither of them is perfect.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Говорю о целой группе или виде? Множественное без артикля или the + прилагательное для людей.
  2. По одному или все вместе? each = по одному. every = все как группа. Оба с существительным и глаголом в единственном числе.
  3. Количество достаточное или нет? a few / a little = достаточно. few / little = не хватает.

Few или a few?

I have a few friends. (Всё нормально.) / I have few friends. (Мне одиноко.)

Частые ошибки

  • The rich is always happy. The rich are not always happy.
  • Every students passed. Every student passed.
  • I studied all the day. I studied all day. / I studied the whole day.
  • I have a little friends. I have a few friends.
every, each и neither требуют глагола в единственном числе: Every student has a book. Neither of them is perfect.

  3. Miqdor yetarlimi yoki yo'qmi? a few / a little = yetarli. few / little = yetarli emas.

Real-life use

Precise articles and determiners are important in reports, surveys, essays and formal speaking.

Describing groups

"The elderly need more support. The French are famous for their food."

Rules and surveys

"Every student must register. Each applicant will receive an email. Most of the students agreed."

Talking about quantity

"Few people came, but a few of them stayed until the end."

IELTS Speaking and Writing

"The elderly are often lonely. Few young people visit them, but a few volunteers help every week."

Examples and drill

Generic

Tigers are endangered. The tiger is endangered. A tiger is dangerous.

The + adjective / nationality

The rich are not always happy. The elderly need support. The French love cheese.

Every, each, all, whole

Every student has a book. Each student got a prize. All the students passed. I studied the whole day.

Both, either, neither

Both of them are good. Neither of them is perfect.

Another, other, the other

Can I have another coffee? Some people agree; other people don't. One is a doctor; the other is a teacher.

Few, a few, little, a little

I have few friends. I have a few friends. We have little time. We have a little time.
