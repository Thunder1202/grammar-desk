Future forms review

General info

English has four main ways to talk about the future. The one we choose shows why we speak: a decision, a plan, an arrangement or a timetable.

We use them for

  • will: instant decisions, offers, promises, opinions: I'll help you. I think it will rain.
  • going to: plans and predictions with evidence: I'm going to study law. Look at the clouds!
  • Present continuous: fixed arrangements: I'm meeting Tom at six.
  • Present simple: timetables: The train leaves at 8.

Words that go with them

tomorrow, next week, tonight, I think, probably, when, as soon as, if.

Russian and Uzbek often use the simple future for all of these. English chooses by the reason: now, plan, arrangement, timetable.

В английском четыре основных способа говорить о будущем. Выбор показывает, зачем мы говорим: решение, план, договорённость или расписание.

Когда используем

  • will: мгновенные решения, предложения, обещания, мнения: I'll help you. I think it will rain.
  • going to: планы и прогнозы по видимым признакам: I'm going to study law. Look at the clouds!
  • Present continuous: твёрдые договорённости: I'm meeting Tom at six.
  • Present simple: расписание: The train leaves at 8.

Слова-подсказки

tomorrow, next week, tonight, I think, probably, when, as soon as, if.

В русском и узбекском часто одно будущее время для всего. В английском выбор зависит от причины: сейчас, план, договорённость, расписание.

Inglizchada kelajak haqida gapirishning to'rtta asosiy usuli bor. Tanlov nima uchun gapirayotganingizni ko'rsatadi: qaror, reja, kelishuv yoki jadval.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • will: bir zumlik qarorlar, takliflar, va'dalar, fikrlar: I'll help you. I think it will rain.
  • going to: rejalar va ko'rinib turgan belgilarga asoslangan bashorat: I'm going to study law. Look at the clouds!
  • Present continuous: qat'iy kelishuvlar: I'm meeting Tom at six.
  • Present simple: jadval: The train leaves at 8.

Yordamchi so'zlar

Teacher's rule

FormUseExample
willinstant decision, offer, promise, opinionI'll carry it. I think it will rain.
going toplan; prediction with evidenceI'm going to learn French. It's going to rain.
present continuousfixed arrangementI'm meeting Tom at six.
present simpletimetableThe train leaves at 8.
  1. will = a decision at the moment of speaking, an offer, a promise, or an opinion about the future (I think, probably, I'm sure): It's heavy. I'll help you.
  2. be going to = a plan made before speaking, or a prediction from evidence we can see: I'm going to start a course. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  3. Present continuous = an arrangement with time and often a person or place: I'm seeing the dentist at four.
  4. Present simple = a timetable or public schedule: The film starts at eight. The bus leaves at 7:30.
  5. Time clauses: after when, before, as soon as, until, if use the present simple, not will: When I arrive, I'll call you. If it rains, we'll stay home.
  6. More than one form can be correct. The difference is the feeling: going to = I have decided, present continuous = it is already arranged.
  1. will = решение в момент речи, предложение, обещание или мнение о будущем (I think, probably, I'm sure): It's heavy. I'll help you.
  2. be going to = план, сделанный до речи, или прогноз по видимым признакам: I'm going to start a course. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  3. Present continuous = договорённость со временем, часто с человеком или местом: I'm seeing the dentist at four.
  4. Present simple = расписание: The film starts at eight. The bus leaves at 7:30.
  5. Придаточные времени: после when, before, as soon as, until, if используется present simple, не will: When I arrive, I'll call you. If it rains, we'll stay home.
  6. Возможны несколько форм. Разница в оттенке: going to = я решил, present continuous = уже договорились.
  1. will = gapirish paytidagi qaror, taklif, va'da yoki kelajak haqidagi fikr (I think, probably, I'm sure): It's heavy. I'll help you.
  2. be going to = gapirishdan oldin qilingan reja yoki ko'rinib turgan belgilarga asoslangan bashorat: I'm going to start a course. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  3. Present continuous = vaqt bilan kelishuv, ko'pincha odam yoki joy bilan: I'm seeing the dentist at four.
  4. Present simple = jadval: The film starts at eight. The bus leaves at 7:30.
  5. Payt ergash gaplari: when, before, as soon as, until, if dan keyin present simple ishlatiladi, will emas: When I arrive, I'll call you. If it rains, we'll stay home.
  6. Bir nechta shakl to'g'ri bo'lishi mumkin. Farq ohangda: going to = qaror qildim, present continuous = allaqachon kelishilgan.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Did I decide just now? Yes → will. Did I decide before? → going to.
  2. Is it arranged with a time or person? Yes → present continuous. Is it a public timetable? → present simple.
  3. Do I see evidence now? Yes → going to. Is it only my opinion? → will.

Common mistakes

  • When I will arrive, I will call you. When I arrive, I'll call you.
  • (The phone rings) I'm going to answer it. (The phone rings) I'll answer it.
  • Look at those clouds! It will rain. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  • The film will start at 8 (timetable). The film starts at 8.
After when, before, as soon as, until and if, never put will in the time or condition part.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я решил только что? Да → will. Решил раньше? → going to.
  2. Договорились о времени или с человеком? Да → present continuous. Это публичное расписание? → present simple.
  3. Вижу признаки сейчас? Да → going to. Это только моё мнение? → will.

Частые ошибки

  • When I will arrive, I will call you. When I arrive, I'll call you.
  • (The phone rings) I'm going to answer it. (The phone rings) I'll answer it.
  • Look at those clouds! It will rain. Look at those clouds! It's going to rain.
  • The film will start at 8 (timetable). The film starts at 8.
После when, before, as soon as, until и if никогда не ставь will в части со временем или условием.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Hozirgina qaror qildimmi? Ha → will. Oldin qaror qilganmidim? → going to.
  2. Vaqt yoki odam bilan kelishilganmi? Ha → present continuous. Bu jamoat jadvalimi? → present simple.
  3. Hozir belgilarni ko'ryapmanmi? Ha → going to. Bu faqat mening fikrimmi? → will.

Real-life use

Native speakers choose a future form without thinking. These situations show the pattern.

Deciding on the spot

"I'll have the soup, please." "It's hot in here. I'll open the window."

Plans and arrangements

"I'm going to start a new course next month. I'm meeting my teacher at five."

Timetables

"The bus leaves at 7:30, and the lesson starts at nine."

IELTS Speaking: talking about the future

"In the future I'm going to study abroad. I think technology will change the way we learn."

English only

Examples and drill

will

I'll help you. I'll call you tonight, I promise. I think she will win.

going to

I'm going to learn French. Look out! You're going to fall.

Present continuous

I'm seeing the dentist at four. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.

Present simple

The film starts at eight. My exam begins at nine.

Time clauses

When I arrive, I'll call you. As soon as he comes, we'll start.

Drill

1. Look at the sky! It ___ rain. (will / is going to)
is going to
2. A: I'm cold. B: I ___ close the window. (will / am going to)
will
3. I ___ my dentist at 4 tomorrow. (see / am seeing)
am seeing
4. The train ___ at 8:15. (leaves / is going to leave)
leaves
5. When I ___ home, I will call you. (get / will get)
get
6. We've decided: we ___ buy a car next year. (will / are going to)
are going to
7. Correct it: If it will rain, we will stay home.
If it rains, we will stay home.
