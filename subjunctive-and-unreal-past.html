Subjunctive and unreal past

General info

The subjunctive is a special verb form we use for demands, suggestions and unreal ideas. English has two main kinds: the base form after words like suggest and insist, and the unreal past, where a past tense form talks about the present or future.

Base form (formal)

I suggest that he go home. (not goes) The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Unreal past

It's time you left. (you should be leaving now) I'd rather you stayed. He talks as if he knew everything.

We use them to

  • Make formal suggestions and demands: The committee recommends that the policy be reviewed.
  • Say it is time to act: It's high time we made a decision.
  • Say what we prefer other people to do: I'd rather you didn't smoke here.
  • Compare with an unreal situation: She looked as if she had seen a ghost.
Russian says чтобы + past form (Я хочу, чтобы он ушёл) and Uzbek uses the -sin ending (u ketsin). English has no such ending: it uses the plain base verb (that he go) or a past form for unreal meaning (It's time he left).

Сослагательное наклонение (subjunctive) — особая форма глагола для требований, предложений и нереальных мыслей. В английском два основных вида: базовая форма после слов вроде suggest и insist и нереальное прошедшее, когда форма прошедшего времени говорит о настоящем или будущем.

Базовая форма (формально)

I suggest that he go home. (не goes) The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Нереальное прошедшее

It's time you left. (тебе уже пора уходить) I'd rather you stayed. He talks as if he knew everything.

Зачем используем

  • Формально предлагать и требовать: The committee recommends that the policy be reviewed.
  • Сказать, что пора действовать: It's high time we made a decision.
  • Сказать, что мы предпочитаем от других: I'd rather you didn't smoke here.
  • Сравнить с нереальной ситуацией: She looked as if she had seen a ghost.
В русском есть «чтобы» + прошедшая форма (Я хочу, чтобы он ушёл), в узбекском — окончание -sin (u ketsin). В английском такого окончания нет: используют простой глагол (that he go) или форму прошедшего для нереального смысла (It's time he left).

Subjunctive (shart-istak mayli) — talab, taklif va real bo'lmagan fikrlar uchun maxsus fe'l shakli. Inglizchada ikki asosiy turi bor: suggest va insist kabi so'zlardan keyin asosiy shakl va real bo'lmagan o'tgan zamon, ya'ni o'tgan zamon shakli hozirgi yoki kelasi zamon haqida gapiradi.

Asosiy shakl (rasmiy)

I suggest that he go home. (goes emas) The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Real bo'lmagan o'tgan zamon

It's time you left. (sizga hozir ketish vaqti keldi) I'd rather you stayed. He talks as if he knew everything.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Rasmiy taklif va talab qilish: The committee recommends that the policy be reviewed.
  • Harakat qilish vaqti kelganini aytish: It's high time we made a decision.
  • Boshqalardan nimani afzal ko'rishimizni aytish: I'd rather you didn't smoke here.
  • Real bo'lmagan holat bilan solishtirish: She looked as if she had seen a ghost.
Rus tilida «чтобы» + o'tgan zamon shakli (Я хочу, чтобы он ушёл), o'zbek tilida -sin qo'shimchasi (u ketsin) bor. Ingliz tilida bunday qo'shimcha yo'q: oddiy fe'l (that he go) yoki real bo'lmagan ma'no uchun o'tgan zamon shakli (It's time he left) ishlatiladi.

  1. Base form: after the trigger words + that, the verb is the base form with no -s and no tense: that he go, that she attend, that they be. It is the same for every person.
  2. Formality and variants: the base form is formal and common in American English. British English often uses should (I suggest that he should go), and in everyday speech people may say he goes. For exams and formal writing use the base form or should.
  3. Trigger words: verbs like suggest, recommend, propose, insist, demand, require, request, advise; adjectives like essential, vital, important, necessary, crucial; nouns like demand, recommendation, suggestion. Not want: say I want him to go.
  4. Be: the verb be stays be: The doctor recommended that the patient be admitted.
  5. Negative: put not before the base verb and do not use do: He insisted that she not go.
  6. Were: with I, he, she, it we use were for unreal ideas: If I were you ... In informal speech was is also heard, but were is the formal choice.
  7. Unreal past: after It's time, would rather and as if, a past tense form has present or future meaning. For an unreal past, use the past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't said that.
  8. Real or unreal: as if + present can describe something that may be true: He looks as if he is ill. as if + past shows it is not true. It's time you left sounds critical. It's time to leave is neutral.

  2. Стиль и варианты: базовая форма формальна и обычна для американского английского. Британский чаще использует should (I suggest that he should go), а в разговорной речи можно услышать he goes. Для экзаменов и формального письма используй базовую форму или should.
  3. Слова-триггеры: глаголы suggest, recommend, propose, insist, demand, require, request, advise; прилагательные essential, vital, important, necessary, crucial; существительные demand, recommendation, suggestion. После want так не говорят: I want him to go.
  4. Be: глагол be остаётся be: The doctor recommended that the patient be admitted.
  5. Отрицание: not ставим перед базовым глаголом, do не нужен: He insisted that she not go.
  6. Were: с I, he, she, it для нереальных мыслей используем were: If I were you ... В разговоре можно услышать was, но формальный вариант — were.
  7. Нереальное прошедшее: после It's time, would rather и as if форма прошедшего времени имеет значение настоящего или будущего. Для нереального прошлого используй past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't said that.
  8. Реальное или нереальное: as if + настоящее может описывать то, что возможно правда: He looks as if he is ill. as if + прошедшее показывает, что это неправда. It's time you left звучит с упрёком. It's time to leave нейтрально.
  1. Asosiy shakl: trigger so'zlar + that dan keyin fe'l asosiy shaklda keladi, -s va zamonsiz: that he go, that she attend, that they be. Barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil.
  2. Uslub va variantlar: asosiy shakl rasmiy va amerika inglizchasida keng tarqalgan. Britaniya inglizchasida ko'pincha should ishlatiladi (I suggest that he should go), oddiy nutqda he goes ham eshitiladi. Imtihon va rasmiy yozuv uchun asosiy shakl yoki should dan foydalaning.
  3. Trigger so'zlar: suggest, recommend, propose, insist, demand, require, request, advise fe'llari; essential, vital, important, necessary, crucial sifatlari; demand, recommendation, suggestion otlari. Want dan keyin bunday aytilmaydi: I want him to go.
  4. Be: be fe'li be bo'lib qoladi: The doctor recommended that the patient be admitted.
  5. Inkor: not ni asosiy fe'ldan oldin qo'ying, do kerak emas: He insisted that she not go.
  6. Were: I, he, she, it bilan real bo'lmagan fikrlar uchun were ishlatamiz: If I were you ... Norasmiy nutqda was ham eshitiladi, lekin rasmiy variant were.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a formal demand, advice or suggestion with that? Yes → base form: I suggest that he go.
  2. Am I saying what should happen now, or what is not true? Yes → past form with present meaning: It's time you left. as if he knew.
  3. Am I talking about the unreal past? Yes → past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't said that.

Build it in 3 steps

The manager insisted that she attends the meeting. 1) Find the trigger: insisted that. 2) Look at the verb after the subject: attends. 3) Use the base form: attend. → The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Why it works

The base form has no tense because the action has not happened yet. It is only wanted or suggested. The unreal past works in a similar way: the past tense shows distance from reality, not past time.

Common mistakes

  • It's time you leave. It's time you left.
  • I want that he goes. I want him to go.
  • I'd rather you to stay. I'd rather you stayed.
  • He is not the boss, but he talks as if he is. He is not the boss, but he talks as if he were.
Past form does not always mean past time. After It's time, would rather and as if, it can mean now. And do not use the subjunctive after want: want + object + to.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это формальное требование, совет или предложение с that? Да → базовая форма: I suggest that he go.
  2. Я говорю, что должно происходить сейчас, или что неправда? Да → форма прошедшего со значением настоящего: It's time you left. as if he knew.
  3. Я говорю о нереальном прошлом? Да → past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't said that.

Строим за 3 шага

The manager insisted that she attends the meeting. 1) Найди триггер: insisted that. 2) Посмотри на глагол после подлежащего: attends. 3) Поставь базовую форму: attend. → The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Почему это работает

У базовой формы нет времени, потому что действие ещё не произошло. Его только хотят или предлагают. Нереальное прошедшее работает похоже: прошедшая форма показывает дистанцию от реальности, а не прошлое время.

Частые ошибки

  • It's time you leave. It's time you left.
  • I want that he goes. I want him to go.
  • I'd rather you to stay. I'd rather you stayed.
  • He is not the boss, but he talks as if he is. He is not the boss, but he talks as if he were.
Форма прошедшего не всегда значит прошлое время. После It's time, would rather и as if она может значить «сейчас». И не используй эту форму после want: want + дополнение + to.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu that bilan rasmiy talab, maslahat yoki taklifmi? Ha → asosiy shakl: I suggest that he go.
  2. Hozir nima bo'lishi kerakligini yoki nima rost emasligini aytyapmanmi? Ha → hozirgi ma'noli o'tgan zamon shakli: It's time you left. as if he knew.
  3. Real bo'lmagan o'tmish haqida gapiryapmanmi? Ha → past perfect: I'd rather you hadn't said that.

3 qadamda yasash

The manager insisted that she attends the meeting. 1) Triggerni toping: insisted that. 2) Egadan keyingi fe'lga qarang: attends. 3) Asosiy shaklni qo'ying: attend. → The manager insisted that she attend the meeting.

Nima uchun ishlaydi

Asosiy shaklda zamon yo'q, chunki ish hali bo'lmagan. U faqat xohlanadi yoki taklif qilinadi. Real bo'lmagan o'tgan zamon ham shunga o'xshash ishlaydi: o'tgan zamon shakli o'tgan vaqtni emas, haqiqatdan uzoqlikni ko'rsatadi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • It's time you leave. It's time you left.
  • I want that he goes. I want him to go.
  • I'd rather you to stay. I'd rather you stayed.
  • He is not the boss, but he talks as if he is. He is not the boss, but he talks as if he were.
O'tgan zamon shakli har doim ham o'tgan vaqtni bildirmaydi. It's time, would rather va as if dan keyin u «hozir» degani bo'lishi mumkin. Va want dan keyin bu shaklni ishlatmang: want + to'ldiruvchi + to.

Real-life use

The subjunctive and unreal past help us make formal proposals, give polite pressure and describe things that only seem true.

Meetings and formal writing

"The committee recommends that the policy be reviewed. We propose that the deadline be extended."

Rules and requirements

"The law requires that every driver wear a seatbelt. It is essential that all staff attend the training."

Urging action politely

"It's high time we started. It's time you thought about your future."

Preferences

"I'd rather we met on Monday. I'd rather you didn't tell anyone."

Describing and storytelling

"She looked as if she had seen a ghost. He talks as though he owned the place."

IELTS Writing and Speaking

"I strongly recommend that the government invest more in public transport. It is vital that schools teach critical thinking." Speaking: "If I were you, I would apply."

Examples and drill

Base-form subjunctive

I suggest that he go home. The manager insisted that she attend the meeting. The doctor recommended that the patient be admitted. They demanded that he not leave.

After adjectives and nouns

It is essential that every student arrive on time. It is vital that the data be checked. The requirement that all drivers wear seatbelts is strict.

It's time and would rather

It's time you left. It's high time we made a decision. I'd rather you stayed. I'd rather you hadn't said that.

as if / as though

He talks as if he knew everything. She looked as though she had seen a ghost. It looks as if it is going to rain. (this may be true)

Were

If I were you, I'd wait. He acts as if he were the boss. I wish it were Friday.
