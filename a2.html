A2

Elementary. 16 topics. Choose one.

  • Future: going toOpen topicWe cover: plans and predictions with going to. You will be able to: talk about your plans and predict what will happen.Разбираем: планы и предсказания с going to. Вы сможете: рассказать о планах и предсказать, что произойдёт.Ko'ramiz: going to bilan rejalar va bashoratlar. Siz qila olasiz: rejalaringiz haqida gapirish va nima bo'lishini bashorat qilish.
  • Future: arrangements and willOpen topicWe cover: present continuous for arrangements, will for decisions and offers. You will be able to: make plans, offers and promises.Разбираем: present continuous для договорённостей, will для решений и предложений. Вы сможете: договариваться, предлагать помощь и обещать.Ko'ramiz: kelishuvlar uchun present continuous, qaror va takliflar uchun will. Siz qila olasiz: reja qilish, taklif va va'da berish.
  • Comparatives and superlativesOpen topicWe cover: -er / -est, more / most, as ... as. You will be able to: compare people and things and say which is the best.Разбираем: -er / -est, more / most, as ... as. Вы сможете: сравнивать людей и предметы и назвать лучшее.Ko'ramiz: -er / -est, more / most, as ... as. Siz qila olasiz: odam va narsalarni solishtirish va eng yaxshisini aytish.
  • QuantifiersOpen topicWe cover: some, any, much, many, a few, a little. You will be able to: talk about amounts of countable and uncountable things.Разбираем: some, any, much, many, a few, a little. Вы сможете: говорить о количестве исчисляемых и неисчисляемых предметов.Ko'ramiz: some, any, much, many, a few, a little. Siz qila olasiz: sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan narsalar miqdori haqida gapirish.
  • Present Perfect (basic)Open topicWe cover: have / has + participle with ever, never, just, already, yet. You will be able to: talk about life experience and recent news.Разбираем: have / has + третья форма с ever, never, just, already, yet. Вы сможете: рассказать об опыте и недавних новостях.Ko'ramiz: ever, never, just, already, yet bilan have / has + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: hayotiy tajriba va yaqindagi yangiliklar haqida gapirish.
  • Past ContinuousOpen topicWe cover: was / were + -ing. You will be able to: describe what was happening at a moment in the past.Разбираем: was / were + -ing. Вы сможете: описать, что происходило в момент в прошлом.Ko'ramiz: was / were + -ing. Siz qila olasiz: o'tmishdagi paytda nima bo'layotganini tasvirlash.
  • Object pronounsOpen topicWe cover: me, you, him, her, it, us, them. You will be able to: avoid repeating nouns and use the right pronoun after verbs and prepositions.Разбираем: me, you, him, her, it, us, them. Вы сможете: не повторять существительные и выбирать местоимение после глаголов и предлогов.Ko'ramiz: me, you, him, her, it, us, them. Siz qila olasiz: otlarni takrorlamaslik va fe'l hamda predlogdan keyin to'g'ri olmoshni tanlash.
  • Modals: must, have to, shouldOpen topicWe cover: obligation, prohibition and advice. You will be able to: talk about rules and give advice.Разбираем: обязанность, запрет и совет. Вы сможете: говорить о правилах и давать советы.Ko'ramiz: majburiyat, taqiq va maslahat. Siz qila olasiz: qoidalar haqida gapirish va maslahat berish.
  • Too, enough, too much / manyOpen topicWe cover: too, enough, too much, too many. You will be able to: say if something is more or less than you need.Разбираем: too, enough, too much, too many. Вы сможете: сказать, что чего-то слишком много или не хватает.Ko'ramiz: too, enough, too much, too many. Siz qila olasiz: biror narsa keraklidan ko'p yoki kam ekanini aytish.
  • Question tagsOpen topicWe cover: isn't it? don't you? can't he?. You will be able to: check information and keep a conversation going.Разбираем: isn't it? don't you? can't he?. Вы сможете: уточнять информацию и поддерживать беседу.Ko'ramiz: isn't it? don't you? can't he?. Siz qila olasiz: ma'lumotni tekshirish va suhbatni davom ettirish.
  • Infinitive vs gerundOpen topicWe cover: verbs followed by to + verb or -ing. You will be able to: choose the right form after common verbs.Разбираем: глаголы с to + глагол или -ing. Вы сможете: выбирать верную форму после частых глаголов.Ko'ramiz: to + fe'l yoki -ing oladigan fe'llar. Siz qila olasiz: keng tarqalgan fe'llardan keyin to'g'ri shaklni tanlash.
  • Indefinite pronounsOpen topicWe cover: something / anything / nothing / everything and the -one, -body, -where forms. You will be able to: talk about people, things and places without naming them.Разбираем: something / anything / nothing / everything и формы с -one, -body, -where. Вы сможете: говорить о людях, предметах и местах, не называя их.Ko'ramiz: something / anything / nothing / everything va -one, -body, -where shakllari. Siz qila olasiz: odam, narsa va joylarni nomlamasdan gapirish.
  • Adjectives and adverbsOpen topicWe cover: how adjectives describe nouns and adverbs describe verbs. You will be able to: describe people, things and actions in detail.Разбираем: как прилагательные описывают существительные, а наречия — глаголы. Вы сможете: подробно описывать людей, вещи и действия.Ko'ramiz: sifat otni, ravish fe'lni qanday tasvirlaydi. Siz qila olasiz: odam, narsa va harakatlarni batafsil tasvirlash.
  • Prepositions of movementOpen topicWe cover: to, into, onto, through, across, along, over. You will be able to: describe movement and directions.Разбираем: to, into, onto, through, across, along, over. Вы сможете: описывать движение и направления.Ko'ramiz: to, into, onto, through, across, along, over. Siz qila olasiz: harakat va yo'nalishlarni tasvirlash.
  • Zero conditionalOpen topicWe cover: if + present, present for facts and habits. You will be able to: talk about things that are always true.Разбираем: if + present, present для фактов и привычек. Вы сможете: говорить о том, что всегда верно.Ko'ramiz: fakt va odatlar uchun if + present, present. Siz qila olasiz: doim to'g'ri bo'lgan narsalar haqida gapirish.
  • Time clauses (when, before, after)Open topicWe cover: when, before, after, as soon as, until + present. You will be able to: talk about the order and time of events.Разбираем: when, before, after, as soon as, until + present. Вы сможете: говорить о порядке и времени событий.Ko'ramiz: when, before, after, as soon as, until + present. Siz qila olasiz: voqealar tartibi va vaqti haqida gapirish.
