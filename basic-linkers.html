Basic linkers

General info

Linkers are small words that join ideas. At A1 we use five: and, but, or, so, because.

  • and — adds information: I like tea and coffee.
  • but — shows a difference: I like tea but I don't like coffee.
  • or — shows a choice: Tea or coffee?
  • so — shows a result: I was tired, so I went to bed.
  • because — shows a reason: I went to bed because I was tired.
Don't mix up so (result) and because (reason): I was tired, so I slept. / I slept because I was tired.

Связки — маленькие слова, которые соединяют идеи. На A1 мы используем пять: and, but, or, so, because.

  • and — добавляет информацию: I like tea and coffee.
  • but — показывает разницу: I like tea but I don't like coffee.
  • or — показывает выбор: Tea or coffee?
  • so — показывает результат: I was tired, so I went to bed.
  • because — показывает причину: I went to bed because I was tired.
Не путайте so (результат) и because (причина): I was tired, so I slept. / I slept because I was tired.

Bog'lovchilar — fikrlarni bog'laydigan kichik so'zlar. A1 da beshtasini ishlatamiz: and, but, or, so, because.

  • and — ma'lumot qo'shadi: I like tea and coffee.
  • but — farqni ko'rsatadi: I like tea but I don't like coffee.
  • or — tanlovni ko'rsatadi: Tea or coffee?
  • so — natijani ko'rsatadi: I was tired, so I went to bed.
  • because — sababni ko'rsatadi: I went to bed because I was tired.
so (natija) va because (sabab) ni aralashtirmang: I was tired, so I slept. / I slept because I was tired.

Teacher's rule

LinkerMeaningExample
andaddsShe sings and dances.
butcontrastSmall but strong.
orchoiceTea or coffee?
soresultIt rained, so we stayed in.
becausereasonWe stayed in because it rained.
  1. and joins similar ideas: I bought bread and milk.
  2. but joins opposite ideas: It's old but useful.
  3. or gives a choice or an alternative: Is it red or blue?
  4. so shows a result: It was late, so I went home.
  5. because gives the reason: I went home because it was late.
  6. When a linker joins two full sentences, put a comma before so and but: I was hungry, so I ate.
  7. so and because are opposites: result vs reason.
  1. and соединяет похожие идеи: I bought bread and milk.
  2. but соединяет противоположные идеи: It's old but useful.
  3. or даёт выбор или альтернативу: Is it red or blue?
  4. so показывает результат: It was late, so I went home.
  5. because даёт причину: I went home because it was late.
  6. Когда связка соединяет два полных предложения, перед so и but ставим запятую: I was hungry, so I ate.
  7. so и because противоположны: результат против причины.
  1. and o'xshash fikrlarni bog'laydi: I bought bread and milk.
  2. but qarama-qarshi fikrlarni bog'laydi: It's old but useful.
  3. or tanlov yoki alternativa beradi: Is it red or blue?
  4. so natijani ko'rsatadi: It was late, so I went home.
  5. because sababni ko'rsatadi: I went home because it was late.
  6. Bog'lovchi ikki to'liq gapni bog'laganda, so va but dan oldin vergul qo'yiladi: I was hungry, so I ate.
  7. so va because qarama-qarshi: natija va sabab.

Student explanation

Ask yourself what the two ideas do

  1. Same idea? → and.
  2. Opposite idea? → but.
  3. A choice? → or.
  4. A result? → so.
  5. A reason? → because.

Common mistakes

  • I was tired, because I went home. I was tired, so I went home.
  • I like cats and I don't like dogs. I like cats but I don't like dogs.
  • Do you want tea and coffee? Do you want tea or coffee?
  • I like tea. and coffee. I like tea and coffee.
Reason and result are opposites. Ask: "What came first?" The reason comes first with because; the result follows with so.

In plain words

These small words are like glue. They join two ideas into one sentence, and each one shows how the ideas are connected.

  • I like tea and coffee. And adds one more thing.
  • It's small but nice. But shows the second idea is different.
  • I went home because I was tired. I was tired, so I went home. Because gives the reason. So gives the result.

Or gives a choice: "Tea or coffee?"

Спроси себя, что делают две идеи

  1. Одинаковая идея? → and.
  2. Противоположная идея? → but.
  3. Выбор? → or.
  4. Результат? → so.
  5. Причина? → because.

Частые ошибки

  • I was tired, because I went home. I was tired, so I went home.
  • I like cats and I don't like dogs. I like cats but I don't like dogs.
  • Do you want tea and coffee? Do you want tea or coffee?
  • I like tea. and coffee. I like tea and coffee.
Причина и результат противоположны. Спроси: «Что было первым?» Причина идёт первой с because, результат — с so.

Простыми словами

Эти маленькие слова как клей. Они соединяют две мысли в одно предложение, и каждое показывает, как мысли связаны.

  • I like tea and coffee. Я люблю чай и кофе. And добавляет ещё что-то.
  • It's small but nice. Он маленький, но красивый. But показывает другую мысль.
  • I went home because I was tired. I was tired, so I went home. Я пошёл домой, потому что устал. Я устал, поэтому пошёл домой. Because даёт причину, so — результат.

Or даёт выбор: "Tea or coffee?"

Ikki fikr nima qilishini o'zingizdan so'rang

  1. Bir xil fikr? → and.
  2. Qarama-qarshi fikr? → but.
  3. Tanlov? → or.
  4. Natija? → so.
  5. Sabab? → because.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I was tired, because I went home. I was tired, so I went home.
  • I like cats and I don't like dogs. I like cats but I don't like dogs.
  • Do you want tea and coffee? Do you want tea or coffee?
  • I like tea. and coffee. I like tea and coffee.
Sabab va natija qarama-qarshi. «Nima birinchi bo'ldi?» deb so'rang. Sabab because bilan oldin keladi, natija so bilan keyin.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Bu kichik so'zlar yelimga o'xshaydi. Ular ikki fikrni bitta gapga bog'laydi va har biri fikrlar qanday bog'langanini ko'rsatadi.

  • I like tea and coffee. Men choy va qahva yoqtiraman. And yana bir narsa qo'shadi.
  • It's small but nice. U kichik, lekin chiroyli. But boshqa fikrni ko'rsatadi.
  • I went home because I was tired. I was tired, so I went home. Charchaganim uchun uyga ketdim. Charchadim, shuning uchun uyga ketdim. Because sabab, so natija.

Or tanlovni bildiradi: "Tea or coffee?"

Real-life use

You use these small words when you tell a story or say why.

Make plans

"I'm free on Friday and Saturday, but not Sunday."

Say why you chose

"Let's take the bus, because the taxi is expensive."

Tell a story

"It started raining, so we went inside."

Give a choice

"Would you like tea or coffee?"

IELTS Speaking: your routine

"I get up early because I like mornings, and I go to the gym before work."
