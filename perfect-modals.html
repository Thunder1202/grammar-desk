Perfect modals

General info

Perfect modals (modal + have + past participle) talk about the past: what was the right thing to do, what was possible, and what was not necessary. They are often used for regret and criticism.

We use them for

  • Regret and criticism: You should have called me. I shouldn't have said that.
  • Missed chances: We could have taken the early train.
  • Unnecessary actions: You needn't have bought flowers.

Words that go with them

should have, shouldn't have, ought to have, could have, would have, needn't have, if only.

Russian: надо было позвонить / не надо было говорить. Uzbek: qo'ng'iroq qilishing kerak edi / aytmasliging kerak edi. English uses should have + participle.

Перфектные модальные (модальный + have + третья форма) говорят о прошлом: что было правильно сделать, что было возможно и что было не нужно. Часто выражают сожаление и упрёк.

Когда используем

  • Сожаление и упрёк: You should have called me. I shouldn't have said that.
  • Упущенные возможности: We could have taken the early train.
  • Ненужные действия: You needn't have bought flowers.

Слова-подсказки

should have, shouldn't have, ought to have, could have, would have, needn't have, if only.

Русский: надо было позвонить / не надо было говорить. Узбекский: qo'ng'iroq qilishing kerak edi / aytmasliging kerak edi. В английском should have + третья форма.

Perfect modallar (modal + have + uchinchi shakl) o'tmish haqida gapiradi: nima qilish to'g'ri edi, nima mumkin edi va nima keraksiz edi. Ko'pincha afsus va tanqid uchun ishlatiladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Afsus va tanqid: You should have called me. I shouldn't have said that.
  • Boy berilgan imkoniyatlar: We could have taken the early train.
  • Keraksiz harakatlar: You needn't have bought flowers.

Yordamchi so'zlar

should have, shouldn't have, ought to have, could have, would have, needn't have, if only.

Rus tili: надо было позвонить / не надо было говорить. O'zbek tili: qo'ng'iroq qilishing kerak edi / aytmasliging kerak edi. Inglizchada should have + uchinchi shakl.

Teacher's rule

ModalMeaningExample
should have / ought to haveright thing, but it did not happenYou should have called me.
shouldn't haveit happened, but it was wrongI shouldn't have said that.
could havea possibility that was not usedWe could have taken the early train.
couldn't haveit was impossibleI couldn't have known.
would haveimaginary past resultI would have helped if you had asked.
needn't haveyou did it, but it was not necessaryYou needn't have bought flowers.
  1. Form: modal + have + past participle: should have gone, could have called.
  2. should have / ought to have = it was the right thing but it did not happen. shouldn't have = it happened but it was wrong.
  3. could have = a past possibility or chance that was not used: You could have called.
  4. would have = an imaginary past result, usually with if: I would have helped if you had asked.
  5. needn't have + participle = you did it, but it was not necessary: You needn't have waited. Compare didn't need to = it was not necessary (we do not know if you did it).
  6. might have can show annoyance: You might have told me!
  1. Форма: модальный + have + третья форма: should have gone, could have called.
  2. should have / ought to have = это было правильно, но не произошло. shouldn't have = это произошло, но было неправильно.
  3. could have = прошлая возможность, которой не воспользовались: You could have called.
  4. would have = воображаемый результат в прошлом, обычно с if: I would have helped if you had asked.
  5. needn't have + третья форма = вы это сделали, но это было не нужно: You needn't have waited. Сравни didn't need to = не было необходимости (неизвестно, сделали ли).
  6. might have может выражать раздражение: You might have told me!
  1. Shakl: modal + have + uchinchi shakl: should have gone, could have called.
  2. should have / ought to have = to'g'ri ish edi, lekin bo'lmadi. shouldn't have = bo'ldi, lekin noto'g'ri edi.
  3. could have = o'tmishdagi, foydalanilmagan imkoniyat: You could have called.
  4. would have = o'tmishdagi xayoliy natija, odatda if bilan: I would have helped if you had asked.
  5. needn't have + uchinchi shakl = qildingiz, lekin keraksiz edi: You needn't have waited. Solishtiring didn't need to = zarurat yo'q edi (qilgan-qilmaganingiz noma'lum).
  6. might have g'azabni bildirishi mumkin: You might have told me!

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Am I talking about a past mistake or regret? Right thing not done → should have. Wrong thing done → shouldn't have.
  2. Was it a chance that was not used? Yes → could have.
  3. Did the person do something unnecessary? Yes → needn't have.

needn't have or didn't need to?

You needn't have bought flowers. (You bought them, and it was unnecessary.) / I didn't need to buy flowers. (It was not necessary. Maybe I bought them, maybe not.)

Common mistakes

  • You should of called me. You should have called me.
  • I should have went to bed earlier. I should have gone to bed earlier.
  • You needn't to have waited. You needn't have waited.
  • I would have helped if you would have asked. I would have helped if you had asked.
Always have, never of. And the participle follows: should have gone, not should have went.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Говорю о прошлой ошибке или сожалении? Правильное не сделано → should have. Неправильное сделано → shouldn't have.
  2. Это была возможность, которой не воспользовались? Да → could have.
  3. Человек сделал что-то ненужное? Да → needn't have.

needn't have или didn't need to?

You needn't have bought flowers. (Купили, и это было не нужно.) / I didn't need to buy flowers. (Не было необходимости. Может, купил, может, нет.)

Частые ошибки

  • You should of called me. You should have called me.
  • I should have went to bed earlier. I should have gone to bed earlier.
  • You needn't to have waited. You needn't have waited.
  • I would have helped if you would have asked. I would have helped if you had asked.
Всегда have, никогда of. И третья форма: should have gone, а не should have went.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. O'tmishdagi xato yoki afsus haqida gapiryapmanmi? To'g'ri ish qilinmagan → should have. Noto'g'ri ish qilingan → shouldn't have.
  2. Bu foydalanilmagan imkoniyatmidi? Ha → could have.
  3. Odam keraksiz ish qildimi? Ha → needn't have.

needn't have yoki didn't need to?

You needn't have bought flowers. (Sotib oldingiz va keraksiz edi.) / I didn't need to buy flowers. (Zarurat yo'q edi. Balki oldim, balki yo'q.)

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • You should of called me. You should have called me.
  • I should have went to bed earlier. I should have gone to bed earlier.
  • You needn't to have waited. You needn't have waited.
  • I would have helped if you would have asked. I would have helped if you had asked.
Doim have, hech qachon of emas. Uchinchi shakl: should have gone, should have went emas.

Real-life use

We use perfect modals when we look back at decisions, mistakes and chances.

Regret

"I should have studied harder. I shouldn't have waited so long."

Criticism

"You should have told me! You shouldn't have shouted at him."

Missed chances

"We could have taken the early train, but we didn't."

Unnecessary actions

"You needn't have bought me a gift!"

IELTS Speaking: talking about a decision

"I should have started learning English earlier, but I could have given up, and I'm glad I didn't."

Examples and drill

Should have and shouldn't have

You should have called me. I shouldn't have said that.

Could have

We could have taken the early train. She could have won.

Would have

I would have helped if you had asked.

Needn't have

You needn't have waited. We needn't have hurried.

Might have (annoyed)

You might have told me!

Drill

1. You look tired. You ___ gone to bed earlier. (should have / shouldn't have)
should have
2. I'm sorry. I ___ said that. It was rude. (should have / shouldn't have)
shouldn't have
3. She had the chance to study abroad. She ___ studied abroad. (could have / couldn't have)
could have
4. We took umbrellas, but it didn't rain. We ___ taken them. (needn't have / shouldn't have)
needn't have
5. I would have helped if you ___ (ask).
had asked
6. Choose: You (should have called / should of called) me.
should have called
7. Correct it: I should have went to bed earlier.
I should have gone to bed earlier.
