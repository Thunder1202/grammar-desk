Modals of deduction

General info

Modals of deduction help us make a logical guess from the evidence we have, when we are not 100% sure.

We use them for

  • Almost sure it is true: The lights are on. They must be at home.
  • Almost sure it is not true: She just left. She can't be home yet.
  • Possible, not sure: He isn't answering. He might be driving.

Words that go with them

must, can't, might, may, could, probably, I think, evidence.

Russian: должно быть / не может быть / может быть. Uzbek: bo'lishi kerak / bo'lishi mumkin emas / bo'lishi mumkin. English uses must / can't / might + verb.

Модальные глаголы предположения помогают сделать логичное предположение по имеющимся признакам, когда мы не уверены на 100%.

Когда используем

  • Почти уверены, что это правда: The lights are on. They must be at home.
  • Почти уверены, что это неправда: She just left. She can't be home yet.
  • Возможно, не уверены: He isn't answering. He might be driving.

Слова-подсказки

must, can't, might, may, could, probably, I think, evidence.

Русский: должно быть / не может быть / может быть. Узбекский: bo'lishi kerak / bo'lishi mumkin emas / bo'lishi mumkin. В английском must / can't / might + глагол.

Taxmin modal fe'llari bizda bor belgilarga qarab mantiqiy taxmin qilishga yordam beradi, 100% ishonchimiz bo'lmaganda.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Rost ekaniga deyarli ishonamiz: The lights are on. They must be at home.
  • Rost emasligiga deyarli ishonamiz: She just left. She can't be home yet.
  • Mumkin, ishonchimiz yo'q: He isn't answering. He might be driving.

Yordamchi so'zlar

must, can't, might, may, could, probably, I think, evidence.

Rus tili: должно быть / не может быть / может быть. O'zbek tili: bo'lishi kerak / bo'lishi mumkin emas / bo'lishi mumkin. Inglizchada must / can't / might + fe'l.

Teacher's rule

CertaintyPresentPast
sure it is trueHe must be at home.He must have left.
possibleShe might be sleeping.She might have forgotten.
sure it is not trueIt can't be true.He can't have seen us.
  1. Use: a logical guess from evidence, not a fact and not a plan.
  2. must = sure it is true: The lights are on. He must be at home.
  3. can't = sure it is not true: She just left. She can't be home yet. Never use mustn't for deduction.
  4. might / may / could = possible, not sure: He might be late.
  5. Past: modal + have + past participle: She must have forgotten. They can't have arrived.
  6. Happening now: modal + be + -ing: He must be working. She might be sleeping.
  1. Употребление: логичное предположение по признакам, не факт и не план.
  2. must = уверены, что это правда: The lights are on. He must be at home.
  3. can't = уверены, что это неправда: She just left. She can't be home yet. Для предположения никогда не используй mustn't.
  4. might / may / could = возможно, не уверены: He might be late.
  5. Прошлое: модальный + have + третья форма: She must have forgotten. They can't have arrived.
  6. Происходит сейчас: модальный + be + -ing: He must be working. She might be sleeping.
  1. Qo'llanishi: belgilarga asoslangan mantiqiy taxmin, fakt ham, reja ham emas.
  2. must = rost ekaniga ishonamiz: The lights are on. He must be at home.
  3. can't = rost emasligiga ishonamiz: She just left. She can't be home yet. Taxmin uchun hech qachon mustn't ishlatmang.
  4. might / may / could = mumkin, ishonchimiz yo'q: He might be late.
  5. O'tmish: modal + have + uchinchi shakl: She must have forgotten. They can't have arrived.
  6. Hozir bo'layotgan: modal + be + -ing: He must be working. She might be sleeping.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. How sure am I? Very sure it is true → must. Very sure it is not true → can't. Not sure → might / may / could.
  2. Is it about now or the past? Now → modal + be / verb. Past → modal + have + participle.
  3. Is it happening right now? Yes → modal + be + -ing.

Common mistakes

  • He mustn't be at home. (I'm sure he isn't.) He can't be at home.
  • He might forgot his keys. He might have forgotten his keys.
  • It can't to be true. It can't be true.
  • She must been tired. She must have been tired.
The opposite of must (deduction) is can't, not mustn't. Mustn't means forbidden.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Насколько я уверен? Очень уверен, что правда → must. Очень уверен, что неправда → can't. Не уверен → might / may / could.
  2. Про сейчас или про прошлое? Сейчас → модальный + be / глагол. Прошлое → модальный + have + третья форма.
  3. Это происходит прямо сейчас? Да → модальный + be + -ing.

Частые ошибки

  • He mustn't be at home. (I'm sure he isn't.) He can't be at home.
  • He might forgot his keys. He might have forgotten his keys.
  • It can't to be true. It can't be true.
  • She must been tired. She must have been tired.
Противоположность must (предположение) — can't, а не mustn't. Mustn't означает «запрещено».

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Qanchalik ishonchim komil? Rost ekaniga juda ishonaman → must. Rost emasligiga juda ishonaman → can't. Ishonchim yo'q → might / may / could.
  2. Hozir haqidami yoki o'tmish haqidami? Hozir → modal + be / fe'l. O'tmish → modal + have + uchinchi shakl.
  3. Aynan hozir bo'lyaptimi? Ha → modal + be + -ing.

Real-life use

We make deductions every day when we see clues and do not have the full facts.

Guessing at home

"The lights are on. They must be at home."

When someone is late

"She isn't answering. She might be driving. She can't have forgotten."

Reacting to news

"That can't be true! He must have made a mistake."

IELTS Speaking: describing a photo

"The man might be waiting for someone. He must be cold because he is wearing a thick coat."
