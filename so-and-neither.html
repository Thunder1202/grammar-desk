So and neither

General info

We use so and neither / nor to agree with another person without repeating the whole sentence.

  • so + auxiliary + subject = "I agree" (positive): I like tea. — So do I.
  • neither + auxiliary + subject = "I agree" (negative): I can't swim. — Neither can I.

Examples

  • A: I love pizza. B: So do I.
  • A: I don't smoke. B: Neither do I. / Nor do I.
  • A: She's tired. B: So am I.
Use the same auxiliary as the first sentence — am/is/are, do/does/did, have, can, will... "I'm tired." → "So am I."

Мы используем so и neither / nor, чтобы согласиться с другим человеком, не повторяя всё предложение.

  • so + вспомогательный глагол + подлежащее = «я согласен» (утверждение): I like tea. — So do I.
  • neither + вспомогательный глагол + подлежащее = «я согласен» (отрицание): I can't swim. — Neither can I.

Примеры

  • A: I love pizza. B: So do I.
  • A: I don't smoke. B: Neither do I. / Nor do I.
  • A: She's tired. B: So am I.
Используй тот же вспомогательный глагол, что и в первом предложении — am/is/are, do/does/did, have, can, will... «I'm tired.» → «So am I.»

so va neither / nor ni boshqa odam bilan rozi bo'lish uchun ishlatamiz, butun gapni takrorlamasdan.

  • so + yordamchi fe'l + ega = "roziman" (darak): I like tea. — So do I.
  • neither + yordamchi fe'l + ega = "roziman" (inkor): I can't swim. — Neither can I.

Misollar

  • A: I love pizza. B: So do I.
  • A: I don't smoke. B: Neither do I. / Nor do I.
  • A: She's tired. B: So am I.
Birinchi gapdagi o'sha yordamchi fe'lni ishlating — am/is/are, do/does/did, have, can, will... "I'm tired." → "So am I."

Teacher's rule

First sentenceAgreement
I am tired.So am I.
I like tea.So do I.
She can sing.So can I.
I can't swim.Neither can I.
I don't smoke.Neither do I.
He wasn't ready.Neither was I.
  1. So + auxiliary + subject for positive agreement: I work hard. — So do I.
  2. Neither + auxiliary + subject for negative agreement: I don't drive. — Neither do I.
  3. The auxiliary must match: I have → So have I; I will → So will I.
  4. If there is no auxiliary, use do / does / did: She went home. — So did I.
  5. Word order is inverted: "So am I", not "So I am".
  6. Informal short forms are also common: "Me too." and "Me neither."
  1. So + вспомогательный + подлежащее для утвердительного согласия: I work hard. — So do I.
  2. Neither + вспомогательный + подлежащее для отрицательного согласия: I don't drive. — Neither do I.
  3. Вспомогательный глагол должен совпадать: I have → So have I; I will → So will I.
  4. Если вспомогательного нет, используем do / does / did: She went home. — So did I.
  5. Порядок слов обратный: «So am I», а не «So I am».
  6. В неформальной речи также употребляют: «Me too.» и «Me neither.»
  1. Darak rozilik uchun so + yordamchi + ega: I work hard. — So do I.
  2. Inkor rozilik uchun neither + yordamchi + ega: I don't drive. — Neither do I.
  3. Yordamchi fe'l mos kelishi kerak: I have → So have I; I will → So will I.
  4. Yordamchi bo'lmasa, do / does / did ishlatamiz: She went home. — So did I.
  5. So'z tartibi teskari: "So am I", "So I am" emas.
  6. Norasmiy qisqa shakllar ham bor: "Me too." va "Me neither."

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. Positive or negative? Positive → so. Negative → neither / nor.
  2. What auxiliary is in the first sentence? Copy it and invert the order.

Common mistakes

  • I like it. So I do. I like it. So do I.
  • I can't drive. So can't I. I can't drive. Neither can I.
  • I am tired. So do I. I am tired. So am I.
  • She went home. So she did. She went home. So did I.
Golden rule: same auxiliary, inverted order — "So am I", "Neither can I".

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Утверждение или отрицание? Утверждение → so. Отрицание → neither / nor.
  2. Какой вспомогательный глагол в первом предложении? Скопируй его и переставь порядок.

Частые ошибки

  • I like it. So I do. I like it. So do I.
  • I can't drive. So can't I. I can't drive. Neither can I.
  • I am tired. So do I. I am tired. So am I.
  • She went home. So she did. She went home. So did I.
Золотое правило: тот же вспомогательный, обратный порядок — «So am I», «Neither can I».

O'zingizga ikkita savol bering

  1. Darakmi yoki inkormi? Darak → so. Inkor → neither / nor.
  2. Birinchi gapda qanday yordamchi fe'l bor? Uni ko'chirib, tartibni teskari qiling.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

Real-life use

These short answers make your conversation sound natural and friendly.

Agreeing with a friend

"I'm so hungry." "So am I!"

Sharing an opinion

"I don't like mornings." "Neither do I."

Finding something in common

"I love travelling." "So do I. Where have you been?"

At work

"I can't come on Friday." "Neither can I."

IELTS Speaking: hobbies

"I enjoy reading, and so does my sister. Neither of us watches much TV."
