Advanced passives

General info

At C1 level the passive does much more than "The window was broken." We use special passives to report opinions and facts, to use infinitives and gerunds with a passive meaning, and to describe sudden events with get.

Three advanced passives

Reporting passive: People say that he is rich. → He is said to be rich. / It is said that he is rich.

Passive infinitive and gerund: She hopes to be promoted. Nobody likes being laughed at.

Get-passive: He got fired last week.

We use them to

  • Report what people think or say, in a formal way: The minister is believed to have resigned.
  • Talk about what happens to us: She hopes to be promoted. He hates being kept waiting.
  • Hide or skip the doer: It was reported that the road had been closed.
  • Show sudden or unexpected events: My bag got stolen.
Russian uses impersonal forms (считается, что он богат) and Uzbek uses deb hisoblanadi (u boy deb hisoblanadi). English can also make the person the subject: He is considered to be rich.

На уровне C1 пассив делает гораздо больше, чем «The window was broken». Особые пассивные конструкции нужны, чтобы передавать мнения и факты, использовать инфинитивы и герундии с пассивным значением и описывать внезапные события с get.

Три продвинутых пассива

Пассив для сообщений: People say that he is rich. → He is said to be rich. / It is said that he is rich.

Пассивный инфинитив и герундий: She hopes to be promoted. Nobody likes being laughed at.

Get-пассив: He got fired last week.

Зачем используем

  • Формально передавать, что люди думают или говорят: The minister is believed to have resigned.
  • Говорить о том, что происходит с нами: She hopes to be promoted. He hates being kept waiting.
  • Скрыть или пропустить деятеля: It was reported that the road had been closed.
  • Показать внезапные или неожиданные события: My bag got stolen.
В русском есть безличные формы (считается, что он богат), в узбекском — deb hisoblanadi (u boy deb hisoblanadi). В английском ещё можно сделать человека подлежащим: He is considered to be rich.

C1 darajada majhul nisbat «The window was broken» dan ancha ko'proq ishlarni qiladi. Maxsus majhul shakllar fikr va faktlarni yetkazish, infinitiv va gerundiyni majhul ma'noda ishlatish hamda get bilan kutilmagan voqealarni tasvirlash uchun kerak.

Uchta ilg'or majhul shakl

Xabar beruvchi majhul: People say that he is rich. → He is said to be rich. / It is said that he is rich.

Majhul infinitiv va gerundiy: She hopes to be promoted. Nobody likes being laughed at.

Get-majhul: He got fired last week.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Odamlar nima o'ylashi yoki aytishini rasmiy yetkazish: The minister is believed to have resigned.
  • Bizga nima bo'layotgani haqida gapirish: She hopes to be promoted. He hates being kept waiting.
  • Ish bajaruvchini yashirish yoki tushirib qoldirish: It was reported that the road had been closed.
  • Kutilmagan voqealarni ko'rsatish: My bag got stolen.
Rus tilida shaxssiz shakllar bor (считается, что он богат), o'zbek tilida deb hisoblanadi (u boy deb hisoblanadi). Ingliz tilida odamni ega qilish ham mumkin: He is considered to be rich.

  1. It-form: It + be + -ed reporting verb + that clause. It works with every tense: It is thought that prices will rise. It was said that he had left.
  2. Subject-form: the subject of the that-clause becomes the subject: He is said to be rich. The verb after it is a to-infinitive.
  3. Choose the infinitive by time: same time or future → to be / to do. Happening now → to be + -ing. Earlier → to have + -ed: He is said to have left. He was said to have left (before that time).
  4. Reporting verbs: say, believe, think, know, report, expect, consider, understand, suppose, claim, allege, estimate, assume. Some verbs (suggest, propose) work only in the it-form.
  5. Passive infinitive: after hope, want, expect, need, and after adjectives: She wants to be told. Use to have been + -ed for earlier: He seems to have been forgotten.
  6. Passive gerund: after enjoy, hate, like, avoid, risk and after prepositions: He hates being interrupted. She avoided being seen. Having been + -ed shows an earlier action.
  7. Get-passive: informal. It shows a change or an event, often sudden or unwelcome: I got stopped by the police. It is not used with state verbs such as know or be.
  8. Agent and style: add by + doer only when it is new or important: The play was written by Shakespeare. Reporting passives are formal (news, academic writing). Do not stack many passives in one sentence.
  1. It-форма: It + be + -ed глагол сообщения + that. Работает во всех временах: It is thought that prices will rise. It was said that he had left.
  2. Subject-форма: подлежащее придаточного становится подлежащим: He is said to be rich. После него идёт to-инфинитив.
  3. Инфинитив по времени: то же время или будущее → to be / to do. Происходит сейчас → to be + -ing. Раньше → to have + -ed: He is said to have left. He was said to have left (до того момента).
  4. Глаголы сообщения: say, believe, think, know, report, expect, consider, understand, suppose, claim, allege, estimate, assume. Некоторые (suggest, propose) работают только в it-форме.
  5. Пассивный инфинитив: после hope, want, expect, need и после прилагательных: She wants to be told. Для раннего действия to have been + -ed: He seems to have been forgotten.
  6. Пассивный герундий: после enjoy, hate, like, avoid, risk и после предлогов: He hates being interrupted. She avoided being seen. Having been + -ed показывает более раннее действие.
  7. Get-пассив: неформальный. Показывает изменение или событие, часто внезапное или неприятное: I got stopped by the police. Не используется со глаголами состояния know, be.
  8. Деятель и стиль: by + деятель добавляем, только если это ново или важно: The play was written by Shakespeare. Пассив для сообщений формален (новости, наука). Не нагромождай много пассивов в одном предложении.
  1. It-shakl: It + be + -ed xabar fe'li + that gap. Hamma zamonlarda ishlaydi: It is thought that prices will rise. It was said that he had left.
  2. Subject-shakl: that-gapning egasi asosiy ega bo'ladi: He is said to be rich. Undan keyin to-infinitiv keladi.
  3. Infinitivni vaqtga qarab tanlang: bir vaqt yoki kelasi zamon → to be / to do. Hozir bo'layotgan → to be + -ing. Oldin bo'lgan → to have + -ed: He is said to have left. He was said to have left (o'sha vaqtdan oldin).
  4. Xabar fe'llari: say, believe, think, know, report, expect, consider, understand, suppose, claim, allege, estimate, assume. Ba'zilari (suggest, propose) faqat it-shaklida ishlaydi.
  5. Majhul infinitiv: hope, want, expect, need va sifatlardan keyin: She wants to be told. Oldingi ish uchun to have been + -ed: He seems to have been forgotten.
  6. Majhul gerundiy: enjoy, hate, like, avoid, risk va predloglardan keyin: He hates being interrupted. She avoided being seen. Having been + -ed oldingi ishni bildiradi.
  7. Get-majhul: norasmiy. O'zgarish yoki voqeani, ko'pincha kutilmagan yoki yoqimsiz voqeani bildiradi: I got stopped by the police. know, be kabi holat fe'llari bilan ishlatilmaydi.
  8. Bajaruvchi va uslub: by + bajaruvchi faqat yangi yoki muhim bo'lsa qo'shiladi: The play was written by Shakespeare. Xabar beruvchi majhul rasmiy (yangiliklar, ilmiy yozuv). Bitta gapga ko'p majhul shakl yig'maslik kerak.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Am I reporting what people think or say? Yes → reporting passive: He is said to be rich.
  2. Did the action happen earlier than the reporting? Yes → to have + -ed: is said to have left.
  3. Is it a sudden or unexpected event, in an informal style? Yes → get-passive: He got fired.

Build it in 3 steps

People believe that the minister has resigned. 1) Take the subject of the that-clause: the minister. 2) Add the passive reporting verb: is believed. 3) Use the perfect infinitive because it is earlier: to have resigned. → The minister is believed to have resigned.

Why it works

The sentence starts with the person or thing we are talking about, and the opinion comes after. It sounds objective because we do not say who has the opinion.

Common mistakes

  • It is said him to be rich. He is said to be rich.
  • The house is said to be built in 1800. The house is said to have been built in 1800.
  • She avoided to be seen. She avoided being seen.
  • I got stolen my wallet. My wallet got stolen.
For an earlier time, always use have + -ed (to have left). After avoid, enjoy, hate use the gerund: being + -ed.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я передаю, что люди думают или говорят? Да → пассив для сообщений: He is said to be rich.
  2. Действие было раньше, чем сообщение? Да → to have + -ed: is said to have left.
  3. Это внезапное или неожиданное событие в неформальном стиле? Да → get-пассив: He got fired.

Строим за 3 шага

People believe that the minister has resigned. 1) Возьми подлежащее придаточного: the minister. 2) Добавь пассивный глагол сообщения: is believed. 3) Поставь перфектный инфинитив, потому что это раньше: to have resigned. → The minister is believed to have resigned.

Почему это работает

Предложение начинается с того, о ком или о чём мы говорим, а мнение идёт после. Оно звучит объективно, потому что мы не говорим, кто так думает.

Частые ошибки

  • It is said him to be rich. He is said to be rich.
  • The house is said to be built in 1800. The house is said to have been built in 1800.
  • She avoided to be seen. She avoided being seen.
  • I got stolen my wallet. My wallet got stolen.
Для более раннего времени всегда have + -ed (to have left). После avoid, enjoy, hate — герундий: being + -ed.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Odamlar nima o'ylashi yoki aytishini yetkazyapmanmi? Ha → xabar beruvchi majhul: He is said to be rich.
  2. Ish xabardan oldin bo'lganmi? Ha → to have + -ed: is said to have left.
  3. Bu norasmiy uslubdagi kutilmagan voqeami? Ha → get-majhul: He got fired.

Real-life use

Advanced passives are common in news, reports and academic writing. The get-passive is common in everyday talk.

News

"The suspect is believed to have left the country. The company is expected to announce its results on Friday."

Academic writing

"It is widely believed that technology improves learning. It has been argued that the policy is unfair."

Work reports

"The project is expected to be completed by June. The team has been asked to submit its plan."

Everyday talk

"I got stopped by the police. She got promoted last month. My phone got stolen."

IELTS Writing and Speaking

"Children are believed to learn languages faster than adults. It is often said that money cannot buy happiness." Speaking: "I hate being kept waiting."
