To be

General info

To be is the most important and the most irregular verb in English. We use it to say who or what someone is, where they are from, how old they are, where they are and what they are like. In the present it has three forms: am, is, are.

We use it for

  • Name, job, nationality: I am a teacher. She is from Uzbekistan.
  • Age: He is 18. (English does not use have for age.)
  • Descriptions and feelings: The tea is hot. We are happy.
  • Places: They are at home.
  • Time and weather: It is 5 o'clock. It is cold.
Uzbek says Men talabaman with a suffix, and Russian often drops the verb (Я студент). English always needs the word: I am a student.

To be (быть) — самый важный и самый неправильный глагол английского. С ним мы говорим, кто человек или что предмет, откуда он, сколько ему лет, где он находится и какой он. В настоящем времени у него три формы: am, is, are.

Когда используем

  • Имя, профессия, национальность: I am a teacher. She is from Uzbekistan.
  • Возраст: He is 18. (Для возраста английский не использует have.)
  • Описание и чувства: The tea is hot. We are happy.
  • Место: They are at home.
  • Время и погода: It is 5 o'clock. It is cold.
В русском в настоящем времени «быть» обычно пропускают (Я студент). В английском глагол обязателен: I am a student.

To be (bo'lmoq) — ingliz tilidagi eng muhim va eng noto'g'ri fe'l. U orqali kim yoki nima ekanligi, qayerlikligi, yoshi, qayerdaligi va qandayligi aytiladi. Hozirgi zamonda uning uchta shakli bor: am, is, are.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Ism, kasb, millat: I am a teacher. She is from Uzbekistan.
  • Yosh: He is 18. (Yosh uchun inglizchada have ishlatilmaydi.)
  • Tavsif va his-tuyg'u: The tea is hot. We are happy.
  • Joy: They are at home.
  • Vaqt va ob-havo: It is 5 o'clock. It is cold.
O'zbek tilida -man, -san qo'shimchasi bor (Men talabaman). Inglizchada esa alohida so'z kerak: I am a student.

Teacher's rule

Ihe, she, ityou, we, they
+I amHe isYou are
−I am notHe isn'tYou aren't
?Am I?Is he?Are you?
  1. Affirmative: I am, he / she / it is, you / we / they are. Short forms: I'm, he's, she's, it's, you're, we're, they're.
  2. Negative: put not after the verb: is not = isn't, are not = aren't. For I we say I'm not (never "amn't").
  3. Question: swap the subject and the verb: You are tired → Are you tired?
  4. Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't. Do not shorten a positive answer (not "Yes, I'm").

Wh- questions

  • What is your name?
  • Where are you from?
  • How old is she?
  1. Утверждение: I am, he / she / it is, you / we / they are. Краткие формы: I'm, he's, she's, it's, you're, we're, they're.
  2. Отрицание: not после глагола: is not = isn't, are not = aren't. С I говорим I'm not (никогда «amn't»).
  3. Вопрос: меняем местами подлежащее и глагол: You are tired → Are you tired?
  4. Краткие ответы: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't. Положительный ответ не сокращаем (не «Yes, I'm»).

Вопросы с Wh-

  • What is your name?
  • Where are you from?
  • How old is she?
  1. Darak gap: I am, he / she / it is, you / we / they are. Qisqa shakllar: I'm, he's, she's, it's, you're, we're, they're.
  2. Inkor: not fe'ldan keyin qo'yiladi: is not = isn't, are not = aren't. I bilan I'm not deymiz ("amn't" yo'q).
  3. So'roq: ega va fe'l o'rni almashadi: You are tired → Are you tired?
  4. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't. Tasdiq javobni qisqartirmaymiz ("Yes, I'm" emas).

Wh- so'roqlar

  • What is your name?
  • Where are you from?
  • How old is she?

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Who is it about? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  2. Negative? Put not right after am / is / are.
  3. Question? Move am / is / are to the front.

Common mistakes

  • He are a doctor. He is a doctor.
  • I have 18 years. I am 18 (years old).
  • Is cold today. It is cold today.
  • Where you are from? Where are you from?
Memory trick: I am, you are, he / she / it is. Say it out loud three times.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. О ком речь? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  2. Отрицание? Поставь not сразу после am / is / are.
  3. Вопрос? Перенеси am / is / are в начало.

Частые ошибки

  • He are a doctor. He is a doctor.
  • I have 18 years. I am 18 (years old).
  • Is cold today. It is cold today.
  • Where you are from? Where are you from?
Запоминалка: I am, you are, he / she / it is. Повтори вслух три раза.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Gap kim haqida? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  2. Inkor? not ni am / is / are dan keyin qo'ying.
  3. So'roq? am / is / are ni gap boshiga olib chiqing.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • He are a doctor. He is a doctor.
  • I have 18 years. I am 18 (years old).
  • Is cold today. It is cold today.
  • Where you are from? Where are you from?
Eslab qolish: I am, you are, he / she / it is. Uch marta ovoz chiqarib ayting.

Real-life use

You use to be in the first minute of almost every conversation.

Introducing yourself

"Hi, I'm Dilnoza. I'm from Samarkand."

Greeting people

"How are you?" "I'm fine, thanks. And you?"

Filling in a form

Name: Aziz Karimov. Age: 18. Nationality: Uzbek.

Describing things

"The room is small, but it is bright."

Time and weather

"It's 7:30. It's cold today."

Asking for help when travelling

"Excuse me, where is the station? Is it far?" "Is this seat free?"
