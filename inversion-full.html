Inversion (full)

General info

At C1 we use inversion in many structures to give emphasis and a formal, written style. The word order changes after fronted negative, limiting, so / such and place expressions.

We use it for

  • Negative and limiting openers: At no time did he admit the mistake. Not only did she win, but she also broke the record.
  • Time and condition links: Not until he left did I realise. Only if you pay will we ship it.
  • So / such: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the noise that nobody slept.
  • Place and motion (literary): Down came the rain. On the hill stands a castle.
  • Formal conditionals: Had I known, ... Should you need ..., Were she here, ...

Words that go with it

no sooner, hardly, not until, only if, so ... that, such ... that, had, should, were.

Russian and Uzbek keep normal order. In English the auxiliary jumps in front of the subject, like in a question, after these openers.

На уровне C1 мы используем инверсию во многих конструкциях для выделения и формального, письменного стиля. Порядок слов меняется после отрицательных, ограничительных, so / such и выражений места в начале предложения.

Когда используем

  • Отрицательные и ограничительные начала: At no time did he admit the mistake. Not only did she win, but she also broke the record.
  • Связки времени и условия: Not until he left did I realise. Only if you pay will we ship it.
  • So / such: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the noise that nobody slept.
  • Место и движение (литературный стиль): Down came the rain. On the hill stands a castle.
  • Формальные условные: Had I known, ... Should you need ..., Were she here, ...

Слова-подсказки

no sooner, hardly, not until, only if, so ... that, such ... that, had, should, were.

В русском и узбекском порядок слов обычный. В английском после этих начал вспомогательный глагол выходит перед подлежащим, как в вопросе.

C1 darajasida inversiya ko'p qoliplarda ta'kid va rasmiy, yozma uslub uchun ishlatiladi. Gap boshidagi inkor, cheklovchi, so / such va joy iboralaridan keyin so'z tartibi o'zgaradi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Inkor va cheklovchi boshlanishlar: At no time did he admit the mistake. Not only did she win, but she also broke the record.
  • Vaqt va shart bog'lanishlari: Not until he left did I realise. Only if you pay will we ship it.
  • So / such: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the noise that nobody slept.
  • Joy va harakat (adabiy uslub): Down came the rain. On the hill stands a castle.
  • Rasmiy shart gaplar: Had I known, ... Should you need ..., Were she here, ...

Yordamchi so'zlar

no sooner, hardly, not until, only if, so ... that, such ... that, had, should, were.

Rus va o'zbek tilida so'z tartibi odatiy. Inglizchada bu boshlanishlardan keyin yordamchi fe'l egadan oldin chiqadi, savoldagidek.

Teacher's rule

GroupPatternExample
negative adverbialsopener + aux + subject + verbAt no time did he admit it. Under no circumstances will refunds be given.
Hardly / No soonerhad + subject + participleHardly had she arrived when it rained. No sooner had she arrived than it rained.
Not only ... butdid / does + subjectNot only did she win, but she also broke the record.
Not until / Only + time or conditioninvert the main clauseNot until he left did I realise. Only if you pay will we ship it.
So / suchso + adjective + be + subject; such + be + subjectSo tired was she that she slept. Such was the noise that nobody slept.
place / motion (literary)adverb + full verb + subjectDown came the rain. On the hill stands a castle.
conditionalsHad / Should / Were + subjectHad I known ... Should you need ... Were she here ...
  1. Rule: after a fronted negative, limiting, so / such or place expression, put the auxiliary (or be, or a modal) before the subject. With no auxiliary, add do / does / did.
  2. Be and modals invert directly: Nowhere is it safer. Never could I have imagined this.
  3. Not until / Only + time or condition: invert the main clause, not the time clause: Not until he left did I realise. Only after midnight did it stop.
  4. Hardly / Scarcely / Barely ... when and No sooner ... than take the past perfect inverted: Hardly had she arrived when it rained.
  5. So / such ... that: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the force of the storm that trees fell.
  6. Place and motion (literary): the full verb goes before the subject, with no auxiliary: Here comes the bus. Down came the rain. With a pronoun subject there is no inversion: Here it comes.
  7. Conditional inversion: Had I known = If I had known. Should you need help = If you need help. Were she here = If she were here. Negative: Had I not known (not Hadn't I known).
  1. Правило: после отрицательного, ограничительного, so / such или выражения места в начале ставим вспомогательный глагол (или be, или модальный) перед подлежащим. Нет вспомогательного — добавляем do / does / did.
  2. Be и модальные переставляются сразу: Nowhere is it safer. Never could I have imagined this.
  3. Not until / Only + время или условие: инверсия в главной части, не в придаточной времени: Not until he left did I realise. Only after midnight did it stop.
  4. Hardly / Scarcely / Barely ... when и No sooner ... than с past perfect и инверсией: Hardly had she arrived when it rained.
  5. So / such ... that: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the force of the storm that trees fell.
  6. Место и движение (литературный стиль): полный глагол перед подлежащим, без вспомогательного: Here comes the bus. Down came the rain. Если подлежащее — местоимение, инверсии нет: Here it comes.
  7. Инверсия в условных: Had I known = If I had known. Should you need help = If you need help. Were she here = If she were here. Отрицание: Had I not known (не Hadn't I known).
  1. Qoida: gap boshidagi inkor, cheklovchi, so / such yoki joy iborasidan keyin yordamchi fe'l (yoki be, yoki modal) egadan oldin qo'yiladi. Yordamchi fe'l bo'lmasa, do / does / did qo'shiladi.
  2. Be va modallar to'g'ridan-to'g'ri o'rin almashadi: Nowhere is it safer. Never could I have imagined this.
  3. Not until / Only + vaqt yoki shart: asosiy gapda inversiya, vaqt gapida emas: Not until he left did I realise. Only after midnight did it stop.
  4. Hardly / Scarcely / Barely ... when va No sooner ... than inversiyali past perfect bilan: Hardly had she arrived when it rained.
  5. So / such ... that: So tired was she that she fell asleep. Such was the force of the storm that trees fell.
  6. Joy va harakat (adabiy uslub): to'liq fe'l egadan oldin, yordamchi fe'lsiz: Here comes the bus. Down came the rain. Ega olmosh bo'lsa inversiya yo'q: Here it comes.
  7. Shart gaplarda inversiya: Had I known = If I had known. Should you need help = If you need help. Were she here = If she were here. Inkor: Had I not known (Hadn't I known emas).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Does the sentence start with a negative, limiting, so / such or place expression? Yes → consider inversion.
  2. Which part inverts? The main clause. Not the clause after not until / only after.
  3. Is there an auxiliary? Yes → move it. No → add do / does / did, except with literary place openers (use the full verb).

Normal order and inversion

She was so tired that she fell asleep. → So tired was she that she fell asleep.

Common mistakes

  • Not until he left I realised. Not until he left did I realise.
  • So tired she was that she slept. So tired was she that she slept.
  • Here comes it. Here it comes.
  • Hadn't I known, I would have called. Had I not known, I would have called.
Inversion is formal. In normal conversation, use normal word order.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Предложение начинается с отрицательного, ограничительного, so / such или выражения места? Да → подумай об инверсии.
  2. Какая часть инвертируется? Главная. Не часть после not until / only after.
  3. Есть вспомогательный глагол? Да → переставь. Нет → добавь do / does / did, кроме литературных начал с местом (используй полный глагол).

Инверсия — формальный стиль. В обычной беседе используй обычный порядок слов.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Gap inkor, cheklovchi, so / such yoki joy iborasi bilan boshlanadimi? Ha → inversiyani o'ylab ko'ring.
  2. Qaysi qism inversiyaga uchraydi? Asosiy gap. not until / only after dan keyingi gap emas.
  3. Yordamchi fe'l bormi? Ha → o'rnini almashtiring. Yo'q → do / does / did qo'shing, joy bilan boshlanadigan adabiy gaplar bundan mustasno (to'liq fe'l ishlating).

Oddiy tartib va inversiya

She was so tired that she fell asleep. → So tired was she that she fell asleep.

Inversiya rasmiy uslub. Oddiy suhbatda odatiy so'z tartibini ishlating.

Real-life use

Full inversion appears in formal writing, speeches, notices and literary storytelling.

Formal notices and rules

"Under no circumstances will refunds be given. Only if you register will you receive access."

Speeches and reports

"Not only did the company grow, but it also doubled its profits. Such was the demand that tickets sold out in an hour."

Storytelling

"Down came the rain. Not until dawn did the storm stop."

IELTS Writing: a formal point

"Not until the twentieth century did most people have access to electricity."

English only

Examples and drill

Negative adverbials

At no time did he admit it. Under no circumstances will refunds be given. Nowhere is it safer.

Hardly and no sooner

Hardly had she arrived when it rained. No sooner had we left than the storm began.

Not until and only

Not until he left did I realise. Only if you pay will we ship it. Only after midnight did it stop.

So and such

So tired was she that she slept. Such was the noise that nobody slept.

Place and motion

Here comes the bus. Down came the rain. On the hill stands a castle.

Conditionals

Had I known, I would have called. Should you need help, call me. Were she here, she would agree.
