Time clauses

General info

A time clause tells us when something happens. It begins with words like when, before, after, as soon as, until, while.

  • when: When I get home, I'll call you.
  • before: Wash your hands before you eat.
  • after: We left after the film ended.
  • as soon as: Call me as soon as you arrive.
  • until / till: Wait here until I come back.
  • while: She read while I cooked.
Important: after these words we use the present, not will, for the future: "When I get home, I'll call you." (not "when I will get").

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. Does the clause start with when / before / after / as soon as / until? Then use the present, not will.
  2. Is it first in the sentence? Then add a comma after it.

Common mistakes

  • When I will finish, I'll call you. When I finish, I'll call you.
  • I'll wait until you will come. I'll wait until you come.
  • After I will eat, I'll go. After I eat, I'll go.
  • Before you will leave, say goodbye. Before you leave, say goodbye.
Think in two parts: "time word + present" for the future — never "will" inside the time clause.

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Придаточное начинается с when / before / after / as soon as / until? Тогда используй настоящее, а не will.
  2. Оно стоит первым в предложении? Тогда поставь запятую после него.

Частые ошибки

  • When I will finish, I'll call you. When I finish, I'll call you.
  • I'll wait until you will come. I'll wait until you come.
  • After I will eat, I'll go. After I eat, I'll go.
  • Before you will leave, say goodbye. Before you leave, say goodbye.
Думай в двух частях: «слово времени + настоящее» для будущего — никогда «will» внутри придаточного времени.

Real-life use

Time clauses help you organise a story or plan the order of events.

Making plans

"As soon as I get the tickets, I'll text you."

Daily routine

"I always have coffee before I start work."

Instructions

"Wait here until the doctor calls your name."

At the airport

"Call me when you land, and we'll meet after you get your bags."

IELTS Speaking: a promise

"When I finish my studies, I'll look for a job in another city."
