Past Simple of to be

General info

was and were are the past forms of am / is / are. We use them to talk about states, places, feelings and descriptions in the past, when the situation is finished.

We use them for

  • Where someone or something was: I was at home yesterday.
  • How it felt or what it was like: The film was great. We were tired.
  • Jobs, age and origin in the past: He was a student in 2020.

Time words that go with it

yesterday, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, yesterday morning.

Uzbek uses edi (Men uyda edim). Russian changes был / была / были by gender and number. English only has two forms: was and were.

was и were — прошедшие формы am / is / are. Мы используем их, чтобы говорить о состояниях, местах, чувствах и описаниях в прошлом, когда ситуация закончилась.

Когда используем

  • Где кто-то или что-то был: I was at home yesterday.
  • Как себя чувствовал или каким было: The film was great. We were tired.
  • Профессия, возраст и происхождение в прошлом: He was a student in 2020.

Teacher's rule

I, he, she, ityou, we, they
+He was here.They were here.
−He wasn't here.They weren't here.
?Was he here?Were they here?
  1. Two forms: was with I, he, she, it. were with you, we, they.
  2. Negative: was not = wasn't, were not = weren't.
  3. Question: swap the subject and the verb: You were tired → Were you tired?
  4. Short answers: Yes, I was. / No, she wasn't. / Yes, they were. / No, we weren't.
  5. There was / There were: There was a problem. There were two cars.

Wh- questions

  • Where were you yesterday?
  • How was your weekend?
  1. Две формы: was с I, he, she, it. were с you, we, they.
  2. Отрицание: was not = wasn't, were not = weren't.
  3. Вопрос: меняем местами подлежащее и глагол: You were tired → Were you tired?
  4. Краткие ответы: Yes, I was. / No, she wasn't. / Yes, they were. / No, we weren't.
  5. There was / There were: There was a problem. There were two cars.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it finished, in the past? Yes → was / were.
  2. Who is it about? I, he, she, it → was. You, we, they → were.
  3. Negative or question? Put not after was / were, or move was / were to the front. Do not add did.

Common mistakes

  • I were at home. I was at home.
  • They was tired. They were tired.
  • Did you were at school? Were you at school?
  • She didn't was happy. She wasn't happy.
was and were work alone. They never need did.

Real-life use

You use was and were every time you talk about yesterday, your past or a trip.

Small talk

"How was your weekend?" "It was great, thanks!"

Explaining why you were late

"Sorry I was late. The bus was full."

Describing a trip

"The hotel was clean and the people were friendly."

Talking about your childhood

"I was a shy child. My teacher was very kind."

IELTS Speaking

"Where were you born?" "It was a small town, and the weather was hot."
