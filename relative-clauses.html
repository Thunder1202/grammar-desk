Relative clauses

General info

A defining relative clause gives the information we need to know which person or thing we mean. It starts with who, which, that, whose, where or when.

We use it for

  • Saying which person: The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
  • Saying which thing: The book that I read was great.
  • Defining words: A baker is a person who makes bread.

Words that go with it

who, which, that, whose, where, when, the one, someone who, a place where.

Russian uses который (человек, который живёт рядом). Uzbek uses -gan, -adigan (yonimda yashaydigan ayol). English uses a separate word: who, which, that.

Определительное придаточное даёт информацию, нужную, чтобы понять, о каком человеке или предмете речь. Оно начинается с who, which, that, whose, where или when.

Когда используем

  • Указать, какой человек: The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
  • Указать, какой предмет: The book that I read was great.
  • Определить слово: A baker is a person who makes bread.

Слова-подсказки

who, which, that, whose, where, when, the one, someone who, a place where.

В русском используется «который» (человек, который живёт рядом). В узбекском -gan, -adigan (yonimda yashaydigan ayol). В английском отдельное слово: who, which, that.

Aniqlovchi ergash gap qaysi odam yoki narsa haqida gapirayotganimizni bilish uchun kerakli ma'lumotni beradi. U who, which, that, whose, where yoki when bilan boshlanadi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Qaysi odam ekanini aytish: The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
  • Qaysi narsa ekanini aytish: The book that I read was great.
  • So'zni ta'riflash: A baker is a person who makes bread.

Yordamchi so'zlar

who, which, that, whose, where, when, the one, someone who, a place where.

Rus tilida «который» ishlatiladi. O'zbek tilida -gan, -adigan (yonimda yashaydigan ayol). Inglizchada alohida so'z: who, which, that.

  1. Defining clause = essential information that says which person or thing. It has no commas: The man who lives next door is a doctor.
  2. who for people, which for things, that for both (common in speech): The book that I read was good.
  3. where = place, when = time, whose + noun = belonging: the girl whose bag was stolen.
  4. Subject or object? If the pronoun is the subject of its clause (who lives), you cannot drop it. If it is the object (the book (that) I read), you can drop it.
  5. Do not repeat the subject: The man who lives next door. Not: who he lives.
  6. No what after a noun: the thing that I need. Not: the thing what I need.
  1. Определительное придаточное = важная информация, указывающая, какой человек или предмет. Запятых нет: The man who lives next door is a doctor.
  2. who для людей, which для вещей, that для обоих (часто в речи): The book that I read was good.
  3. where = место, when = время, whose + существительное = принадлежность: the girl whose bag was stolen.
  4. Подлежащее или дополнение? Если местоимение — подлежащее своей части (who lives), опускать нельзя. Если дополнение (the book (that) I read), можно опустить.
  5. Не повторяй подлежащее: The man who lives next door. Не: who he lives.
  6. Никакого what после существительного: the thing that I need. Не: the thing what I need.
  1. Aniqlovchi ergash gap = qaysi odam yoki narsa ekanini ko'rsatuvchi muhim ma'lumot. Vergul yo'q: The man who lives next door is a doctor.
  2. who odamlar uchun, which narsalar uchun, that ikkalasi uchun (og'zaki nutqda keng tarqalgan): The book that I read was good.
  3. where = joy, when = vaqt, whose + ot = egalik: the girl whose bag was stolen.
  4. Ega yoki to'ldiruvchi? Agar olmosh o'z qismining egasi bo'lsa (who lives), tushirib bo'lmaydi. Agar to'ldiruvchi bo'lsa (the book (that) I read), tushirish mumkin.
  5. Egani takrorlamang: The man who lives next door. who he lives emas.
  6. what yo'q otdan keyin: the thing that I need. the thing what I need emas.

Non-defining clauses

A non-defining clause adds extra information. The noun is already clear. Use commas, use who / which (never that), and you cannot drop the pronoun: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor. Tashkent, which is the capital, is very big.

Неопределительные придаточные

Неопределительное придаточное добавляет лишнюю информацию. Существительное и так понятно. Нужны запятые, who / which (не that), местоимение опускать нельзя: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor. Tashkent, which is the capital, is very big.

Noaniqlovchi ergash gaplar

Noaniqlovchi ergash gap qo'shimcha ma'lumot beradi. Ot allaqachon tushunarli. Vergul kerak, who / which ishlatiladi (that emas), olmoshni tushirib bo'lmaydi: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor. Tashkent, which is the capital, is very big.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Who or what am I describing? Person → who / that. Thing → which / that. Place → where. Time → when. Belonging → whose.
  2. Is the pronoun the doer in the clause? Yes (who lives) → keep it. No, it is the object (the book I read) → you can drop it.
  3. Is the clause essential? Yes → no commas.

Joining two sentences

I met a woman. She speaks five languages. → I met a woman who speaks five languages.

Common mistakes

  • The man which lives next door is kind. The man who lives next door is kind.
  • The book what I read was good. The book that I read was good.
  • The woman who she lives next door is a nurse. The woman who lives next door is a nurse.
  • The girl who bag was stolen is upset. The girl whose bag was stolen is upset.
One subject only: the pronoun replaces he / she / it. Never write who he or which it.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Кого или что я описываю? Человек → who / that. Предмет → which / that. Место → where. Время → when. Принадлежность → whose.
  2. Местоимение — действующее лицо в этой части? Да (who lives) → оставляем. Нет, это дополнение (the book I read) → можно опустить.
  3. Часть обязательна по смыслу? Да → без запятых.

Соединяем два предложения

Real-life use

We use relative clauses to describe, to identify and to explain what words mean.

Identifying people

"That's the girl who sits next to me. The man that called is my uncle."

Describing places and things

"It's the shop which sells fresh bread. This is the café where we first met."

Explaining words

"A baker is a person who makes bread. A library is a place where you borrow books."

IELTS Speaking: describing a friend

"My best friend is someone who always listens to me. We met at the school where I studied English."

English only

Examples and drill

People

The woman who lives next door is a nurse. The man that called is my uncle.

Things

The book which I read was great. The phone that I bought is fast.

Dropping the pronoun (object)

The book (that) I read was great. The film (which) we saw was boring.

Whose, where, when

The girl whose bag was stolen is upset. The café where we met is closed. I remember the day when we met.

Non-defining (extra information)

My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor. Tashkent, which is the capital, is very big.
