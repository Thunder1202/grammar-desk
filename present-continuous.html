Present Continuous

General info

Present Continuous describes an action that is happening now, or around now, and is not finished. It also shows that a situation is temporary or changing.

We use it for

  • Right now: She is reading a book.
  • Around now (temporary): I am studying English this month.
  • Changing situations: The weather is getting colder.

Time words that go with it

now, right now, at the moment, today, this week, this year. Also the signals Look! and Listen!

Uzbek has o'qiyapman, which is very close to this tense. Russian uses one present for both (Я читаю), but English must choose: habit = Present Simple, now = Present Continuous.

Present Continuous описывает действие, которое происходит сейчас или в данный период и ещё не закончено. Оно также показывает, что ситуация временная или меняется.

Когда используем

  • Прямо сейчас: She is reading a book.
  • В данный период (временно): I am studying English this month.
  • Меняющиеся ситуации: The weather is getting colder.

Teacher's rule

Ihe, she, ityou, we, they
+I am workingHe is workingYou are working
−I am not workingHe isn't workingYou aren't working
?Am I working?Is he working?Are you working?
  1. Affirmative: subject + am / is / are + verb-ing.
  2. Negative: put not after am / is / are: She isn't sleeping.
  3. Question: move am / is / are to the front: Is he working? Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't.
  4. Not used in this tense: state verbs such as know, like, love, want, need, understand. Say I know, not "I am knowing".

Spelling of -ing

  • Most verbs: + ing (work → working)
  • Ends in silent -e: drop the e (make → making)
  • One syllable, consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter (run → running, sit → sitting)
  • Ends in -ie: ie → ying (lie → lying)
  1. Утверждение: подлежащее + am / is / are + глагол-ing.
  2. Отрицание: not после am / is / are: She isn't sleeping.
  3. Вопрос: am / is / are в начало: Is he working? Краткие ответы: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't.
  4. Не используются: глаголы состояния: know, like, love, want, need, understand. Говорим I know, а не «I am knowing».

Правописание -ing

  • Большинство глаголов: + ing (work → working)
  • Оканчивается на немое -e: e отбрасываем (make → making)
  • Один слог, согласная + гласная + согласная: удваиваем последнюю (run → running, sit → sitting)
  • Оканчивается на -ie: ie → ying (lie → lying)
  1. Darak gap: ega + am / is / are + fe'l-ing.
  2. Inkor: not am / is / are dan keyin: She isn't sleeping.
  3. So'roq: am / is / are gap boshiga: Is he working? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I am. / No, he isn't.
  4. Ishlatilmaydi: holat fe'llari: know, like, love, want, need, understand. I know deymiz, "I am knowing" emas.

-ing yozilishi

  • Ko'pchilik fe'llar: + ing (work → working)
  • Jim -e bilan tugasa: e tushadi (make → making)
  • Bir bo'g'in, undosh + unli + undosh: oxirgi harf ikkilanadi (run → running, sit → sitting)
  • -ie bilan tugasa: ie → ying (lie → lying)

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it happening now, or around now? Look around. If you can see it, use Present Continuous.
  2. Who is it about? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. Which form of the verb? Add -ing. The verb never takes -s here.

Common mistakes

  • She is work now. She is working now.
  • I working now. I am working now.
  • He is runing. He is running.
  • I am knowing the answer. I know the answer.
Two parts, always together: am / is / are + verb-ing. One part alone is a mistake.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это происходит сейчас или в данный период? Посмотри вокруг. Если видишь действие, используй Present Continuous.
  2. О ком речь? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. Какая форма глагола? Добавь -ing. Здесь глагол никогда не берёт -s.

Частые ошибки

  • She is work now. She is working now.
  • I working now. I am working now.
  • He is runing. He is running.
  • I am knowing the answer. I know the answer.
Две части всегда вместе: am / is / are + глагол-ing. Одна часть без другой — ошибка.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu hozir yoki shu davrda bo'lyaptimi? Atrofga qarang. Ishni ko'rsangiz, Present Continuous ishlating.
  2. Gap kim haqida? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. Fe'lning qaysi shakli? -ing qo'shing. Bu yerda fe'l hech qachon -s olmaydi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She is work now. She is working now.
  • I working now. I am working now.
  • He is runing. He is running.
  • I am knowing the answer. I know the answer.
Ikki qism har doim birga: am / is / are + fe'l-ing. Bittasi yolg'iz bo'lsa, xato.

Real-life use

People use Present Continuous to say what is going on at this moment.

Phone calls and messages

"Hi, I'm walking to work. Are you coming?" "I'm waiting outside."

Describing a picture (IELTS Speaking style)

"In this picture, two people are sitting at a table and they are drinking coffee."

Noticing things

"Look! It's snowing." "Listen! Someone is singing."

Online meetings

"Sorry, I can't hear you. Are you sharing your screen?"

Talking about this period

"I'm learning English this year. I'm preparing for IELTS."
