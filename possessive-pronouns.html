Possessive pronouns

General info

Possessive pronouns show who something belongs to, without repeating the noun: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.

The pairs

  • my → mine · your → yours · his → his · her → hers
  • our → ours · their → theirs

How we use them

  • After the verb be: This book is mine.
  • At the start of a sentence: Mine is the red one.
  • They replace my + noun: This is my pen = This pen is mine.
Possessive pronouns never take an apostrophe: yours, hers, ours, theirs (never "your's"). Do not confuse its (belonging to it) with it's (it is).

Притяжательные местоимения показывают, кому что принадлежит, без повторения существительного: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.

Пары

  • my → mine · your → yours · his → his · her → hers
  • our → ours · their → theirs

Как используем

  • После глагола be: This book is mine.
  • В начале предложения: Mine is the red one.
  • Заменяют my + существительное: This is my pen = This pen is mine.
Притяжательные местоимения пишутся без апострофа: yours, hers, ours, theirs (никогда «your's»). Не путайте its (принадлежащий ему) и it's (it is).

Egalik olmoshlari narsa kimga tegishli ekanini, otdan foydalanmasdan ko'rsatadi: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs.

Juftliklar

  • my → mine · your → yours · his → his · her → hers
  • our → ours · their → theirs

Qanday ishlatamiz

  • be fe'lidan keyin: This book is mine.
  • Gap boshida: Mine is the red one.
  • my + ot o'rnini bosadi: This is my pen = This pen is mine.
Egalik olmoshlarida apostrof bo'lmaydi: yours, hers, ours, theirs ("your's" emas). its (unga tegishli) bilan it's (it is) ni aralashtirmang.

  1. mine = my + noun. This is my pen. = This pen is mine.
  2. yours = your + noun: Is this bag yours?
  3. his stays the same: That car is his.
  4. hers = her + noun: The coat is hers. (no apostrophe)
  5. ours = our + noun: The house is ours.
  6. theirs = their + noun: The seats are theirs.
  7. After a possessive pronoun there is no noun: "It's mine", not "It's mine book".

Why say mine instead of my book?

When it is clear what we are talking about, we don't repeat the noun. mine / yours / hers… take the place of "my + noun", so the sentence is shorter.

"Is this your pen?" — "Yes, it's mine." (= my pen) · My bag is red. Hers is blue. (= her bag)

Student explanation

Ask yourself one question

Is there a noun (word for a person, thing or idea) after the word? Yes → use my / your / her (before the noun). No → use mine / yours / hers (standing alone).

Common mistakes

  • This is mine book. This is my book. / This book is mine.
  • The book is her's. The book is hers.
  • This is our's. This is ours.
  • It's your's. It's yours.
Remember: never add an apostrophe to a possessive pronoun (word used instead of a name).

In plain words

You already know my book. Mine does the same job, but it stands alone. The thing is already clear, so you do not need to say it again.

  • This is my book. → This book is mine. We do not repeat the word book.
  • Is this your pen or mine? Mine means my pen.
  • That car is theirs. Theirs means their car.

Think of it as a short way to finish the sentence. There is never a thing after mine, yours, hers, ours or theirs.

Задай себе один вопрос

Есть ли после слова существительное (это слово-предмет)? Да → используй my / your / her (перед существительным). Нет → используй mine / yours / hers (самостоятельно).

Частые ошибки

  • This is mine book. This is my book. / This book is mine.
  • The book is her's. The book is hers.
  • This is our's. This is ours.
  • It's your's. It's yours.
Запомни: к притяжательному местоимению (слово вместо имени) никогда не добавляется апостроф.

Простыми словами

Вы уже знаете my book. Mine делает то же самое, но стоит отдельно. Предмет и так понятен, поэтому его не повторяем.

  • This is my book. → This book is mine. Эта книга моя. Слово book не повторяем.
  • Is this your pen or mine? Это твоя ручка или моя? Mine значит my pen.
  • That car is theirs. Та машина их. Theirs значит their car.

Это короткий способ закончить предложение. После mine, yours, hers, ours и theirs предмет никогда не ставится.

O'zingizga bitta savol bering

So'zdan keyin ot (odam yoki narsa nomi) bormi? Ha → my / your / her (otdan oldin). Yo'q → mine / yours / hers (yakka holda).

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • This is mine book. This is my book. / This book is mine.
  • The book is her's. The book is hers.
  • This is our's. This is ours.
  • It's your's. It's yours.
Esda tuting: egalik olmoshiga (ism o'rnida keladigan so'z) hech qachon apostrof qo'shilmaydi.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Siz my book ni bilasiz. Mine ham shu ishni qiladi, lekin alohida keladi. Narsa allaqachon aniq, shuning uchun uni takrorlamaymiz.

  • This is my book. → This book is mine. Bu kitob meniki. Book so'zini takrorlamaymiz.
  • Is this your pen or mine? Bu sening ruchkangmi yoki menikimi? Mine my pen degani.
  • That car is theirs. U mashina ularniki. Theirs their car degani.

Bu gapni tugatishning qisqa usuli. Mine, yours, hers, ours va theirs dan keyin narsa kelmaydi.

Real-life use

You use mine, yours and ours to say who has something.

At school

"Is this pen yours or mine?"

At home

"That's not our car; it's theirs."

Choose things

"My bag is black and hers is red."

With friends

"This seat is mine, but the others are free."

IELTS Speaking: family

"My parents have a small shop, and the shop next to it is ours too."
