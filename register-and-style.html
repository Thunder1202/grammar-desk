Register and style

General info

Register is the level of formality in language. We can say the same thing in a formal, neutral or informal way. The right choice depends on who reads it, where it appears and why we write or speak.

Same message, three registers

Informal: Can you tell me what time it starts?

Neutral: Could you tell me what time it starts?

Formal: I would be grateful if you could inform me of the starting time.

What changes

  • Vocabulary: get → buy → purchase.
  • Grammar: contractions and short sentences (informal); passives, nouns and longer sentences (formal).
  • Politeness: Give me the file → Could you send me the file? → Would you be so kind as to send me the file?
  • Openings and endings: Hi / Hello / Dear Sir or Madam; Cheers / Best wishes / Yours faithfully.
Russian has ты and вы, and Uzbek has sen and siz, so formality is in the pronoun. English has only you, so formality lives in word choice, grammar and tone.

Регистр (register) — уровень формальности языка. Одно и то же можно сказать формально, нейтрально или неформально. Выбор зависит от того, кто читает, где это появляется и зачем мы пишем или говорим.

Одно сообщение, три регистра

Неформально: Can you tell me what time it starts?

Нейтрально: Could you tell me what time it starts?

Формально: I would be grateful if you could inform me of the starting time.

Что меняется

  • Лексика: get → buy → purchase.
  • Грамматика: сокращения и короткие предложения (неформально); пассив, существительные и длинные предложения (формально).
  • Вежливость: Give me the file → Could you send me the file? → Would you be so kind as to send me the file?
  • Начало и конец письма: Hi / Hello / Dear Sir or Madam; Cheers / Best wishes / Yours faithfully.
В русском есть «ты» и «вы», в узбекском — sen и siz, поэтому формальность видна в местоимении. В английском только you, поэтому формальность живёт в выборе слов, грамматике и тоне.

Register (uslub darajasi) — tilning rasmiylik darajasi. Bir xil fikrni rasmiy, neytral yoki norasmiy aytish mumkin. Tanlov matnni kim o'qishiga, u qayerda chiqishiga va biz nima uchun yozishimiz yoki gapirishimizga bog'liq.

Bitta xabar, uchta uslub

Norasmiy: Can you tell me what time it starts?

Neytral: Could you tell me what time it starts?

Rasmiy: I would be grateful if you could inform me of the starting time.

Nima o'zgaradi

  • Lug'at: get → buy → purchase.
  • Grammatika: qisqartmalar va qisqa gaplar (norasmiy); majhul nisbat, otlar va uzun gaplar (rasmiy).
  • Xushmuomalalik: Give me the file → Could you send me the file? → Would you be so kind as to send me the file?
  • Xat boshi va oxiri: Hi / Hello / Dear Sir or Madam; Cheers / Best wishes / Yours faithfully.
Rus tilida «ты» va «вы», o'zbek tilida sen va siz bor, shuning uchun rasmiylik olmoshda bilinadi. Ingliz tilida faqat you bor, shuning uchun rasmiylik so'z tanlash, grammatika va ohangda bo'ladi.

  1. Think who, where, why: who is the reader (a boss, a friend, a stranger)? Where does the text appear (an exam, a chat, a letter)? What is your aim (to ask, to complain, to persuade)?
  2. Neutral is the safe choice: when you are not sure, write clearly, with no slang and few contractions.
  3. Vocabulary: single, often Latin-based words are more formal (purchase, require, assist). Phrasal verbs, slang and everyday words are more informal.
  4. Grammar: formal English uses passives, nominalisation, whom, the subjunctive, inversion and longer sentences. Informal English uses contractions, short sentences, ellipsis and question tags.
  5. Politeness: more indirect means more formal and polite. Compare: Give me the file. / Can you send me the file? / Could you possibly send me the file? / I was wondering if you could send me the file.
  6. Do not mix: slang in an essay looks careless, and very formal language in a chat sounds cold or funny. Keep one register in one text.
  7. Tone markers: formal writing avoids contractions, exclamation marks, slang and vague words like stuff, kids, loads of. Use children, numerous, a great deal of.
  8. Letter endings: Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully. Dear Mr Jones → Yours sincerely. For IELTS Writing use a formal or neutral style, and for Speaking a neutral to informal style.

Make a text more formal in five steps

StepInformalFormal
1. remove contractionsWe can't come.We cannot attend.
2. replace phrasal verbs and slangWe need to sort out loads of stuff.We need to resolve numerous issues.
3. use less personal structuresWe think you made a mistake.It appears that an error has been made.
4. make requests indirectSend me the file.I would be grateful if you could send me the file.
5. use formal linkersBut it's too expensive.However, the cost is too high.

Hedging: sounding careful, not weak

Formal and academic English avoids strong claims. Softening words make your writing more polite and more accurate.

Too strongHedged
This is the best solution.This may be the most effective solution.
Social media causes depression.Social media appears to be linked to depression.
Everyone agrees.Most experts tend to agree.

Speaking vs writing

Even at the same level of formality, speech and writing differ. Speech uses short sentences, fillers (well, you know, actually), question tags and ellipsis ("Coming?"). Writing uses full sentences, clear paragraphs and linking words. For IELTS Speaking, natural and neutral is better than very formal.

  1. Подумай: кто, где, зачем: кто читатель (начальник, друг, незнакомец)? Где появится текст (экзамен, чат, письмо)? Какая цель (спросить, пожаловаться, убедить)?
  2. Нейтральный — безопасный выбор: если не уверен, пиши ясно, без сленга и с малым числом сокращений.
  3. Лексика: отдельные слова, часто латинского происхождения, формальнее (purchase, require, assist). Фразовые глаголы, сленг и повседневные слова неформальнее.
  4. Грамматика: формальный английский использует пассив, номинализацию, whom, сослагательное, инверсию и длинные предложения. Неформальный — сокращения, короткие предложения, пропуск слов и разделительные вопросы.
  5. Вежливость: чем косвеннее, тем формальнее и вежливее. Сравни: Give me the file. / Can you send me the file? / Could you possibly send me the file? / I was wondering if you could send me the file.
  6. Не смешивай: сленг в эссе выглядит небрежно, а очень формальный язык в чате звучит холодно или смешно. В одном тексте держи один регистр.
  7. Маркеры тона: формальный текст избегает сокращений, восклицательных знаков, сленга и расплывчатых слов вроде stuff, kids, loads of. Используй children, numerous, a great deal of.
  8. Концовки писем: Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully. Dear Mr Jones → Yours sincerely. Для IELTS Writing — формальный или нейтральный стиль, для Speaking — нейтральный или неформальный.

В официальном и научном английском избегают слишком сильных утверждений. Смягчающие слова делают текст вежливее и точнее.

Устная и письменная речь

Даже при одинаковой степени официальности речь и письмо различаются. В речи — короткие предложения, слова-паразиты (well, you know, actually), разделительные вопросы и эллипсис («Coming?»). В письме — полные предложения, чёткие абзацы и связки. Для IELTS Speaking естественный нейтральный стиль лучше очень официального.

  1. O'ylang: kim, qayerda, nima uchun: o'quvchi kim (boshliq, do'st, begona)? Matn qayerda chiqadi (imtihon, chat, xat)? Maqsad nima (so'rash, shikoyat qilish, ishontirish)?
  2. Neytral uslub xavfsiz tanlov: ishonchingiz komil bo'lmasa, aniq yozing, jargonsiz va kam qisqartmalar bilan.
  3. Lug'at: bitta so'zdan iborat, ko'pincha lotin ildizli so'zlar rasmiyroq (purchase, require, assist). Phrasal verb, jargon va kundalik so'zlar norasmiyroq.
  4. Grammatika: rasmiy inglizchada majhul nisbat, nominalizatsiya, whom, subjunctive, inversiya va uzun gaplar ishlatiladi. Norasmiy inglizchada qisqartmalar, qisqa gaplar, so'z tushirish va question tag ishlatiladi.
  5. Xushmuomalalik: qanchalik bilvosita bo'lsa, shunchalik rasmiy va xushmuomala. Solishtiring: Give me the file. / Can you send me the file? / Could you possibly send me the file? / I was wondering if you could send me the file.
  6. Aralashtirmang: esseda jargon beparvo ko'rinadi, chatda juda rasmiy til esa sovuq yoki kulgili eshitiladi. Bitta matnda bitta uslubni saqlang.
  7. Ohang belgilari: rasmiy yozuv qisqartmalar, undov belgilari, jargon va stuff, kids, loads of kabi noaniq so'zlardan qochadi. children, numerous, a great deal of dan foydalaning.
  8. Xat oxiri: Dear Sir or Madam → Yours faithfully. Dear Mr Jones → Yours sincerely. IELTS Writing uchun rasmiy yoki neytral, Speaking uchun neytral yoki norasmiy uslub.

Og'zaki va yozma nutq

Rasmiylik darajasi bir xil bo'lsa ham, og'zaki va yozma nutq farq qiladi. Og'zakida — qisqa gaplar, to'ldiruvchi so'zlar (well, you know, actually), dumcha savollar va ellipsis ("Coming?"). Yozmada — to'liq gaplar, aniq xatboshilar va bog'lovchilar. IELTS Speaking uchun juda rasmiy uslubdan ko'ra tabiiy neytral uslub yaxshiroq.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Who will read this? A boss or a stranger → formal or neutral. A friend → informal.
  2. Where will it appear? An exam, a report, a job application → formal. A chat → informal.
  3. What do I want? To ask politely, to persuade, to complain → choose a polite, indirect form.

Make it formal in 3 steps

Hi, can you tell me what time the meeting starts? 1) Change the greeting: Dear Ms Brown. 2) Replace informal words and make the request indirect: Could you please inform me ... 3) Remove contractions and slang. → Dear Ms Brown, Could you please inform me what time the meeting starts? Yours sincerely,

Why it works

Readers judge us by our style. The right register shows respect, makes our meaning clear and helps us reach our goal.

Common mistakes

  • Dear Sir, I wanna apply for the job. Dear Sir, I would like to apply for the position.
  • Kids are gonna have loads of problems. Children will face numerous problems.
  • Dear Mr Jones, ... Yours faithfully, Dear Mr Jones, ... Yours sincerely,
  • Dear Sir, Thanks a lot!!! Dear Sir, Thank you very much.
If you know the name, use Yours sincerely. If you do not know the name (Dear Sir or Madam), use Yours faithfully.

In plain words

Words have clothes. You wear different clothes to a wedding and to the gym. In the same way, you use different words with your boss and with your friend. The meaning can be the same, but the feeling changes.

  • Hi! Got your email. → I have received your email. Friend, then boss.
  • We got a refund. → We obtained a refund. Everyday word, then formal word.
  • Sorry, it's gonna be late. → I regret to inform you of the delay. Spoken, then business letter.

Before you write, ask: who will read this? Then choose your clothes.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Кто это прочитает? Начальник или незнакомец → формально или нейтрально. Друг → неформально.
  2. Где это появится? Экзамен, отчёт, заявка на работу → формально. Чат → неформально.
  3. Чего я хочу? Вежливо попросить, убедить, пожаловаться → выбери вежливую косвенную форму.

Делаем формально за 3 шага

Hi, can you tell me what time the meeting starts? 1) Поменяй приветствие: Dear Ms Brown. 2) Замени неформальные слова и сделай просьбу косвенной: Could you please inform me ... 3) Убери сокращения и сленг. → Dear Ms Brown, Could you please inform me what time the meeting starts? Yours sincerely,

Почему это работает

Читатели судят нас по стилю. Правильный регистр показывает уважение, делает мысль ясной и помогает достичь цели.

Частые ошибки

  • Dear Sir, I wanna apply for the job. Dear Sir, I would like to apply for the position.
  • Kids are gonna have loads of problems. Children will face numerous problems.
  • Dear Mr Jones, ... Yours faithfully, Dear Mr Jones, ... Yours sincerely,
  • Dear Sir, Thanks a lot!!! Dear Sir, Thank you very much.
Если знаешь имя — Yours sincerely. Если не знаешь имени (Dear Sir or Madam) — Yours faithfully.

Простыми словами

У слов есть одежда. На свадьбу и в спортзал одеваются по-разному. Так и со словами: с начальником и другом они разные. Смысл может быть тот же, но ощущение меняется.

  • Hi! Got your email. → I have received your email. Для друга, потом для начальника.
  • We got a refund. → We obtained a refund. Обычное слово, потом официальное.
  • Sorry, it's gonna be late. → I regret to inform you of the delay. Разговорно, потом деловое письмо.

Прежде чем писать, спросите: кто это прочтёт? Затем выбирайте одежду.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

So'zlarning kiyimi bor. To'yga va sport zaliga boshqa-boshqa kiyinasiz. Xuddi shunday, boshliq va do'st bilan boshqa so'zlar ishlatasiz. Ma'no bir xil bo'lishi mumkin, lekin taassurot o'zgaradi.

  • Hi! Got your email. → I have received your email. Do'st uchun, keyin boshliq uchun.
  • We got a refund. → We obtained a refund. Oddiy so'z, keyin rasmiy so'z.
  • Sorry, it's gonna be late. → I regret to inform you of the delay. Og'zaki, keyin rasmiy xat.

Yozishdan oldin so'rang: buni kim o'qiydi? Keyin kiyimni tanlang.

Real-life use

Choosing the right register helps in applications, exams, emails and everyday talk.

Job applications

"Dear Hiring Manager, I am writing to apply for the position of marketing assistant. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss my application."

Emails to a teacher or boss

"Dear Ms Brown, I am sorry to trouble you. Could you please let me know the deadline? Kind regards,"

Texting a friend

"Hey, you free later? Let's grab a coffee!"

Complaints

"I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the service I received. I would appreciate a prompt reply."

IELTS Writing and Speaking

Writing: "Children will face numerous problems." (formal or neutral, no slang). Speaking: "I'd say it's a pretty good idea." (neutral to informal, natural).

English only

Examples and drill

Informal, neutral and formal words

get / buy / purchase. help / help / assist. need / need / require. tell / tell / inform. put off / delay / postpone.

Three levels of a request

Informal: Can you help me? Neutral: Could you help me, please? Formal: Would you be able to assist me?

Three levels of a refusal

Informal: No way, I can't make it. Neutral: Sorry, I can't come. Formal: Unfortunately, I am unable to attend.

Openings and endings

Informal: Hi Sam / Cheers. Neutral: Hello Sam / Best wishes. Formal: Dear Mr Jones / Yours sincerely. Dear Sir or Madam / Yours faithfully.
