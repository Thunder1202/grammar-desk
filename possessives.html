Possessives

General info

Possessives show that something belongs to a person, or that two people or things are connected. At A1 you need two kinds: possessive adjectives (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) and the possessive 's (Aziz's book).

We use them for

  • Ownership: This is my phone.
  • Family and relationships: Her brother is a doctor.
  • Parts of the body and personal things: He washes his hands.
  • Answering Whose?: Whose bag is this? It's Dilnoza's.
Uzbek adds a suffix (kitobim = my book). English puts a separate word before the noun, and the word depends on the owner, not on the thing.

Притяжательные формы показывают, что вещь принадлежит человеку или что люди и предметы связаны между собой. На уровне A1 нужны два вида: притяжательные прилагательные (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) и притяжательный 's (Aziz's book).

Когда используем

  • Принадлежность: This is my phone.
  • Семья и отношения: Her brother is a doctor.
  • Части тела и личные вещи: He washes his hands.
  • Ответ на вопрос Whose?: Whose bag is this? It's Dilnoza's.
В русском есть мой, твой, наш. В английском слово стоит перед существительным и зависит от владельца, а не от предмета: his sister, her brother.

Egalik shakllari narsa kimgadir tegishli ekanini yoki odamlar va narsalar o'zaro bog'liqligini ko'rsatadi. A1 darajada ikki turi kerak: egalik sifatlari (my, your, his, her, its, our, their) va egalik 's (Aziz's book).

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Egalik: This is my phone.
  • Oila va qarindoshlik: Her brother is a doctor.
  • Tana a'zolari va shaxsiy buyumlar: He washes his hands.
  • Whose? savoliga javob: Whose bag is this? It's Dilnoza's.
O'zbek tilida qo'shimcha bor (kitobim). Inglizchada esa ot oldiga alohida so'z qo'yiladi va u narsaga emas, egasiga bog'liq: his sister, her brother.

  1. Position: the possessive adjective goes before a noun and never with an article: my book (not the my book).
  2. No change: it does not change with the noun: my book, my books.
  3. Owner decides: Tom → his mother, Anna → her mother.
  4. Possessive 's: singular noun + 's (the teacher's pen, Aziz's bag). Plural with -s: only an apostrophe (the students' books). Irregular plural: children's toys.
  5. Do not mix up: its (belongs to it) and it's (it is).
  1. Место: притяжательное прилагательное стоит перед существительным и без артикля: my book (не the my book).
  2. Не изменяется: не зависит от существительного: my book, my books.
  3. Решает владелец: Tom → his mother, Anna → her mother.
  4. Притяжательный 's: существительное в ед. числе + 's (the teacher's pen, Aziz's bag). Во мн. числе на -s только апостроф (the students' books). Неправильное мн. число: children's toys.
  5. Не путай: its (принадлежит ему) и it's (it is).
  1. O'rni: egalik sifati otdan oldin keladi va artikl bilan ishlatilmaydi: my book ("the my book" emas).
  2. O'zgarmaydi: otga qarab o'zgarmaydi: my book, my books.
  3. Egasi hal qiladi: Tom → his mother, Anna → her mother.
  4. Egalik 's: birlikdagi ot + 's (the teacher's pen, Aziz's bag). -s bilan tugaydigan ko'plikda faqat apostrof (the students' books). Qoidasiz ko'plik: children's toys.
  5. Adashtirmang: its (unga tegishli) va it's (it is).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Who owns it? The owner decides the word, not the thing.
  2. Is the owner a pronoun or a name? Pronoun (I, he, they) → my, his, their. Name or noun → name + 's.
  3. its or it's? Can you say "it is"? Yes → it's. No → its.

Common mistakes

  • The my book is new. My book is new.
  • This is Aziz book. This is Aziz's book.
  • It's name is Max. Its name is Max.
  • Sara loves his job. Sara loves her job.
Memory trick: the word changes with the owner (he → his), never with the thing owned.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Кто владелец? Слово выбирает владелец, а не предмет.
  2. Владелец — местоимение или имя? Местоимение (I, he, they) → my, his, their. Имя или существительное → имя + 's.
  3. its или it's? Можно сказать "it is"? Да → it's. Нет → its.

Частые ошибки

  • The my book is new. My book is new.
  • This is Aziz book. This is Aziz's book.
  • It's name is Max. Its name is Max.
  • Sara loves his job. Sara loves her job.
Запоминалка: слово меняется по владельцу (he → his), а не по предмету.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Egasi kim? So'zni narsa emas, egasi tanlaydi.
  2. Ega olmoshmi yoki ismmi? Olmosh (I, he, they) → my, his, their. Ism yoki ot → ism + 's.
  3. its yoki it's? "it is" deb ayta olasizmi? Ha → it's. Yo'q → its.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • The my book is new. My book is new.
  • This is Aziz book. This is Aziz's book.
  • It's name is Max. Its name is Max.
  • Sara loves his job. Sara loves her job.
Eslab qolish: so'z egasiga qarab o'zgaradi (he → his), narsaga qarab emas.

Real-life use

Possessives appear whenever you talk about people and things around you.

Introducing family

"This is my sister. Her name is Madina."

Checking who owns something

"Is this your pen?" "No, it's Aziz's."

Lost property

"Excuse me, is this your bag? I found it near our classroom."

Talking about home and work

"My flat is near the metro. Our manager is very kind."

Apps and websites

"Enter your phone number." "Your order is ready."

Ownership

Animals and things
