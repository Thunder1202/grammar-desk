Reflexive and reciprocal pronouns

General info

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and the object are the same person: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

Reflexive

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + reflexive = alone, without help: I did it by myself.

Reciprocal

  • each other / one another = A does it to B and B does it to A: They love each other.
English uses fewer reflexive verbs than Russian or Uzbek. We say "I feel tired", not "I feel myself tired", and "He got dressed", not "He dressed himself".

Возвратные местоимения используются, когда подлежащее и дополнение — одно и то же лицо: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

Возвратные

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + возвратное = один, без помощи: I did it by myself.

Взаимные

  • each other / one another = A делает B, а B делает A: They love each other.
В английском меньше возвратных глаголов, чем в русском или узбекском. Говорим «I feel tired», а не «I feel myself tired», и «He got dressed», а не «He dressed himself».

O'zlik olmoshlari ega va to'ldiruvchi bir shaxs bo'lganda ishlatiladi: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

O'zlik

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + o'zlik olmoshi = yolg'iz, yordamsiz: I did it by myself.

O'zaro

  • each other / one another = A B ga, B A ga qiladi: They love each other.
Inglizchada rus yoki o'zbek tilidagidan kamroq o'zlik fe'llari ishlatiladi. "I feel tired" deymiz, "I feel myself tired" emas, va "He got dressed", "He dressed himself" emas.

Teacher's rule

SubjectReflexive
Imyself
you (one)yourself
he / she / ithimself / herself / itself
weourselves
you (many)yourselves
theythemselves
  1. Use a reflexive when the subject and object are the same: She taught herself English.
  2. by + reflexive = alone or without help: He lives by himself.
  3. each other (usually two) and one another (often more) are reciprocal: They help each other.
  4. Common verbs with a reflexive: hurt, enjoy, teach, cut, introduce, look after. "Enjoy yourself!"
  5. Do not add a reflexive where the action is already clear: "I got dressed", not "I dressed myself".
  6. Do not use a reflexive after feel, relax, concentrate, meet in the everyday sense: "I feel good", not "I feel myself good".

Emphasis: I did it myself

A reflexive can also add emphasis: "nobody else" or "that person, not someone less important".

Did you make this cake yourself? — Yes, I made it myself! · The director himself answered my email.

Why not "I washed myself"?

Many Russian and Uzbek verbs show the action is done to yourself (умываться, одеваться, yuvinmoq, kiyinmoq). In English, many such verbs need no reflexive, because the meaning is clear.

Usually no reflexiveExample
wash, shave, dress, get dressedI got dressed and washed.
feel, relax, concentrate, worryI feel tired. Relax! I can't concentrate.
meet, kiss, hug (each other is optional)We met in 2019.
  1. Используй возвратное, когда подлежащее и дополнение совпадают: She taught herself English.
  2. by + возвратное = один или без помощи: He lives by himself.
  3. each other (обычно двое) и one another (часто больше) — взаимные: They help each other.
  4. Частые глаголы с возвратным: hurt, enjoy, teach, cut, introduce, look after. «Enjoy yourself!»
  5. Не добавляй возвратное, где действие и так ясно: «I got dressed», а не «I dressed myself».
  6. Не используй возвратное после feel, relax, concentrate, meet в бытовом смысле: «I feel good», а не «I feel myself good».

Усиление: I did it myself

Возвратное местоимение может усиливать: «сам, а не кто-то другой» или «сам лично».

Почему не «I washed myself»?

Многие русские глаголы с -ся показывают, что действие направлено на себя (умываться, одеваться). В английском многим таким глаголам возвратное местоимение не нужно — смысл и так понятен.

  2. by + o'zlik olmoshi = yolg'iz yoki yordamsiz: He lives by himself.
  3. each other (odatda ikki kishi) va one another (ko'pincha ko'proq) o'zaro: They help each other.
  4. O'zlik olmoshi bilan ko'p ishlatiladigan fe'llar: hurt, enjoy, teach, cut, introduce, look after. "Enjoy yourself!"
  5. Harakat allaqachon aniq bo'lsa, o'zlik olmoshini qo'shmang: "I got dressed", "I dressed myself" emas.
  6. Kundalik ma'noda feel, relax, concentrate, meet dan keyin o'zlik olmoshini ishlatmang: "I feel good", "I feel myself good" emas.

Student explanation

Ask yourself one question

Is the object (who or what gets the action) the same person as the subject (who or what does the action)? Yes → reflexive (myself...). If two people do it to each other → each other.

Common mistakes

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (meaning each other) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
Don't translate "себя"/"o'zini" word for word. Use a reflexive only when English needs it.

In plain words

Myself is like looking in a mirror: the action comes back to the same person. Each other is like two people facing each other: each does it to the other.

  • She cut herself. She did it, and she got hurt.
  • I made this cake myself. No one helped me.
  • Ali and Olim help each other. Ali helps Olim, and Olim helps Ali.

Mirror: myself, yourself, himself, herself. Two people: each other.

Задай себе один вопрос

Дополнение (на кого или что направлено действие) — то же лицо, что и подлежащее (кто делает действие)? Да → возвратное (myself...). Если двое делают это друг другу → each other.

Частые ошибки

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (имея в виду друг друга) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
Не переводи «себя» слово в слово. Используй возвратное только тогда, когда это нужно английскому.

Простыми словами

Myself — как взгляд в зеркало: действие возвращается к тому же человеку. Each other — как двое лицом к лицу: каждый делает это другому.

  • She cut herself. Она порезалась. Сделала сама и пострадала сама.
  • I made this cake myself. Я сам испёк этот торт. Никто не помогал.
  • Ali and Olim help each other. Али и Олим помогают друг другу. Али помогает Олиму, Олим помогает Али.

Зеркало: myself, yourself, himself, herself. Двое: each other.

O'zingizga bitta savol bering

To'ldiruvchi ega bilan bir xil shaxsmi? Ha → o'zlik olmoshi (myself...). Ikki kishi buni bir-biriga qilsa → each other.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (bir-biriga ma'nosida) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
"o'zini" ni so'zma-so'z tarjima qilmang. O'zlik olmoshini (ism o'rnida keladigan so'z) faqat ingliz tili talab qilganda ishlating.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Myself ko'zguga qaragandek: ish o'sha odamning o'ziga qaytadi. Each other yuzma-yuz turgan ikki kishidek: har biri ikkinchisiga qiladi.

  • She cut herself. U o'zini kesib oldi. O'zi qildi, o'zi jarohat oldi.
  • I made this cake myself. Bu tortni o'zim pishirdim. Hech kim yordam bermadi.
  • Ali and Olim help each other. Ali va Olim bir-biriga yordam beradi. Ali Olimga, Olim Aliga.

Ko'zgu: myself, yourself, himself, herself. Ikki kishi: each other.

Real-life use

You use reflexives for actions you do to yourself, and reciprocals for actions between people.

Talking about yourself

"I taught myself to cook."

At work

"Please introduce yourself to the team."

Encouraging someone

"Relax and enjoy yourself!"

Relationships

"My parents respect each other."

IELTS Speaking: a mistake you made

"I cut myself while I was cooking, so I learned to be more careful."
