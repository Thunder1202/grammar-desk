Reflexive and reciprocal pronouns

General info

Reflexive pronouns are used when the subject and the object are the same person: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

Reflexive

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + reflexive = alone, without help: I did it by myself.

Reciprocal

  • each other / one another = A does it to B and B does it to A: They love each other.
English uses fewer reflexive verbs than Russian or Uzbek. We say "I feel tired", not "I feel myself tired", and "He got dressed", not "He dressed himself".

Возвратные местоимения используются, когда подлежащее и дополнение — одно и то же лицо: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

Возвратные

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + возвратное = один, без помощи: I did it by myself.

Взаимные

  • each other / one another = A делает B, а B делает A: They love each other.
В английском меньше возвратных глаголов, чем в русском или узбекском. Говорим «I feel tired», а не «I feel myself tired», и «He got dressed», а не «He dressed himself».

O'zlik olmoshlari ega va to'ldiruvchi bir shaxs bo'lganda ishlatiladi: myself, yourself, himself, herself, itself, ourselves, yourselves, themselves.

O'zlik

  • I hurt myself. She looked at herself in the mirror.
  • By + o'zlik olmoshi = yolg'iz, yordamsiz: I did it by myself.

O'zaro

  • each other / one another = A B ga, B A ga qiladi: They love each other.
Inglizchada rus yoki o'zbek tilidagidan kamroq o'zlik fe'llari ishlatiladi. "I feel tired" deymiz, "I feel myself tired" emas, va "He got dressed", "He dressed himself" emas.

  1. Use a reflexive when the subject and object are the same: She taught herself English.
  2. by + reflexive = alone or without help: He lives by himself.
  3. each other (usually two) and one another (often more) are reciprocal: They help each other.
  4. Common verbs with a reflexive: hurt, enjoy, teach, cut, introduce, look after. "Enjoy yourself!"
  5. Do not add a reflexive where the action is already clear: "I got dressed", not "I dressed myself".
  6. Do not use a reflexive after feel, relax, concentrate, meet in the everyday sense: "I feel good", not "I feel myself good".

Student explanation

Ask yourself one question

Is the object the same person as the subject? Yes → reflexive (myself...). If two people do it to each other → each other.

Common mistakes

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (meaning each other) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
Don't translate "себя"/"o'zini" word for word. Use a reflexive only when English needs it.

Задай себе один вопрос

Дополнение — то же лицо, что и подлежащее? Да → возвратное (myself...). Если двое делают это друг другу → each other.

Частые ошибки

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (имея в виду друг друга) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
Не переводи «себя» слово в слово. Используй возвратное только тогда, когда это нужно английскому.

O'zingizga bitta savol bering

To'ldiruvchi ega bilan bir xil shaxsmi? Ha → o'zlik olmoshi (myself...). Ikki kishi buni bir-biriga qilsa → each other.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I hurt me. I hurt myself.
  • He cut him with a knife. He cut himself with a knife.
  • They write to themselves every week. (bir-biriga ma'nosida) They write to each other every week.
  • I always enjoy. I always enjoy myself.
"o'zini" ni so'zma-so'z tarjima qilmang. O'zlik olmoshini faqat ingliz tili talab qilganda ishlating.

Real-life use

You use reflexives for actions you do to yourself, and reciprocals for actions between people.

Talking about yourself

"I taught myself to cook."

At work

"Please introduce yourself to the team."

Encouraging someone

"Relax and enjoy yourself!"

Relationships

"My parents respect each other."

IELTS Speaking: a mistake you made

"I cut myself while I was cooking, so I learned to be more careful."

Examples and drill

myself / yourself

I made this cake myself. Did you paint it yourself?

himself / herself

He shaved himself. She introduced herself.

ourselves / themselves

We enjoyed ourselves. They taught themselves English.

each other

They love each other. We help each other.

by + reflexive

She lives by herself. I did it by myself.
