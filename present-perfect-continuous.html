Present Perfect Continuous

General info

Present Perfect Continuous shows an activity that started in the past and is still going on, or has just stopped and we can see the result. It focuses on the activity and how long it lasted.

We use it for

  • An activity continuing until now: I have been studying for two hours.
  • A recent activity with a visible result: You're wet. Have you been running?
  • How long questions: How long have you been waiting?

Words that go with it

for, since, all day, recently, lately, how long.

Russian uses the present tense (я учу английский уже шесть лет). Uzbek uses -yapman (olti yildan beri o'rganyapman). English must use have / has been + -ing.

Present Perfect Continuous показывает действие, которое началось в прошлом и всё ещё идёт, или только что закончилось и виден результат. Акцент на самом процессе и его длительности.

Когда используем

  • Действие длится до сих пор: I have been studying for two hours.
  • Недавнее действие с видимым результатом: You're wet. Have you been running?
  • Вопросы how long: How long have you been waiting?

Слова-подсказки

for, since, all day, recently, lately, how long.

В русском используется настоящее время (я учу английский уже шесть лет). В узбекском -yapman (olti yildan beri o'rganyapman). В английском обязательно have / has been + -ing.

Present Perfect Continuous o'tmishda boshlanib hali davom etayotgan yoki hozirgina tugab natijasi ko'rinib turgan ishni bildiradi. Asosiy e'tibor jarayon va uning davomiyligiga qaratiladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Hozirgacha davom etgan ish: I have been studying for two hours.
  • Natijasi ko'rinib turgan yaqindagi ish: You're wet. Have you been running?
  • How long savollari: How long have you been waiting?

Yordamchi so'zlar

for, since, all day, recently, lately, how long.

Rus tilida hozirgi zamon (я учу английский уже шесть лет). O'zbek tilida -yapman (olti yildan beri o'rganyapman). Inglizchada have / has been + -ing majburiy.

Teacher's rule

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
+I have been working.She has been working.
−I haven't been working.She hasn't been working.
?Have you been working?Has she been working?
  1. Form: have / has + been + verb-ing. Short forms: I've been, she's been.
  2. Unfinished activity with for / since: I have been learning English for six years. How long have you been waiting?
  3. Recent activity with a present result: You're dirty. Have you been playing in the garden?
  4. Negative: haven't / hasn't been + -ing. Question: Have / Has + subject + been + -ing. Short answers: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  5. Continuous or simple? Continuous = the activity, maybe unfinished: I've been painting the room. Simple = the result or the number: I've painted the room. I've read 50 pages.
  6. State verbs (know, like, want, love, believe) do not take the continuous: I have known her for years.
  1. Форма: have / has + been + глагол-ing. Краткие формы: I've been, she's been.
  2. Незаконченное действие с for / since: I have been learning English for six years. How long have you been waiting?
  3. Недавнее действие с результатом сейчас: You're dirty. Have you been playing in the garden?
  4. Отрицание: haven't / hasn't been + -ing. Вопрос: Have / Has + подлежащее + been + -ing. Краткие ответы: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  5. Continuous или simple? Continuous = сам процесс, возможно не закончен: I've been painting the room. Simple = результат или число: I've painted the room. I've read 50 pages.
  6. Глаголы состояния (know, like, want, love, believe) не употребляются в continuous: I have known her for years.
  1. Shakl: have / has + been + fe'l-ing. Qisqa shakllar: I've been, she's been.
  2. Tugamagan ish for / since bilan: I have been learning English for six years. How long have you been waiting?
  3. Hozirgi natijali yaqindagi ish: You're dirty. Have you been playing in the garden?
  4. Inkor: haven't / hasn't been + -ing. So'roq: Have / Has + ega + been + -ing. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  5. Continuous yoki simple? Continuous = jarayonning o'zi, ehtimol tugamagan: I've been painting the room. Simple = natija yoki son: I've painted the room. I've read 50 pages.
  6. Holat fe'llari (know, like, want, love, believe) continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi: I have known her for years.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the focus on the activity or how long? Yes → Present Perfect Continuous.
  2. Is the focus on the result or the number? Yes → Present Perfect simple: I have written three emails.
  3. Is the verb a state verb? Yes → Present Perfect simple only: I have known him for years.

Common mistakes

  • I have been wait for an hour. I have been waiting for an hour.
  • I have been knowing him for years. I have known him for years.
  • How long do you learn English? How long have you been learning English?
  • He has be sleeping. He has been sleeping.
Check the three parts: have / has + been + -ing. Do not drop been or -ing.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Акцент на процессе или на длительности? Да → Present Perfect Continuous.
  2. Акцент на результате или числе? Да → Present Perfect simple: I have written three emails.
  3. Это глагол состояния? Да → только Present Perfect simple: I have known him for years.

Частые ошибки

  • I have been wait for an hour. I have been waiting for an hour.
  • I have been knowing him for years. I have known him for years.
  • How long do you learn English? How long have you been learning English?
  • He has be sleeping. He has been sleeping.
Проверь три части: have / has + been + -ing. Не пропускай been и -ing.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. E'tibor jarayonga yoki davomiylikkami? Ha → Present Perfect Continuous.
  2. E'tibor natija yoki songami? Ha → Present Perfect simple: I have written three emails.
  3. Bu holat fe'lidimi? Ha → faqat Present Perfect simple: I have known him for years.

Real-life use

We use it to explain why we are tired, late or busy, and to talk about how long we have been doing something.

Explaining

"Sorry I'm late. I've been waiting for the bus for forty minutes."

Job interview

"I've been working as a tutor for two years, and I've been preparing students for exams."

Small talk

"How long have you been living here?"

IELTS Speaking: talking about a long activity

"I've been learning English for six years, and I've been practising speaking every day this year."
