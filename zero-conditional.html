Zero conditional

General info

The zero conditional talks about things that are always true — facts, rules and habits. We use if + present simple, present simple.

  • If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
  • If I drink coffee late, I can't sleep.
  • Plants die if they don't get water.

We use it for

  • Facts and science: If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
  • Rules and instructions: If the red light is on, don't enter.
  • Personal habits: If I get home late, I eat little.
Both parts are in the present simple. You can often use when instead of if with the same meaning: When you heat water, it boils.

Нулевой условный говорит о том, что всегда верно — о фактах, правилах и привычках. Используем if + present simple, present simple.

  • If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
  • If I drink coffee late, I can't sleep.
  • Plants die if they don't get water.

Когда используем

  • Факты и наука: If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
  • Правила и инструкции: If the red light is on, don't enter.
  • Личные привычки: If I get home late, I eat little.
Обе части в present simple. Часто можно использовать when вместо if с тем же значением: When you heat water, it boils.

Nolinchi shart mayli har doim to'g'ri bo'lgan narsalar — faktlar, qoidalar va odatlar haqida gapiradi. if + present simple, present simple ishlatamiz.

  • If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.
  • If I drink coffee late, I can't sleep.
  • Plants die if they don't get water.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Faktlar va fan: If you mix red and blue, you get purple.
  • Qoidalar va ko'rsatmalar: If the red light is on, don't enter.
  • Shaxsiy odatlar: If I get home late, I eat little.
Ikkala qism ham present simple da. Ko'pincha if o'rniga when ishlatsa bo'ladi, ma'no bir xil: When you heat water, it boils.

Teacher's rule

If-clauseMain clause
If + present simplepresent simple
If you heat ice,it melts.
If it rains,the streets get wet.
If I don't sleep,I feel tired.
  1. Form: If + present simple, present simple.
  2. You can change the order: It boils if you heat it. (no comma when if is in the middle)
  3. Meaning: a general truth — the result is always the same.
  4. if and when are often both possible here.
  5. Do not use will in the zero conditional: If you heat water, it boils (not "will boil").
  6. Compare: zero conditional = always true; first conditional = one real future possibility.
  1. Форма: If + present simple, present simple.
  2. Порядок можно менять: It boils if you heat it. (без запятой, когда if в середине)
  3. Значение: общая истина — результат всегда одинаковый.
  4. if и when здесь часто взаимозаменяемы.
  5. Не используем will в нулевом условном: If you heat water, it boils (не «will boil»).
  6. Сравни: нулевой условный = всегда верно; первый условный = одна реальная будущая возможность.
  1. Shakl: If + present simple, present simple.
  2. Tartibni o'zgartirsa bo'ladi: It boils if you heat it. (if o'rtada bo'lsa, vergul yo'q)
  3. Ma'no: umumiy haqiqat — natija har doim bir xil.
  4. Bu yerda if va when ko'pincha ikkalasi ham bo'ladi.
  5. Nolinchi shart maylida will ishlatilmaydi: If you heat water, it boils ("will boil" emas).

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. Is it always true, every time? Yes → zero conditional.
  2. Both parts present simple? They should be.

Common mistakes

  • If you heat water, it will boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If you will heat water, it boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If I am tired, I went to bed. If I am tired, I go to bed.
  • When water freezes, it became ice. When water freezes, it becomes ice.
If the meaning is "every time, always", keep both verbs in the present.

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Это верно всегда, каждый раз? Да → нулевой условный.
  2. Обе части в present simple? Так и должно быть.

Частые ошибки

  • If you heat water, it will boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If you will heat water, it boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If I am tired, I went to bed. If I am tired, I go to bed.
  • When water freezes, it became ice. When water freezes, it becomes ice.
Если смысл «каждый раз, всегда», держи оба глагола в настоящем времени.

O'zingizga ikkita savol bering

  1. Bu har doim, har safar to'g'rimi? Ha → nolinchi shart mayli.
  2. Ikkala qism present simple da mi? Shunday bo'lishi kerak.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • If you heat water, it will boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If you will heat water, it boils. If you heat water, it boils.
  • If I am tired, I went to bed. If I am tired, I go to bed.
  • When water freezes, it became ice. When water freezes, it becomes ice.
Agar ma'no «har safar, har doim» bo'lsa, ikkala fe'lni ham hozirgi zamonda saqlang.

Real-life use

You use the zero conditional for facts, instructions and your own habits.

Science class

"If you cool water below zero, it freezes."

Instructions

"If the alarm rings, leave the building quickly."

Habits

"If I don't eat breakfast, I feel hungry all morning."

Health advice

"If you exercise every day, you feel better."

IELTS Speaking: daily routine

"If I have free time, I usually read or go for a walk."

English only

Examples and drill

Science

If you heat ice, it melts. Water boils if you heat it to 100°C.

Rules

If the light is red, don't cross. If you press this button, the door opens.

Habits

If I drink coffee late, I can't sleep. I feel tired if I don't sleep well.

General truths

Plants die if they don't get water. If you mix red and blue, you get purple.

Drill

1. If you ___ (heat) ice, it melts.
heat
2. Water ___ (freeze) if the temperature is below 0.
freezes
3. If I ___ (not / sleep) enough, I feel tired.
don't sleep
4. When it ___ (rain), the streets get wet.
rains
5. If you press this button, the machine ___ (start).
starts
6. If children ___ (not / eat), they get hungry.
don't eat
7. Correct it: If you heat water, it will boils.
If you heat water, it boils.
8. Correct it: When I am hungry, I ate.
When I am hungry, I eat.
