Prepositions of place and time

General info

Prepositions are small words that link a noun to the rest of the sentence. They show place (where), time (when) and direction. The trickiest trio is in, on, at, because each one is used for both place and time.

We use them for

  • Place: The book is on the table. She is in the kitchen.
  • Time: I get up at 7 o'clock. We meet on Monday. It is cold in January.
  • Relationships between things: next to, between, behind, in front of, under, near.
Uzbek adds a suffix (stolda, soat 5 da) and Russian changes the noun ending. English puts a separate small word before the noun.

Предлоги — маленькие слова, которые связывают существительное с остальной частью предложения. Они показывают место (где), время (когда) и направление. Самая сложная тройка — in, on, at, потому что каждый используется и для места, и для времени.

Когда используем

  • Место: The book is on the table. She is in the kitchen.
  • Время: I get up at 7 o'clock. We meet on Monday. It is cold in January.
  • Отношения между предметами: next to, between, behind, in front of, under, near.
В русском меняется окончание существительного. В английском перед существительным ставится отдельное маленькое слово.

Predloglar — otni gapning qolgan qismiga bog'laydigan kichik so'zlar. Ular joy (qayerda), vaqt (qachon) va yo'nalishni ko'rsatadi. Eng qiyin uchlik — in, on, at, chunki ularning har biri ham joy, ham vaqt uchun ishlatiladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Joy: The book is on the table. She is in the kitchen.
  • Vaqt: I get up at 7 o'clock. We meet on Monday. It is cold in January.
  • Narsalar orasidagi munosabat: next to, between, behind, in front of, under, near.
O'zbek tilida qo'shimcha qo'shiladi (stolda, soat 5 da). Inglizchada esa ot oldiga alohida kichik so'z qo'yiladi.

  1. Time: at = exact time, on = days and dates, in = longer periods (months, years, seasons, parts of the day).
  2. Place: at = a point or address, on = a surface, in = inside something (a room, a city, a country).
  3. Other place words: under, next to, between, behind, in front of, near, opposite.
  4. Exceptions: in the morning / afternoon / evening but at night. on Friday morning. No preposition with this, next, last, every: next Monday, every day.
  5. Direction: go to school, go to the bank, but go home (no preposition).
  1. Время: at = точное время, on = дни и даты, in = более долгие периоды (месяцы, годы, времена года, части суток).
  2. Место: at = точка или адрес, on = поверхность, in = внутри чего-то (комната, город, страна).
  3. Другие слова места: under, next to, between, behind, in front of, near, opposite.
  4. Исключения: in the morning / afternoon / evening, но at night. on Friday morning. Без предлога с this, next, last, every: next Monday, every day.
  5. Направление: go to school, go to the bank, но go home (без предлога).
  1. Vaqt: at = aniq vaqt, on = kunlar va sanalar, in = uzoqroq davrlar (oylar, yillar, fasllar, kun qismlari).
  2. Joy: at = nuqta yoki manzil, on = sirt, in = biror narsa ichida (xona, shahar, davlat).
  3. Boshqa joy so'zlari: under, next to, between, behind, in front of, near, opposite.
  4. Istisnolar: in the morning / afternoon / evening, lekin at night. on Friday morning. this, next, last, every bilan predlog ishlatilmaydi: next Monday, every day.
  5. Yo'nalish: go to school, go to the bank, lekin go home (predlogsiz).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it time or place? Decide this first.
  2. If time: exact time → at. Day or date → on. Longer period → in.
  3. If place: a point → at. A surface → on. Inside → in.

Common mistakes

  • See you in Monday. See you on Monday.
  • My birthday is at May. My birthday is in May.
  • I wake up in 7 o'clock. I wake up at 7 o'clock.
  • I go to home at 5. I go home at 5.
Memory trick: at = a point, on = a surface or a day, in = inside or a long time.

In plain words

In, on and at are like a map. In is a big box, on is a surface, and at is one exact point. The same picture works for time.

  • The keys are in the bag. See you in May. Inside something, or inside a big period of time.
  • The book is on the table. See you on Monday. On a surface, or on one day.
  • I am at the door. See you at 5. One exact point, in a place or on the clock.

Time trick: at for a clock time, on for a day, in for a month or year.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это время или место? Сначала реши это.
  2. Если время: точное время → at. День или дата → on. Долгий период → in.
  3. Если место: точка → at. Поверхность → on. Внутри → in.

Частые ошибки

  • See you in Monday. See you on Monday.
  • My birthday is at May. My birthday is in May.
  • I wake up in 7 o'clock. I wake up at 7 o'clock.
  • I go to home at 5. I go home at 5.
Запоминалка: at = точка, on = поверхность или день, in = внутри или долгий период.

Простыми словами

In, on и at похожи на карту. In — большая коробка, on — поверхность, at — одна точная точка. Та же картинка работает для времени.

  • The keys are in the bag. See you in May. Ключи в сумке. Увидимся в мае. Внутри чего-то или внутри большого отрезка времени.
  • The book is on the table. See you on Monday. Книга на столе. Увидимся в понедельник. На поверхности или в один день.
  • I am at the door. See you at 5. Я у двери. Увидимся в 5. Одна точная точка.

Подсказка о времени: at — часы, on — день, in — месяц или год.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu vaqtmi yoki joymi? Avval shuni hal qiling.
  2. Vaqt bo'lsa: aniq vaqt → at. Kun yoki sana → on. Uzoq davr → in.
  3. Joy bo'lsa: nuqta → at. Sirt → on. Ichida → in.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • See you in Monday. See you on Monday.
  • My birthday is at May. My birthday is in May.
  • I wake up in 7 o'clock. I wake up at 7 o'clock.
  • I go to home at 5. I go home at 5.
Eslab qolish: at = nuqta, on = sirt yoki kun, in = ichida yoki uzoq davr.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

In, on va at xaritaga o'xshaydi. In katta quti, on sirt, at esa bitta aniq nuqta. Vaqt uchun ham shu rasm ishlaydi.

  • The keys are in the bag. See you in May. Kalitlar sumkada. Mayda ko'rishamiz. Biror narsaning ichida yoki katta vaqt ichida.
  • The book is on the table. See you on Monday. Kitob stol ustida. Dushanbada ko'rishamiz. Sirt ustida yoki bir kunda.
  • I am at the door. See you at 5. Men eshik oldidaman. Soat 5 da ko'rishamiz. Bitta aniq nuqta.

Vaqt uchun yordam: at soat uchun, on kun uchun, in oy yoki yil uchun.

Real-life use

We use in, on and at when we talk about plans, places and the way to go.

Make a time to meet

"The meeting is on Tuesday at 3 o'clock."

Show the way

"The bank is next to the pharmacy, opposite the school."

Saying where you live and work

"I live in Tashkent. I work at a school."

Tickets and times

"The train leaves at 8:10 on Friday."

IELTS Speaking

"I usually study in the evening, at home, at my desk."
