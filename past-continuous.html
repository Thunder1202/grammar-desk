Past Continuous

General info

Past Continuous shows an action that was in progress at a moment in the past. It was not finished yet at that moment.

We use it for

  • A moment in the past: At 8 pm yesterday I was studying.
  • An action interrupted: I was walking home when it started to rain.
  • Two actions at the same time: While I was cooking, my brother was playing.

Words that go with it

at 8 pm, at that time, when, while, all day, all evening.

Russian uses the simple past (я делал). Uzbek -yotgan edim is close (o'qiyotgan edim = I was reading). In English use was / were + -ing.

Past Continuous показывает действие, которое шло в определённый момент в прошлом. В этот момент оно ещё не закончилось.

Когда используем

  • Момент в прошлом: At 8 pm yesterday I was studying.
  • Прерванное действие: I was walking home when it started to rain.
  • Два действия одновременно: While I was cooking, my brother was playing.

Слова-подсказки

at 8 pm, at that time, when, while, all day, all evening.

В русском используется простое прошедшее (я делал). В узбекском близко -yotgan edim (o'qiyotgan edim). В английском: was / were + -ing.

Past Continuous o'tmishdagi ma'lum paytda davom etayotgan ishni bildiradi. O'sha paytda u hali tugamagan edi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • O'tmishdagi payt: At 8 pm yesterday I was studying.
  • Uzilgan ish: I was walking home when it started to rain.
  • Bir vaqtdagi ikki ish: While I was cooking, my brother was playing.

Yordamchi so'zlar

at 8 pm, at that time, when, while, all day, all evening.

Rus tilida oddiy o'tgan zamon (я делал). O'zbek tilidagi -yotgan edim yaqin (o'qiyotgan edim). Inglizchada: was / were + -ing.

Teacher's rule

I / he / she / ityou / we / they
+I was working.They were working.
−I wasn't working.They weren't working.
?Was she working?Were you working?
  1. Form: was / were + verb-ing. I, he, she, it → was. You, we, they → were.
  2. Spelling of -ing: make → making (drop -e), run → running (double the consonant), study → studying.
  3. Negative: wasn't / weren't. Question: Was / Were + subject + -ing. Short answers: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
  4. Interrupted action: long action in Past Continuous, short action in Past Simple, joined by when: I was cooking when she called.
  5. Two actions at once: use while + Past Continuous: While I was studying, he was playing.
  6. State verbs (know, like, want, love, understand) are not used in continuous forms: I knew the answer. Not: I was knowing.
  1. Форма: was / were + глагол-ing. I, he, she, it → was. You, we, they → were.
  2. Правописание -ing: make → making (убираем -e), run → running (удваиваем согласную), study → studying.
  3. Отрицание: wasn't / weren't. Вопрос: Was / Were + подлежащее + -ing. Краткие ответы: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
  4. Прерванное действие: длинное действие в Past Continuous, короткое в Past Simple, связка when: I was cooking when she called.
  5. Два действия сразу: while + Past Continuous: While I was studying, he was playing.
  6. Глаголы состояния (know, like, want, love, understand) не используются в continuous: I knew the answer. Не: I was knowing.
  1. Shakl: was / were + fe'l-ing. I, he, she, it → was. You, we, they → were.
  2. -ing imlosi: make → making (-e tushadi), run → running (undosh ikkilanadi), study → studying.
  3. Inkor: wasn't / weren't. So'roq: Was / Were + ega + -ing. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
  4. Uzilgan ish: uzoq ish Past Continuous da, qisqa ish Past Simple da, when bilan bog'lanadi: I was cooking when she called.
  5. Bir vaqtda ikki ish: while + Past Continuous: While I was studying, he was playing.
  6. Holat fe'llari (know, like, want, love, understand) continuous shaklda ishlatilmaydi: I knew the answer. Emas: I was knowing.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Was the action in progress at that moment? Yes → Past Continuous. Finished fact → Past Simple.
  2. Did something interrupt it? Yes → long action: was / were + -ing. Short action: Past Simple, with when.
  3. Two actions together? Yes → while + Past Continuous.

Past Simple or Past Continuous?

I watched a film. (I finished it.) / I was watching a film. (I was in the middle of it.)

Common mistakes

  • I was go home. I was going home.
  • They was playing. They were playing.
  • I was cooking when she was calling. I was cooking when she called.
  • I was knowing him. I knew him.
Only the long action takes -ing. The short action that interrupts it stays in Past Simple.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Действие шло в тот момент? Да → Past Continuous. Законченный факт → Past Simple.
  2. Его что-то прервало? Да → длинное действие: was / were + -ing. Короткое: Past Simple, со словом when.
  3. Два действия вместе? Да → while + Past Continuous.

Past Simple или Past Continuous?

I watched a film. (Досмотрел до конца.) / I was watching a film. (Был в процессе.)

Частые ошибки

  • I was go home. I was going home.
  • They was playing. They were playing.
  • I was cooking when she was calling. I was cooking when she called.
  • I was knowing him. I knew him.
Только длинное действие получает -ing. Короткое, которое его прерывает, остаётся в Past Simple.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. O'sha paytda ish davom etayotgan edimi? Ha → Past Continuous. Tugagan fakt → Past Simple.
  2. Uni nimadir to'xtatdimi? Ha → uzoq ish: was / were + -ing. Qisqa ish: Past Simple, when bilan.
  3. Ikki ish birga bo'ldimi? Ha → while + Past Continuous.

Past Simple yoki Past Continuous?

I watched a film. (Oxirigacha ko'rdim.) / I was watching a film. (Jarayonda edim.)

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I was go home. I was going home.
  • They was playing. They were playing.
  • I was cooking when she was calling. I was cooking when she called.
  • I was knowing him. I knew him.
Faqat uzoq ish -ing oladi. Uni to'xtatgan qisqa ish Past Simple da qoladi.

Real-life use

We use Past Continuous to tell stories and explain what we were doing.

Telling a story

"It was raining and people were running to the bus when I saw the accident."

Explaining

"Sorry, I didn't answer. I was driving."

Asking about the past

"What were you doing at 8 last night?" "I was studying for my exam."

IELTS Speaking: telling a memory

"Last summer I was working at my aunt's shop when I decided to learn English seriously."
