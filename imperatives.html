Imperatives

General info

Imperatives are the verb form we use to give orders, instructions, advice and invitations. We use the base form of the verb with no subject.

Form

  • Order / instruction: Open the door. Turn left.
  • Negative: Don't + verb: Don't run. Don't be late.
  • Polite: add please: Please sit down. Close the door, please.

We use it for

  • Instructions: Mix the eggs and add sugar.
  • Advice: Take a rest.
  • Invitations: Come in. Have a seat.
  • Warnings: Be careful!
The imperative has no subject. We understand "you" but we do not say it: (You) Close the door.

Императив (повелительное наклонение) — форма глагола для приказов, инструкций, советов и приглашений. Используем базовую форму глагола без подлежащего.

Форма

  • Приказ / инструкция: Open the door. Turn left.
  • Отрицание: Don't + глагол: Don't run. Don't be late.
  • Вежливо: добавить please: Please sit down. Close the door, please.

Для чего используем

  • Инструкции: Mix the eggs and add sugar.
  • Совет: Take a rest.
  • Приглашение: Come in. Have a seat.
  • Предупреждение: Be careful!
В императиве нет подлежащего. Мы подразумеваем "you", но не говорим его: (You) Close the door.

Buyruq mayli — buyruq, ko'rsatma, maslahat va taklif berish uchun ishlatiladigan fe'l shakli. Fe'lning asosiy shaklini egasiz ishlatamiz.

Shakl

  • Buyruq / ko'rsatma: Open the door. Turn left.
  • Inkor: Don't + fe'l: Don't run. Don't be late.
  • Odobli: please qo'shiladi: Please sit down. Close the door, please.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • Ko'rsatmalar: Mix the eggs and add sugar.
  • Maslahat: Take a rest.
  • Taklif: Come in. Have a seat.
  • Ogohlantirish: Be careful!
Buyruq maylida ega yo'q. "you" nazarda tutiladi, lekin aytilmaydi: (You) Close the door.

Teacher's rule

  1. Use the base verb, with no subject: Sit down. Open your books.
  2. Negative: Don't + base verb: Don't touch it. Don't be afraid.
  3. The verb be has its own forms: Be quiet! Don't be late!
  4. Add please to be polite: Please wait here. Sit down, please.
  5. Let's + verb makes a suggestion with we: Let's go. Let's not wait.
  6. In writing, imperative sentences often end with a full stop or an exclamation mark, not a question mark.
  1. Базовый глагол без подлежащего: Sit down. Open your books.
  2. Отрицание: Don't + базовый глагол: Don't touch it. Don't be afraid.
  3. У глагола be свои формы: Be quiet! Don't be late!
  4. Добавь please для вежливости: Please wait here. Sit down, please.
  5. Let's + глагол — предложение с we: Let's go. Let's not wait.
  6. В письме императив обычно заканчивается точкой или восклицательным знаком, а не вопросительным.
  1. Asosiy fe'l, egasiz: Sit down. Open your books.
  2. Inkor: Don't + asosiy fe'l: Don't touch it. Don't be afraid.
  3. be fe'lining o'z shakllari bor: Be quiet! Don't be late!
  4. Odob uchun please qo'shamiz: Please wait here. Sit down, please.
  5. Let's + fe'l — we bilan taklif: Let's go. Let's not wait.
  6. Yozuvda buyruq gap odatda nuqta yoki undov belgisi bilan tugaydi, so'roq belgisi bilan emas.

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. Am I telling someone to do something? Use the base verb, no subject.
  2. Am I telling them not to do it? Add Don't before the verb.

Common mistakes

  • You open the door. Open the door.
  • Not run. Don't run.
  • Don't to run. Don't run.
  • Let's to go. Let's go.
Negative is always don't, even for be: Don't be late.

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Я велю кому-то что-то сделать? Базовый глагол, без подлежащего.
  2. Я велю не делать это? Добавь Don't перед глаголом.

Частые ошибки

  • You open the door. Open the door.
  • Not run. Don't run.
  • Don't to run. Don't run.
  • Let's to go. Let's go.
Отрицание всегда don't, даже для be: Don't be late.

O'zingizga ikkita savol bering

  1. Kimgadir biror ish qilishni aytayapmanmi? Asosiy fe'l, egasiz.
  2. Qilmaslikni aytayapmanmi? Fe'ldan oldin Don't qo'shamiz.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • You open the door. Open the door.
  • Not run. Don't run.
  • Don't to run. Don't run.
  • Let's to go. Let's go.
Inkor har doim don't, be uchun ham: Don't be late.

Real-life use

You hear and use imperatives every day — in recipes, on signs, in class and with friends.

Following a recipe

"Heat the oil. Add the onion and cook for five minutes."

Classroom

"Open your books at page ten. Don't write in the book."

Public signs

"Push. Don't touch. Keep off the grass."

Giving directions

"Go straight and turn right at the lights."

Being polite at home

"Please close the window. Don't be late for dinner."

Examples and drill

Positive

Negative

Don't run. Don't be afraid. Don't forget your keys.

With "be"

Be careful. Be quiet. Don't be silly.

Suggestions with "let's"

Let's go. Let's start. Let's not wait.
