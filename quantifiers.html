Quantifiers

General info

Quantifiers are small words that tell us how much or how many: some, any, much, many, a lot of, a few, a little.

We use them for

  • Saying an amount without a number: I have some friends in London.
  • Asking about amount: How many students are there? How much time do we have?
  • Small or big amount: a few apples, a little milk, a lot of work.

Words that go with them

countable (apples, students), uncountable (water, time, money), how many, how much.

First check the noun: can you count it? Countable → many / a few. Uncountable → much / a little.

Кванторы — маленькие слова, которые показывают сколько: some, any, much, many, a lot of, a few, a little.

Когда используем

  • Количество без числа: I have some friends in London.
  • Вопрос о количестве: How many students are there? How much time do we have?
  • Мало или много: a few apples, a little milk, a lot of work.

Слова-подсказки

исчисляемые (apples, students), неисчисляемые (water, time, money), how many, how much.

Сначала проверь существительное: его можно посчитать? Исчисляемое → many / a few. Неисчисляемое → much / a little.

Miqdor so'zlari — qancha ekanini bildiradigan kichik so'zlar: some, any, much, many, a lot of, a few, a little.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Son aytmasdan miqdor: I have some friends in London.
  • Miqdor haqida so'rash: How many students are there? How much time do we have?
  • Oz yoki ko'p: a few apples, a little milk, a lot of work.

Yordamchi so'zlar

sanaladigan (apples, students), sanalmaydigan (water, time, money), how many, how much.

Avval otni tekshiring: uni sanash mumkinmi? Sanaladigan → many / a few. Sanalmaydigan → much / a little.

  1. some: in positive sentences. any: in negatives and questions.
  2. Offers and requests: use some in questions: Would you like some tea? Can I have some water?
  3. many + countable, much + uncountable. Use them mostly in negatives and questions: I don't have many books. How much time?
  4. a lot of / lots of: both types, mostly in positive sentences: I have a lot of friends. She drinks a lot of water.
  5. a few (countable) and a little (uncountable) = a small amount, but enough: I have a few friends.
  6. no = not any: I have no time = I don't have any time.
  1. some: в утверждениях. any: в отрицаниях и вопросах.
  2. Предложения и просьбы: some и в вопросах: Would you like some tea? Can I have some water?

  4. a lot of / lots of: с обоими типами, чаще в утверждениях: I have a lot of friends. She drinks a lot of water.
  5. a few (исчисляемое) и a little (неисчисляемое) = немного, но достаточно: I have a few friends.
  6. no = not any: I have no time = I don't have any time.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Can I count the noun? Yes → many / a few / How many. No → much / a little / How much.
  2. Is the sentence positive, negative or a question? Positive → some. Negative or question → any.
  3. Is it big or small? Big → a lot of. Small → a few / a little.

Common mistakes

  • I don't have some money. I don't have any money.
  • How many water do you drink? How much water do you drink?
  • There are much people here. There are many people here.
  • I have many money. I have a lot of money.
When unsure, a lot of works with every noun in positive sentences.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Можно ли посчитать существительное? Да → many / a few / How many. Нет → much / a little / How much.
  2. Предложение утвердительное, отрицательное или вопрос? Утверждение → some. Отрицание или вопрос → any.
  3. Много или мало? Много → a lot of. Мало → a few / a little.

Частые ошибки

  • I don't have some money. I don't have any money.
  • How many water do you drink? How much water do you drink?
  • There are much people here. There are many people here.
  • I have many money. I have a lot of money.
Если сомневаешься, a lot of подходит к любому существительному в утверждениях.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Otni sanash mumkinmi? Ha → many / a few / How many. Yo'q → much / a little / How much.
  2. Gap darakmi, inkormi yoki so'roqmi? Darak → some. Inkor yoki so'roq → any.
  3. Ko'pmi yoki ozmi? Ko'p → a lot of. Oz → a few / a little.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I don't have some money. I don't have any money.
  • How many water do you drink? How much water do you drink?
  • There are much people here. There are many people here.
  • I have many money. I have a lot of money.
Ikkilansangiz, darak gaplarda a lot of har qanday ot bilan ishlaydi.

Real-life use

You use these words every time you shop, cook or talk about your day.

Shopping

"How much is this? Do you have any smaller sizes? I need a few things."

Cooking

"We need some rice and a little oil. There isn't much sugar left."

Offers

"Would you like some tea? Can I have some water, please?"

IELTS Speaking: describing your city

"There are a lot of parks, but there isn't much public transport. A few places are very crowded."

English only

Examples and drill

some and any

I have some friends here. I don't have any brothers. Do you have any questions?

many and much

There aren't many shops here. We don't have much time. How many people came? How much is it?

a lot of

She has a lot of homework. They eat a lot of rice.

a few and a little

I have a few good ideas. Add a little salt, please.

no

There is no milk. We have no time.

Drill

1. I don't have ___ money. (some / any)
any
2. How ___ students are in your class? (much / many)
many
3. How ___ time do we have? (much / many)
much
4. I have ___ friends in Samarkand. (a few / a little)
a few
5. Would you like ___ coffee? (some / any)
some
6. There is only ___ milk in the fridge. (a few / a little)
a little
7. Correct it: She has many money.
She has a lot of money. / She has much money (only in negatives or questions: She doesn't have much money.)
