Can / can't

General info

can is a modal verb, a helper that adds meaning to the main verb. At A1 we use it for ability (what you are able to do), permission (what you are allowed to do), and polite requests and offers. can't means not able or not allowed.

We use it for

  • Ability: I can swim. She can speak three languages.
  • Permission: You can park here. Can I sit here?
  • Requests: Can you help me, please?
  • Offers: Can I help you?
Uzbek says suzolaman (-a olaman), Russian says могу or умею. English uses one word, can, for ability, permission and requests.

can — модальный глагол, помощник, который добавляет смысл основному глаголу. На уровне A1 он выражает умение (что ты способен делать), разрешение (что тебе можно) и вежливые просьбы и предложения. can't значит «не могу» или «нельзя».

Когда используем

  • Умение: I can swim. She can speak three languages.
  • Разрешение: You can park here. Can I sit here?
  • Просьбы: Can you help me, please?
  • Предложения: Can I help you?
В русском «могу» и «умею». В английском одно слово can для умения, разрешения и просьб.

can — modal fe'l, asosiy fe'lga ma'no qo'shadigan yordamchi. A1 darajada u qobiliyat (nimani qila olasiz), ruxsat (nima mumkin) hamda xushmuomala iltimos va takliflar uchun ishlatiladi. can't — qila olmaydi yoki mumkin emas.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Qobiliyat: I can swim. She can speak three languages.
  • Ruxsat: You can park here. Can I sit here?
  • Iltimos: Can you help me, please?
  • Taklif: Can I help you?
O'zbek tilida suzolaman (-a olaman). Inglizchada qobiliyat, ruxsat va iltimos uchun bitta so'z: can.

Teacher's rule

FormExample
+subject + can + base verbShe can drive.
−subject + can't + base verbShe can't drive.
?Can + subject + base verb?Can she drive?
  1. No change: can is the same for every person. No -s: He can (not "he cans").
  2. Bare verb after can: no to, no -ing: I can swim (not "can to swim").
  3. Negative: can't (full form: cannot).
  4. Question: Can goes first, no do / does: Can you swim? Short answers: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
  5. Meanings: ability, permission, request, offer.

Pronunciation

can is weak in positive sentences (/kən/). can't is strong (/kɑːnt/), so listen for the vowel.

  1. Не изменяется: can одинаков для всех лиц. Без -s: He can (не «he cans»).
  2. После can глагол без to: без to и без -ing: I can swim (не «can to swim»).
  3. Отрицание: can't (полная форма: cannot).
  4. Вопрос: Can в начале, без do / does: Can you swim? Краткие ответы: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
  5. Значения: умение, разрешение, просьба, предложение.

Произношение

can в утверждениях слабый (/kən/). can't сильный (/kɑːnt/), слушай гласный звук.

  1. O'zgarmaydi: can barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil. -s yo'q: He can ("he cans" emas).
  2. can dan keyin fe'l to siz: to ham, -ing ham yo'q: I can swim ("can to swim" emas).
  3. Inkor: can't (to'liq shakli: cannot).
  4. So'roq: Can boshiga, do / does siz: Can you swim? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
  5. Ma'nolari: qobiliyat, ruxsat, iltimos, taklif.

Talaffuz

can darak gapda kuchsiz (/kən/). can't kuchli (/kɑːnt/), unli tovushga quloq soling.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Ability, permission or request? It does not matter. The word is the same: can.
  2. Who is it about? Nobody changes can. No -s.
  3. Which verb (action word) comes next? The bare verb. For a negative (sentence with not) use can't. For a question put Can first.

Common mistakes

  • She cans swim. She can swim.
  • I can to swim. I can swim.
  • Does he can drive? Can he drive?
  • He can swims. He can swim.
can is a helper. It never changes, and the verb after it stays bare.

In plain words

Can tells what is possible: what you are able to do, or what is allowed. Can't says the opposite. Think of a door: can means it is open, can't means it is closed.

  • I can swim. I know how to do this.
  • You can't park here. This is not allowed.
  • Can you help me? You ask if it is possible for someone.

After can, the action word stays simple. We say "She can swim", never "She can swims" or "She can to swim".

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Умение, разрешение или просьба? Неважно. Слово одно: can.
  2. О ком речь? can не меняется ни для кого. Без -s.
  3. Какой глагол (это слово-действие) дальше? Начальная форма. Для отрицания (предложение с not) can't. Для вопроса Can в начало.

Частые ошибки

  • She cans swim. She can swim.
  • I can to swim. I can swim.
  • Does he can drive? Can he drive?
  • He can swims. He can swim.
can — помощник. Он не меняется, а глагол после него остаётся в начальной форме.

Простыми словами

Can говорит о том, что возможно: что вы умеете или что разрешено. Can't значит обратное. Представьте дверь: can — открыта, can't — закрыта.

  • I can swim. Я умею плавать.
  • You can't park here. Здесь нельзя парковаться. Это запрещено.
  • Can you help me? Вы можете мне помочь? Спрашиваем, возможно ли это.

После can слово-действие не меняется. Говорим "She can swim", а не "She can swims" и не "She can to swim".

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Qobiliyat, ruxsat yoki iltimosmi? Farqi yo'q. So'z bitta: can.
  2. Gap kim haqida? can hech kim uchun o'zgarmaydi. -s yo'q.
  3. Keyingi fe'l (harakat so'zi) qaysi? Boshlang'ich shakl. Inkorda (not qatnashgan gap) can't. So'roqda Can boshiga.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She cans swim. She can swim.
  • I can to swim. I can swim.
  • Does he can drive? Can he drive?
  • He can swims. He can swim.
can — yordamchi. U o'zgarmaydi, undan keyingi fe'l esa boshlang'ich shaklda qoladi.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Can nima mumkinligini bildiradi: nima qila olishingizni yoki nimaga ruxsat borligini. Can't teskarisini bildiradi. Eshikni tasavvur qiling: can ochiq, can't yopiq.

  • I can swim. Men suza olaman.
  • You can't park here. Bu yerda mashina qo'yib bo'lmaydi. Ruxsat yo'q.
  • Can you help me? Menga yordam bera olasizmi? Bu mumkinligini so'raymiz.

Can dan keyin harakat so'zi o'zgarmaydi. "She can swim" deymiz, "She can swims" yoki "She can to swim" emas.

Real-life use

You use can every day. Use it to say what you can do, to ask for things and to talk about rules.

What you can do (CV, job interviews)

"I can speak English, Russian and Uzbek. I can use Excel."

Ask if you can do something

"Can I open the window?" "Can I borrow your pen?"

Ask nicely

"Can you help me, please?" "Can I have the bill, please?"

Help people

"Can I help you?" "I can carry that for you."

Rules and signs

"You can't park here." "You can't use phones in the exam."

IELTS Speaking

"I can cook a few simple dishes, but I can't bake very well."
