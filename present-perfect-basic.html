Present Perfect (basic)

General info

Present Perfect connects the past with now. We do not say when it happened. The experience or the result is what matters.

We use it for

  • Life experience: I have been to Dubai. Have you ever tried sushi?
  • Recent news with a result now: I have lost my keys (I can't find them now).
  • Just, already, yet: She has just left. I have already eaten. Have you finished yet?

Time words that go with it

ever, never, just, already, yet, so far, recently.

Russian has no Present Perfect: it uses the simple past (я был). Uzbek -gan is close (borganman = I have been). In English you must use have / has.

Present Perfect связывает прошлое с настоящим. Мы не говорим, когда это произошло. Важны опыт или результат.

Когда используем

  • Жизненный опыт: I have been to Dubai. Have you ever tried sushi?
  • Недавние новости с результатом сейчас: I have lost my keys (сейчас не могу их найти).
  • Just, already, yet: She has just left. I have already eaten. Have you finished yet?

Слова-подсказки

ever, never, just, already, yet, so far, recently.

В русском нет Present Perfect: используется простое прошедшее (я был). В узбекском близко -gan (borganman). В английском обязательно нужны have / has.

Present Perfect o'tmishni hozir bilan bog'laydi. Qachon bo'lganini aytmaymiz. Tajriba yoki natija muhim.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Hayotiy tajriba: I have been to Dubai. Have you ever tried sushi?
  • Hozirgi natijali yangilik: I have lost my keys (hozir topa olmayapman).
  • Just, already, yet: She has just left. I have already eaten. Have you finished yet?

Yordamchi so'zlar

  1. Form: have / has + past participle. Short forms: I've, she's, they've.
  2. Regular verbs: participle = verb + -ed (visited). Irregular verbs: learn the third column (seen, done, eaten).
  3. Negative: haven't / hasn't. Question: Have / Has + subject + participle. Short answers: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  4. ever / never: experience. Ever goes in questions: Have you ever been to Paris? Never means not ever: I have never eaten sushi.
  5. just / already / yet: just and already go between have and the participle: I have already eaten. Yet goes at the end, in negatives and questions: I haven't finished yet.
  6. No finished time: never use yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago. For those use Past Simple.
  1. Форма: have / has + третья форма глагола. Краткие формы: I've, she's, they've.
  2. Правильные глаголы: форма = глагол + -ed (visited). Неправильные: учи третью колонку (seen, done, eaten).
  3. Отрицание: haven't / hasn't. Вопрос: Have / Has + подлежащее + третья форма. Краткие ответы: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  4. ever / never: опыт. Ever в вопросах: Have you ever been to Paris? Never = не когда-либо: I have never eaten sushi.
  5. just / already / yet: just и already между have и третьей формой: I have already eaten. Yet в конце, в отрицаниях и вопросах: I haven't finished yet.
  6. Без конкретного времени: не используй yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago. С ними нужен Past Simple.
  1. Shakl: have / has + fe'lning uchinchi shakli. Qisqa shakllar: I've, she's, they've.
  2. To'g'ri fe'llar: shakl = fe'l + -ed (visited). Noto'g'ri fe'llar: uchinchi ustunni yodlang (seen, done, eaten).
  3. Inkor: haven't / hasn't. So'roq: Have / Has + ega + uchinchi shakl. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't.
  4. ever / never: tajriba. Ever so'roqda: Have you ever been to Paris? Never = hech qachon: I have never eaten sushi.
  5. just / already / yet: just va already have va uchinchi shakl orasida: I have already eaten. Yet oxirida, inkor va so'roqda: I haven't finished yet.
  6. Aniq vaqt yo'q: yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago ishlatilmaydi. Ular bilan Past Simple.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Do I say when it happened? Yes (yesterday, in 2020) → Past Simple. No → Present Perfect.
  2. Is it experience or a result I can see now? Yes → Present Perfect.
  3. Which participle? Regular → -ed. Irregular → check the table.

Been or gone?

She has been to Dubai = she went and came back. She has gone to Dubai = she is there now.

Common mistakes

  • I have went to Dubai. I have been to Dubai.
  • She have finished. She has finished.
  • I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday.
  • Did you ever been to Paris? Have you ever been to Paris?
A finished time word (yesterday, ago) kills Present Perfect. Use Past Simple.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Говорю, когда это было? Да (yesterday, in 2020) → Past Simple. Нет → Present Perfect.
  2. Это опыт или результат, который виден сейчас? Да → Present Perfect.
  3. Какая третья форма? Правильный → -ed. Неправильный → смотри таблицу.

Been или gone?

She has been to Dubai = она съездила и вернулась. She has gone to Dubai = она сейчас там.

Частые ошибки

  • I have went to Dubai. I have been to Dubai.
  • She have finished. She has finished.
  • I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday.
  • Did you ever been to Paris? Have you ever been to Paris?
Слово с конкретным прошлым временем (yesterday, ago) = Past Simple, не Present Perfect.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Qachon bo'lganini aytyapmanmi? Ha (yesterday, in 2020) → Past Simple. Yo'q → Present Perfect.
  2. Bu tajribami yoki hozir ko'rinib turgan natijami? Ha → Present Perfect.
  3. Qaysi uchinchi shakl? To'g'ri → -ed. Noto'g'ri → jadvalga qarang.

Been yoki gone?

She has been to Dubai = borib qaytdi. She has gone to Dubai = hozir o'sha yerda.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I have went to Dubai. I have been to Dubai.
  • She have finished. She has finished.
  • I have seen him yesterday. I saw him yesterday.
  • Did you ever been to Paris? Have you ever been to Paris?
Aniq o'tgan vaqt so'zi (yesterday, ago) bo'lsa, Past Simple ishlating.

Real-life use

People use Present Perfect to share experience and news.

Getting to know someone

"Have you ever been abroad? I have never flown in a plane."

News

"I've lost my phone. Have you seen it?"

At work or school

"I've already finished the report. Have you sent the email yet?"

IELTS Speaking: talking about experience

"I have studied English for five years. I have taken the IELTS test once."

Examples and drill

Experience

I have been to Dubai. She has never eaten sushi. Have you ever tried plov in Samarkand?

Result now

I have lost my keys. He has broken his phone.

Just, already, yet

They have just arrived. I have already done it. We haven't eaten yet.

Present Perfect or Past Simple?

I have seen that film. (when? not important) / I saw that film last week. (finished time)

Drill

1. I ___ (visit) Samarkand many times.
have visited
2. She ___ (not finish) her homework yet.
hasn't finished
3. ___ you ever (eat) sushi?
Have ... eaten
4. He has ___ (go / been) to London. He is back home now.
been
5. We have ___ eaten. (just / yet)
just (or already)
6. Choose: I (have seen / saw) that film last week.
saw
7. Correct it: She have never been to Paris.
She has never been to Paris.
