Too, enough, too much / many

General info

Too means more than we want: it is a problem. Enough means as much as we need. We use them to say if something is OK or not OK.

We use them for

  • Too = a problem: The tea is too hot. I can't drink it.
  • Enough = OK: The room is big enough for us.
  • Not enough = a problem: I don't have enough time.

Words that go with them

too + adjective, too much, too many, enough, not enough, to + verb.

Russian: слишком / достаточно. Uzbek: juda (ortiqcha) / yetarli. English word order is different: enough comes after an adjective but before a noun.

Too значит «больше, чем нужно»: это проблема. Enough значит «столько, сколько нужно». Так мы говорим, нормально что-то или нет.

Когда используем

  • Too = проблема: The tea is too hot. I can't drink it.
  • Enough = нормально: The room is big enough for us.
  • Not enough = проблема: I don't have enough time.

Слова-подсказки

too + прилагательное, too much, too many, enough, not enough, to + глагол.

Русский: слишком / достаточно. Узбекский: juda (ortiqcha) / yetarli. Порядок слов в английском другой: enough стоит после прилагательного, но перед существительным.

Too «keraklidan ko'p» degani: bu muammo. Enough «kerakli miqdorda» degani. Bu bilan biror narsa joyidami yoki yo'qligini aytamiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Too = muammo: The tea is too hot. I can't drink it.
  • Enough = joyida: The room is big enough for us.
  • Not enough = muammo: I don't have enough time.

Yordamchi so'zlar

too + sifat, too much, too many, enough, not enough, to + fe'l.

Rus tili: слишком / достаточно. O'zbek tili: juda (ortiqcha) / yetarli. Inglizcha so'z tartibi boshqa: enough sifatdan keyin, otdan oldin keladi.

  1. too + adjective or adverb = more than needed, a problem: too hot, too fast.
  2. too much + uncountable (traffic, sugar, time). too many + countable (cars, people). Never use too alone before a noun.
  3. enough goes after an adjective or adverb: tall enough, fast enough. It goes before a noun: enough time.
  4. not enough = less than needed: I don't have enough money. It isn't warm enough.
  5. + to + verb: too tired to study, old enough to drive, enough money to buy it.
  6. very or too? very = a lot, no problem: It's very hot (I like it). too = a problem: It's too hot (I can't stay).

  3. enough стоит после прилагательного или наречия: tall enough, fast enough. Перед существительным: enough time.
  4. not enough = меньше, чем нужно: I don't have enough money. It isn't warm enough.
  5. + to + глагол: too tired to study, old enough to drive, enough money to buy it.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a problem because there is too much? Yes → too / too much / too many. Is it a problem because there is too little? → not enough.
  2. What comes after? Adjective → too + adjective, or adjective + enough. Noun → too much / too many / enough + noun.
  3. Can I count the noun? Yes → too many. No → too much.

Common mistakes

  • It is too much hot. It is too hot.
  • There are too much people. There are too many people.
  • The room is enough big. The room is big enough.
  • I am very tired to walk. I am too tired to walk.
too + adjective, adjective + enough, enough + noun. Learn these three patterns.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это проблема, потому что слишком много? Да → too / too much / too many. Проблема, потому что слишком мало? → not enough.
  2. Что идёт дальше? Прилагательное → too + прилагательное или прилагательное + enough. Существительное → too much / too many / enough + существительное.
  3. Существительное можно посчитать? Да → too many. Нет → too much.

Частые ошибки

  • It is too much hot. It is too hot.
  • There are too much people. There are too many people.
  • The room is enough big. The room is big enough.
  • I am very tired to walk. I am too tired to walk.
too + прилагательное, прилагательное + enough, enough + существительное. Запомни три модели.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Juda ko'p bo'lgani uchun muammomi? Ha → too / too much / too many. Juda kam bo'lgani uchun muammomi? → not enough.
  2. Keyin nima keladi? Sifat → too + sifat yoki sifat + enough. Ot → too much / too many / enough + ot.
  3. Otni sanash mumkinmi? Ha → too many. Yo'q → too much.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • It is too much hot. It is too hot.
  • There are too much people. There are too many people.
  • The room is enough big. The room is big enough.

Real-life use

We use these words to complain, to decide and to give reasons.

Shopping

"These shoes are too small. Do you have a bigger size?"

Complaining

"There is too much noise here, and there aren't enough chairs."

Cooking

"Is there enough salt? It's too sweet."

IELTS Speaking: describing your city

"My city has too much traffic and not enough parks. The buses are cheap, but they aren't fast enough."

English only

Examples and drill

Too

The soup is too hot. He speaks too fast. I'm too tired to study.

Too much and too many

There is too much traffic. We have too many problems.

Enough

The room is big enough. Do we have enough chairs? He is old enough to vote.

Not enough

I don't have enough money. It isn't warm enough to swim.
