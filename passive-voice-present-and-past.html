Passive voice (present and past)

General info

In the passive voice the action or the thing is more important than the person who does it. We use be + past participle.

We use it for

  • The doer is unknown: My bike was stolen last night.
  • The doer is obvious or unimportant: English is spoken all over the world.
  • Describing facts and processes: These cars are made in Germany.

Words that go with it

is / are / was / were + past participle, by, made, built, written, spoken.

Russian: «был украден», «говорят» / Uzbek: -ildi, -ilgan (o'g'irlandi, qurilgan). English always needs be + past participle.

В страдательном залоге действие или предмет важнее, чем тот, кто действует. Мы используем be + третья форма глагола.

Когда используем

  • Исполнитель неизвестен: My bike was stolen last night.
  • Исполнитель очевиден или неважен: English is spoken all over the world.
  • Факты и процессы: These cars are made in Germany.

Слова-подсказки

is / are / was / were + третья форма, by, made, built, written, spoken.

Русский: «был украден», «говорят» / Узбекский: -ildi, -ilgan (o'g'irlandi, qurilgan). В английском всегда нужно be + третья форма.

Majhul nisbatda ish yoki narsa uni qiluvchi odamdan muhimroq bo'ladi. be + uchinchi shakl ishlatamiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Bajaruvchi noma'lum: My bike was stolen last night.
  • Bajaruvchi aniq yoki ahamiyatsiz: English is spoken all over the world.
  • Faktlar va jarayonlar: These cars are made in Germany.

Yordamchi so'zlar

is / are / was / were + uchinchi shakl, by, made, built, written, spoken.

Rus tili: «был украден», «говорят» / O'zbek tili: -ildi, -ilgan (o'g'irlandi, qurilgan). Inglizchada doim be + uchinchi shakl kerak.

  1. Form: be + past participle. Present simple: am / is / are + participle. Past simple: was / were + participle. The tense is shown by be.
  2. Use: the action or object matters more than the doer; the doer is unknown, obvious or unimportant: The window was broken. English is spoken worldwide.
  3. Making a passive: the object of the active sentence becomes the subject: They built this bridge in 1990. → This bridge was built in 1990.
  4. by + doer only if it is important or surprising: Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.
  5. Negative: isn't / aren't / wasn't / weren't + participle. Question: Is / Was + subject + participle? Where was it made?
  6. Only verbs with an object can be passive. Happen, arrive, die, go have no passive: The accident happened. Not: The accident was happened.
  1. Форма: be + третья форма. Present simple: am / is / are + третья форма. Past simple: was / were + третья форма. Время показывает be.
  2. Употребление: действие или предмет важнее исполнителя; исполнитель неизвестен, очевиден или неважен: The window was broken. English is spoken worldwide.
  3. Как образовать: дополнение активного предложения становится подлежащим: They built this bridge in 1990. → This bridge was built in 1990.
  4. by + исполнитель только если важно или неожиданно: Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.
  5. Отрицание: isn't / aren't / wasn't / weren't + третья форма. Вопрос: Is / Was + подлежащее + третья форма? Where was it made?
  6. Только глаголы с дополнением бывают в пассиве. Happen, arrive, die, go пассива не имеют: The accident happened. Не: The accident was happened.
  1. Shakl: be + uchinchi shakl. Present simple: am / is / are + uchinchi shakl. Past simple: was / were + uchinchi shakl. Zamonni be ko'rsatadi.
  2. Qo'llanishi: ish yoki narsa bajaruvchidan muhimroq; bajaruvchi noma'lum, aniq yoki ahamiyatsiz: The window was broken. English is spoken worldwide.
  3. Qanday yasaladi: aniq nisbatdagi gapning to'ldiruvchisi ega bo'ladi: They built this bridge in 1990. → This bridge was built in 1990.
  4. by + bajaruvchi faqat muhim yoki kutilmagan bo'lsa: Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.
  5. Inkor: isn't / aren't / wasn't / weren't + uchinchi shakl. So'roq: Is / Was + ega + uchinchi shakl? Where was it made?
  6. Faqat to'ldiruvchisi bor fe'llar majhul nisbatda bo'ladi. Happen, arrive, die, go da majhul nisbat yo'q: The accident happened. The accident was happened emas.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the doer unknown or unimportant? Yes → passive.
  2. Which tense? Now or always → am / is / are + participle. Finished past → was / were + participle.
  3. Does the verb have an object? No (happen, arrive) → no passive.

Active to passive in 3 steps

1) Take the object. 2) Add the right form of be. 3) Add the past participle. Active: They sell tickets online. Passive: Tickets are sold online.

Common mistakes

  • The car was stole. The car was stolen.
  • English speaks in many countries. English is spoken in many countries.
  • The accident was happened yesterday. The accident happened yesterday.
  • This bridge built in 1990. This bridge was built in 1990.
Never skip be, and never use the base verb: not "was steal", but "was stolen".

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Исполнитель неизвестен или неважен? Да → пассив.
  2. Какое время? Сейчас или всегда → am / is / are + третья форма. Завершённое прошлое → was / were + третья форма.
  3. У глагола есть дополнение? Нет (happen, arrive) → пассива нет.

Из актива в пассив за 3 шага

1) Возьми дополнение. 2) Поставь нужную форму be. 3) Добавь третью форму. Актив: They sell tickets online. Пассив: Tickets are sold online.

Частые ошибки

  • The car was stole. The car was stolen.
  • English speaks in many countries. English is spoken in many countries.
  • The accident was happened yesterday. The accident happened yesterday.
  • This bridge built in 1990. This bridge was built in 1990.
Не пропускай be и не используй основную форму: не «was steal», а «was stolen».

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bajaruvchi noma'lum yoki ahamiyatsizmi? Ha → majhul nisbat.
  2. Qaysi zamon? Hozir yoki doim → am / is / are + uchinchi shakl. Tugagan o'tmish → was / were + uchinchi shakl.
  3. Fe'lda to'ldiruvchi bormi? Yo'q (happen, arrive) → majhul nisbat yo'q.

Aniqdan majhulga 3 qadamda

1) To'ldiruvchini oling. 2) be ning kerakli shaklini qo'ying. 3) Uchinchi shaklni qo'shing. Aniq: They sell tickets online. Majhul: Tickets are sold online.

Real-life use

The passive is common in news, signs, descriptions and formal speech.

News

"A man was arrested last night. The road was closed for two hours."

Signs and notices

"Tickets are sold online. Smoking is not allowed here."

Describing products

"These shoes are made in Italy. The coffee is grown in Brazil."

IELTS Speaking: describing your school or city

"My school was built in 1995, and English is taught to all students."

English only

Examples and drill

Present simple passive

Cars are made here. English is spoken in many countries. Tickets are sold online.

Past simple passive

My bike was stolen. The bridge was built in 1990. Hamlet was written by Shakespeare.

Negative and question

The room wasn't cleaned. Where was it made? Is breakfast included?

No passive

The accident happened. She arrived late.
