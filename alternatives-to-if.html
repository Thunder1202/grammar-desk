Alternatives to if

General info

If is the most common word for conditions, but at C1 level we use other words to be more precise. Each one adds a small extra meaning: an exception, a limit, a warning or an imagined situation.

If and its alternatives

If you don't hurry, we'll miss the train. → Unless you hurry, we'll miss the train. → Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the train.

You can stay if you are quiet. → You can stay as long as you are quiet. → You can stay provided that you are quiet.

We use them to

  • Show an exception: I'll go unless it rains.
  • Stress the condition: You can borrow it provided that you return it.
  • Warn about the result: Take a map, otherwise you'll get lost.
  • Imagine a situation: Suppose you won the lottery, what would you do?
  • Prepare for a possibility: Take an umbrella in case it rains.
Russian says если не / при условии, что / иначе and Uzbek says agar ... bo'lmasa / sharti bilan / aks holda. English has a separate word for each idea, so choose by meaning.

  • Предупредить о результате: Take a map, otherwise you'll get lost.
  • Представить ситуацию: Suppose you won the lottery, what would you do?

  1. unless = if ... not. Use a positive verb after it: Unless you hurry, we'll be late. Do not say unless you don't hurry.
  2. When not to use unless: not for results that happen if something does not happen, and not for feelings. Say I'll be surprised if he doesn't come (not unless).
  3. provided / providing / as long as / on condition that: they mean only if and stress the condition. Use the present for the future: Provided that you pay, you can stay (not will pay). On condition that is formal and used in contracts.
  4. otherwise / or else: they start a new clause and mean if not. Use will / would: Take a coat; otherwise you'll be cold. For the past use would have: I took a coat; otherwise I would have been cold.
  5. suppose / supposing: usually in questions. Past simple = unreal now: Suppose you won the lottery? Past perfect = unreal past: Suppose you had missed the flight? Present = real possibility: Suppose he calls, what will you say?
  6. in case is not if: in case means we prepare before something that may happen. Take an umbrella in case it rains (before the rain). I'll take an umbrella if it rains (after the rain starts).
  7. but for + noun means if it were not for / if it had not been for: But for the rain, we would have gone.
  8. Style and variety: in formal writing use provided that, on condition that and unless. Do not use the same word every time. Remember: after these words use the present for the future, not will.

  2. Когда не использовать unless: не для результата, который происходит, если что-то не случится, и не для чувств. Говори I'll be surprised if he doesn't come (не unless).
  3. provided / providing / as long as / on condition that: значат «только если» и подчёркивают условие. Для будущего используй настоящее: Provided that you pay, you can stay (не will pay). On condition that формально и встречается в договорах.
  4. otherwise / or else: начинают новую часть и значат «если нет». Используй will / would: Take a coat; otherwise you'll be cold. Для прошлого would have: I took a coat; otherwise I would have been cold.
  5. suppose / supposing: обычно в вопросах. Past simple = нереально сейчас: Suppose you won the lottery? Past perfect = нереальное прошлое: Suppose you had missed the flight? Настоящее = реальная возможность: Suppose he calls, what will you say?
  6. in case — это не if: in case значит, что мы готовимся заранее к тому, что может случиться. Take an umbrella in case it rains (до дождя). I'll take an umbrella if it rains (когда дождь начался).
  7. but for + существительное значит «если бы не»: But for the rain, we would have gone.
  8. Стиль и разнообразие: в формальном письме используй provided that, on condition that и unless. Не повторяй одно и то же слово. Помни: после этих слов для будущего используем настоящее, а не will.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it an exception, an "if not"? Yes → unless.
  2. Do I stress that the condition must be met? Yes → provided that / as long as.
  3. Am I warning about what happens if not? Yes → otherwise.

Build it in 3 steps

If you don't hurry, we'll miss the train. 1) The idea is "if not", so choose unless. 2) Remove the not: you hurry. 3) Put it together. → Unless you hurry, we'll miss the train. (Or as a warning: Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the train.)

Why it works

Each word says something more than if. Unless names the exception. Provided that names the rule. Otherwise names the bad result. The reader understands your meaning faster.

Common mistakes

  • Unless you don't hurry, we'll be late. Unless you hurry, we'll be late.
  • Provided that you will pay, you can stay. Provided that you pay, you can stay.
  • Take an umbrella in case it will rain. Take an umbrella in case it rains.
  • Hurry up, unless we'll miss the bus. Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the bus.
After these words use the present, not will. And never put a negative after unless: it already means not.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это исключение, «если не»? Да → unless.
  2. Я подчёркиваю, что условие должно выполняться? Да → provided that / as long as.
  3. Я предупреждаю, что будет, если нет? Да → otherwise.

Строим за 3 шага

If you don't hurry, we'll miss the train. 1) Мысль «если не», значит выбираем unless. 2) Убери not: you hurry. 3) Собери предложение. → Unless you hurry, we'll miss the train. (Или как предупреждение: Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the train.)

Почему это работает

Каждое слово говорит больше, чем if. Unless называет исключение. Provided that называет правило. Otherwise называет плохой результат. Читатель быстрее понимает твою мысль.

Частые ошибки

  • Unless you don't hurry, we'll be late. Unless you hurry, we'll be late.
  • Provided that you will pay, you can stay. Provided that you pay, you can stay.
  • Take an umbrella in case it will rain. Take an umbrella in case it rains.
  • Hurry up, unless we'll miss the bus. Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the bus.
После этих слов используем настоящее, а не will. И никогда не ставь отрицание после unless: оно уже значит «не».

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu istisnomi, «agar ... bo'lmasa»mi? Ha → unless.
  2. Shart bajarilishi kerakligini ta'kidlayapmanmi? Ha → provided that / as long as.
  3. Aks holda nima bo'lishi haqida ogohlantiryapmanmi? Ha → otherwise.

3 qadamda yasash

Har bir so'z if dan ko'proq narsa aytadi. Unless istisnoni nomlaydi. Provided that qoidani nomlaydi. Otherwise yomon natijani nomlaydi. O'quvchi fikringizni tezroq tushunadi.

Real-life use

Alternatives to if make rules, advice, warnings and negotiations sound precise and professional.

Rules and contracts

"Entry is free provided that you register in advance. Staff may leave early on condition that their work is finished."

Advice and warnings

"Wear a coat, otherwise you'll be cold. Don't touch it unless you are sure."

Travel

"Take some cash in case the card doesn't work. You can stay as long as you like."

Business and negotiation

"We will sign the contract as long as the price is fixed. Supposing we offered a discount, would you order more?"

IELTS Writing and Speaking

"Governments must act now; otherwise the damage will be permanent. The problem will continue unless action is taken." Speaking: "Suppose you could live anywhere, where would you go?"

English only

Examples and drill

unless

I'll go unless it rains. Unless you hurry, we'll be late. She won't come unless you ask her.

provided that / as long as / on condition that

You can borrow it provided that you return it. You can stay as long as you are quiet. He agreed on condition that nobody was told.

otherwise / or else

Hurry up, otherwise we'll miss the train. Be quiet, or else I'll leave. I took a coat; otherwise I would have been cold.

suppose / supposing / in case

Suppose you won the lottery, what would you do? Take an umbrella in case it rains. Write it down in case you forget.

but for / even if

But for your help, I would have failed. I'll go even if it rains.
