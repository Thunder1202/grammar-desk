Reported speech (statements)

General info

Reported speech tells someone what another person said, without using the exact words. The tense usually moves one step back.

We use it for

  • Telling what someone said: She said she was tired.
  • Passing on a message: He told me he would call later.
  • Telling a story or news: They said they had found the keys.

Words that go with it

said, told, that, then, the next day, the day before, there.

Russian and Uzbek keep the original tense (она сказала, что устала / charchaganini aytdi). English moves the tense back: said she was tired.

Косвенная речь передаёт чужие слова без точных слов говорящего. Время обычно сдвигается на шаг назад.

Когда используем

  • Рассказать, что сказал человек: She said she was tired.
  • Передать сообщение: He told me he would call later.
  • Рассказ или новости: They said they had found the keys.

Слова-подсказки

said, told, that, then, the next day, the day before, there.

В русском и узбекском время сохраняется (она сказала, что устала / charchaganini aytdi). В английском время сдвигается назад: said she was tired.

Ko'chirma gap boshqa odam aytganini aniq so'zlarsiz yetkazadi. Zamon odatda bir qadam orqaga suriladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Kimdir nima degani haqida gapirish: She said she was tired.
  • Xabar yetkazish: He told me he would call later.
  • Hikoya yoki yangiliklar: They said they had found the keys.

Yordamchi so'zlar

said, told, that, then, the next day, the day before, there.

Rus va o'zbek tilida zamon saqlanadi (она сказала, что устала / charchaganini aytdi). Inglizchada zamon orqaga suriladi: said she was tired.

Teacher's rule

Direct speechReported speech
"I work here."She said she worked there.
"I am working."She said she was working.
"I have finished."She said she had finished.
"I went home."She said she had gone home.
"I will call you."She said she would call me.
"I can swim."She said she could swim.
  1. Backshift: when said / told is in the past, move the tense one step back: present → past, past → past perfect, will → would, can → could.
  2. say or tell: said (that) + clause. told + person (+ that) + clause: He told me he was tired. Not: He said me.
  3. Pronouns change with the meaning: "I like your bag," she said. → She said she liked my bag.
  4. Time and place words: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there.
  5. No backshift when the reporting verb is present (She says she is tired) or when the fact is still true (optional).
  6. Punctuation: no quotation marks and no comma: She said that she was tired. That is optional.
  1. Сдвиг времён: когда said / told в прошедшем, время идёт на шаг назад: настоящее → прошедшее, прошедшее → past perfect, will → would, can → could.
  2. say или tell: said (that) + предложение. told + человек (+ that) + предложение: He told me he was tired. Не: He said me.
  3. Местоимения меняются по смыслу: "I like your bag," she said. → She said she liked my bag.
  4. Слова времени и места: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there.
  5. Без сдвига, если глагол речи в настоящем (She says she is tired) или факт всё ещё верен (необязательно).
  6. Пунктуация: без кавычек и запятой: She said that she was tired. That можно опустить.
  1. Zamon surilishi: said / told o'tgan zamonda bo'lsa, zamon bir qadam orqaga suriladi: hozirgi → o'tgan, o'tgan → past perfect, will → would, can → could.
  2. say yoki tell: said (that) + gap. told + odam (+ that) + gap: He told me he was tired. He said me emas.
  3. Olmoshlar ma'noga qarab o'zgaradi: "I like your bag," she said. → She said she liked my bag.
  4. Vaqt va joy so'zlari: now → then, today → that day, tomorrow → the next day, yesterday → the day before, here → there.
  5. Surilishsiz, agar gapirish fe'li hozirgi zamonda bo'lsa (She says she is tired) yoki fakt hali ham to'g'ri bo'lsa (shart emas).
  6. Tinish belgilari: qo'shtirnoq va vergul yo'q: She said that she was tired. That ni tushirib qoldirish mumkin.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the reporting verb in the past? Yes → move the tense back. No (She says) → keep it.
  2. Do I say who heard it? Yes → told me. No → said.
  3. Who is speaking now? Change I / you / my / your so they match the new speaker.

Step by step

"I will call you tomorrow," he said. 1) said → past. 2) will → would. 3) you → me. 4) tomorrow → the next day. → He said he would call me the next day.

Common mistakes

  • He said me he was tired. He told me he was tired.
  • She told that she was busy. She said she was busy. / She told me she was busy.
  • He said he has finished. He said he had finished.
  • She said she will call me the next day. She said she would call me the next day.
Said + clause. Told + person + clause. Never said me, never told that.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Глагол речи в прошедшем? Да → сдвигаем время назад. Нет (She says) → оставляем.
  2. Говорю, кто услышал? Да → told me. Нет → said.
  3. Кто говорит сейчас? Меняй I / you / my / your под нового говорящего.

Шаг за шагом

Частые ошибки

  • He said me he was tired. He told me he was tired.
  • She told that she was busy. She said she was busy. / She told me she was busy.
  • He said he has finished. He said he had finished.
  • She said she will call me the next day. She said she would call me the next day.
Said + предложение. Told + человек + предложение. Никогда не said me, не told that.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Gapirish fe'li o'tgan zamondami? Ha → zamonni orqaga suring. Yo'q (She says) → o'zgartirmang.
  2. Kim eshitganini aytyapmanmi? Ha → told me. Yo'q → said.
  3. Hozir kim gapiryapti? I / you / my / your ni yangi gapiruvchiga moslang.

Real-life use

We report what people said every day: messages, news, instructions and stories.

Passing on a message

"Tom called. He said he would be late, and he told me to start without him."

Telling a story

"She told me she had lost her phone, so I said I would help her look for it."

News

"The teacher said the exam was moved to Friday."

IELTS Speaking: talking about advice

"My teacher told me I should practise speaking every day, so I said I would try."

English only

Examples and drill

Present to past

"I like tea." → She said she liked tea. "I am working." → He said he was working.

Past to past perfect

"I saw Tom." → She said she had seen Tom.

will and can

"I will help." → He said he would help. "I can swim." → She said she could swim.

Time and place

"I'll come tomorrow." → He said he would come the next day.
