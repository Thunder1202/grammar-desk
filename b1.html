B1

Intermediate. 18 topics. Choose one.

  • Present Perfect vs Past SimpleOpen topicWe cover: finished time versus time connected to now. You will be able to: decide which tense fits experiences, news and stories.Разбираем: законченное время и время, связанное с настоящим. Вы сможете: выбирать время для опыта, новостей и историй.Ko'ramiz: tugagan vaqt va hozir bilan bog'liq vaqt. Siz qila olasiz: tajriba, yangilik va hikoya uchun to'g'ri zamonni tanlash.
  • Present Perfect ContinuousOpen topicWe cover: have / has been + -ing, for and since. You will be able to: say how long something has been going on.Разбираем: have / has been + -ing, for и since. Вы сможете: сказать, как долго что-то продолжается.Ko'ramiz: have / has been + -ing, for va since. Siz qila olasiz: biror ish qancha vaqtdan beri davom etayotganini aytish.
  • Past PerfectOpen topicWe cover: had + participle for the earlier past. You will be able to: tell a story in the right order and explain reasons.Разбираем: had + третья форма для более раннего прошлого. Вы сможете: рассказывать истории по порядку и объяснять причины.Ko'ramiz: oldingi o'tmish uchun had + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: hikoyani to'g'ri tartibda aytish va sabablarni tushuntirish.
  • Future forms reviewOpen topicWe cover: will, going to, present continuous, present simple. You will be able to: pick the best future form for decisions, plans and timetables.Разбираем: will, going to, present continuous, present simple. Вы сможете: выбирать лучшую форму будущего для решений, планов и расписаний.Ko'ramiz: will, going to, present continuous, present simple. Siz qila olasiz: qaror, reja va jadval uchun eng mos kelasi shaklni tanlash.
  • First ConditionalOpen topicWe cover: if + present, will + verb. You will be able to: talk about real future situations, warnings and promises.Разбираем: if + present, will + глагол. Вы сможете: говорить о реальных ситуациях в будущем, предупреждениях и обещаниях.Ko'ramiz: if + present, will + fe'l. Siz qila olasiz: kelajakdagi real holatlar, ogohlantirish va va'dalar haqida gapirish.
  • Second ConditionalOpen topicWe cover: if + past, would + verb. You will be able to: talk about imaginary situations and give advice with If I were you.Разбираем: if + past, would + глагол. Вы сможете: говорить о воображаемых ситуациях и давать советы с If I were you.Ko'ramiz: if + past, would + fe'l. Siz qila olasiz: xayoliy holatlar haqida gapirish va If I were you bilan maslahat berish.
  • Passive voice (present and past)Open topicWe cover: am / is / are / was / were + participle. You will be able to: say what is done or was done when the doer is not important.Разбираем: am / is / are / was / were + третья форма. Вы сможете: сказать, что делается или было сделано, когда исполнитель неважен.Ko'ramiz: am / is / are / was / were + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: bajaruvchi muhim bo'lmaganda nima qilinishi yoki qilinganini aytish.
  • Modals of deductionOpen topicWe cover: must, can't, might in the present. You will be able to: make logical guesses from evidence.Разбираем: must, can't, might в настоящем. Вы сможете: делать логичные предположения по признакам.Ko'ramiz: hozirgi zamonda must, can't, might. Siz qila olasiz: belgilarga qarab mantiqiy taxmin qilish.
  • Reported speech (statements)Open topicWe cover: backshift, say / tell, pronouns and time words. You will be able to: report what someone said.Разбираем: сдвиг времён, say / tell, местоимения и слова времени. Вы сможете: передать, что сказал человек.Ko'ramiz: zamon surilishi, say / tell, olmosh va vaqt so'zlari. Siz qila olasiz: kimdir aytganini yetkazish.
  • Relative clausesOpen topicWe cover: who, which, that, whose, where, when. You will be able to: describe people, things and places in one sentence.Разбираем: who, which, that, whose, where, when. Вы сможете: описывать людей, предметы и места в одном предложении.Ko'ramiz: who, which, that, whose, where, when. Siz qila olasiz: odam, narsa va joylarni bitta gapda tasvirlash.
  • Used to / wouldOpen topicWe cover: past habits and states that changed. You will be able to: talk about how life was different in the past.Разбираем: прошлые привычки и состояния, которые изменились. Вы сможете: рассказать, чем прошлая жизнь отличалась от нынешней.Ko'ramiz: o'zgargan o'tmishdagi odat va holatlar. Siz qila olasiz: o'tmishda hayot qanday boshqacha bo'lganini aytish.
  • Gerund and infinitiveOpen topicWe cover: verb patterns, make / let, meaning changes. You will be able to: use verbs like stop, remember and try with the right form.Разбираем: глагольные модели, make / let, изменение значения. Вы сможете: правильно использовать stop, remember и try.Ko'ramiz: fe'l qoliplari, make / let, ma'no o'zgarishi. Siz qila olasiz: stop, remember va try fe'llarini to'g'ri shaklda ishlatish.
  • Articles reviewOpen topicWe cover: a / an, the and no article. You will be able to: use articles accurately in longer texts.Разбираем: a / an, the и отсутствие артикля. Вы сможете: точно использовать артикли в длинных текстах.Ko'ramiz: a / an, the va artiklsiz. Siz qila olasiz: uzun matnlarda artikllarni aniq ishlatish.
  • Phrasal verbsOpen topicWe cover: separable, inseparable and common verbs. You will be able to: use everyday phrasal verbs and place the object correctly.Разбираем: разделяемые, неразделяемые и частые глаголы. Вы сможете: использовать частые фразовые глаголы и правильно ставить дополнение.Ko'ramiz: ajraladigan, ajralmaydigan va keng tarqalgan fe'llar. Siz qila olasiz: kundalik phrasal verblarni ishlatish va to'ldiruvchini to'g'ri qo'yish.
  • Reflexive and reciprocal pronounsOpen topicWe cover: myself, yourself, each other, one another. You will be able to: talk about actions on yourself and between people.Разбираем: myself, yourself, each other, one another. Вы сможете: говорить о действиях над собой и между людьми.Ko'ramiz: myself, yourself, each other, one another. Siz qila olasiz: o'ziga qaratilgan va odamlar orasidagi harakatlar haqida gapirish.
  • So and neither (So do I / Neither do I)Open topicWe cover: So do I, Neither do I and short answers. You will be able to: agree and disagree briefly.Разбираем: So do I, Neither do I и краткие ответы. Вы сможете: кратко соглашаться и не соглашаться.Ko'ramiz: So do I, Neither do I va qisqa javoblar. Siz qila olasiz: qisqa tarzda rozi bo'lish va rozi bo'lmaslik.
  • Past Perfect ContinuousOpen topicWe cover: had been + -ing for ongoing past actions. You will be able to: explain how long something had been happening.Разбираем: had been + -ing для длительных действий в прошлом. Вы сможете: объяснять, как долго что-то происходило.Ko'ramiz: o'tmishdagi davomiy harakatlar uchun had been + -ing. Siz qila olasiz: biror narsa qancha davom etganini tushuntirish.
  • So and suchOpen topicWe cover: so + adjective and such + noun for emphasis. You will be able to: emphasise qualities and results.Разбираем: so + прилагательное и such + существительное для усиления. Вы сможете: подчёркивать качества и результаты.Ko'ramiz: ta'kidlash uchun so + sifat va such + ot. Siz qila olasiz: sifat va natijalarni ta'kidlash.
