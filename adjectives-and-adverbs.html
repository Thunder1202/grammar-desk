Adjectives and adverbs

General info

An adjective describes a noun (a happy child). An adverb describes a verb, an adjective or another adverb (She sings happily).

Adjective

  • before a noun: a slow car
  • after be / seem / look / feel: The car is slow.

Adverb

  • often made by adding -ly: slow → slowly: He drives slowly.
  • tells us how, when or where: quickly, today, here.
Adjectives do not change for plural: two big cars (not "bigs"). Adverbs usually come after the verb or at the end: She speaks English well.

Прилагательное описывает существительное (a happy child). Наречие описывает глагол, прилагательное или другое наречие (She sings happily).

Прилагательное

  • перед существительным: a slow car
  • после be / seem / look / feel: The car is slow.

Наречие

  • часто образуется добавлением -ly: slow → slowly: He drives slowly.
  • говорит как, когда или где: quickly, today, here.
Прилагательные не меняются по числу: two big cars (не «bigs»). Наречия обычно стоят после глагола или в конце: She speaks English well.

Sifat otni tavsiflaydi (a happy child). Ravish fe'lni, sifatni yoki boshqa ravishni tavsiflaydi (She sings happily).

Sifat

  • otdan oldin: a slow car
  • be / seem / look / feel dan keyin: The car is slow.

Ravish

  • ko'pincha -ly qo'shib yasaladi: slow → slowly: He drives slowly.
  • qanday, qachon yoki qayerda ekanini bildiradi: quickly, today, here.
Sifatlar ko'plikda o'zgarmaydi: two big cars ("bigs" emas). Ravishlar odatda fe'ldan keyin yoki gap oxirida keladi: She speaks English well.

Teacher's rule

AdjectiveAdverb
quickquickly
happyhappily
carefulcarefully
goodwell
fastfast (same)
hardhard (same)
  1. Adjectives describe nouns; adverbs describe verbs.
  2. Most adverbs = adjective + -ly: quick → quickly.
  3. Adjectives ending in consonant + y → change y to i then -ly: happy → happily.
  4. Irregular: good → well.
  5. Some words are the same as adjective and adverb: fast, hard, late, early.
  6. Be careful: hard = with effort; hardly = almost not.
  7. After be / look / feel / taste / seem use an adjective: She looks happy.
  1. Прилагательные описывают существительные; наречия описывают глаголы.
  2. Большинство наречий = прилагательное + -ly: quick → quickly.
  3. Прилагательные на согласную + y → меняем y на i, затем -ly: happy → happily.
  4. Неправильно: good → well.
  5. Некоторые слова одинаковы как прилагательное и наречие: fast, hard, late, early.
  6. Внимание: hard = с усилием; hardly = почти не.
  7. После be / look / feel / taste / seem используем прилагательное: She looks happy.
  1. Sifatlar otlarni; ravishlar fe'llarni tavsiflaydi.
  2. Ko'p ravishlar = sifat + -ly: quick → quickly.
  3. Undosh + y bilan tugagan sifatlar → y ni i ga o'zgartirib, -ly: happy → happily.
  4. Noto'g'ri: good → well.
  5. Ba'zi so'zlar sifat ham, ravish ham bo'ladi: fast, hard, late, early.
  6. Ehtiyot bo'ling: hard = kuch bilan; hardly = deyarli emas.
  7. be / look / feel / taste / seem dan keyin sifat keladi: She looks happy.

Student explanation

Ask yourself one question

What does the word describe — a noun or a verb? A noun → adjective. A verb → adverb.

Common mistakes

  • She sings good. She sings well.
  • He is a very quickly runner. He is a very quick runner. / He runs very quickly.
  • She drives careful. She drives carefully.
  • They work hardly. They work hard.
If the word describes a verb, it is almost always an adverb ending in -ly (but good → well).

Задай себе один вопрос

Что описывает слово — существительное или глагол? Существительное → прилагательное. Глагол → наречие.

Частые ошибки

  • She sings good. She sings well.
  • He is a very quickly runner. He is a very quick runner. / He runs very quickly.
  • She drives careful. She drives carefully.
  • They work hardly. They work hard.
Если слово описывает глагол, это почти всегда наречие на -ly (но good → well).

O'zingizga bitta savol bering

So'z nimani tavsiflaydi — otni yoki fe'lni? Ot → sifat. Fe'l → ravish.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She sings good. She sings well.
  • He is a very quickly runner. He is a very quick runner. / He runs very quickly.
  • She drives careful. She drives carefully.
  • They work hardly. They work hard.
Agar so'z fe'lni tavsiflasa, bu deyarli har doim -ly bilan tugagan ravish (lekin good → well).

Real-life use

You use adjectives to describe and adverbs to say how someone does something.

Describing people

"My sister is very kind and she speaks softly."

Describing food

"This soup tastes delicious."

At work

"She works hard and always answers quickly."

Driving advice

"Please drive carefully; the road is wet."

IELTS Speaking: a person you admire

"My father is a calm person. He listens carefully and speaks slowly."

English only

Examples and drill

Adjective before a noun

a beautiful song, a careful driver, a fast car.

Adjective after "be"

The song is beautiful. She is a pretty girl. Wait: She is kind.

Adverb after a verb

He runs fast. She sings beautifully. They work hard.

Irregular

good → well, fast → fast, hard → hard.
