Future: arrangements and will

General info

English has several ways to talk about the future. Here are two. Present Continuous is for arrangements: plans already agreed with other people, usually with a time or place. will is for decisions made now, offers, promises and opinions about the future.

We use them for

  • Arrangements (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Decisions made now (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Offers and promises (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Predictions and opinions (will): I think it will be sunny.

Time words that go with them

tomorrow, tonight, next week, on Friday, soon, I think, probably, maybe.

Russian uses one future (я буду). English must choose: agreed plan = Present Continuous, decision now = will.

В английском несколько способов говорить о будущем. Вот два. Present Continuous — для договорённостей: планов, уже согласованных с другими людьми, обычно со временем или местом. will — для решений, принятых сейчас, предложений, обещаний и мнений о будущем.

Когда используем

  • Договорённости (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Решения сейчас (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Предложения и обещания (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Прогнозы и мнения (will): I think it will be sunny.

Слова-подсказки

В русском одно будущее время (я буду). В английском нужно выбирать: договорённость = Present Continuous, решение сейчас = will.

Inglizchada kelajak haqida gapirishning bir necha yo'li bor. Mana ikkitasi. Present Continuous — kelishuvlar uchun: boshqalar bilan kelishilgan, odatda vaqti yoki joyi bor rejalar. will — hozir qilingan qarorlar, takliflar, va'dalar hamda kelajak haqidagi fikrlar uchun.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Kelishuvlar (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Hozirgi qarorlar (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Taklif va va'dalar (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Bashorat va fikrlar (will): I think it will be sunny.

Teacher's rule

Arrangementwill
+I'm meeting Anna at 6.I'll help you.
−I'm not meeting Anna.I won't help you.
?Are you meeting Anna?Will you help me?
  1. Arrangement: am / is / are + verb-ing, with a time or place, agreed with others: We're having dinner at 8.
  2. will + base verb: the same for every person, no -s: she will come. Short form: I'll, she'll, they'll.
  3. Negative: will not = won't.
  4. Question: Will + subject + base verb: Will you help me? Short answers: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
  5. Choosing: will for decisions made now, offers, promises and opinions (I think...). going to for plans made earlier.
  6. Word order with probably: after will (She will probably pass) but before won't (He probably won't come).

Three futures side by side

English has no single future tense. Choose the form by asking: is it fixed with other people, is it my plan, or am I deciding now?

FormWhenExample
Present Continuousan arrangement: a time is fixed, other people knowI'm seeing the dentist at 3 tomorrow.
going toa plan or intention you have already decidedI'm going to look for a new job.
willa decision now, an offer, a promise, an opinionI'll carry that for you. I won't tell anyone.

Shall I…? Shall we…?

To offer help or make a suggestion, ask with shall (only with I and we): Shall I open the window? Shall we go now? Answers: Yes, please. / No, thanks. / Good idea.

won't can also mean "refuses to": My little brother won't eat vegetables. The car won't start.
  1. Договорённость: am / is / are + глагол-ing, со временем или местом, согласовано с другими: We're having dinner at 8.
  2. will + глагол: одинаково для всех лиц, без -s: she will come. Краткая форма: I'll, she'll, they'll.
  3. Отрицание: will not = won't.
  4. Вопрос: Will + подлежащее + глагол: Will you help me? Краткие ответы: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
  5. Выбор: will для решений сейчас, предложений, обещаний и мнений (I think...). going to для планов, сделанных раньше.
  6. Порядок слов с probably: после will (She will probably pass), но перед won't (He probably won't come).

Shall I…? Shall we…?

Чтобы предложить помощь или что-то сделать вместе, спрашиваем с shall (только с I и we): Shall I open the window? Shall we go now? Ответы: Yes, please. / No, thanks. / Good idea.

won't может значить «отказывается»: My little brother won't eat vegetables. The car won't start.
  1. Kelishuv: am / is / are + fe'l-ing, vaqt yoki joy bilan, boshqalar bilan kelishilgan: We're having dinner at 8.
  2. will + fe'l: barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil, -s yo'q: she will come. Qisqa shakl: I'll, she'll, they'll.
  3. Inkor: will not = won't.
  4. So'roq: Will + ega + fe'l: Will you help me? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
  5. Tanlash: hozirgi qarorlar, takliflar, va'dalar va fikrlar (I think...) uchun will. Oldin qilingan rejalar uchun going to.
  6. probably bilan so'z tartibi: will dan keyin (She will probably pass), lekin won't dan oldin (He probably won't come).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Did I agree this with someone, with a time or place? Yes → Present Continuous.
  2. Am I deciding now, offering or promising? Yes → will.
  3. What comes after will? The base verb (action word). No -s and no to.

Common mistakes

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
A fixed plan with a time and a person = -ing. A decision right now = will.

In plain words

Think of two things: your diary and a sudden idea. If it is already in your diary with a time, use am, is or are + -ing. If you decide right now, use will.

  • I'm meeting Anna at 6. It is in the diary. Anna knows too.
  • The phone is ringing. I'll answer it! You decide at this moment.
  • I think it will rain. This is only your opinion about the future.

Will is also for promises: "I'll call you tonight."

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я договорился с кем-то, есть время или место? Да → Present Continuous.
  2. Я решаю сейчас, предлагаю или обещаю? Да → will.
  3. Что после will? Начальная форма глагола (это слово-действие). Без -s и без to.

Частые ошибки

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
Твёрдый план со временем и человеком = -ing. Решение прямо сейчас = will.

Простыми словами

Представьте две вещи: ваш ежедневник и внезапную идею. Если это уже в ежедневнике со временем, используйте am, is или are + -ing. Если решаете прямо сейчас, используйте will.

  • I'm meeting Anna at 6. Я встречаюсь с Анной в 6. Это записано, Анна тоже знает.
  • The phone is ringing. I'll answer it! Телефон звонит. Я отвечу! Решение прямо сейчас.
  • I think it will rain. Думаю, будет дождь. Это лишь ваше мнение о будущем.

Will также для обещаний: "I'll call you tonight."

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Kimdir bilan kelishganmanmi, vaqti yoki joyi bormi? Ha → Present Continuous.
  2. Hozir qaror qilyapmanmi, taklif yoki va'da beryapmanmi? Ha → will.
  3. will dan keyin nima? Fe'lning (harakat so'zi) boshlang'ich shakli. -s ham, to ham yo'q.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
Vaqti va odami bor qat'iy reja = -ing. Aynan hozirgi qaror = will.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Ikki narsani tasavvur qiling: kundaligingiz va to'satdan kelgan fikr. Agar vaqti bilan kundalikka yozilgan bo'lsa, am, is yoki are + -ing ishlating. Hozir qaror qilsangiz, will ishlating.

  • I'm meeting Anna at 6. Soat 6 da Anna bilan uchrashaman. Bu yozilgan, Anna ham biladi.
  • The phone is ringing. I'll answer it! Telefon jiringlayapti. Men javob beraman! Qaror hozir qilindi.
  • I think it will rain. Menimcha, yomg'ir yog'adi. Bu kelajak haqidagi fikringiz, xolos.

Will va'da uchun ham ishlatiladi: "I'll call you tonight."

Real-life use

You use these two forms when you make plans or offer help.

Make plans

"I'm meeting my friends at 7 tonight. Are you free on Saturday?"

Help people

"That looks heavy. I'll carry it for you."

Promises

"I'll call you when I arrive. I won't forget."

In a cafe

"I'll have the soup, please." (you decide while speaking)

IELTS Speaking: what you think about the future

"I think computers will change our schools. I don't think it will be easy."
