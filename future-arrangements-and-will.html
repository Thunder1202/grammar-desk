Future: arrangements and will

General info

English has several ways to talk about the future. Here are two. Present Continuous is for arrangements: plans already agreed with other people, usually with a time or place. will is for decisions made now, offers, promises and opinions about the future.

We use them for

  • Arrangements (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Decisions made now (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Offers and promises (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Predictions and opinions (will): I think it will be sunny.

Time words that go with them

tomorrow, tonight, next week, on Friday, soon, I think, probably, maybe.

Russian uses one future (я буду). English must choose: agreed plan = Present Continuous, decision now = will.

В английском несколько способов говорить о будущем. Вот два. Present Continuous — для договорённостей: планов, уже согласованных с другими людьми, обычно со временем или местом. will — для решений, принятых сейчас, предложений, обещаний и мнений о будущем.

Когда используем

  • Договорённости (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Решения сейчас (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Предложения и обещания (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Прогнозы и мнения (will): I think it will be sunny.

Слова-подсказки

tomorrow, tonight, next week, on Friday, soon, I think, probably, maybe.

В русском одно будущее время (я буду). В английском нужно выбирать: договорённость = Present Continuous, решение сейчас = will.

Inglizchada kelajak haqida gapirishning bir necha yo'li bor. Mana ikkitasi. Present Continuous — kelishuvlar uchun: boshqalar bilan kelishilgan, odatda vaqti yoki joyi bor rejalar. will — hozir qilingan qarorlar, takliflar, va'dalar hamda kelajak haqidagi fikrlar uchun.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Kelishuvlar (Present Continuous): I'm meeting Anna at 6 tomorrow. We're flying to Dubai on Friday.
  • Hozirgi qarorlar (will): "It's cold." "I'll close the window."
  • Taklif va va'dalar (will): I'll help you. I'll call you tonight.
  • Bashorat va fikrlar (will): I think it will be sunny.

Yordamchi so'zlar

  1. Arrangement: am / is / are + verb-ing, with a time or place, agreed with others: We're having dinner at 8.
  2. will + base verb: the same for every person, no -s: she will come. Short form: I'll, she'll, they'll.
  3. Negative: will not = won't.
  4. Question: Will + subject + base verb: Will you help me? Short answers: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
  5. Choosing: will for decisions made now, offers, promises and opinions (I think...). going to for plans made earlier.
  6. Word order with probably: after will (She will probably pass) but before won't (He probably won't come).

  3. Отрицание: will not = won't.
  4. Вопрос: Will + подлежащее + глагол: Will you help me? Краткие ответы: Yes, I will. / No, I won't.
  5. Выбор: will для решений сейчас, предложений, обещаний и мнений (I think...). going to для планов, сделанных раньше.
  6. Порядок слов с probably: после will (She will probably pass), но перед won't (He probably won't come).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Did I agree this with someone, with a time or place? Yes → Present Continuous.
  2. Am I deciding now, offering or promising? Yes → will.
  3. What comes after will? The base verb. No -s and no to.

Common mistakes

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
A fixed plan with a time and a person = -ing. A decision right now = will.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я договорился с кем-то, есть время или место? Да → Present Continuous.
  2. Я решаю сейчас, предлагаю или обещаю? Да → will.
  3. Что после will? Начальная форма глагола. Без -s и без to.

Частые ошибки

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
Твёрдый план со временем и человеком = -ing. Решение прямо сейчас = will.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Kimdir bilan kelishganmanmi, vaqti yoki joyi bormi? Ha → Present Continuous.
  2. Hozir qaror qilyapmanmi, taklif yoki va'da beryapmanmi? Ha → will.
  3. will dan keyin nima? Fe'lning boshlang'ich shakli. -s ham, to ham yo'q.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I will to help you. I will help you.
  • She will comes. She will come.
  • Do you will come? Will you come?
  • I will meeting Anna tomorrow. I'm meeting Anna tomorrow.
Vaqti va odami bor qat'iy reja = -ing. Aynan hozirgi qaror = will.

Real-life use

You switch between these two forms whenever you make plans or offer help.

Making plans

"I'm meeting my friends at 7 tonight. Are you free on Saturday?"

Offering help

"That looks heavy. I'll carry it for you."

Promises

"I'll call you when I arrive. I won't forget."

Ordering in a cafe

"I'll have the soup, please." (you decide while speaking)

IELTS Speaking: opinions about the future

"I think technology will change education. I don't think it will be easy."
