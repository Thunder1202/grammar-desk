Indefinite pronouns

General info

Indefinite pronouns are words that do not name an exact person, thing or place. We make them from some-, any-, no- and every- plus -thing, -body / -one or -where.

The four groups

  • some- (in positive sentences): something, someone / somebody, somewhere
  • any- (in negatives and questions): anything, anyone / anybody, anywhere
  • no- (negative meaning): nothing, no one / nobody, nowhere
  • every- (all of them): everything, everyone / everybody, everywhere

We use them for

  • People: Someone is at the door.
  • Things: I need something to eat.
  • Places: Let's go somewhere quiet.
The rule is the same as some / any: use some- in positive sentences, any- in negatives and questions. These words are singular: Everyone is ready.

Неопределённые местоимения — это слова, которые не называют точного человека, предмет или место. Они образуются от some-, any-, no- и every- плюс -thing, -body / -one или -where.

Четыре группы

  • some- (в утверждениях): something, someone / somebody, somewhere
  • any- (в отрицаниях и вопросах): anything, anyone / anybody, anywhere
  • no- (отрицательное значение): nothing, no one / nobody, nowhere
  • every- (всё / все): everything, everyone / everybody, everywhere

Когда используем

  • Люди: Someone is at the door.
  • Предметы: I need something to eat.
  • Места: Let's go somewhere quiet.
Правило то же, что у some / any: some- в утверждениях, any- в отрицаниях и вопросах. Эти слова стоят в единственном числе: Everyone is ready.

Noaniq olmoshlar — aniq bir shaxs, narsa yoki joyni bildirmaydigan so'zlar. Ular some-, any-, no- va every- ga -thing, -body / -one yoki -where qo'shilib yasaladi.

To'rt guruh

  • some- (darak gaplarda): something, someone / somebody, somewhere
  • any- (inkor va so'roq gaplarda): anything, anyone / anybody, anywhere
  • no- (inkor ma'no): nothing, no one / nobody, nowhere
  • every- (hamma / hamma narsa): everything, everyone / everybody, everywhere

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Odamlar: Someone is at the door.
  • Narsalar: I need something to eat.
  • Joylar: Let's go somewhere quiet.
Qoida some / any bilan bir xil: darak gapda some-, inkor va so'roqda any-. Bu so'zlar birlikda keladi: Everyone is ready.

  2. any- in negative sentences: I don't know anyone here. There isn't anything in the fridge.
  3. any- in questions: Is there anything to drink? Did you go anywhere yesterday?
  4. some- in offers and requests (even in questions): Would you like something to drink? Can I ask you something?

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the sentence positive, negative or a question? Positive → some-. Negative or question → any-.
  2. Do I mean a person, a thing or a place? Person → -one / -body, thing → -thing, place → -where.
  3. Is the meaning already negative? Then use no- with a positive verb.

Common mistakes

  • I don't know nothing. I don't know anything. / I know nothing.
  • Everyone are happy. Everyone is happy.
  • Do you have something to eat? (a general question) Do you have anything to eat?
  • I have any money. I have some money.
English uses only one negative word in a sentence. "I don't know nothing" is a mistake.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Предложение утвердительное, отрицательное или вопрос? Утверждение → some-. Отрицание или вопрос → any-.
  2. О ком или о чём речь? Человек → -one / -body, предмет → -thing, место → -where.
  3. Смысл уже отрицательный? Тогда используй no- с положительным глаголом.

Частые ошибки

  • I don't know nothing. I don't know anything. / I know nothing.
  • Everyone are happy. Everyone is happy.
  • Do you have something to eat? (общий вопрос) Do you have anything to eat?
  • I have any money. I have some money.
В английском в предложении только одно отрицательное слово. «I don't know nothing» — ошибка.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Gap darak, inkor yoki so'roqmi? Darak → some-. Inkor yoki so'roq → any-.
  2. Odam, narsa yoki joyni nazarda tutyapmanmi? Odam → -one / -body, narsa → -thing, joy → -where.
  3. Ma'no allaqachon inkormi? Unda darak fe'l bilan no- ishlatiladi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I don't know nothing. I don't know anything. / I know nothing.
  • Everyone are happy. Everyone is happy.
  • Do you have something to eat? (umumiy so'roq) Do you have anything to eat?
  • I have any money. I have some money.
Inglizchada gapda faqat bitta inkor so'zi bo'ladi. "I don't know nothing" — xato.

Real-life use

You use these words every day when you don't know the exact person, thing or place.

On the phone

"Someone called you, but I didn't get a name."

Shopping

"I'm looking for something for my mum. Is there anything cheaper?"

At home

"There's nothing in the fridge. Let's go somewhere for dinner."

Travelling

"I don't know anyone in this city. Everything looks new to me."

IELTS Speaking: your free time

"I do something relaxing every weekend. I go somewhere quiet and I don't talk to anyone."
