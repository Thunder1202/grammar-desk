Past Simple

General info

Past Simple talks about actions and situations that are finished in the past. Regular verbs add -ed. Irregular verbs have their own past forms that you learn one by one. The form is the same for every person.

We use it for

  • Finished actions: I watched a film last night.
  • A series of actions (stories): She woke up, had breakfast and left.
  • Past habits and states that ended: We lived in Samarkand in 2015.

Time words that go with it

yesterday, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, when I was a child.

Uzbek adds -di (ishladim) and Russian changes the verb by gender (работал / работала). English has one form for everyone: worked.

Past Simple говорит о действиях и ситуациях, которые закончились в прошлом. Правильные глаголы получают -ed. У неправильных глаголов свои формы прошедшего времени, их учат по одной. Форма одинакова для всех лиц.

Когда используем

  • Законченные действия: I watched a film last night.
  • Цепочка действий (рассказ): She woke up, had breakfast and left.
  • Прошлые привычки и состояния, которые закончились: We lived in Samarkand in 2015.

Слова-подсказки

yesterday, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, when I was a child.

В русском глагол меняется по роду (работал / работала). В английском одна форма для всех: worked.

Past Simple o'tmishda tugagan ish va holatlar haqida gapiradi. Qoidali fe'llarga -ed qo'shiladi. Qoidasiz fe'llarning o'z o'tgan zamon shakllari bor, ularni birma-bir o'rganish kerak. Shakl barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Tugagan ishlar: I watched a film last night.
  • Ketma-ket ishlar (hikoya): She woke up, had breakfast and left.
  • Tugagan o'tmishdagi odat va holatlar: We lived in Samarkand in 2015.

Yordamchi so'zlar

yesterday, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020, when I was a child.

O'zbek tilida -di qo'shiladi (ishladim). Inglizchada hamma uchun bitta shakl: worked.

Teacher's rule

RegularIrregular
+I workedI went
−I didn't workI didn't go
?Did you work?Did you go?
  1. Affirmative: regular verb + -ed, irregular verb = its second form. The same for I, you, he, she, it, we, they.
  2. Negative: didn't + base verb: She didn't go.
  3. Question: Did + subject + base verb: Did he call? Short answers: Yes, I did. / No, he didn't.
  4. After did / didn't the main verb goes back to the base form.

Spelling of -ed

  • Most verbs: + ed (work → worked)
  • Ends in -e: + d (like → liked)
  • Consonant + y: y → ied (study → studied)
  • One syllable, consonant + vowel + consonant: double the last letter (stop → stopped)

Common irregular verbs

go → went, have → had, see → saw, do → did, get → got, make → made, come → came, take → took, eat → ate, buy → bought, write → wrote, say → said.

Pronouncing -ed

worked /t/, played /d/, wanted /id/.

  1. Утверждение: правильный глагол + -ed, неправильный = его вторая форма. Одинаково для I, you, he, she, it, we, they.
  2. Отрицание: didn't + глагол: She didn't go.
  3. Вопрос: Did + подлежащее + глагол: Did he call? Краткие ответы: Yes, I did. / No, he didn't.
  4. После did / didn't основной глагол возвращается в начальную форму.

Правописание -ed

  • Большинство глаголов: + ed (work → worked)
  • Оканчивается на -e: + d (like → liked)
  • Согласная + y: y → ied (study → studied)
  • Один слог, согласная + гласная + согласная: удваиваем последнюю (stop → stopped)

Частые неправильные глаголы

go → went, have → had, see → saw, do → did, get → got, make → made, come → came, take → took, eat → ate, buy → bought, write → wrote, say → said.

Произношение -ed

worked /t/, played /d/, wanted /id/.

  1. Darak gap: qoidali fe'l + -ed, qoidasiz fe'l = uning ikkinchi shakli. I, you, he, she, it, we, they uchun bir xil.
  2. Inkor: didn't + fe'l: She didn't go.
  3. So'roq: Did + ega + fe'l: Did he call? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I did. / No, he didn't.
  4. did / didn't dan keyin asosiy fe'l boshlang'ich shaklga qaytadi.

-ed yozilishi

  • Ko'pchilik fe'llar: + ed (work → worked)
  • -e bilan tugasa: + d (like → liked)
  • Undosh + y: y → ied (study → studied)
  • Bir bo'g'in, undosh + unli + undosh: oxirgi harf ikkilanadi (stop → stopped)

Ko'p ishlatiladigan qoidasiz fe'llar

go → went, have → had, see → saw, do → did, get → got, make → made, come → came, take → took, eat → ate, buy → bought, write → wrote, say → said.

-ed talaffuzi

worked /t/, played /d/, wanted /id/.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it finished, in the past? Yes → Past Simple.
  2. Positive sentence? Regular verb + -ed. Irregular verb: use its second form.
  3. Negative or question? Use did. The main verb goes back to the base form.

Common mistakes

  • She didn't went. She didn't go.
  • Did you watched it? Did you watch it?
  • I goed to school. I went to school.
  • He studyed all night. He studied all night.
Only one word shows the past: either did or the main verb. Never both.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это закончилось, в прошлом? Да → Past Simple.
  2. Утверждение? Правильный глагол + -ed. Неправильный: вторая форма.
  3. Отрицание или вопрос? Используй did. Основной глагол возвращается в начальную форму.

Частые ошибки

  • She didn't went. She didn't go.
  • Did you watched it? Did you watch it?
  • I goed to school. I went to school.
  • He studyed all night. He studied all night.
Прошедшее время показывает только одно слово: либо did, либо основной глагол. Никогда оба.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu tugaganmi, o'tmishdami? Ha → Past Simple.
  2. Darak gapmi? Qoidali fe'l + -ed. Qoidasiz fe'l: ikkinchi shakli.
  3. Inkor yoki so'roqmi? did ishlating. Asosiy fe'l boshlang'ich shaklga qaytadi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She didn't went. She didn't go.
  • Did you watched it? Did you watch it?
  • I goed to school. I went to school.
  • He studyed all night. He studied all night.
O'tgan zamonni faqat bitta so'z ko'rsatadi: yoki did, yoki asosiy fe'l. Hech qachon ikkalasi emas.

Real-life use

Past Simple is the tense of stories, news and answers about yesterday.

Talking about yesterday

"What did you do yesterday?" "I studied and met a friend."

Telling a story

"I woke up late, missed the bus and arrived at 9."

Job interviews

"I worked in sales for two years. I left because I wanted a new challenge."

Travel

"We visited Samarkand and took lots of photos."

IELTS Speaking

"Did you enjoy school?" "Yes, I did. I liked my teachers."
