Cohesive devices for writing

General info

Cohesion means how the parts of a text are connected so that the reader can follow it easily. At C1 level we use four main tools: reference, substitution, ellipsis and linking phrases.

Without and with cohesion

The company launched a new phone. The company's new phone sold well. The company was happy with the new phone's sales.

→ The company launched a new phone. It sold well, and this encouraged the firm to expand.

The four tools

  • Reference: it, they, this, that, such, the former, the latter.
  • Substitution: one / ones, do so, so, not.
  • Ellipsis: leaving out repeated words: Tom likes tea and Anna (likes) coffee.
  • Linking phrases: however, as a result, moreover, in contrast.
Russian and Uzbek also use pronouns and linking words (этот, таким образом / bu, shuning uchun). English writers often use this + noun (this trend, this decision) to sum up an idea. Learn to use it.

Когезия (cohesion) — это то, как части текста связаны между собой, чтобы читателю было легко следить за мыслью. На уровне C1 мы используем четыре основных инструмента: ссылки, замену, пропуск слов и связующие фразы.

Без связности и со связностью

The company launched a new phone. The company's new phone sold well. The company was happy with the new phone's sales.

→ The company launched a new phone. It sold well, and this encouraged the firm to expand.

Четыре инструмента

  • Ссылка: it, they, this, that, such, the former, the latter.
  • Замена: one / ones, do so, so, not.
  • Пропуск слов: не повторяем слова: Tom likes tea and Anna (likes) coffee.
  • Связующие фразы: however, as a result, moreover, in contrast.
В русском и узбекском тоже есть местоимения и связующие слова (этот, таким образом / bu, shuning uchun). В английском часто используют this + существительное (this trend, this decision), чтобы подытожить мысль. Научись этому.

Kogeziya (cohesion) — matn qismlari o'quvchi fikrni oson kuzatishi uchun qanday bog'langanligi. C1 darajada to'rtta asosiy vositadan foydalanamiz: ishora, almashtirish, so'z tushirish va bog'lovchi iboralar.

Bog'lanishsiz va bog'lanish bilan

The company launched a new phone. The company's new phone sold well. The company was happy with the new phone's sales.

→ The company launched a new phone. It sold well, and this encouraged the firm to expand.

To'rtta vosita

  • Ishora: it, they, this, that, such, the former, the latter.
  • Almashtirish: one / ones, do so, so, not.
  • So'z tushirish: takroriy so'zlarni tushirib qoldirish: Tom likes tea and Anna (likes) coffee.
  • Bog'lovchi iboralar: however, as a result, moreover, in contrast.
Rus va o'zbek tilida ham olmosh va bog'lovchi so'zlar bor (этот, таким образом / bu, shuning uchun). Ingliz tilida fikrni jamlash uchun ko'pincha this + ot (this trend, this decision) ishlatiladi. Buni o'rganing.

  1. Clear reference: every it, this or they must point to something clear. If the reader can choose two things, name it: this trend, this decision, such measures.
  2. this / such + noun: it sums up an idea and links to the next one: Prices rose. This increase hurt families. Use the former / the latter (formal) for two items: the former = the first, the latter = the second.
  3. Substitution: one / ones replace a countable noun (the blue one). Do not use one after my, your, his: say my brother's, not my one. do so replaces a verb phrase (formal): he did so. so / not replace a clause after think, hope, expect, believe: I think so. I hope not.
  4. Ellipsis: after and, but or or you can leave out a repeated subject or verb: She opened the door and walked in. Tom likes tea and Anna coffee. Do not leave out so much that the meaning is unclear.
  5. Position and punctuation: put a comma after a linking phrase at the start: Moreover, ... Before however use a semicolon: ...; however, ... In the middle use commas: The plan, however, failed.
  6. Choose by meaning: moreover / furthermore add a stronger point. However / nevertheless contrast. As a result / consequently show a result. Do not use however when you mean and.
  7. Lexical cohesion: vary words to avoid repetition (rise, increase, growth), but repeat key terms when clarity matters. Synonyms must keep the same meaning.
  8. Do not overuse: not every sentence needs a link. Too many sound mechanical. Put known information first and new information at the end of the sentence, and the text will flow.
  1. Ясная ссылка: каждое it, this или they должно указывать на что-то ясное. Если читатель может выбрать из двух вещей, назови: this trend, this decision, such measures.
  2. this / such + существительное: подытоживает мысль и связывает со следующей: Prices rose. This increase hurt families. The former / the latter (формально) — для двух предметов: the former = первое, the latter = второе.
  3. Замена: one / ones заменяют исчисляемое существительное (the blue one). После my, your, his one не используем: my brother's, не my one. do so заменяет глагольную группу (формально): he did so. so / not заменяют придаточное после think, hope, expect, believe: I think so. I hope not.
  4. Пропуск слов: после and, but или or можно опустить повторяющееся подлежащее или глагол: She opened the door and walked in. Tom likes tea and Anna coffee. Не опускай столько, чтобы смысл стал неясным.
  5. Место и пунктуация: после связующей фразы в начале ставим запятую: Moreover, ... Перед however — точка с запятой: ...; however, ... В середине — запятые: The plan, however, failed.
  6. Выбирай по смыслу: moreover / furthermore добавляют более сильный довод. However / nevertheless — противопоставление. As a result / consequently — результат. Не используй however, когда имеешь в виду «и».
  7. Лексическая связность: меняй слова, чтобы не повторяться (rise, increase, growth), но повторяй ключевые термины, когда нужна ясность. Синонимы должны сохранять смысл.
  8. Не переусердствуй: не каждому предложению нужна связка. Слишком много звучит механически. Ставь известную информацию в начало, а новую в конец предложения, и текст потечёт.
  1. Aniq ishora: har bir it, this yoki they aniq narsaga ishora qilishi kerak. O'quvchi ikki narsadan birini tanlashi mumkin bo'lsa, nomini ayting: this trend, this decision, such measures.
  2. this / such + ot: fikrni jamlaydi va keyingisiga bog'laydi: Prices rose. This increase hurt families. Ikki narsa uchun the former / the latter (rasmiy): the former = birinchisi, the latter = ikkinchisi.
  3. Almashtirish: one / ones sanaladigan otni almashtiradi (the blue one). my, your, his dan keyin one ishlatilmaydi: my brother's deng, my one emas. do so fe'l birikmasini almashtiradi (rasmiy): he did so. so / not think, hope, expect, believe dan keyin gapni almashtiradi: I think so. I hope not.
  4. So'z tushirish: and, but yoki or dan keyin takroriy ega yoki fe'lni tushirish mumkin: She opened the door and walked in. Tom likes tea and Anna coffee. Ma'no tushunarsiz bo'lib qolmasligi uchun juda ko'p tushirmang.
  5. Joyi va tinish belgilari: boshdagi bog'lovchi iboradan keyin vergul qo'yiladi: Moreover, ... however dan oldin nuqtali vergul: ...; however, ... O'rtada vergullar: The plan, however, failed.
  6. Ma'noga qarab tanlang: moreover / furthermore kuchliroq dalil qo'shadi. However / nevertheless qarama-qarshilikni bildiradi. As a result / consequently natijani ko'rsatadi. «va» demoqchi bo'lsangiz however ishlatmang.
  7. Leksik bog'lanish: takrorlamaslik uchun so'zlarni almashtiring (rise, increase, growth), lekin aniqlik muhim bo'lsa asosiy atamalarni takrorlang. Sinonimlar ma'noni saqlashi kerak.
  8. Haddan oshirmang: har bir gapga bog'lovchi kerak emas. Juda ko'p bo'lsa mexanik eshitiladi. Ma'lum ma'lumotni boshiga, yangisini gap oxiriga qo'ying, matn silliq oqadi.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Am I repeating the same word or phrase? Yes → use it, this + noun, one or ellipsis.
  2. Is the link between two ideas clear? No → add a linking phrase: moreover, however, as a result.
  3. Will the reader know what "this" means? Not sure → use this + noun: this trend, this decision.

Build it in 3 steps

Many people work from home. Working from home saves time. Working from home can cause loneliness. 1) Replace the repetition: This arrangement saves time. 2) Show the relationship: the second point is a contrast, so use however. 3) Join the sentences. → Many people work from home. This arrangement saves time; however, it can also cause loneliness.

Why it works

Cohesive devices act like signposts. They show the reader where the text is going, so a long text stays clear and easy to follow.

Common mistakes

  • I like the red car, but my brother prefers the blue. I like the red car, but my brother prefers the blue one.
  • I think it so. I think so.
  • She asked him to leave, and he did it so. She asked him to leave, and he did so.
  • The plan was cheap. However, it was also fast. The plan was cheap. Moreover, it was also fast.
A link tells the reader how two ideas relate. Choose it by meaning (addition, contrast, result), not by habit.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я повторяю одно и то же слово или фразу? Да → используй it, this + существительное, one или пропуск слов.
  2. Связь между двумя мыслями ясна? Нет → добавь связующую фразу: moreover, however, as a result.
  3. Поймёт ли читатель, что значит «this»? Не уверен → используй this + существительное: this trend, this decision.

Строим за 3 шага

Many people work from home. Working from home saves time. Working from home can cause loneliness. 1) Замени повтор: This arrangement saves time. 2) Покажи отношение: вторая мысль — противопоставление, значит however. 3) Соедини предложения. → Many people work from home. This arrangement saves time; however, it can also cause loneliness.

Почему это работает

Средства связности работают как указатели. Они показывают читателю, куда идёт текст, поэтому длинный текст остаётся ясным и лёгким для чтения.

Частые ошибки

  • I like the red car, but my brother prefers the blue. I like the red car, but my brother prefers the blue one.
  • I think it so. I think so.
  • She asked him to leave, and he did it so. She asked him to leave, and he did so.
  • The plan was cheap. However, it was also fast. The plan was cheap. Moreover, it was also fast.
Связка показывает читателю, как связаны две мысли. Выбирай её по смыслу (добавление, противопоставление, результат), а не по привычке.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bir xil so'z yoki iborani takrorlayapmanmi? Ha → it, this + ot, one yoki so'z tushirishdan foydalaning.
  2. Ikki fikr orasidagi bog'lanish aniqmi? Yo'q → bog'lovchi ibora qo'shing: moreover, however, as a result.
  3. O'quvchi «this» nimaligini biladimi? Ishonchim komil emas → this + ot ishlating: this trend, this decision.

Real-life use

Cohesive devices make long texts clear. They matter in exams, reports, emails and presentations.

IELTS Writing

Examiners score coherence and cohesion. "Many people prefer cities. This trend is growing; however, rural life still attracts some families. As a result, governments must plan for both."

Academic essays

"Pollution has increased sharply. Such a rise is a serious concern. Moreover, the former problem (noise) is also growing, while the latter (waste) is easier to solve."

Reports and emails

"The first option is cheaper; the second is faster. The former suits small teams, the latter large ones. For this reason, we recommend the second."

Presentations

"Moving on to the next point ... As I mentioned earlier ... In conclusion ..."

In speaking

Keep it light: "Actually, ..." "Besides, ..." "That's why ..." "Anyway, ..."

Examples and drill

Reference

Sales fell. This worried investors. Prices rose sharply. This increase hurt families. Tom and Anna came; the former was late.

Substitution

I like the red car; my brother prefers the blue one. She asked him to leave, and he did so. Will it rain? I think so. I hope not.

Ellipsis

Tom likes tea and Anna coffee. She opened the door and walked in. I'd like to help, but I can't.

Linking phrases

Addition: moreover, in addition. Contrast: however, in contrast, nevertheless. Result: as a result, consequently. Example: for instance. Summary: in conclusion, overall.

A full paragraph

Many people work from home. This arrangement saves time; however, it can also cause loneliness. As a result, some companies now offer office days. Overall, flexibility seems to work best.
