Question forms

General info

Questions ask for information. There are two main types: yes / no questions, which are answered with yes or no, and Wh- questions, which start with what, where, who, when, why or how. In English the word order changes: a helper verb (be, can, do, does, did) comes first.

We use them for

  • Yes / no questions: Do you like tea? Is she at home?
  • Wh- questions: Where do you live? What is your name?
  • Subject questions (who or what is the subject): Who lives here? What happened?
Russian and Uzbek keep the statement order and use intonation or a particle (Siz ishlaysizmi?). English changes the word order and needs do / does / did or be.

Вопросы запрашивают информацию. Есть два основных типа: общие вопросы (yes / no), на которые отвечают «да» или «нет», и специальные вопросы (Wh-), которые начинаются с what, where, who, when, why или how. В английском меняется порядок слов: вспомогательный глагол (be, can, do, does, did) стоит первым.

Когда используем

  • Общие вопросы: Do you like tea? Is she at home?
  • Специальные вопросы: Where do you live? What is your name?
  • Вопросы к подлежащему (who или what — подлежащее): Who lives here? What happened?
В русском порядок слов как в утверждении, вопрос выражает интонация (Ты работаешь?). В английском меняется порядок слов и нужны do / does / did или be.

Savollar ma'lumot so'raydi. Ikki asosiy turi bor: yes / no savollar (ha yoki yo'q deb javob beriladi) va Wh- savollar (what, where, who, when, why yoki how bilan boshlanadi). Inglizchada so'z tartibi o'zgaradi: yordamchi fe'l (be, can, do, does, did) birinchi keladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Yes / no savollar: Do you like tea? Is she at home?
  • Wh- savollar: Where do you live? What is your name?
  • Ega haqidagi savollar (who yoki what ega bo'lsa): Who lives here? What happened?
O'zbek tilida so'z tartibi o'zgarmaydi, -mi yuklamasi yoki ohang ishlatiladi (Siz ishlaysizmi?). Inglizchada so'z tartibi o'zgaradi va do / does / did yoki be kerak.

Teacher's rule

Yes / noWh- question
beIs she a teacher?Where is she?
doDo you work?Where do you work?
didDid you go?Where did you go?
canCan you swim?How well can you swim?
  1. Yes / no: helper first: Are you...? Do you...? Did you...? Can you...?
  2. Wh- question: Wh-word + helper + subject + base verb: Where do you live?
  3. After do / does / did the main verb is in the base form: Where does she work?
  4. With be there is no do: Where is he? How old are you?
  5. Subject questions: when who or what is the subject, no do: Who lives here? What happened?
  6. Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, she isn't. / Yes, he did.

Question words

what (thing), where (place), when (time), who (person), why (reason), how (way), which (choice), whose (owner), how many / how much, how old, how often.

Intonation

The voice rises at the end of yes / no questions and falls at the end of Wh- questions.

  1. Общие вопросы: вспомогательный глагол первым: Are you...? Do you...? Did you...? Can you...?
  2. Специальные вопросы: Wh-слово + вспомогательный + подлежащее + глагол: Where do you live?
  3. После do / does / did основной глагол в начальной форме: Where does she work?
  4. С be do не нужен: Where is he? How old are you?
  5. Вопросы к подлежащему: когда who или what — подлежащее, do нет: Who lives here? What happened?
  6. Краткие ответы: Yes, I do. / No, she isn't. / Yes, he did.

Вопросительные слова

what (что), where (где), when (когда), who (кто), why (почему), how (как), which (какой из), whose (чей), how many / how much, how old, how often.

Интонация

В общих вопросах голос повышается в конце, в специальных понижается.

  1. Yes / no savollar: yordamchi fe'l birinchi: Are you...? Do you...? Did you...? Can you...?
  2. Wh- savollar: Wh-so'z + yordamchi + ega + fe'l: Where do you live?
  3. do / does / did dan keyin asosiy fe'l boshlang'ich shaklda: Where does she work?
  4. be bilan do kerak emas: Where is he? How old are you?
  5. Ega haqidagi savollar: who yoki what ega bo'lsa, do yo'q: Who lives here? What happened?
  6. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I do. / No, she isn't. / Yes, he did.

So'roq so'zlari

what (nima), where (qayerda), when (qachon), who (kim), why (nega), how (qanday), which (qaysi), whose (kimning), how many / how much, how old, how often.

Ohang

Yes / no savollarda ovoz oxirida ko'tariladi, Wh- savollarda pasayadi.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is there a helper in the statement? (am, is, are, can, will) Put it first.
  2. No helper? Add do / does / did, and the main verb (action word) goes back to base form.
  3. Need a question word? Put the Wh-word before the helper.

Common mistakes

  • Where you live? Where do you live?
  • Does she works here? Does she work here?
  • What you are doing? What are you doing?
  • Who does live here? Who lives here?
Wh-word + helper + subject + verb. The helper always comes before the subject.

In plain words

In English, a question changes the order of the words. A small helper word jumps to the front to show that you are asking. It is like raising your hand first.

  • You are a student. → Are you a student? Are jumps to the front.
  • You like tea. → Do you like tea? There is no helper here, so we bring do.
  • Where do you live? The question word goes first, then the helper.

Simple order to remember: question word, helper, person, action.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Есть ли вспомогательный глагол (это слово-действие) (слово-помощник: do, have, be) в утверждении? (am, is, are, can, will) Поставь его первым.
  2. Нет? Добавь do / does / did, а основной глагол вернётся в начальную форму.
  3. Нужно вопросительное слово? Поставь Wh-слово перед вспомогательным.

Частые ошибки

  • Where you live? Where do you live?
  • Does she works here? Does she work here?
  • What you are doing? What are you doing?
  • Who does live here? Who lives here?
Wh-слово + вспомогательный + подлежащее + глагол. Вспомогательный всегда перед подлежащим.

Простыми словами

В английском вопрос меняет порядок слов. Маленькое слово-помощник прыгает вперёд и показывает, что вы спрашиваете. Как будто вы сначала поднимаете руку.

  • You are a student. → Are you a student? Ты студент? Are прыгает вперёд.
  • You like tea. → Do you like tea? Ты любишь чай? Помощника нет, поэтому берём do.
  • Where do you live? Где ты живёшь? Вопросительное слово первое, потом помощник.

Простой порядок: вопросительное слово, помощник, человек, действие.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Darak gapda yordamchi fe'l (harakat so'zi) (do, have, be kabi so'z) bormi? (am, is, are, can, will) Uni birinchi qo'ying.
  2. Yo'qmi? do / does / did qo'shing, asosiy fe'l esa boshlang'ich shaklga qaytadi.
  3. So'roq so'zi kerakmi? Wh-so'zni yordamchi fe'ldan oldin qo'ying.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • Where you live? Where do you live?
  • Does she works here? Does she work here?
  • What you are doing? What are you doing?
  • Who does live here? Who lives here?
Wh-so'z + yordamchi + ega + fe'l. Yordamchi fe'l har doim egadan oldin keladi.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Ingliz tilida so'roq gap so'zlar tartibini o'zgartiradi. Kichik yordamchi so'z oldinga sakraydi va so'rayotganingizni ko'rsatadi. Xuddi avval qo'l ko'targandek.

  • You are a student. → Are you a student? Sen talabamisan? Are oldinga sakraydi.
  • You like tea. → Do you like tea? Choy yoqtirasanmi? Yordamchi yo'q, shuning uchun do olamiz.
  • Where do you live? Qayerda yashaysan? So'roq so'zi birinchi, keyin yordamchi.

Oddiy tartib: so'roq so'zi, yordamchi, odam, harakat.

Real-life use

We often start to talk with a question, so you will use these every day.

Meeting someone new

"What's your name? Where are you from? What do you do?"

Shopping

"How much is this? Do you have it in blue?"

Travel

"What time does the train leave? Where is platform 2?"

Ask for help

"Can you repeat that, please? How do you spell your name?"

In class

"What does this word mean? How do you say this in English?"
