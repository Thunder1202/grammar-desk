Ellipsis and substitution

General info

Ellipsis means leaving out words that are easy to understand. Substitution means replacing words with a short form. Both make speech and writing more natural and less repetitive.

Ellipsis

  • (I'm) Sorry I'm late. / (Have you) Got a minute?
  • She can play the piano and he can (play the piano) too.
  • I asked him to help, but he didn't (help).

Substitution

  • so / not: I think so. I hope not.
  • do: He works harder than I do.
  • one / ones: I like the red one.
  • the same: My car is old; hers is the same.
Use ellipsis and substitution to avoid repetition. They are very common in natural spoken English and in C2-level writing.

Takrorni oldini olish uchun ellipsis va substitution ishlating. Ular tabiiy so'zlashuv nutqida va C2 darajadagi yozuvda juda keng tarqalgan.

Teacher's rule

DeviceReplacesExample
so / nota whole clauseI think so. I hope not.
do / does / dida verb phraseShe earns more than he does.
one / onesa nounthe tall one, the blue ones
the samea repeated phraseMine is the same.
  1. Ellipsis — leave out the auxiliary when it is understood: "Want a coffee?" (= Do you want a coffee?)
  2. Leave out a repeated verb after an auxiliary: I'll try, but I don't think I'll be able to (try).
  3. so / not replaces a clause after verbs like think, hope, expect, believe, suppose, be afraid: I think so. I'm afraid not.
  4. do / does / did substitutes a verb phrase: She sings better than he does.
  5. one / ones substitutes a noun: I'll take the small one.
  6. the same substitutes a repeated comparison: His car is old; mine is the same.
  7. Ellipsis is common in clauses joined by and / but / or and in comparisons.
  1. Эллипсис — опускаем вспомогательный глагол, когда он понятен: «Want a coffee?» (= Do you want a coffee?)
  2. Опускаем повторяющийся глагол после вспомогательного: I'll try, but I don't think I'll be able to (try).
  3. so / not заменяет целое предложение после глаголов think, hope, expect, believe, suppose, be afraid: I think so. I'm afraid not.
  4. do / does / did заменяет глагольную фразу: She sings better than he does.
  5. one / ones заменяет существительное: I'll take the small one.
  6. the same заменяет повторяющееся сравнение: His car is old; mine is the same.
  7. Эллипсис часто встречается в частях, соединённых and / but / or, и в сравнениях.
  1. Ellipsis — tushunarli bo'lsa, yordamchi fe'lni tushirib qoldiramiz: "Want a coffee?" (= Do you want a coffee?)
  2. Yordamchi fe'ldan keyin takrorlangan fe'lni tushiramiz: I'll try, but I don't think I'll be able to (try).
  3. so / not think, hope, expect, believe, suppose, be afraid kabi fe'llardan keyin butun gap o'rnini bosadi: I think so. I'm afraid not.
  4. do / does / did fe'l iborasini almashtiradi: She sings better than he does.
  5. one / ones otni almashtiradi: I'll take the small one.
  6. the same takrorlangan taqqoslashni almashtiradi: His car is old; mine is the same.
  7. Ellipsis and / but / or bilan bog'langan qismlarda va taqqoslashlarda keng tarqalgan.

Student explanation

Ask yourself: is the word repeated?

  1. Repeated verb phrase? → remove it (ellipsis) or use do.
  2. Repeated clause? → so / not.
  3. Repeated noun? → one / ones.

Common mistakes

  • She can play the piano and he can play the piano too. She can play the piano and he can too.
  • I'll drive if you want to drive. I'll drive if you want to.
  • Will it rain? I hope it doesn't rain. Will it rain? I hope not.
  • I like the red one shirt. I like the red one.
If you can understand the missing words from the context, you can usually leave them out.

Спроси себя: слово повторяется?

  1. Повторяется глагольная фраза? → убери её (эллипсис) или используй do.
  2. Повторяется целое предложение? → so / not.
  3. Повторяется существительное? → one / ones.

Частые ошибки

  • She can play the piano and he can play the piano too. She can play the piano and he can too.
  • I'll drive if you want to drive. I'll drive if you want to.
  • Will it rain? I hope it doesn't rain. Will it rain? I hope not.
  • I like the red one shirt. I like the red one.
Если пропущенные слова понятны из контекста, их обычно можно опустить.

O'zingizdan so'rang: so'z takrorlanyaptimi?

  1. Fe'l iborasi takrorlanyaptimi? → uni olib tashlang (ellipsis) yoki do ishlating.
  2. Butun gap takrorlanyaptimi? → so / not.
  3. Ot takrorlanyaptimi? → one / ones.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I'll drive if you want to drive. I'll drive if you want to.

Agar tushirilgan so'zlar kontekstdan tushunarli bo'lsa, ularni odatda tushirib qoldirsa bo'ladi.

Real-life use

Ellipsis and substitution make you sound fluent and help you write more elegantly.

Casual conversation

"Coming to the party?" "Maybe. Depends on work."

Agreeing and disagreeing

"It's going to rain." "I hope not."

Comparing

"I earn less than my brother does."

Shopping

"Which bag do you like?" "The black one."

IELTS Writing: avoiding repetition

"Some people prefer city life; others prefer the countryside, and many argue the same about work."
