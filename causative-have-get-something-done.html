Causative: have / get something done

General info

The causative (have / get + object + past participle) shows that someone else does something for us, or that something happens to us. We do not do the action ourselves.

We use it for

  • Services: I had my car repaired. I get my hair cut every month.
  • Arranging work to be done: We are having the house painted.
  • Unwanted events: She had her bag stolen.

Words that go with it

have / get + object + participle, by, at the salon, at the garage, every month.

Russian: я постригся (в парикмахерской) / Uzbek: sochimni oldirdim. English shows it with have / get + object + participle.

Causative (have / get + дополнение + третья форма) показывает, что кто-то другой делает что-то для нас или что-то с нами случается. Мы сами действие не выполняем.

Когда используем

  • Услуги: I had my car repaired. I get my hair cut every month.
  • Организация работы: We are having the house painted.
  • Нежелательные события: She had her bag stolen.

Слова-подсказки

Teacher's rule

TenseFormExample
present simplehave / has + object + participleI have my hair cut every month.
past simplehad + object + participleShe had her car repaired.
present continuousam / is / are having + object + participleHe's having his teeth checked.
present perfecthave had + object + participleI've had my phone fixed.
futurewill have + object + participleI'll have the house painted.
informalget + object + participleI got my hair cut.
  1. Form: have + object + past participle = someone else does a service for us: I had my car repaired.
  2. get is more informal: I got my phone fixed.
  3. Tense is shown by have / get. Questions and negatives use do / did: Where did you have your hair cut? I didn't have it cut.
  4. Compare: I repaired my car (I did it myself). / I had my car repaired (someone else did it).
  5. Active pattern: have someone + base verb (I had the mechanic repair it). get someone + to + verb (I got my brother to help).
  6. Unwanted events: the same pattern for bad experiences: She had her bag stolen. He had his phone taken.

Why not "I cut my hair"?

In Russian you can say «Я подстригся» even if a barber did it. In English, "I cut my hair" means you did it yourself. When someone else does the job for you, you need have / get + object + participle.

  2. get более разговорный: I got my phone fixed.
  3. Время выражается через have / get. Вопросы и отрицания с do / did: Where did you have your hair cut? I didn't have it cut.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Who does the action? Someone else → have / get + object + participle. I do it → normal active verb (action word).
  2. Is it a service or something bad that happened? Both use the same pattern.
  3. Which tense (verb form that shows time)? Put it in have / get: I'm having, I had, I've had, I'll have.

Compare

I cut my hair. (I used scissors.) / I had my hair cut. (A hairdresser cut it.)

Common mistakes

  • I cut my hair at the salon yesterday. I had my hair cut at the salon yesterday.
  • I had repaired my car by a mechanic. I had my car repaired by a mechanic.
  • She had her hair to cut. She had her hair cut.
  • He had stolen his wallet. (someone stole it) He had his wallet stolen.
The object (who or what gets the action) comes BEFORE the participle (form like done, seen, eaten): have my car repaired, not have repaired my car.

In plain words

Use this when you pay or ask someone else to do a job for you. You are the boss of the job, not the worker.

  • I cut my hair. I did it myself.
  • I had my hair cut. A hairdresser did it for me.
  • We need to get the car repaired. A mechanic will do it.

The order is important: have + the thing + done. "I had cut my hair" means something else.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Кто выполняет действие? Кто-то другой → have / get + дополнение + третья форма. Я сам → обычный глагол (это слово-действие).
  2. Это услуга или что-то плохое? Модель одна и та же.
  3. Какое время? Ставь его в have / get: I'm having, I had, I've had, I'll have.

Сравни

I cut my hair. (Сам ножницами.) / I had my hair cut. (Парикмахер подстриг.)

Частые ошибки

  • I cut my hair at the salon yesterday. I had my hair cut at the salon yesterday.
  • I had repaired my car by a mechanic. I had my car repaired by a mechanic.
  • She had her hair to cut. She had her hair cut.
  • He had stolen his wallet. (someone stole it) He had his wallet stolen.
Дополнение (на кого или что направлено действие) стоит ПЕРЕД третьей формой (форма вроде done, seen, eaten): have my car repaired, а не have repaired my car.

Простыми словами

Используйте это, когда платите или просите кого-то сделать работу за вас. Вы начальник этой работы, а не работник.

  • I cut my hair. Я подстригся сам.
  • I had my hair cut. Меня подстригли. Это сделал парикмахер.
  • We need to get the car repaired. Нам нужно отремонтировать машину. Это сделает механик.

Порядок важен: have + предмет + done. "I had cut my hair" значит другое.

Real-life use

We use the causative for services, home repairs, appointments and bad luck.

Services and repairs

"I need to get my laptop repaired. Where do you have your hair cut?"

Appointments

"I'm having my teeth checked on Friday. We're having the kitchen painted."

Bad experiences

"He had his phone stolen on the bus."

IELTS Speaking: talking about habits and plans

"I usually have my hair cut once a month, and I'm planning to have my room painted soon."
