Reported speech (full)

General info

Full reported speech goes beyond statements. We also report questions, commands and requests, and we use many reporting verbs such as suggest, advise, warn and promise.

We use it for

  • Reporting questions: She asked where I lived.
  • Reporting commands and requests: He told me to sit down. She asked me not to be late.
  • Reporting with special verbs: They suggested going out. He promised to call.

Words that go with it

asked, told, if, whether, to + verb, not to, suggested, advised, warned, promised.

Russian and Uzbek keep the question word order. English reports a question as a statement: where I lived, not where did I live.

Полная косвенная речь — это не только утверждения. Мы передаём также вопросы, приказы и просьбы и используем много глаголов речи: suggest, advise, warn, promise.

Когда используем

  • Передача вопросов: She asked where I lived.
  • Передача приказов и просьб: He told me to sit down. She asked me not to be late.
  • Особые глаголы речи: They suggested going out. He promised to call.

Слова-подсказки

asked, told, if, whether, to + глагол, not to, suggested, advised, warned, promised.

В русском и узбекском порядок слов вопроса сохраняется. В английском вопрос передаётся как утверждение: where I lived, а не where did I live.

To'liq ko'chirma gap faqat darak gaplar emas. Biz savollar, buyruq va iltimoslarni ham yetkazamiz va suggest, advise, warn, promise kabi ko'p gapirish fe'llarini ishlatamiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Savollarni yetkazish: She asked where I lived.
  • Buyruq va iltimoslarni yetkazish: He told me to sit down. She asked me not to be late.
  • Maxsus gapirish fe'llari: They suggested going out. He promised to call.

Yordamchi so'zlar

asked, told, if, whether, to + fe'l, not to, suggested, advised, warned, promised.

Rus va o'zbek tilida savol so'z tartibi saqlanadi. Inglizchada savol darak gap sifatida yetkaziladi: where I lived, where did I live emas.

  1. Questions: ask + (if / whether or a wh-word) + statement order. The tense moves back and there is no question mark: She asked where I lived. Not: asked where did I live.
  2. Yes / no questions use if or whether: He asked if I was ready.
  3. Commands and requests: told / asked / ordered + object + (not) to + verb: She told me to wait. He asked me not to be late.
  4. Modals: can → could, will → would, must → had to. Could, would, should and might do not change.
  5. Reporting verbs: suggest / admit / deny + -ing. Advise / warn / invite + object + to. Promise / offer / refuse / agree + to + verb.
  6. Time words change as before (tomorrow → the next day). No backshift if the reporting verb is present or the fact is still true.
  1. Вопросы: ask + (if / whether или вопросительное слово) + порядок слов утверждения. Время сдвигается назад, вопросительного знака нет: She asked where I lived. Не: asked where did I live.
  2. Вопросы да / нет с if или whether: He asked if I was ready.
  3. Приказы и просьбы: told / asked / ordered + дополнение + (not) to + глагол: She told me to wait. He asked me not to be late.
  4. Модальные: can → could, will → would, must → had to. Could, would, should и might не меняются.
  5. Глаголы речи: suggest / admit / deny + -ing. Advise / warn / invite + дополнение + to. Promise / offer / refuse / agree + to + глагол.
  6. Слова времени меняются как раньше (tomorrow → the next day). Без сдвига, если глагол речи в настоящем или факт ещё верен.
  1. Savollar: ask + (if / whether yoki so'roq so'z) + darak gap tartibi. Zamon orqaga suriladi, so'roq belgisi yo'q: She asked where I lived. asked where did I live emas.
  2. Ha / yo'q savollari if yoki whether bilan: He asked if I was ready.
  3. Buyruq va iltimoslar: told / asked / ordered + to'ldiruvchi + (not) to + fe'l: She told me to wait. He asked me not to be late.
  4. Modallar: can → could, will → would, must → had to. Could, would, should va might o'zgarmaydi.
  5. Gapirish fe'llari: suggest / admit / deny + -ing. Advise / warn / invite + to'ldiruvchi + to. Promise / offer / refuse / agree + to + fe'l.
  6. Vaqt so'zlari o'zgaradi avvalgidek (tomorrow → the next day). Gapirish fe'li hozirgi zamonda bo'lsa yoki fakt hali ham to'g'ri bo'lsa, surilish yo'q.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a question? Yes → ask + if / wh-word + statement order. No inversion, no do / does / did.
  2. Is it an order or a request? Yes → told / asked + object + to + verb. Negative → not to.
  3. Is there a special reporting verb? Check its pattern: -ing, object + to, or to + verb.

Step by step

"Where do you live?" she asked me. 1) asked. 2) where. 3) statement order: I live. 4) tense back: I lived. → She asked me where I lived.

Common mistakes

  • She asked where did I live. She asked where I lived.
  • He asked me do I like tea. He asked me if I liked tea.
  • She told me sit down. She told me to sit down.
  • He told me don't touch it. He told me not to touch it.
A reported question is never a question: no inversion, no question mark.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это вопрос? Да → ask + if / вопросительное слово + порядок утверждения. Без инверсии, без do / does / did.
  2. Это приказ или просьба? Да → told / asked + дополнение + to + глагол. Отрицание → not to.
  3. Есть особый глагол речи? Проверь модель: -ing, дополнение + to или to + глагол.

Шаг за шагом

"Where do you live?" she asked me. 1) asked. 2) where. 3) порядок утверждения: I live. 4) время назад: I lived. → She asked me where I lived.

Частые ошибки

  • She asked where did I live. She asked where I lived.
  • He asked me do I like tea. He asked me if I liked tea.
  • She told me sit down. She told me to sit down.
  • He told me don't touch it. He told me not to touch it.
Косвенный вопрос уже не вопрос: без инверсии, без вопросительного знака.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu savolmi? Ha → ask + if / so'roq so'z + darak gap tartibi. Inversiyasiz, do / does / did siz.
  2. Bu buyruq yoki iltimosmi? Ha → told / asked + to'ldiruvchi + to + fe'l. Inkor → not to.
  3. Maxsus gapirish fe'li bormi? Qolipini tekshiring: -ing, to'ldiruvchi + to yoki to + fe'l.

Qadamma-qadam

"Where do you live?" she asked me. 1) asked. 2) where. 3) darak gap tartibi: I live. 4) zamon orqaga: I lived. → She asked me where I lived.

Real-life use

We report questions, orders and advice when we tell others about interviews, doctors, teachers and bosses.

Job interview

"They asked me why I wanted the job. They asked if I had experience."

Doctor and instructions

"The doctor told me to rest and not to work for a week."

Reporting advice

"My teacher suggested practising speaking every day, and she advised me to read more."

IELTS Speaking: talking about advice

"My uncle warned me not to give up, and he promised to help me prepare."

Examples and drill

Wh- questions

"What time does it start?" → He asked what time it started.

Yes / no questions

"Did you see him?" → She asked if I had seen him.

Commands and requests

"Close the door." → He told me to close the door. "Please don't talk." → She asked us not to talk.

Reporting verbs

He promised to call. She advised me to rest. They suggested going out. He admitted breaking the window.
