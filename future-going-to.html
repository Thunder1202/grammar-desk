Future: going to

General info

going to talks about the future. We use it for plans and intentions that were decided before we speak, and for predictions when we can see evidence now.

We use it for

  • Plans and intentions: We're going to visit Samarkand next month.
  • Predictions with evidence: Look at those clouds. It's going to rain.
  • Things about to happen: Watch out! You're going to fall.

Time words that go with it

tomorrow, tonight, soon, next week, next year, this summer, in two days.

Russian says собираюсь and Uzbek says -moqchiman (bormoqchiman). English uses be + going to + verb.

going to говорит о будущем. Мы используем его для планов и намерений, решённых до разговора, и для прогнозов, когда сейчас видны признаки.

Когда используем

  • Планы и намерения: We're going to visit Samarkand next month.
  • Прогнозы по признакам: Look at those clouds. It's going to rain.
  • То, что вот-вот случится: Watch out! You're going to fall.

Teacher's rule

?Am I going to work?Is he going to work?Are you going to work?
  1. Form: am / is / are + going to + base verb.
  2. Negative: put not after be: She isn't going to come.
  3. Question: be goes first: Are you going to come? Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
  4. Two meanings: a plan decided before now, or a prediction based on what you see now.
  5. With go and come we often use Present Continuous instead: I'm going to the cinema (not "I'm going to go").

Spoken English

In fast speech, going to sounds like "gonna". Use it when speaking informally, but write going to.

  1. Форма: am / is / are + going to + глагол.
  2. Отрицание: not после be: She isn't going to come.
  3. Вопрос: be в начале: Are you going to come? Краткие ответы: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
  4. Два значения: план, решённый до сейчас, или прогноз по тому, что видно сейчас.
  5. С go и come часто используем Present Continuous: I'm going to the cinema (не «I'm going to go»).

Разговорная речь

В быстрой речи going to звучит как «gonna». В разговоре можно, но писать нужно going to.

  1. Shakl: am / is / are + going to + fe'l.
  2. Inkor: not be dan keyin: She isn't going to come.
  3. So'roq: be boshiga: Are you going to come? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
  4. Ikki ma'no: avval qaror qilingan reja yoki hozir ko'rinib turgan narsaga asoslangan bashorat.
  5. go va come bilan ko'pincha Present Continuous ishlatiladi: I'm going to the cinema ("I'm going to go" emas).

Og'zaki nutq

Tez gapirganda going to "gonna" deb eshitiladi. Og'zaki nutqda mumkin, lekin yozganda going to yoziladi.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Did I decide before now, or can I see evidence? Yes → going to.
  2. Who is it about? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. What comes after to? The base verb. No -s and no -ing.

Common mistakes

  • I going to call him. I'm going to call him.
  • She is going to works. She is going to work.
  • He doesn't going to come. He isn't going to come.
  • Do you going to travel? Are you going to travel?
Three parts, always together: be + going to + verb.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Я решил до сих пор или вижу признаки? Да → going to.
  2. О ком речь? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. Что после to? Начальная форма глагола. Без -s и без -ing.

Частые ошибки

  • I going to call him. I'm going to call him.
  • She is going to works. She is going to work.
  • He doesn't going to come. He isn't going to come.
  • Do you going to travel? Are you going to travel?
Три части всегда вместе: be + going to + глагол.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Oldin qaror qilganmanmi yoki dalilni ko'ryapmanmi? Ha → going to.
  2. Gap kim haqida? I → am. He, she, it → is. You, we, they → are.
  3. to dan keyin nima? Fe'lning boshlang'ich shakli. -s ham, -ing ham yo'q.

Real-life use

People use going to all the time to talk about plans and to warn or predict.

Talking about plans

"What are you going to do this weekend?" "I'm going to visit my grandparents."

Goals and resolutions

"I'm going to pass my exam this year."

Weather

"It's going to rain. Take an umbrella."

Warnings

"Careful! You're going to spill it."

IELTS Speaking

"What are you going to do after school?" "I'm going to study at university."
