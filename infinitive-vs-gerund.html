Infinitive vs gerund

General info

After some verbs we use the infinitive (to + verb). After other verbs we use the gerund (verb + -ing). The first verb decides which one.

We use them for

  • Gerund after some verbs: I enjoy reading. She finished working.
  • Infinitive after other verbs: I want to learn. He decided to leave.
  • Gerund after prepositions: I'm good at drawing. Thanks for helping.

Words that go with them

enjoy, finish, mind, want, need, hope, decide, would like, good at, interested in.

Russian and Uzbek use one form after any verb (я хочу читать, я люблю читать / o'qishni yaxshi ko'raman). English needs you to learn which verb takes which form.

После одних глаголов мы используем инфинитив (to + глагол). После других — герундий (глагол + -ing). Выбор зависит от первого глагола.

Когда используем

  • Герундий после некоторых глаголов: I enjoy reading. She finished working.
  • Инфинитив после других: I want to learn. He decided to leave.
  • Герундий после предлогов: I'm good at drawing. Thanks for helping.

Слова-подсказки

enjoy, finish, mind, want, need, hope, decide, would like, good at, interested in.

В русском и узбекском после любого глагола одна форма (я хочу читать, я люблю читать / o'qishni yaxshi ko'raman). В английском нужно знать, какой глагол какую форму берёт.

Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin infinitiv (to + fe'l), boshqalaridan keyin gerundiy (fe'l + -ing) ishlatamiz. Qaysi shakl kerakligini birinchi fe'l belgilaydi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Ba'zi fe'llardan keyin gerundiy: I enjoy reading. She finished working.
  • Boshqalaridan keyin infinitiv: I want to learn. He decided to leave.
  • Predloglardan keyin gerundiy: I'm good at drawing. Thanks for helping.

Yordamchi so'zlar

enjoy, finish, mind, want, need, hope, decide, would like, good at, interested in.

Rus va o'zbek tilida har qanday fe'ldan keyin bitta shakl (я хочу читать, я люблю читать / o'qishni yaxshi ko'raman). Inglizchada qaysi fe'l qaysi shaklni olishini bilish kerak.

  1. Gerund (-ing) after: enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep, suggest, practise, give up. I finished doing my homework.
  2. Infinitive (to + verb) after: want, need, hope, decide, plan, learn, promise, would like. She decided to study law.
  3. like, love, hate, start, begin take both forms with the same meaning: I like swimming = I like to swim. But would like takes only the infinitive.
  4. After a preposition always use the gerund: interested in learning, before leaving, good at cooking, thank you for coming.
  5. Purpose: to + verb answers "why?": I went to the shop to buy bread.
  6. Gerund as subject: Swimming is good for you.
  1. Герундий (-ing) после: enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep, suggest, practise, give up. I finished doing my homework.
  2. Инфинитив (to + глагол) после: want, need, hope, decide, plan, learn, promise, would like. She decided to study law.
  3. like, love, hate, start, begin принимают обе формы с тем же значением: I like swimming = I like to swim. Но would like только с инфинитивом.
  4. После предлога всегда герундий: interested in learning, before leaving, good at cooking, thank you for coming.
  5. Цель: to + глагол отвечает на «зачем?»: I went to the shop to buy bread.
  6. Герундий как подлежащее: Swimming is good for you.
  1. Gerundiy (-ing) quyidagilardan keyin: enjoy, finish, mind, avoid, keep, suggest, practise, give up. I finished doing my homework.
  2. Infinitiv (to + fe'l) quyidagilardan keyin: want, need, hope, decide, plan, learn, promise, would like. She decided to study law.
  3. like, love, hate, start, begin ikkala shaklni ham oladi, ma'no bir xil: I like swimming = I like to swim. Lekin would like faqat infinitiv bilan.
  4. Predlogdan keyin doim gerundiy: interested in learning, before leaving, good at cooking, thank you for coming.
  5. Maqsad: to + fe'l «nima uchun?» savoliga javob beradi: I went to the shop to buy bread.
  6. Ega sifatida gerundiy: Swimming is good for you.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is there a preposition before the verb? Yes → gerund: good at drawing.
  2. Which verb comes first? enjoy, finish, mind → -ing. want, need, hope, decide → to + verb.
  3. Am I saying why? Yes → to + verb: I called to ask.

Common mistakes

  • I enjoy to read. I enjoy reading.
  • I want going home. I want to go home.
  • I'm interested in learn English. I'm interested in learning English.
  • She decided leaving. She decided to leave.
Not sure? Check the first verb. Verbs of wanting and planning take to. Verbs of liking and finishing take -ing.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Перед глаголом стоит предлог? Да → герундий: good at drawing.
  2. Какой глагол идёт первым? enjoy, finish, mind → -ing. want, need, hope, decide → to + глагол.
  3. Я говорю, зачем? Да → to + глагол: I called to ask.

Частые ошибки

  • I enjoy to read. I enjoy reading.
  • I want going home. I want to go home.
  • I'm interested in learn English. I'm interested in learning English.
  • She decided leaving. She decided to leave.
Не уверен? Посмотри на первый глагол. Желание и планы → to. Удовольствие и завершение → -ing.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Fe'l oldida predlog bormi? Ha → gerundiy: good at drawing.
  2. Birinchi qaysi fe'l keladi? enjoy, finish, mind → -ing. want, need, hope, decide → to + fe'l.
  3. Nima uchun ekanini aytyapmanmi? Ha → to + fe'l: I called to ask.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I enjoy to read. I enjoy reading.
  • I want going home. I want to go home.
  • I'm interested in learn English. I'm interested in learning English.
  • She decided leaving. She decided to leave.
Ishonchingiz komil emasmi? Birinchi fe'lga qarang. Xohlash va rejalar → to. Yoqtirish va tugatish → -ing.

Real-life use

These patterns appear in almost every conversation about plans and hobbies.

Hobbies

"I enjoy playing football. I like to cook on weekends."

Plans

"I hope to travel next year. We decided to move to a bigger flat."

Invitations and offers

"Would you like to come with us? Do you mind waiting a minute?"

IELTS Speaking: talking about your interests

"I'm interested in learning languages. I use my phone to practise vocabulary, and I want to improve my speaking."

Examples and drill

Gerund

I enjoy reading. She finished cooking. Do you mind opening the window?

Infinitive

I want to learn. They decided to stay. We need to hurry.

Both

I like swimming. I like to swim. She started working. She started to work.

After prepositions

He is good at drawing. Thanks for helping. Think before speaking.

Purpose

I went to the shop to buy bread.
