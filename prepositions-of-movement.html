Prepositions of movement

General info

Prepositions of movement show the direction of a movement — where someone or something goes.

  • to — towards a place: I go to school.
  • into — moving inside: She walked into the room.
  • out of — moving outside: He ran out of the building.
  • onto — moving onto a surface: The cat jumped onto the table.
  • across — from one side to the other: We walked across the bridge.
  • through — in one side and out the other: The train went through a tunnel.
  • along — following a line: We walked along the river.
  • over — above something: The plane flew over the city.
  • around — in a circle, on all sides: We drove around the town.
Use to for a destination, into for going inside, and through for going from one end to the other.

Предлоги движения показывают направление движения — куда кто-то или что-то движется.

  • to — по направлению к месту: I go to school.
  • into — движение внутрь: She walked into the room.
  • out of — движение наружу: He ran out of the building.
  • onto — движение на поверхность: The cat jumped onto the table.
  • across — с одной стороны на другую: We walked across the bridge.
  • through — внутрь и наружу с другой стороны: The train went through a tunnel.
  • along — вдоль линии: We walked along the river.
  • over — над чем-то: The plane flew over the city.
  • around — по кругу, вокруг: We drove around the town.
to — для пункта назначения, into — внутрь, through — от одного конца до другого.

Harakat predloglari harakat yo'nalishini — kimdir yoki nima qayerga ketayotganini bildiradi.

  • to — joy tomon: I go to school.
  • into — ichkariga: She walked into the room.
  • out of — tashqariga: He ran out of the building.
  • onto — yuzaga: The cat jumped onto the table.
  • across — bir tomondan ikkinchisiga: We walked across the bridge.
  • through — bir tomondan kirib, ikkinchisidan chiqib: The train went through a tunnel.
  • along — chiziq bo'ylab: We walked along the river.
  • over — ustidan: The plane flew over the city.
  • around — aylana bo'ylab, atrofida: We drove around the town.
to — manzil uchun, into — ichkariga, through — bir uchidan ikkinchisiga.

  2. into / out of show going inside or outside: He got into the car. She came out of the house.
  3. onto / off show going on or off a surface: He jumped onto the stage. She fell off the bike.
  4. across = from one side to the other; through = in one side and out the other.
  5. along follows a line; over is above; around is in a circle.
  6. With arrive: arrive at a place, arrive in a city or country.
  1. to + пункт назначения: She went to the shop. Но go home без to: I went home.
  2. into / out of — вход внутрь или выход наружу: He got into the car. She came out of the house.
  3. onto / off — движение на поверхность или с неё: He jumped onto the stage. She fell off the bike.
  4. across = с одной стороны на другую; through = внутрь и наружу с другой стороны.
  5. along — вдоль линии; over — над; around — по кругу.
  6. С arrive: arrive at (место), arrive in (город или страна).

  2. into / out of — ichkariga yoki tashqariga: He got into the car. She came out of the house.
  3. onto / off — yuzaga yoki yuzadan: He jumped onto the stage. She fell off the bike.
  4. across = bir tomondan ikkinchisiga; through = bir tomondan kirib, ikkinchisidan chiqib.
  5. along — chiziq bo'ylab; over — ustidan; around — aylana bo'ylab.
  6. arrive bilan: arrive at (joy), arrive in (shahar yoki davlat).

Student explanation

Ask yourself: what kind of movement?

  1. To a place? → to.
  2. Inside or outside? → into / out of.
  3. From side to side, or in and out? → across / through.
  4. Following a line, above, or in a circle? → along / over / around.

Common mistakes

  • I went to home. I went home.
  • She walked in the room. She walked into the room.
  • We walked across the tunnel. We walked through the tunnel.
  • He got on the car. He got into the car.
Remember: go home, arrive home — no preposition before home.

Спроси себя: какое это движение?

  1. К месту? → to.
  2. Внутрь или наружу? → into / out of.
  3. Сбоку на бок или внутрь-наружу? → across / through.
  4. Вдоль линии, над, по кругу? → along / over / around.

Частые ошибки

  • I went to home. I went home.
  • She walked in the room. She walked into the room.
  • We walked across the tunnel. We walked through the tunnel.
  • He got on the car. He got into the car.
Запомни: go home, arrive home — без предлога перед home.

O'zingizdan so'rang: qanday harakat?

  1. Joyga? → to.
  2. Ichkarigami yoki tashqarigami? → into / out of.
  3. Yonma-yonmi yoki ich-ichidanmi? → across / through.
  4. Chiziq bo'ylab, ustidan yoki aylana bo'ylab? → along / over / around.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • I went to home. I went home.
  • She walked in the room. She walked into the room.
  • We walked across the tunnel. We walked through the tunnel.
  • He got on the car. He got into the car.
Esda tuting: go home, arrive home — home dan oldin predlog yo'q.

Real-life use

You need these prepositions to give directions and describe journeys.

Giving directions

"Go along this street, then across the square."

Public transport

"Get into the bus and go through the centre."

Travel

"We drove around the island and walked over the hills."

At the airport

"Go through security and into the departure lounge."

IELTS Speaking: a journey

"We drove along the coast, went through a long tunnel and came out of it near the beach."
