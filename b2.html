B2

Upper-intermediate. 14 topics. Choose one.

  • Third ConditionalOpen topicWe cover: if + past perfect, would have + participle. You will be able to: talk about regrets and what could have been different.Разбираем: if + past perfect, would have + третья форма. Вы сможете: говорить о сожалениях и о том, что могло быть иначе.Ko'ramiz: if + past perfect, would have + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: afsuslar va nima boshqacha bo'lishi mumkin edi haqida gapirish.
  • Mixed conditionalsOpen topicWe cover: past cause with present result, and the reverse. You will be able to: link different times in one conditional sentence.Разбираем: причина в прошлом и результат сейчас, и наоборот. Вы сможете: связывать разное время в одном условном предложении.Ko'ramiz: o'tmishdagi sabab va hozirgi natija, va aksincha. Siz qila olasiz: bitta shart gapda turli vaqtlarni bog'lash.
  • Passive voice (all tenses)Open topicWe cover: be + participle in every tense and with modals. You will be able to: turn active sentences into passives in any tense.Разбираем: be + третья форма во всех временах и с модальными. Вы сможете: превращать активные предложения в пассивные в любом времени.Ko'ramiz: barcha zamonlar va modallar bilan be + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: aniq nisbatdagi gapni istalgan zamonda majhul nisbatga aylantirish.
  • Reported speech (full)Open topicWe cover: questions, commands, requests and reporting verbs. You will be able to: report whole conversations, not only statements.Разбираем: вопросы, приказы, просьбы и глаголы речи. Вы сможете: точно передавать разговоры, а не только утверждения.Ko'ramiz: savollar, buyruqlar, iltimoslar va gapirish fe'llari. Siz qila olasiz: suhbatlarni faqat darak gaplar emas, to'liq yetkazish.
  • Future perfect and continuousOpen topicWe cover: will be -ing and will have + participle. You will be able to: say what will be in progress or finished by a future time.Разбираем: will be -ing и will have + третья форма. Вы сможете: сказать, что будет идти или закончится к моменту в будущем.Ko'ramiz: will be -ing va will have + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: kelajakdagi paytda nima davom etayotgan yoki tugagan bo'lishini aytish.
  • Past modals of deductionOpen topicWe cover: must have, can't have, might have. You will be able to: guess what happened in the past from clues.Разбираем: must have, can't have, might have. Вы сможете: догадываться о том, что произошло, по признакам.Ko'ramiz: must have, can't have, might have. Siz qila olasiz: belgilarga qarab o'tmishda nima bo'lganini taxmin qilish.
  • Perfect modalsOpen topicWe cover: should have, could have, would have, needn't have. You will be able to: express regret, criticism and missed chances.Разбираем: should have, could have, would have, needn't have. Вы сможете: выражать сожаление, упрёк и упущенные возможности.Ko'ramiz: should have, could have, would have, needn't have. Siz qila olasiz: afsus, tanqid va boy berilgan imkoniyatlarni ifodalash.
  • Relative clauses (advanced)Open topicWe cover: non-defining clauses, whom, of which, reduced clauses. You will be able to: write longer, more precise sentences with extra information.Разбираем: неопределительные придаточные, whom, of which, сокращённые обороты. Вы сможете: писать более длинные и точные предложения с дополнительной информацией.Ko'ramiz: noaniqlovchi gaplar, whom, of which, qisqartirilgan gaplar. Siz qila olasiz: qo'shimcha ma'lumotli uzunroq va aniqroq gaplar yozish.
  • Wish / If onlyOpen topicWe cover: wish + past, past perfect and would. You will be able to: express regrets and wishes about now and the past.Разбираем: wish + past, past perfect и would. Вы сможете: выражать сожаления и желания о настоящем и прошлом.Ko'ramiz: wish + past, past perfect va would. Siz qila olasiz: hozir va o'tmish haqida afsus va istaklarni ifodalash.
  • Causative: have / get something doneOpen topicWe cover: have / get + object + participle. You will be able to: say what services other people do for you.Разбираем: have / get + дополнение + третья форма. Вы сможете: сказать, какие услуги для вас делают другие.Ko'ramiz: have / get + to'ldiruvchi + uchinchi shakl. Siz qila olasiz: boshqalar siz uchun qilgan xizmatlarni aytish.
  • Inversion (basic)Open topicWe cover: Never have I, Rarely does he, Not only did. You will be able to: use a first set of formal emphasis structures.Разбираем: Never have I, Rarely does he, Not only did. Вы сможете: использовать первые формальные конструкции для выделения.Ko'ramiz: Never have I, Rarely does he, Not only did. Siz qila olasiz: ta'kid uchun birinchi rasmiy qoliplarni ishlatish.
  • Linking words and cohesionOpen topicWe cover: although, despite, however, because of, so that. You will be able to: connect ideas so your speaking and writing flow.Разбираем: although, despite, however, because of, so that. Вы сможете: связывать идеи так, чтобы речь и письмо были плавными.Ko'ramiz: although, despite, however, because of, so that. Siz qila olasiz: nutq va yozuvingiz silliq bo'lishi uchun g'oyalarni bog'lash.
  • Advanced articles and determinersOpen topicWe cover: the + adjective, every, each, all, whole, few, a few. You will be able to: talk about groups and amounts precisely.Разбираем: the + прилагательное, every, each, all, whole, few, a few. Вы сможете: точно говорить о группах и количестве.Ko'ramiz: the + sifat, every, each, all, whole, few, a few. Siz qila olasiz: guruh va miqdorlar haqida aniq gapirish.
  • Future in the pastOpen topicWe cover: would, was/were going to and was about to for the future seen from the past. You will be able to: report plans and tell stories.Разбираем: would, was/were going to и was about to для будущего из прошлого. Вы сможете: передавать планы и рассказывать истории.Ko'ramiz: o'tmishdan ko'rilgan kelajak uchun would, was/were going to va was about to. Siz qila olasiz: rejalarni yetkazish va hikoya qilish.
