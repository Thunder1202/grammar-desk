A1

Beginner. 19 topics. Choose one.

  • To be (am / is / are)Open topicWe cover: am / is / are in statements, negatives and questions. You will be able to: introduce yourself and describe people and things.Разбираем: am / is / are в утверждениях, отрицаниях и вопросах. Вы сможете: представиться и описать людей и предметы.Ko'ramiz: am / is / are darak, inkor va so'roq gaplarda. Siz qila olasiz: o'zingizni tanishtirish va odam hamda narsalarni tasvirlash.
  • PossessivesOpen topicWe cover: my / your / his / her, 's and whose. You will be able to: say who things belong to.Разбираем: my / your / his / her, 's и whose. Вы сможете: сказать, кому что принадлежит.Ko'ramiz: my / your / his / her, 's va whose. Siz qila olasiz: narsa kimga tegishli ekanini aytish.
  • Present SimpleOpen topicWe cover: habits and routines, with do / does. You will be able to: talk about your daily life and ask about other people's.Разбираем: привычки и распорядок, с do / does. Вы сможете: рассказать о своём дне и спросить о других.Ko'ramiz: odatlar va kun tartibi, do / does bilan. Siz qila olasiz: kundalik hayotingiz haqida gapirish va boshqalardan so'rash.
  • Present ContinuousOpen topicWe cover: am / is / are + -ing for now. You will be able to: say what is happening at the moment.Разбираем: am / is / are + -ing для действий сейчас. Вы сможете: сказать, что происходит прямо сейчас.Ko'ramiz: hozirgi ish uchun am / is / are + -ing. Siz qila olasiz: hozir nima bo'layotganini aytish.
  • Present Simple vs ContinuousOpen topicWe cover: habits versus actions in progress. You will be able to: choose the right tense for routines and for now.Разбираем: привычки и действия в процессе. Вы сможете: выбирать верное время для привычек и для «сейчас».Ko'ramiz: odat va jarayondagi ish farqi. Siz qila olasiz: odatlar va hozirgi ish uchun to'g'ri zamonni tanlash.
  • There is / There areOpen topicWe cover: describing what exists in a place. You will be able to: describe a room, a city or a picture.Разбираем: описание того, что находится в месте. Вы сможете: описать комнату, город или картинку.Ko'ramiz: joyda nima borligini tasvirlash. Siz qila olasiz: xona, shahar yoki rasmni tasvirlash.
  • Countable and uncountable nounsOpen topicWe cover: nouns you can count and nouns you cannot. You will be able to: use a / an, some and any correctly.Разбираем: существительные, которые можно и нельзя посчитать. Вы сможете: правильно использовать a / an, some и any.Ko'ramiz: sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar. Siz qila olasiz: a / an, some va any ni to'g'ri ishlatish.
  • Articles: a / an / theOpen topicWe cover: when to use a, an, the or nothing. You will be able to: choose the right article in simple sentences.Разбираем: когда ставить a, an, the или ничего. Вы сможете: выбирать верный артикль в простых предложениях.Ko'ramiz: a, an, the yoki artiklsiz qachon ishlatiladi. Siz qila olasiz: oddiy gaplarda to'g'ri artiklni tanlash.
  • Past Simple of to beOpen topicWe cover: was / were in statements, negatives and questions. You will be able to: say where you were and how things were.Разбираем: was / were в утверждениях, отрицаниях и вопросах. Вы сможете: сказать, где вы были и как всё было.Ko'ramiz: was / were darak, inkor va so'roq gaplarda. Siz qila olasiz: qayerda bo'lganingiz va nima qanday bo'lganini aytish.
  • Past SimpleOpen topicWe cover: regular and irregular verbs, did / didn't. You will be able to: tell what happened yesterday or last week.Разбираем: правильные и неправильные глаголы, did / didn't. Вы сможете: рассказать, что произошло вчера или на прошлой неделе.Ko'ramiz: to'g'ri va noto'g'ri fe'llar, did / didn't. Siz qila olasiz: kecha yoki o'tgan hafta nima bo'lganini aytib berish.
  • Can / can'tOpen topicWe cover: ability and permission. You will be able to: say what you can do and ask for permission.Разбираем: умение и разрешение. Вы сможете: сказать, что вы умеете, и попросить разрешения.Ko'ramiz: qobiliyat va ruxsat. Siz qila olasiz: nima qila olishingizni aytish va ruxsat so'rash.
  • Have gotOpen topicWe cover: have got / has got for possession and description. You will be able to: talk about what you have, your family and how people look.Разбираем: have got / has got для принадлежности и описания. Вы сможете: рассказать, что у вас есть, о семье и внешности.Ko'ramiz: egalik va tavsif uchun have got / has got. Siz qila olasiz: nimangiz borligi, oila va tashqi ko'rinish haqida gapirish.
  • Prepositions of place and timeOpen topicWe cover: in, on, at for places and times. You will be able to: say where things are and when things happen.Разбираем: in, on, at для мест и времени. Вы сможете: сказать, где что находится и когда что происходит.Ko'ramiz: joy va vaqt uchun in, on, at. Siz qila olasiz: narsa qayerda va voqea qachon bo'lishini aytish.
  • Question formsOpen topicWe cover: yes / no and wh- questions. You will be able to: ask clear questions about people, places and time.Разбираем: вопросы да / нет и с вопросительными словами. Вы сможете: задавать ясные вопросы о людях, местах и времени.Ko'ramiz: ha / yo'q va so'roq so'zli savollar. Siz qila olasiz: odamlar, joylar va vaqt haqida aniq savol berish.
  • Demonstratives (this / that / these / those)Open topicWe cover: this / that / these / those for things near and far. You will be able to: point at and describe objects around you.Разбираем: this / that / these / those для близких и далёких предметов. Вы сможете: указывать на предметы вокруг вас.Ko'ramiz: yaqin va uzoq narsalar uchun this / that / these / those. Siz qila olasiz: atrofdagi narsalarga ishora qilish.
  • Imperatives (commands and instructions)Open topicWe cover: commands, instructions and polite orders. You will be able to: give directions and tell people what to do.Разбираем: команды, инструкции и вежливые просьбы. Вы сможете: давать указания и говорить, что делать.Ko'ramiz: buyruq, ko'rsatma va muloyim topshiriqlar. Siz qila olasiz: yo'nalish berish va nima qilishni aytish.
  • Plural nounsOpen topicWe cover: regular and irregular plurals (book - books, man - men). You will be able to: talk about more than one thing.Разбираем: правильные и неправильные формы мн. числа (book - books, man - men). Вы сможете: говорить о нескольких предметах.Ko'ramiz: to'g'ri va noto'g'ri ko'plik shakllari (book - books, man - men). Siz qila olasiz: bir nechta narsa haqida gapirish.
  • Possessive pronounsOpen topicWe cover: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. You will be able to: say what belongs to whom without repeating nouns.Разбираем: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. Вы сможете: говорить, что кому принадлежит, без повтора существительных.Ko'ramiz: mine, yours, his, hers, ours, theirs. Siz qila olasiz: otni takrorlamasdan narsa kimga tegishli ekanini aytish.
  • Basic linkers (and, but, or, because, so)Open topicWe cover: and, but, or, because, so. You will be able to: join ideas and give reasons.Разбираем: and, but, or, because, so. Вы сможете: соединять мысли и объяснять причины.Ko'ramiz: and, but, or, because, so. Siz qila olasiz: fikrlarni bog'lash va sabab aytish.
