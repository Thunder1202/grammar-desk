Passive voice (all tenses)

General info

The passive voice works in every tense. The rule is always the same: the right tense of be + the past participle. The tense is shown by be.

We use it for

  • Actions in progress: The road is being repaired.
  • Completed actions with a result: The flight has been cancelled.
  • Future and obligation: The winner will be announced. This must be finished.

Words that go with it

is being, was being, has been, had been, will be, can be, must be, by.

Russian and Uzbek show the passive with endings (ремонтируется, tuzatilmoqda). English changes be: is being, has been, will be.

Страдательный залог работает во всех временах. Правило всегда одно: нужная форма be + третья форма глагола. Время показывает be.

Когда используем

  • Действия в процессе: The road is being repaired.
  • Завершённые действия с результатом: The flight has been cancelled.
  • Будущее и обязанность: The winner will be announced. This must be finished.

Слова-подсказки

is being, was being, has been, had been, will be, can be, must be, by.

В русском и узбекском пассив через окончания (ремонтируется, tuzatilmoqda). В английском меняется be: is being, has been, will be.

Majhul nisbat barcha zamonlarda ishlaydi. Qoida doim bir xil: be ning kerakli shakli + uchinchi shakl. Zamonni be ko'rsatadi.

  • Natijali tugagan ishlar: The flight has been cancelled.
  • Kelajak va majburiyat: The winner will be announced. This must be finished.

Teacher's rule

TenseActivePassive
present simpleThey make cars.Cars are made.
present continuousThey are repairing the road.The road is being repaired.
past simpleSomeone stole my bike.My bike was stolen.
past continuousThey were painting the house.The house was being painted.
present perfectThey have cleaned the room.The room has been cleaned.
past perfectThey had finished the work.The work had been finished.
willThey will announce the winner.The winner will be announced.
modalYou must finish this.This must be finished.
  1. Form: the right tense of be + past participle. The object of the active sentence becomes the subject.
  2. Continuous: being + participle: is being repaired, was being painted.
  3. Perfect: been + participle: has been cleaned, had been finished.
  4. Future and modals: will / can / must / should + be + participle: will be announced, must be finished.
  5. Two objects (give, send, show, offer): the person often becomes the subject: I was given a book. (= A book was given to me.)
  6. by + doer only when it is important. Verbs without an object (happen, arrive) cannot be passive. The tense of the sentence does not change.
  1. Форма: нужное время be + третья форма. Дополнение активного предложения становится подлежащим.
  2. Continuous: being + третья форма: is being repaired, was being painted.
  3. Perfect: been + третья форма: has been cleaned, had been finished.
  4. Будущее и модальные: will / can / must / should + be + третья форма: will be announced, must be finished.
  5. Два дополнения (give, send, show, offer): подлежащим часто становится человек: I was given a book. (= A book was given to me.)
  6. by + исполнитель только если важно. Глаголы без дополнения (happen, arrive) в пассиве не бывают. Время предложения не меняется.
  1. Shakl: be ning kerakli zamoni + uchinchi shakl. Aniq nisbatdagi gapning to'ldiruvchisi ega bo'ladi.
  2. Continuous: being + uchinchi shakl: is being repaired, was being painted.
  3. Perfect: been + uchinchi shakl: has been cleaned, had been finished.
  4. Kelajak va modallar: will / can / must / should + be + uchinchi shakl: will be announced, must be finished.
  5. Ikkita to'ldiruvchi (give, send, show, offer): ko'pincha odam ega bo'ladi: I was given a book. (= A book was given to me.)
  6. by + bajaruvchi faqat muhim bo'lsa. To'ldiruvchisiz fe'llar (happen, arrive) majhul nisbatda bo'lmaydi. Gapning zamoni o'zgarmaydi.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Which tense (verb form that shows time) is the active sentence? Keep the same tense in the passive (the action happens to someone or something), and put it in be.
  2. Is it continuous or perfect? Continuous → being + participle. Perfect → been + participle.
  3. Is there a modal (word like can, must, should) or will? Yes → modal / will + be + participle.

Active to passive in 4 steps

1) Find the tense. 2) Make the object (who or what gets the action) the subject. 3) Put be in the same tense. 4) Add the past participle (form like done, seen, eaten). Active: They are building a bridge. Passive: A bridge is being built.

Common mistakes

  • The road is repairing. The road is being repaired.
  • The room has cleaned. The room has been cleaned.
  • The winner will announce tomorrow. The winner will be announced tomorrow.
  • The house was painting when I arrived. The house was being painted when I arrived.
The word right before the participle is always be, being or been. If it is missing, the passive is wrong.

In plain words

The idea stays the same in every time: the thing that gets the action comes first. Only the small word be changes, to show when it happens. The last word never changes.

  • The road is being repaired. Workers are doing it right now.
  • The room has been cleaned. It is clean now, and we don't care who did it.
  • The results will be published on Friday. This will happen later.

To build it, take the normal time form of be, then add a word like done, made or written.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. В каком времени активное предложение? В пассиве (действие совершают над кем-то или чем-то) то же время, оно выражается через be.
  2. Это continuous или perfect? Continuous → being + третья форма. Perfect → been + третья форма.
  3. Есть модальный или will? Да → модальный / will + be + третья форма.

Из актива в пассив за 4 шага

1) Найди время. 2) Сделай дополнение (на кого или что направлено действие) подлежащим. 3) Поставь be в том же времени. 4) Добавь третью форму (форма вроде done, seen, eaten). Актив: They are building a bridge. Пассив: A bridge is being built.

Частые ошибки

  • The road is repairing. The road is being repaired.
  • The room has cleaned. The room has been cleaned.
  • The winner will announce tomorrow. The winner will be announced tomorrow.
  • The house was painting when I arrived. The house was being painted when I arrived.
Прямо перед третьей формой всегда стоит be, being или been. Если их нет, пассив неверный.

Простыми словами

Идея одна во всех временах: первым идёт то, с чем что-то делают. Меняется только маленькое слово be и показывает когда. Последнее слово не меняется.

  • The road is being repaired. Дорогу ремонтируют. Рабочие делают это прямо сейчас.
  • The room has been cleaned. Комнату убрали. Она чистая, кто убрал, неважно.
  • The results will be published on Friday. Результаты опубликуют в пятницу. Это случится позже.

Чтобы построить форму, возьмите be в нужном времени и добавьте слово вроде done, made или written.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Aniq nisbatdagi gap qaysi zamonda? Majhul (ish kimgadir nisbatan qilinadi) nisbatda ham xuddi shu zamon, u be orqali ifodalanadi.
  2. Continuous yoki perfectmi? Continuous → being + uchinchi shakl. Perfect → been + uchinchi shakl.
  3. Modal yoki will bormi? Ha → modal / will + be + uchinchi shakl.

Aniqdan majhulga 4 qadamda

1) Zamonni toping. 2) To'ldiruvchini ega qiling. 3) be ni xuddi shu zamonda qo'ying. 4) Uchinchi shaklni (done, seen, eaten kabi shakl) qo'shing. Aniq: They are building a bridge. Majhul: A bridge is being built.

Uchinchi shakldan oldin doim be, being yoki been turadi. Ular yo'q bo'lsa, majhul nisbat noto'g'ri.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

G'oya har qanday zamonda bir xil: ish qilinadigan narsa birinchi keladi. Faqat kichik be so'zi o'zgarib, qachonligini ko'rsatadi. Oxirgi so'z o'zgarmaydi.

  • The road is being repaired. Yo'l ta'mirlanyapti. Ishchilar buni hozir qilyapti.
  • The room has been cleaned. Xona tozalangan. U toza, kim qilgani muhim emas.
  • The results will be published on Friday. Natijalar juma kuni e'lon qilinadi. Bu keyinroq bo'ladi.

Shaklni tuzish uchun be ni kerakli zamonda oling va done, made yoki written kabi so'zni qo'shing.

Real-life use

We use the passive to report news, announcements, work tasks and facts.

News

"A new bridge is being built. Three men have been arrested."

Announcements

"The flight has been cancelled. Passengers will be informed by email."

Work

"The report must be finished by Friday. The meeting was postponed."

IELTS Speaking: describing change in your city

"A lot of new buildings are being built in my city, and the old market has been replaced by a park."
