Articles: a / an / the

General info

Articles are small words that stand before a noun: a, an, the. They show whether you mean any one thing or one specific thing. Sometimes English uses no article at all.

We use them for

  • a / an: mention something for the first time, or say what someone is: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: a specific thing that both people know, a second mention, or something unique: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • No article: plural and uncountable nouns in general: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
Russian and Uzbek have no articles, but English needs them. Uzbek bir is close to a.

Артикли — маленькие слова перед существительным: a, an, the. Они показывают, говорим ли мы о любом предмете или о конкретном. Иногда в английском артикля нет совсем.

Когда используем

  • a / an: первое упоминание или кто-то кем-то является: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: конкретный предмет, известный обоим, повторное упоминание или уникальный предмет: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • Без артикля: мн. число и неисчисляемые в общем смысле: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
В русском артиклей нет, а в английском они обязательны.

Artikllar — ot oldida turadigan kichik so'zlar: a, an, the. Ular har qanday bitta narsa yoki aniq bir narsa haqida gapirayotganingizni ko'rsatadi. Ba'zan inglizchada artikl umuman ishlatilmaydi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • a / an: birinchi marta aytilganda yoki kim ekanini aytganda: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: ikkalasi biladigan aniq narsa, ikkinchi marta aytilgan yoki noyob narsa: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • Artiklsiz: umumiy ma'nodagi ko'plik va sanalmaydigan otlar: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
O'zbek tilida artikl yo'q, inglizchada esa kerak. O'zbekcha bir taxminan a ga to'g'ri keladi.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the noun singular and countable? If not, do not use a / an.
  2. Does the listener know which one? Yes → the. No → a / an.
  3. Am I talking about things in general? Yes → no article (Dogs are friendly).

Common mistakes

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I have a umbrella. I have an umbrella.
  • I like the music. (in general) I like music.
  • The Uzbekistan is beautiful. Uzbekistan is beautiful.
Not sure? Ask "which one?" If the listener already knows the answer, use the.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Существительное исчисляемое и в ед. числе? Если нет, a / an не нужен.
  2. Знает ли слушающий, о каком речь? Да → the. Нет → a / an.
  3. Я говорю о чём-то в общем? Да → без артикля (Dogs are friendly).

Частые ошибки

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I have a umbrella. I have an umbrella.
  • I like the music. (in general) I like music.
  • The Uzbekistan is beautiful. Uzbekistan is beautiful.
Не уверен? Спроси «какой именно?». Если слушающий уже знает ответ, ставь the.

Real-life use

Articles appear in every sentence, so small mistakes here are very common and very easy to fix.

Ordering food

"I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please."

Saying your job

"I'm a teacher. I work at a school."

Giving instructions

"Open the door. Turn off the light."

Telling a story

"I saw a man. The man was carrying a box."

Talking about likes

"I love music. I hate cold weather."
