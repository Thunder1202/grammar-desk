Articles: a / an / the

General info

Articles are small words that stand before a noun: a, an, the. They show whether you mean any one thing or one specific thing. Sometimes English uses no article at all.

We use them for

  • a / an: mention something for the first time, or say what someone is: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: a specific thing that both people know, a second mention, or something unique: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • No article: plural and uncountable nouns in general: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
Russian and Uzbek have no articles, but English needs them. Uzbek bir is close to a.

Артикли — маленькие слова перед существительным: a, an, the. Они показывают, говорим ли мы о любом предмете или о конкретном. Иногда в английском артикля нет совсем.

Когда используем

  • a / an: первое упоминание или кто-то кем-то является: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: конкретный предмет, известный обоим, повторное упоминание или уникальный предмет: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • Без артикля: мн. число и неисчисляемые в общем смысле: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
В русском артиклей нет, а в английском они обязательны.

Artikllar — ot oldida turadigan kichik so'zlar: a, an, the. Ular har qanday bitta narsa yoki aniq bir narsa haqida gapirayotganingizni ko'rsatadi. Ba'zan inglizchada artikl umuman ishlatilmaydi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • a / an: birinchi marta aytilganda yoki kim ekanini aytganda: I have a car. She is a teacher.
  • the: ikkalasi biladigan aniq narsa, ikkinchi marta aytilgan yoki noyob narsa: The car is red. The sun is hot.
  • Artiklsiz: umumiy ma'nodagi ko'plik va sanalmaydigan otlar: Dogs are friendly. I like music.
O'zbek tilida artikl yo'q, inglizchada esa kerak. O'zbekcha bir taxminan a ga to'g'ri keladi.

  1. a / an go only with singular countable nouns: a pen, an idea.
  2. a or an? It depends on the SOUND, not the letter: a university, an hour, an apple, a book.
  3. First and second mention: I have a dog. The dog is black.
  4. the is used for unique things (the sun, the internet) and when speaker and listener know which one: Open the door.
  5. No article: plural and uncountable nouns in general (Dogs are friendly), languages, names, meals (have breakfast), most countries (Uzbekistan). But: the USA, the UK.
  6. Jobs: she is a doctor, he is an engineer.

Choose in three questions

  1. Is it plural or uncountable, and do you mean it in general? → no article: I love cats. Water is important.
  2. Does the listener know which one? (there is only one, you said it before, or it is clear from the situation) → the: Close the door, please.
  3. Is it one of many, and you say it for the first time? → a / an: I want a sandwich.

Выбирай с помощью трёх вопросов

  1. Это множественное число или неисчисляемое, и ты говоришь вообще? → без артикля: I love cats. Water is important.
  2. Слушатель знает, какой именно? (он один такой, о нём уже говорили или это понятно из ситуации) → the: Close the door, please.
  3. Это один из многих, и ты говоришь о нём впервые? → a / an: I want a sandwich.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the noun (word for a person, thing or idea) singular (only one) and countable? If not, do not use a / an.
  2. Does the listener know which one? Yes → the. No → a / an.
  3. Am I talking about things in general? Yes → no article (Dogs are friendly).

Common mistakes

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I have a umbrella. I have an umbrella.
  • I like the music. (in general) I like music.
  • The Uzbekistan is beautiful. Uzbekistan is beautiful.
Not sure? Ask "which one?" If the listener already knows the answer, use the.

In plain words

A and an mean "one of many, any one". The means "that one, you know which one". Think of it like this: first you meet a new thing, then you both know it.

  • I saw a dog. A dog, any dog. This is new information.
  • The dog was big. That same dog. Now we both know which one.
  • Please close the door. There is only one door here, so you know which.

Use an instead of a when the next word starts with a vowel sound: an apple, an hour.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Существительное (это слово-предмет) исчисляемое и в ед. числе? Если нет, a / an не нужен.
  2. Знает ли слушающий, о каком речь? Да → the. Нет → a / an.
  3. Я говорю о чём-то в общем? Да → без артикля (Dogs are friendly).

Частые ошибки

  • She is teacher. She is a teacher.
  • I have a umbrella. I have an umbrella.
  • I like the music. (in general) I like music.
  • The Uzbekistan is beautiful. Uzbekistan is beautiful.
Не уверен? Спроси «какой именно?». Если слушающий уже знает ответ, ставь the.

Простыми словами

A и an значат «один из многих, какой-то». The значит «тот самый, ты знаешь какой». Представьте так: сначала вы встречаете что-то новое, а потом оба знаете, о чём речь.

  • I saw a dog. Я видел собаку. Какую-то собаку, это новая информация.
  • The dog was big. Собака была большая. Та самая, теперь оба знаем какая.
  • Please close the door. Закройте дверь, пожалуйста. Дверь здесь одна, понятно какая.

Ставьте an вместо a, если следующее слово начинается с гласного звука: an apple, an hour.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Ot (odam yoki narsa nomi) birlikdagi (faqat bitta) sanaladigan otmi? Yo'q bo'lsa, a / an kerak emas.
  2. Tinglovchi qaysi ekanini biladimi? Ha → the. Yo'q → a / an.
  3. Umumiy gapiryapmanmi? Ha → artiklsiz (Dogs are friendly).

Ishonchingiz komil emasmi? "Qaysi biri?" deb so'rang. Tinglovchi javobni bilsa, the ishlating.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

A va an "ko'pdan biri, qaysidir biri" degani. The "o'sha, qaysi ekanini bilasan" degani. Shunday tasavvur qiling: avval yangi narsaga duch kelasiz, keyin ikkalangiz ham uni bilasiz.

  • I saw a dog. Men it ko'rdim. Qaysidir it, bu yangi ma'lumot.
  • The dog was big. It katta edi. O'sha it, endi ikkalamiz ham bilamiz.
  • Please close the door. Eshikni yoping, iltimos. Bu yerda bitta eshik bor, qaysi ekani aniq.

Keyingi so'z unli tovush bilan boshlansa, a o'rniga an ishlating: an apple, an hour.

Real-life use

We use a, an and the in almost every sentence. Small mistakes are common and easy to fix.

Ask for food

"I'd like a coffee and a sandwich, please."

Saying your job

"I'm a teacher. I work at a school."

Tell people what to do

"Open the door. Turn off the light."

Tell a story

"I saw a man. The man was carrying a box."

What you like

"I love music. I hate cold weather."
