Countable and uncountable nouns

General info

Countable nouns are things you can count one by one: a book, two books, three students. Uncountable nouns are things you cannot count that way: water, milk, rice, money, time, information, advice, homework, news. They have no plural and no a / an.

What goes with what

  • Countable: a / an, numbers, plural -s: an apple, five apples.
  • Uncountable: no a / an, no plural: water (not "a water", not "waters").
  • some: positive sentences, with plural countables and uncountables: I have some apples. I have some milk.
  • any: negatives and questions: I don't have any apples. Do you have any milk?
Russian and Uzbek treat some of these words as countable (новости, maslahatlar). In English information, advice and news are uncountable.

Исчисляемые существительные — то, что можно считать по одному: a book, two books, three students. Неисчисляемые — то, что так считать нельзя: water, milk, rice, money, time, information, advice, homework, news. У них нет множественного числа и нет a / an.

Что с чем сочетается

  • Исчисляемые: a / an, числа, мн. число -s: an apple, five apples.
  • Неисчисляемые: без a / an, без мн. числа: water (не «a water», не «waters»).
  • some: в утверждениях, с исчисляемыми во мн. числе и неисчисляемыми: I have some apples. I have some milk.
  • any: в отрицаниях и вопросах: I don't have any apples. Do you have any milk?
В русском «новости» можно во множественном числе. В английском information, advice и news — неисчисляемые.

Sanaladigan otlar — birma-bir sanash mumkin bo'lgan narsalar: a book, two books, three students. Sanalmaydigan otlar — shunday sanab bo'lmaydigan narsalar: water, milk, rice, money, time, information, advice, homework, news. Ularning ko'pligi va a / an yo'q.

Nima bilan ishlatiladi

  • Sanaladigan: a / an, sonlar, ko'plik -s: an apple, five apples.
  • Sanalmaydigan: a / an yo'q, ko'plik yo'q: water ("a water" ham, "waters" ham emas).
  • some: darak gaplarda, ko'plikdagi sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlar bilan: I have some apples. I have some milk.
  • any: inkor va so'roqda: I don't have any apples. Do you have any milk?
O'zbek tilida "maslahatlar" deyish mumkin. Inglizchada information, advice va news sanalmaydi.

  1. Countable: a / an with one, -s with more: a pen, two pens.
  2. Uncountable: no a / an, no -s, singular verb: The information is useful.
  3. some / any: some in positive sentences, any in negatives and questions: I have some money. I don't have any money. Do you have any money?
  4. Offers and requests use some: Would you like some tea? Can I have some water?
  5. Measure words help you count uncountables: a glass of water, a cup of tea, a bottle of milk, a slice of bread, a piece of advice.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Can I count it one by one? Yes → countable (an apple). No → uncountable (milk).
  2. Is the sentence positive, negative or a question? Positive → some. Negative or question → any.
  3. Need a number for an uncountable noun? Add a measure word: a glass of water.

Common mistakes

  • I need an advice. I need some advice. / a piece of advice.
  • She gave me many informations. She gave me a lot of information.
  • I don't have some money. I don't have any money.
  • How many water do you drink? How much water do you drink?
If you cannot say "one information", the word is uncountable.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Можно ли считать по одному? Да → исчисляемое (an apple). Нет → неисчисляемое (milk).
  2. Предложение утвердительное, отрицательное или вопрос? Утверждение → some. Отрицание или вопрос → any.
  3. Нужно число для неисчисляемого? Добавь слово-меру: a glass of water.

Частые ошибки

  • I need an advice. I need some advice. / a piece of advice.
  • She gave me many informations. She gave me a lot of information.
  • I don't have some money. I don't have any money.
  • How many water do you drink? How much water do you drink?
Если нельзя сказать "one information", слово неисчисляемое.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Birma-bir sanay olamanmi? Ha → sanaladigan (an apple). Yo'q → sanalmaydigan (milk).
  2. Gap darak, inkor yoki so'roqmi? Darak → some. Inkor yoki so'roq → any.
  3. Sanalmaydigan ot uchun son kerakmi? O'lchov so'zi qo'shing: a glass of water.

Real-life use

You choose between some, any, many and much every time you shop, cook or ask for something.

Shopping

"Do we have any milk? We need some bread and eggs."

Restaurants

"Could I have some water, please?" "Would you like some tea?"

Recipes

"Add two eggs and some sugar. Don't add any salt."

Asking for help

"Can you give me some advice?" "I don't have any time today."

Talking about quantity

"How much money do you need?" "How many people are coming?"
