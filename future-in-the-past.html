Future in the past

General info

Future in the past describes a future that was seen from a point in the past. We use would, was / were going to or the past continuous.

  • I knew he would help me.
  • She said she was going to call.
  • They were leaving the next day.

We use it for

  • Reported speech: He said he would come.
  • Past plans that did not happen: I was going to visit, but I got ill.
  • What was about to happen: The plane was about to take off.
Future in the past = "the future from a past point of view". In reported speech, will becomes would and am going to becomes was going to.

Будущее в прошедшем описывает будущее, которое видели из прошлого момента. Используем would, was / were going to или past continuous.

  • I knew he would help me.
  • She said she was going to call.
  • They were leaving the next day.

Когда используем

  • Косвенная речь: He said he would come.
  • Прошлые планы, которые не сбылись: I was going to visit, but I got ill.
  • Что вот-вот должно было случиться: The plane was about to take off.
Future in the past = «будущее с точки зрения прошлого». В косвенной речи will становится would, а am going to — was going to.

O'tmishdagi kelasi zamon o'tmish nuqtai nazaridan ko'rilgan kelajakni bildiradi. would, was / were going to yoki past continuous ishlatamiz.

  • I knew he would help me.
  • She said she was going to call.
  • They were leaving the next day.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • O'zlashtirilgan nutq: He said he would come.
  • Amalga oshmagan o'tgan rejalar: I was going to visit, but I got ill.
  • Bo'lish arafasida bo'lgan narsa: The plane was about to take off.
O'tmishdagi kelasi zamon = «o'tmish nuqtai nazaridan kelajak». O'zlashtirilgan nutqda will → would, am going to → was going to.

Teacher's rule

FormMeaningExample
would + verbfuture seen from the pastHe said he would call.
was / were going to + verbpast plan or intentionI was going to buy it.
past continuousarranged futureThey were meeting at six.
was / were about to + verbthe near futureI was about to leave.
  1. would + base verb = will in reported speech: She said, "I will call" → She said she would call.
  2. was / were going to = plans and intentions from the past: I was going to buy it.
  3. The past continuous shows an arranged future: They were meeting at six.
  4. was / were about to + verb = the very near future: I was about to leave.
  5. The formal was / were to + verb also works: The meeting was to begin at noon.
  6. We use it after past reporting verbs: said, thought, knew, expected, hoped.

Why do we need it?

Sometimes we stand at a point in the past and look forward from there. The future of that past moment may now be in the past too, so every future form moves one step back.

Future (from now)Future in the past (from then)
I think it will rain.I thought it would rain.
I'm going to quit.I was going to quit.
We're meeting at six.We were meeting at six.
The film is about to start.The film was about to start.

Plans that changed

was going to and was supposed to often show that the plan didn't happen: I was going to call you, but my phone died. The train was supposed to arrive at 6, but it was late.

In stories, would looks ahead dramatically: She smiled and waved. She didn't know it would be the last time they met.
  1. would + базовый глагол = will в косвенной речи: She said, "I will call" → She said she would call.
  2. was / were going to = планы и намерения из прошлого: I was going to buy it.
  3. Past continuous показывает запланированное будущее: They were meeting at six.
  4. was / were about to + глагол = очень близкое будущее: I was about to leave.
  5. Формальное was / were to + глагол тоже работает: The meeting was to begin at noon.
  6. Используем после глаголов передачи в прошлом: said, thought, knew, expected, hoped.

Зачем это нужно?

Иногда мы стоим в точке прошлого и смотрим оттуда вперёд. Будущее для того момента сейчас тоже может быть прошлым, поэтому каждая форма будущего сдвигается на шаг назад.

Будущее (от сейчас)Будущее в прошедшем (от того момента)
I think it will rain.I thought it would rain.
I'm going to quit.I was going to quit.
We're meeting at six.We were meeting at six.
The film is about to start.The film was about to start.

Планы, которые изменились

was going to и was supposed to часто показывают, что план не осуществился: I was going to call you, but my phone died. The train was supposed to arrive at 6, but it was late.

В рассказах would драматично заглядывает вперёд: She smiled and waved. She didn't know it would be the last time they met.
  1. would + asosiy fe'l = o'zlashtirilgan nutqda will: She said, "I will call" → She said she would call.
  2. was / were going to = o'tmishdagi reja va niyat: I was going to buy it.
  3. Past continuous rejalashtirilgan kelajakni ko'rsatadi: They were meeting at six.
  4. was / were about to + fe'l = juda yaqin kelajak: I was about to leave.
  5. Rasmiy was / were to + fe'l ham bo'ladi: The meeting was to begin at noon.
  6. O'tmishdagi ko'chirma fe'llardan keyin ishlatiladi: said, thought, knew, expected, hoped.

Student explanation

Ask yourself one question

Am I looking at a future event from a point in the past? If yes, use would, was/were going to, the past continuous or was about to.

Common mistakes

  • He said he will come. He said he would come.
  • I was going to went. I was going to go.
  • She said she is going to travel. She said she was going to travel.
  • I knew I will win. I knew I would win.
Rule: will → would; am / is / are going to → was / were going to.

In plain words

You are in a story about the past, and you talk about what was still ahead at that time. Often it is a plan that did not happen, or something you felt sure about.

  • I knew she would be late. Then, she was not late yet. Later she was.
  • We were going to leave at six, but the flight was cancelled. That was the plan, but it did not happen.

Will becomes would. Am going to becomes was going to.

Частые ошибки

  • He said he will come. He said he would come.
  • I was going to went. I was going to go.
  • She said she is going to travel. She said she was going to travel.
  • I knew I will win. I knew I would win.
Правило: will → would; am / is / are going to → was / were going to.

Простыми словами

Вы в рассказе о прошлом и говорите о том, что тогда было ещё впереди. Часто это план, который не сбылся, или то, в чём вы были уверены.

  • I knew she would be late. Я знал, что она опоздает. Тогда она ещё не опоздала, потом опоздала.
  • We were going to leave at six, but the flight was cancelled. Мы собирались вылететь в шесть, но рейс отменили. Такой был план, но он не сбылся.

Will становится would. Am going to становится was going to.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Siz o'tmish haqidagi hikoyadasiz va o'sha paytda hali oldinda bo'lgan narsa haqida gapirasiz. Ko'pincha bu amalga oshmagan reja yoki ishongan narsangiz.

  • I knew she would be late. U kechikishini bilardim. O'shanda hali kechikmagan edi, keyin kechikdi.
  • We were going to leave at six, but the flight was cancelled. Oltida ketmoqchi edik, lekin parvoz bekor qilindi. Reja shunday edi, amalga oshmadi.

Will would ga aylanadi. Am going to was going to ga aylanadi.

Real-life use

You use future in the past to tell stories and report what people planned or expected.

Telling a story

"I thought the meeting would be short, but it lasted hours."

Talking about missed plans

"We were going to travel, but the flights were cancelled."

Reporting

"She said she would send the report by Friday."

Narrating a scene

"I was about to leave when the phone rang."

IELTS Speaking: a decision

"I had planned to study abroad, but in the end I decided to stay. I was going to apply last year."
