Object pronouns

General info

Object pronouns replace a noun after a verb or a preposition, so we do not repeat it: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

We use them for

  • After a verb: I like Anna. → I like her.
  • After a preposition: This gift is for my parents. → This gift is for them.
  • Avoiding repetition: Where is the key? I can't find it.

Words that go with them

help, call, tell, give, show, with, for, to, about.

Russian changes the word by case (меня, ему, нас). Uzbek adds endings (meni, unga, bizga). English has one set of object pronouns.

Объектные местоимения заменяют существительное после глагола или предлога, чтобы не повторяться: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

Когда используем

  • После глагола: I like Anna. → I like her.
  • После предлога: This gift is for my parents. → This gift is for them.
  • Чтобы не повторяться: Where is the key? I can't find it.

Слова-подсказки

help, call, tell, give, show, with, for, to, about.

В русском слово меняется по падежам (меня, ему, нас). В узбекском добавляются окончания (meni, unga, bizga). В английском один набор объектных местоимений.

Object olmoshlari fe'l yoki predlogdan keyin otni almashtiradi, takrorlamaslik uchun: me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Fe'ldan keyin: I like Anna. → I like her.
  • Predlogdan keyin: This gift is for my parents. → This gift is for them.
  • Takrorlamaslik uchun: Where is the key? I can't find it.

Yordamchi so'zlar

help, call, tell, give, show, with, for, to, about.

Rus tilida so'z kelishik bilan o'zgaradi (меня, ему, нас). O'zbek tilida qo'shimchalar qo'shiladi (meni, unga, bizga). Inglizchada bitta object olmoshlari to'plami bor.

  2. After verbs: help me, call her, tell them, know him.
  3. After prepositions: with me, for him, to us, about them, from her.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the pronoun (word used instead of a name) the doer? Yes → subject (I, he, she, we, they). No → object (me, him, her, us, them).
  2. Does it come after a verb (action word) or a preposition (small word like in, on, at)? Yes → object pronoun.
  3. Who or what is it? Choose by person: a man → him, a woman → her, a thing → it, many people → them.

Common mistakes

  • She loves he. She loves him.
  • Give the book to I. Give the book to me.
  • Her is my sister. She is my sister.
  • I like they. I like them.
Test: she loves ___. If the word is the one being loved, it is an object (who or what gets the action): him.

In plain words

Every sentence has someone who does the action and someone who gets it. I is the one doing. Me is the one getting. It is like throwing and catching a ball.

  • I help him. I do the helping. He gets the help, so we say him.
  • She loves us. She does the loving. We get it, so we say us.
  • This is for you. Come with me. After small words like for and with, use me, him, her, us, them.

We never say "She helps I" or "I know he".

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Местоимение (слово вместо имени) делает действие? Да → подлежащее (I, he, she, we, they). Нет → объектное (me, him, her, us, them).
  2. Оно стоит после глагола (это слово-действие) или предлога (маленькое слово: in, on, at)? Да → объектное.
  3. Кто или что это? Мужчина → him, женщина → her, вещь → it, много людей → them.

Частые ошибки

  • She loves he. She loves him.
  • Give the book to I. Give the book to me.
  • Her is my sister. She is my sister.
  • I like they. I like them.
Проверка: she loves ___. Кого любят, то и объект: him.

Простыми словами

В каждом предложении есть тот, кто делает действие, и тот, на кого оно направлено. I — тот, кто делает. Me — тот, кто получает. Как бросить и поймать мяч.

  • I help him. Я помогаю ему. Я делаю, он получает, поэтому him.
  • She loves us. Она любит нас. Она делает, мы получаем, поэтому us.
  • This is for you. Come with me. Это для тебя. Пойдём со мной. После for и with ставим me, him, her, us, them.

Никогда не говорим "She helps I" или "I know he".

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Olmosh (ism o'rnida keladigan so'z) ishni bajaryaptimi? Ha → ega (I, he, she, we, they). Yo'q → object (me, him, her, us, them).
  2. U fe'l (harakat so'zi) yoki predlogdan (in, on, at kabi kichik so'z) keyin turibdimi? Ha → object olmoshi.
  3. Bu kim yoki nima? Erkak → him, ayol → her, narsa → it, ko'p odam → them.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • She loves he. She loves him.
  • Give the book to I. Give the book to me.
  • Her is my sister. She is my sister.
  • I like they. I like them.
Tekshiruv: she loves ___. Sevilayotgan odam object: him.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Har bir gapda ishni qiluvchi va ishni qabul qiluvchi bor. I qiluvchi. Me qabul qiluvchi. Xuddi to'p otish va ushlashdek.

  • I help him. Men unga yordam beraman. Men qilaman, u oladi, shuning uchun him.
  • She loves us. U bizni yaxshi ko'radi. U qiladi, biz olamiz, shuning uchun us.
  • This is for you. Come with me. Bu sen uchun. Men bilan yur. For va with dan keyin me, him, her, us, them keladi.

Hech qachon "She helps I" yoki "I know he" demaymiz.

Real-life use

Object pronouns are in almost every sentence we say.

Ask for help

"Can you help me? I can't open it."

Messages

"Call me when you arrive. I will wait for you."

Shopping

"Can I try it on? Can you show me the blue ones?"

IELTS Speaking: a friend

"My best friend is very important to me. I met her at school and we talk to each other every day."

Examples and drill

After a verb

With give / show / tell

Don't say it twice

I bought a new bag. I love it.
