Present Simple

General info

Present Simple is the basic tense of English. We use it to talk about what is generally true, or what happens again and again. It does not describe what is happening at this exact moment.

We use it for

  • Facts and general truths: The sun rises in the east.
  • Habits and routines: I get up at 7 every day.
  • Fixed timetables: The bus leaves at 9:15.
  • States (feelings, opinions, possession): She likes jazz. They have two cats.

Time words that go with it

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, every week, on Mondays, in the morning, at weekends.

It is the first tense learners meet at A1, and it appears in almost every real conversation.

Present Simple — основное время английского языка. Мы используем его, когда говорим о том, что верно вообще или повторяется снова и снова. Оно не описывает то, что происходит прямо сейчас.

Когда используем

  • Факты и общеизвестные истины: The sun rises in the east.
  • Привычки и распорядок: I get up at 7 every day.
  • Расписания: The bus leaves at 9:15.
  • Состояния (чувства, мнения, владение): She likes jazz. They have two cats.

Слова-подсказки

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day, every week, on Mondays, in the morning, at weekends.

Это первое время, которое изучают на уровне A1, и оно встречается почти в каждом разговоре.

Present Simple — ingliz tilidagi asosiy zamon. U umuman to'g'ri bo'lgan yoki qayta-qayta takrorlanadigan ishlar haqida gapirganda ishlatiladi. U aynan hozir bo'layotgan ishni bildirmaydi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Faktlar va umumiy haqiqatlar: The sun rises in the east.
  • Odatlar va kun tartibi: I get up at 7 every day.
  • Jadvallar: The bus leaves at 9:15.
  • Holatlar (his-tuyg'u, fikr, egalik): She likes jazz. They have two cats.

Yordamchi so'zlar

Teacher's rule

I, you, we, theyhe, she, it
+I workHe works
−I don't workHe doesn't work
?Do you work?Does he work?
  1. Affirmative: subject + base verb. With he, she, it add -s.
  2. Negative: do not / does not + base verb. The verb loses its -s: He doesn't work.
  3. Question: Do / Does + subject + base verb. Short answers: Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't.

Spelling with he, she, it

  • Most verbs: + s (play → plays, work → works)
  • Verbs ending in -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + es (watch → watches, go → goes)
  • Consonant + y: y → ies (study → studies)
  • Irregular: have → has
  1. Утверждение: подлежащее + основной глагол. С he, she, it добавляем -s.
  2. Отрицание: do not / does not + глагол. Глагол теряет -s: He doesn't work.
  3. Вопрос: Do / Does + подлежащее + глагол. Краткие ответы: Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't.

Правописание с he, she, it

  • Большинство глаголов: + s (play → plays, work → works)
  • Глаголы на -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + es (watch → watches, go → goes)
  • Согласная + y: y → ies (study → studies)
  • Исключение: have → has
  1. Darak gap: ega + fe'lning asosiy shakli. he, she, it bilan -s qo'shiladi.
  2. Inkor: do not / does not + fe'l. Fe'l -s ni yo'qotadi: He doesn't work.
  3. So'roq: Do / Does + ega + fe'l. Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't.

he, she, it bilan yozilishi

  • Ko'pchilik fe'llar: + s (play → plays, work → works)
  • -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o bilan tugaydigan fe'llar: + es (watch → watches, go → goes)
  • Undosh + y: y → ies (study → studies)
  • Istisno: have → has

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a habit, a fact or a timetable? Then use Present Simple.
  2. Who is it about? One other person or thing (he, she, it)? Put -s on the verb.
  3. Is it a negative or a question? Let do / does do the work. The main verb stays plain.

Common mistakes

  • He work here. He works here.
  • She doesn't likes tea. She doesn't like tea.
  • Does he works? Does he work?
  • I am go to school. I go to school.
Only one word in the sentence carries the -s: either does, or the main verb. Never both.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это привычка, факт или расписание? Тогда Present Simple.
  2. О ком речь? Об одном человеке или предмете (he, she, it)? Добавь -s к глаголу.
  3. Это отрицание или вопрос? Пусть работает do / does. Основной глагол остаётся без изменений.

Частые ошибки

  • He work here. He works here.
  • She doesn't likes tea. She doesn't like tea.
  • Does he works? Does he work?
  • I am go to school. I go to school.
Только одно слово в предложении несёт -s: либо does, либо основной глагол. Никогда оба.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Bu odat, fakt yoki jadvalmi? Unda Present Simple.
  2. Gap kim haqida? Bitta odam yoki narsa (he, she, it) bo'lsa, fe'lga -s qo'shing.
  3. Bu inkor yoki so'roqmi? Ishni do / does bajaradi. Asosiy fe'l o'zgarmaydi.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • He work here. He works here.
  • She doesn't likes tea. She doesn't like tea.
  • Does he works? Does he work?
  • I am go to school. I go to school.
Gapda -s faqat bitta so'zda bo'ladi: yoki does'da, yoki asosiy fe'lda. Hech qachon ikkalasida emas.

Real-life use

People use Present Simple dozens of times a day. These are the moments you will hear it most.

Introducing yourself

"I'm Aziz. I live in Tashkent and I study English."

Talking about your day

"I start work at 9 and I finish at 5. I usually have lunch at one."

Giving instructions

"You turn left, then you go straight. The bank is on the right."

Timetables and plans that are fixed

"The train leaves at 8:10. The film starts at 7."

Explaining how things work

"The app saves your work automatically."

Small talk

"Do you like football? What time do you usually get up?"

English only

Examples and drill

Facts

Water boils at 100°C. Cats sleep a lot. London is in England.

Habits

She drinks tea every morning. We play football on Fridays.

Timetables

The shop opens at 9. My lesson starts at 4 o'clock.

States

I like coffee. He has a big family. They know the answer.

Adverbs of frequency

They go before the main verb but after be: She always drinks tea. He is never late.
