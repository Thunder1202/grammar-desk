Nominalisation

General info

Nominalisation means turning a verb or an adjective into a noun (or a noun phrase). We do it to say an action or a quality as a "thing": decide → decision, fail → failure, important → importance.

Normal and nominalised

People worry because the climate is changing quickly. → The rapid change in climate causes concern.

We use it to

  • Sound formal and academic: The decision to close the factory shocked workers.
  • Pack more information into one sentence: The rapid growth of the economy led to a fall in unemployment.
  • Talk about ideas, not people: The lack of funding slowed the research.
  • Link sentences: Prices rose. This rise made people spend less.

Where we see it

Academic essays, reports, news articles, formal emails, IELTS Writing.

Russian and Uzbek also have nouns from verbs (решение, развитие / qaror, rivojlanish). English forms them with endings such as -tion, -ment, -ness and -ity, and uses them much more in formal writing.

Номинализация — это превращение глагола или прилагательного в существительное (или группу с существительным). Так мы называем действие или качество как «предмет»: decide → decision, fail → failure, important → importance.

Обычно и с номинализацией

People worry because the climate is changing quickly. → The rapid change in climate causes concern.

Зачем используем

  • Звучать формально и академично: The decision to close the factory shocked workers.
  • Вместить больше информации в одно предложение: The rapid growth of the economy led to a fall in unemployment.
  • Говорить об идеях, а не о людях: The lack of funding slowed the research.
  • Связывать предложения: Prices rose. This rise made people spend less.

Где встречается

Академические эссе, отчёты, новости, формальные письма, IELTS Writing.

В русском и узбекском тоже есть существительные от глаголов (решение, развитие / qaror, rivojlanish). В английском их образуют окончания -tion, -ment, -ness, -ity, и в формальном письме их используют гораздо чаще.

Nominalizatsiya — fe'l yoki sifatni otga (yoki ot bilan birikmaga) aylantirish. Shunda harakat yoki sifat «narsa» kabi nomlanadi: decide → decision, fail → failure, important → importance.

Oddiy va nominalizatsiya bilan

People worry because the climate is changing quickly. → The rapid change in climate causes concern.

  1. Verb → noun, adverb → adjective: The economy grew quickly → the rapid growth of the economy. The noun needs an adjective, not an adverb.
  2. Who does it: the subject or object becomes an of-phrase or a possessive: The company expanded → the expansion of the company / the company's expansion.
  3. New main verb: when the verb becomes a noun, we need a new verb to link ideas: cause, lead to, result in, contribute to, give rise to, bring about, be due to, be linked to.
  4. Noun + preposition: learn them together: increase in, demand for, effect on, solution to. See the table above.
  5. Articles and number: some nouns are uncountable (growth, pollution, development) and some are countable (a decision, an increase, a failure). Use the when the noun is specific: the growth of the economy.
  6. Often used with there: There was a sharp rise in prices. There has been a decline in sales.
  7. Do not overdo it: too many nouns make a sentence heavy and hard to read (the implementation of the utilisation of ...). Mix nouns with verbs and keep sentences clear.
  8. Register: nominalisation is formal and written. In speech and in friendly emails use verbs: We decided to close it. (not The decision was made to close it.)
  1. Глагол → существительное, наречие → прилагательное: The economy grew quickly → the rapid growth of the economy. Перед существительным нужно прилагательное, а не наречие.
  2. Кто действует: подлежащее или дополнение становится оборотом с of или притяжательной формой: The company expanded → the expansion of the company / the company's expansion.
  3. Новый главный глагол: когда глагол стал существительным, нужен новый глагол для связи идей: cause, lead to, result in, contribute to, give rise to, bring about, be due to, be linked to.
  4. Существительное + предлог: учи вместе: increase in, demand for, effect on, solution to. См. таблицу выше.
  5. Артикли и число: одни существительные неисчисляемые (growth, pollution, development), другие исчисляемые (a decision, an increase, a failure). The ставим, когда существительное конкретное: the growth of the economy.
  6. Часто с there: There was a sharp rise in prices. There has been a decline in sales.
  7. Не переусердствуй: слишком много существительных делают предложение тяжёлым и трудным для чтения (the implementation of the utilisation of ...). Смешивай существительные с глаголами и пиши ясно.
  8. Стиль: номинализация формальна и письменна. В речи и в дружеских письмах используй глаголы: We decided to close it. (не The decision was made to close it.)
  1. Fe'l → ot, ravish → sifat: The economy grew quickly → the rapid growth of the economy. Ot oldidan ravish emas, sifat keladi.
  2. Kim qiladi: ega yoki to'ldiruvchi of birikmasi yoki egalik shakliga aylanadi: The company expanded → the expansion of the company / the company's expansion.
  3. Yangi asosiy fe'l: fe'l otga aylangach, g'oyalarni bog'lash uchun yangi fe'l kerak: cause, lead to, result in, contribute to, give rise to, bring about, be due to, be linked to.
  4. Ot + predlog: birga yodlang: increase in, demand for, effect on, solution to. Yuqoridagi jadvalga qarang.
  5. Artikl va son: ba'zi otlar sanalmaydi (growth, pollution, development), ba'zilari sanaladi (a decision, an increase, a failure). Ot aniq bo'lsa the qo'yiladi: the growth of the economy.
  6. Ko'pincha there bilan: There was a sharp rise in prices. There has been a decline in sales.
  7. Haddan oshirmang: juda ko'p ot gapni og'ir va o'qishga qiyin qiladi (the implementation of the utilisation of ...). Otlarni fe'llar bilan aralashtiring va aniq yozing.
  8. Uslub: nominalizatsiya rasmiy va yozma. Nutqda va do'stona xatlarda fe'l ishlating: We decided to close it. (The decision was made to close it. emas.)

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is my text formal? Essay, report, formal email → nominalisation fits. Chat or speech → keep verbs.
  2. Is there a verb or adjective that names an idea? decide, fail, grow, important → turn it into a noun.
  3. Does the sentence need a new verb? Yes → add cause, lead to, result in, contribute to.

Build it in 3 steps

People worry because the climate is changing quickly. 1) Find the idea words: changing, quickly, worry. 2) Make nouns: change, rapid (adjective), concern → rapid change, concern. 3) Add a linking verb: causes. → The rapid change in climate causes concern.

Why it works

A noun phrase can carry a whole idea, so the sentence holds more information and sounds objective. The idea becomes the subject, and the real subject (people) can disappear.

Common mistakes

  • The decide to close the factory was difficult. The decision to close the factory was difficult.
  • There was a rapidly increase in prices. There was a rapid increase in prices.
  • The reason of the delay was the weather. The reason for the delay was the weather.
  • The increase of prices worries people. The increase in prices worries people.
Noun before noun? Use an adjective, not an adverb (rapid rise, not rapidly rise). And learn the preposition with every noun.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Мой текст формальный? Эссе, отчёт, формальное письмо → номинализация подходит. Разговор или речь → оставь глаголы.
  2. Есть ли глагол или прилагательное, которое называет идею? decide, fail, grow, important → преврати в существительное.
  3. Нужен ли предложению новый глагол? Да → добавь cause, lead to, result in, contribute to.

Строим за 3 шага

People worry because the climate is changing quickly. 1) Найди слова-идеи: changing, quickly, worry. 2) Сделай существительные: change, rapid (прилагательное), concern → rapid change, concern. 3) Добавь связующий глагол: causes. → The rapid change in climate causes concern.

Почему это работает

Существительное с описанием несёт целую идею, поэтому предложение вмещает больше информации и звучит объективно. Идея становится подлежащим, а настоящий деятель (people) может исчезнуть.

Частые ошибки

  • The decide to close the factory was difficult. The decision to close the factory was difficult.
  • There was a rapidly increase in prices. There was a rapid increase in prices.
  • The reason of the delay was the weather. The reason for the delay was the weather.
  • The increase of prices worries people. The increase in prices worries people.
Перед существительным ставим прилагательное, а не наречие (rapid rise, не rapidly rise). И учи предлог вместе с каждым существительным.

Real-life use

Nominalisation is a writing tool. It makes essays, reports and formal messages sound clear, objective and professional.

Academic essays

"The rapid growth of social media has changed the way people communicate. The spread of false information remains a serious problem."

News and reports

"The decision to raise interest rates caused a fall in house prices. The arrival of new competitors led to a drop in profits."

Formal and work emails

"We apologise for the delay in delivery. Thank you for your quick response to our request."

IELTS Writing Task 1

"There was a sharp rise in sales between 2015 and 2018, followed by a gradual decline."

IELTS Writing Task 2

"The replacement of workers by machines leads to job losses. The lack of public transport contributes to traffic congestion."

In speaking

Keep it natural. Use verbs: "I decided to move." One nominal phrase is fine: "The main reason for that is the cost."

Examples and drill

Verbs to nouns

The government decided to act. → The government's decision to act ... They developed a new drug. → The development of a new drug ... Many people agreed. → There was widespread agreement.

Adjectives to nouns

It is important to rest. → The importance of rest ... The test was difficult. → The difficulty of the test ... He is able to lead. → His ability to lead ...

Whole sentences

Because the weather was bad, the match was cancelled. → Bad weather led to the cancellation of the match.

Many people are unemployed, so the government is worried. → High unemployment is a concern for the government.

Pollution has increased, and this harms health. → The increase in pollution has a negative effect on health.

Linking verbs after a nominal subject

cause, lead to, result in, contribute to, give rise to, bring about, be linked to, be due to.
