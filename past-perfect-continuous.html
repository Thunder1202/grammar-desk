Past perfect continuous

General info

The past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) describes an action that was happening for a period of time before another past event.

  • She had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived.
  • They were tired because they had been working all day.

We use it for

  • Duration before a past moment: I had been studying since 8 a.m.
  • The cause of a past situation: He was out of breath because he had been running.
  • Emphasis on "how long", not on a finished result.
Form: had been + verb-ing, the same for all persons: I / you / he had been doing.

Past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) описывает действие, которое длилось какое-то время перед другим прошедшим событием.

  • She had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived.
  • They were tired because they had been working all day.

Когда используем

  • Длительность до момента в прошлом: I had been studying since 8 a.m.
  • Причина прошлой ситуации: He was out of breath because he had been running.
  • Акцент на «как долго», а не на завершённом результате.
Форма: had been + глагол-ing, одинаково для всех лиц: I / you / he had been doing.

Past perfect continuous (had been + -ing) boshqa o'tgan voqeadan oldin bir muddat davom etgan harakatni bildiradi.

  • She had been waiting for an hour when the bus arrived.
  • They were tired because they had been working all day.

Nima uchun ishlatamiz

  • O'tmishdagi bir paytdan oldingi davomiylik: I had been studying since 8 a.m.
  • O'tmishdagi holatning sababi: He was out of breath because he had been running.
  • "Qancha vaqt" ga urg'u, tugallangan natijaga emas.
Shakl: had been + fe'l-ing, barcha shaxslarda bir xil: I / you / he had been doing.

Student explanation

Ask yourself two questions

  1. Did the action last for a period of time before another past event? Yes → past perfect continuous.
  2. Do I want to emphasise "how long" (the process)? Yes → continuous, not simple.

Common mistakes

  • She had been wait for an hour. She had been waiting for an hour.
  • I had been knowing her for years. I had known her for years.
  • When I arrived, she was waited. When I arrived, she had been waiting.
  • They have been working before he came. They had been working before he came.
If the verb (action word) is a state verb (know, have, be, like), use the past perfect simple instead.

In plain words

Think of a long action in the past that was still going on, or had just stopped, at a later past moment. It shows how long something had been happening before that moment.

  • Her eyes were red because she had been crying. The long crying came first. The red eyes came after.
  • They had been waiting for two hours when the bus came. Two hours of waiting, then the bus.

It looks back from a moment in the past, and it often answers "how long?" or "why?".

Задай себе два вопроса

  1. Действие длилось какое-то время до другого прошлого события? Да → past perfect continuous.
  2. Хочу подчеркнуть «как долго» (процесс)? Да → continuous, а не simple.

Частые ошибки

  • She had been wait for an hour. She had been waiting for an hour.
  • I had been knowing her for years. I had known her for years.
  • When I arrived, she was waited. When I arrived, she had been waiting.
  • They have been working before he came. They had been working before he came.
Если глагол (это слово-действие) состояния (know, have, be, like), используй past perfect simple.

Простыми словами

Представьте долгое действие в прошлом, которое ещё шло или только что закончилось к более позднему моменту. Форма показывает, как долго что-то длилось до этого момента.

  • Her eyes were red because she had been crying. Её глаза были красными, потому что она плакала. Сначала долгий плач, потом красные глаза.
  • They had been waiting for two hours when the bus came. Они ждали два часа, когда пришёл автобус. Два часа ожидания, потом автобус.

Это взгляд назад из момента в прошлом. Часто отвечает на «как долго?» или «почему?».

  2. "Qancha vaqt" (jarayon) ga urg'u bermoqchimanmi? Ha → continuous, simple emas.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

O'tmishda uzoq davom etgan va keyingi o'tgan paytda hali ham davom etayotgan yoki endi to'xtagan ishni tasavvur qiling. Shakl o'sha paytgacha qancha davom etganini ko'rsatadi.

  • Her eyes were red because she had been crying. Uning ko'zlari qizargan edi, chunki yig'lagan edi. Avval uzoq yig'i, keyin qizil ko'zlar.
  • They had been waiting for two hours when the bus came. Avtobus kelganda ular ikki soatdan beri kutayotgan edi. Ikki soat kutish, keyin avtobus.

Bu o'tmishdagi paytdan orqaga qarash. Ko'pincha "qancha vaqt?" yoki "nega?" savoliga javob beradi.

Real-life use

You use the past perfect continuous to explain what had been happening before a past moment.

Telling a story

"When I arrived, they had been waiting for half an hour."

Explaining a feeling

"She was exhausted because she had been studying all night."

At work

"He got the job because he had been preparing for months."

Weather and results

"The ground was wet because it had been raining."

IELTS Speaking: a busy day

"By the time I got home, I had been working since early morning, so I went straight to bed."

English only

Examples and drill

Duration

They had been driving for six hours. She had been learning English for years.

Cause of a past situation

He was tired because he had been running. The grass was wet because it had been raining.

With "when"

I had been studying when the phone rang.

Negative and questions

I hadn't been sleeping well. Had you been waiting long?
