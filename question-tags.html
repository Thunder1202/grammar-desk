Question tags

General info

A question tag is a short question at the end of a sentence. We use it to check information or to ask someone to agree.

We use it for

  • Checking: You are a student, aren't you?
  • Agreeing in small talk: It's cold today, isn't it?
  • Asking for confirmation: You have the tickets, haven't you?

Words that go with them

isn't it, don't you, can't he, didn't they, right?

Russian says «да?», «правда?», «не так ли?». Uzbek says «shundaymi?», «to'g'rimi?». English changes the tag every time, so you need a rule.

Разделительный вопрос — короткий вопрос в конце предложения. Мы проверяем информацию или просим согласиться.

Когда используем

  • Проверка: You are a student, aren't you?
  • Согласие в беседе: It's cold today, isn't it?
  • Подтверждение: You have the tickets, haven't you?

Слова-подсказки

isn't it, don't you, can't he, didn't they, right?

По-русски говорят «да?», «правда?», «не так ли?». По-узбекски «shundaymi?», «to'g'rimi?». В английском хвостик каждый раз меняется, поэтому нужно правило.

Question tag — gap oxiridagi qisqa savol. Biz ma'lumotni tekshiramiz yoki rozilik so'raymiz.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Tekshirish: You are a student, aren't you?
  • Suhbatda rozilik: It's cold today, isn't it?
  • Tasdiqlash: You have the tickets, haven't you?

Yordamchi so'zlar

isn't it, don't you, can't he, didn't they, right?

Rus tilida «да?», «правда?» deyiladi. O'zbek tilida «shundaymi?», «to'g'rimi?». Inglizchada tag har safar o'zgaradi, shuning uchun qoida kerak.

Teacher's rule

StatementTagRule
+She is happy, isn't she?positive → negative
−You aren't tired, are you?negative → positive
+They can swim, can't they?same modal
+He likes tea, doesn't he?present simple → do / does
+We went there, didn't we?past simple → did
+You have finished, haven't you?same auxiliary
  1. Opposite: positive sentence → negative tag. Negative sentence → positive tag.
  2. Same auxiliary: use the auxiliary from the sentence (is, are, can, have, will). If there is none, use do / does (present simple) or did (past simple).
  3. Pronoun: the tag always has a pronoun, never a name or noun: Anna is late, isn't she? The boys are here, aren't they?
  4. Short forms: negative tags are contracted: isn't, don't, can't. Not: is not she.
  5. Special: I am → aren't I? Let's → shall we?
  6. Intonation: voice goes down = you are sure and only checking. Voice goes up = you really are not sure.
  1. Наоборот: утверждение → отрицательный хвостик. Отрицание → положительный хвостик.
  2. Тот же вспомогательный: берём вспомогательный из предложения (is, are, can, have, will). Если его нет, ставим do / does (Present Simple) или did (Past Simple).
  3. Местоимение: в хвостике всегда местоимение, не имя и не существительное: Anna is late, isn't she? The boys are here, aren't they?
  4. Краткие формы: отрицательный хвостик сокращается: isn't, don't, can't. Не: is not she.
  5. Особые случаи: I am → aren't I? Let's → shall we?
  6. Интонация: голос идёт вниз = ты уверен и просто проверяешь. Голос идёт вверх = ты правда не уверен.
  1. Teskari: darak gap → inkor tag. Inkor gap → darak tag.
  2. Bir xil yordamchi fe'l: gapdagi yordamchi fe'lni oling (is, are, can, have, will). U bo'lmasa do / does (Present Simple) yoki did (Past Simple).
  3. Olmosh: tagda doim olmosh bo'ladi, ism yoki ot emas: Anna is late, isn't she? The boys are here, aren't they?
  4. Qisqa shakllar: inkor tag qisqartiriladi: isn't, don't, can't. is not she emas.
  5. Maxsus holatlar: I am → aren't I? Let's → shall we?
  6. Intonatsiya: ovoz pasayadi = ishonasiz va faqat tekshiryapsiz. Ovoz ko'tariladi = haqiqatan ishonchingiz yo'q.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the sentence positive or negative? Positive → negative tag. Negative → positive tag.
  2. Is there an auxiliary or modal? Yes → repeat it. No → do / does / did.
  3. Who is the subject? Change it to a pronoun: Anna → she, the boys → they, this → it.

Common mistakes

  • You like tea, do you? You like tea, don't you?
  • He can swim, isn't he? He can swim, can't he?
  • Tom is here, isn't Tom? Tom is here, isn't he?
  • I am late, amn't I? I am late, aren't I?
Make the tag in three steps: opposite, same auxiliary, pronoun.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Предложение утвердительное или отрицательное? Утверждение → отрицательный хвостик. Отрицание → положительный.
  2. Есть вспомогательный или модальный глагол? Да → повторяем. Нет → do / does / did.
  3. Кто подлежащее? Меняем на местоимение: Anna → she, the boys → they, this → it.

Частые ошибки

  • You like tea, do you? You like tea, don't you?
  • He can swim, isn't he? He can swim, can't he?
  • Tom is here, isn't Tom? Tom is here, isn't he?
  • I am late, amn't I? I am late, aren't I?
Хвостик делаем в три шага: наоборот, тот же вспомогательный, местоимение.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Gap darakmi yoki inkormi? Darak → inkor tag. Inkor → darak tag.
  2. Yordamchi yoki modal fe'l bormi? Ha → takrorlang. Yo'q → do / does / did.
  3. Ega kim? Olmoshga almashtiring: Anna → she, the boys → they, this → it.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • You like tea, do you? You like tea, don't you?
  • He can swim, isn't he? He can swim, can't he?
  • Tom is here, isn't Tom? Tom is here, isn't he?
  • I am late, amn't I? I am late, aren't I?
Tagni uch qadamda yasang: teskari, bir xil yordamchi fe'l, olmosh.

Real-life use

Question tags make speech sound natural and friendly.

Small talk

"It's cold today, isn't it?" "Yes, it is."

Checking plans

"You have the tickets, haven't you? We're meeting at six, aren't we?"

Checking information

"You don't work on Sundays, do you?"

IELTS Speaking: friendly tone

"It's a nice day, isn't it?" Use tags rarely in the test, but you will hear them often from native speakers.

English only

Examples and drill

Positive → negative tag

She is happy, isn't she? You like tea, don't you? He went home, didn't he?

Negative → positive tag

You aren't tired, are you? They don't live here, do they? We haven't met, have we?

Special

I am right, aren't I? Let's start, shall we?
