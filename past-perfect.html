Past Perfect

General info

Past Perfect shows an action that happened before another action in the past. It is the "earlier past".

We use it for

  • The earlier of two past actions: When we arrived, the film had started.
  • Explaining a reason in the past: I was late because I had missed the bus.
  • Experience up to a past moment: She had never seen snow before she visited Canada.

Words that go with it

before, after, by the time, when, already, just, never ... before.

Russian and Uzbek usually use the plain past for both actions. English marks the earlier one with had + past participle.

Past Perfect показывает действие, которое произошло раньше другого действия в прошлом. Это «более раннее прошлое».

Когда используем

  • Более раннее из двух прошлых действий: When we arrived, the film had started.
  • Причина в прошлом: I was late because I had missed the bus.
  • Опыт до момента в прошлом: She had never seen snow before she visited Canada.

Слова-подсказки

before, after, by the time, when, already, just, never ... before.

В русском и узбекском для обоих действий обычно простое прошедшее. В английском более раннее действие отмечается had + третья форма.

Past Perfect o'tmishdagi boshqa ishdan oldin sodir bo'lgan ishni bildiradi. Bu «oldingi o'tmish».

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Ikki o'tgan ishdan oldingisi: When we arrived, the film had started.
  • O'tmishdagi sabab: I was late because I had missed the bus.
  • O'tmishdagi paytgacha bo'lgan tajriba: She had never seen snow before she visited Canada.

Yordamchi so'zlar

before, after, by the time, when, already, just, never ... before.

Rus va o'zbek tilida ikkala ish uchun odatda oddiy o'tgan zamon ishlatiladi. Inglizchada oldingi ish had + uchinchi shakl bilan belgilanadi.

  1. Form: had + past participle, the same for all persons. Short forms: I'd, she'd. Negative: hadn't. Question: Had + subject + participle? Short answers: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
  2. Use: the earlier of two past actions: When I got home, the guests had left.
  3. Order: Past Perfect for the earlier action, Past Simple for the later one. Time words: before, after, by the time, when, already, just.
  4. After / before: After I had finished, I went home. I had finished before I went home.
  5. One action, or actions in order: use only Past Simple: I got up, had breakfast and left. Do not use Past Perfect.
  6. Past of Present Perfect: I have lost my keys (now) → I had lost my keys (when I got home).
  1. Форма: had + третья форма, для всех лиц одинаково. Краткие формы: I'd, she'd. Отрицание: hadn't. Вопрос: Had + подлежащее + третья форма? Краткие ответы: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
  2. Употребление: более раннее из двух прошлых действий: When I got home, the guests had left.
  3. Порядок: Past Perfect для более раннего, Past Simple для более позднего. Слова: before, after, by the time, when, already, just.
  4. After / before: After I had finished, I went home. I had finished before I went home.
  5. Одно действие или события по порядку: только Past Simple: I got up, had breakfast and left. Past Perfect не нужен.
  6. Прошедшее от Present Perfect: I have lost my keys (сейчас) → I had lost my keys (когда пришёл домой).
  1. Shakl: had + uchinchi shakl, barcha shaxslar uchun bir xil. Qisqa shakllar: I'd, she'd. Inkor: hadn't. So'roq: Had + ega + uchinchi shakl? Qisqa javoblar: Yes, I had. / No, I hadn't.
  2. Qo'llanishi: ikki o'tgan ishdan oldingisi: When I got home, the guests had left.
  3. Tartib: oldingi ish uchun Past Perfect, keyingisi uchun Past Simple. So'zlar: before, after, by the time, when, already, just.
  4. After / before: After I had finished, I went home. I had finished before I went home.
  5. Bitta ish yoki ketma-ket ishlar: faqat Past Simple: I got up, had breakfast and left. Past Perfect kerak emas.
  6. Present Perfect ning o'tgani: I have lost my keys (hozir) → I had lost my keys (uyga kelganimda).

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Are there two past actions? Yes → find which happened first. No → Past Simple only.
  2. Which was earlier? The earlier one → had + participle. The later one → Past Simple.
  3. Do I explain a reason or a surprise? Often yes → I was late because I had missed the bus.

Past Simple or Past Perfect?

When I arrived, the train left. (it left after I arrived) / When I arrived, the train had left. (it left before I arrived)

Common mistakes

  • When we arrived, the film has started. When we arrived, the film had started.
  • I had went home early. I had gone home early.
  • When I got home, I realised I forgot my keys. When I got home, I realised I had forgotten my keys.
  • Had you ate before you came? Had you eaten before you came?
Draw a line: earlier action → had + participle. Later action → Past Simple.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Есть два прошлых действия? Да → найди, какое было первым. Нет → только Past Simple.
  2. Какое было раньше? Более раннее → had + третья форма. Более позднее → Past Simple.
  3. Объясняю причину или неожиданность? Часто да → I was late because I had missed the bus.

Past Simple или Past Perfect?

When I arrived, the train left. (уехал после моего приезда) / When I arrived, the train had left. (уехал до моего приезда)

Частые ошибки

  • When we arrived, the film has started. When we arrived, the film had started.
  • I had went home early. I had gone home early.
  • When I got home, I realised I forgot my keys. When I got home, I realised I had forgotten my keys.
  • Had you ate before you came? Had you eaten before you came?
Нарисуй линию: более раннее действие → had + третья форма. Более позднее → Past Simple.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Ikkita o'tgan ish bormi? Ha → qaysi biri birinchi bo'lganini toping. Yo'q → faqat Past Simple.
  2. Qaysi biri oldin bo'lgan? Oldingisi → had + uchinchi shakl. Keyingisi → Past Simple.
  3. Sabab yoki kutilmagan holatni tushuntiryapmanmi? Ko'pincha ha → I was late because I had missed the bus.

Past Simple yoki Past Perfect?

When I arrived, the train left. (men kelgandan keyin ketdi) / When I arrived, the train had left. (men kelmasdan oldin ketgan edi)

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • When we arrived, the film has started. When we arrived, the film had started.
  • I had went home early. I had gone home early.
  • When I got home, I realised I forgot my keys. When I got home, I realised I had forgotten my keys.
  • Had you ate before you came? Had you eaten before you came?
Chiziq chizing: oldingi ish → had + uchinchi shakl. Keyingi ish → Past Simple.

Real-life use

We use Past Perfect to explain reasons and to tell stories in the right order.

Explaining

"I was late because I had missed the bus."

Telling a story

"When I opened the door, everyone had already gone home. Someone had left the lights on."

Giving news

"She was upset because she had lost her phone."

IELTS Speaking: talking about a past experience

"By the time I took the test, I had practised every day for months, so I felt ready."

Reason

Before and after
