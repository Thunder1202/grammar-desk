Relative clauses (advanced)

General info

At B2 relative clauses become more flexible and formal. We use extra information with commas, preposition + whom / which, quantifier + of whom / of which, and shortened clauses with -ing and -ed.

We use them for

  • Extra information: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  • Formal style: The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • Commenting on a whole sentence: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • Shorter, written style: The man standing there is my uncle.

Words that go with them

whom, whose, of which, of whom, which (whole clause), -ing, -ed, commas.

Russian uses «который», «в котором», «что». Uzbek uses -gan, -ib turgan. English has more choices: whom, of which, which for a whole sentence.

На уровне B2 придаточные становятся гибче и формальнее. Мы используем дополнительную информацию с запятыми, предлог + whom / which, количество + of whom / of which и сокращённые обороты с -ing и -ed.

Когда используем

  • Дополнительная информация: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  • Формальный стиль: The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • Комментарий ко всему предложению: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • Короче, письменный стиль: The man standing there is my uncle.

Слова-подсказки

whom, whose, of which, of whom, which (всё предложение), -ing, -ed, запятые.

В русском «который», «в котором», «что». В узбекском -gan, -ib turgan. В английском больше вариантов: whom, of which, which для всего предложения.

B2 darajasida nisbiy gaplar moslashuvchan va rasmiyroq bo'ladi. Vergulli qo'shimcha ma'lumot, predlog + whom / which, miqdor + of whom / of which va -ing, -ed bilan qisqartirilgan gaplar ishlatiladi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Qo'shimcha ma'lumot: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  • Rasmiy uslub: The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • Butun gapga izoh: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • Qisqaroq, yozma uslub: The man standing there is my uncle.

Yordamchi so'zlar

whom, whose, of which, of whom, which (butun gap), -ing, -ed, vergullar.

Rus tilida «который», «в котором», «что». O'zbek tilida -gan, -ib turgan. Inglizchada tanlov ko'proq: whom, of which, butun gap uchun which.

Teacher's rule

TypeStructureExample
non-definingnoun, who / which ...,My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
preposition + whom / whichformalThe woman to whom I spoke. (informal: the woman I spoke to)
quantifiersome / many / all / none of whom / whichShe has three sons, two of whom are doctors.
whole sentence, whichHe passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
reduced (-ing)activeThe man standing there is my uncle.
reduced (-ed)passiveThe book written by him is famous.
  1. Non-defining clauses add extra information. Use commas, use who / which (never that), and do not drop the pronoun: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  2. Preposition + whom / which is formal: The woman to whom I spoke. In informal English the preposition goes at the end: The woman I spoke to. Never "to who".
  3. Quantifier + of whom / of which: She has three sons, two of whom are doctors. The books, many of which are old, ...
  4. which for a whole clause: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  5. Reduced clauses: drop who / which + be. Active → -ing: The man (who is) standing there. Passive → -ed: The book (which was) written by him.
  6. whose + noun works for people and things: a company whose profits fell.

who or whom?

Use whom only when the pronoun is the object of its clause, and mostly in formal English or after a preposition. In everyday speech, who (or nothing) is normal.

StyleExample
formalThe candidate whom we interviewed was excellent.
formal, after a prepositionThe manager to whom I complained apologised.
everydayThe candidate (who) we interviewed was excellent. The manager I complained to apologised.

, which or what?

, which refers back to a whole idea already said. what = "the thing that" and starts a new idea; it never follows a noun.

She didn't call, which was strange. · What I need is a holiday. (not "The thing what I need")

Why reduce a clause?

Reduced clauses make writing shorter and smoother. They are common in news, reports and academic texts.

Full clauseReduced
Passengers who are travelling to Paris should go to Gate 5.Passengers travelling to Paris should go to Gate 5.
The cars that were made in this factory are exported.The cars made in this factory are exported.
He was the first person who arrived.He was the first person to arrive.
After the first, the last, the only and superlatives, a to-infinitive can replace the clause: the only one to pass, the youngest player to score.
  1. Неопределительные придаточные добавляют лишнюю информацию. Запятые, who / which (не that), местоимение опускать нельзя: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  2. Предлог + whom / which — формальный стиль: The woman to whom I spoke. В неформальной речи предлог в конце: The woman I spoke to. Никогда «to who».
  3. Количество + of whom / of which: She has three sons, two of whom are doctors. The books, many of which are old, ...
  4. which для всего предложения: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  5. Сокращённые обороты: опускаем who / which + be. Актив → -ing: The man (who is) standing there. Пассив → -ed: The book (which was) written by him.
  6. whose + существительное подходит для людей и предметов: a company whose profits fell.

who или whom?

whom используем, только когда местоимение — дополнение в своей части, и чаще всего в официальной речи или после предлога. В обычной речи нормально who (или ничего).

СтильПример
официальныйThe candidate whom we interviewed was excellent.
официальный, после предлогаThe manager to whom I complained apologised.
разговорныйThe candidate (who) we interviewed was excellent. The manager I complained to apologised.

, which или what?

, which относится ко всей уже сказанной мысли. what = «то, что» и начинает новую мысль; после существительного его не ставят.

She didn't call, which was strange. · What I need is a holiday. (а не «The thing what I need»)

Зачем сокращать придаточное?

Сокращённые обороты делают текст короче и плавнее. Они часты в новостях, отчётах и научных текстах.

Полное придаточноеСокращённое
Passengers who are travelling to Paris should go to Gate 5.Passengers travelling to Paris should go to Gate 5.
The cars that were made in this factory are exported.The cars made in this factory are exported.
He was the first person who arrived.He was the first person to arrive.
После the first, the last, the only и превосходной степени придаточное можно заменить инфинитивом: the only one to pass, the youngest player to score.
  1. Noaniqlovchi gaplar qo'shimcha ma'lumot beradi. Vergul, who / which (that emas), olmoshni tushirib bo'lmaydi: My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
  2. Predlog + whom / which rasmiy uslub: The woman to whom I spoke. Norasmiy nutqda predlog oxirida: The woman I spoke to. Hech qachon «to who» emas.
  3. Miqdor + of whom / of which: She has three sons, two of whom are doctors. The books, many of which are old, ...
  4. Butun gap uchun which: He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  5. Qisqartirilgan gaplar: who / which + be tushiriladi. Aniq nisbat → -ing: The man (who is) standing there. Majhul nisbat → -ed: The book (which was) written by him.
  6. whose + ot odamlar va narsalar uchun ishlaydi: a company whose profits fell.

who yoki whom?

whom olmosh o'z qismida to'ldiruvchi bo'lganda va asosan rasmiy tilda yoki predlogdan keyin ishlatiladi. Kundalik nutqda who (yoki hech narsa) odatiy.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is the information extra? Yes → commas, who / which, no that.
  2. Is there a preposition (small word like in, on, at)? Formal → preposition + whom / which. Informal → preposition at the end.
  3. Can I shorten it? Active → -ing. Passive → -ed. Drop who / which + be.

Combining two sentences

She has three sons. Two of them are doctors. → She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.

Common mistakes

  • The woman to who I spoke was kind. The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • He passed the exam, that surprised everyone. He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • She has three sons, two of them are doctors. She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.
  • My brother, that lives in Dubai, is a doctor. My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
After a comma or a preposition, never use that. Use who, whom or which.

In plain words

There are two kinds of extra information. One tells you which person or thing, and the sentence needs it. The other is a bonus fact, placed between commas, and the sentence still works without it.

  • My brother, who lives in Seoul, is an engineer. A bonus fact. I have only one brother.
  • She is the writer whose book won the prize. Whose shows that the book is hers.
  • The people I work with are kind. We can drop who here, because I is the one doing the action.

Test: cover the words between commas. If the sentence is still clear, the commas are right.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Информация лишняя? Да → запятые, who / which, без that.
  2. Есть предлог (маленькое слово: in, on, at)? Формально → предлог + whom / which. Неформально → предлог в конце.
  3. Можно сократить? Актив → -ing. Пассив → -ed. Опускаем who / which + be.

Соединяем два предложения

She has three sons. Two of them are doctors. → She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.

Частые ошибки

  • The woman to who I spoke was kind. The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • He passed the exam, that surprised everyone. He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • She has three sons, two of them are doctors. She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.
  • My brother, that lives in Dubai, is a doctor. My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
После запятой или предлога никогда не используй that. Используй who, whom или which.

Простыми словами

Есть два вида дополнительных сведений. Одни говорят, какой именно человек или предмет, без них предложение не работает. Другие — бонусный факт между запятыми, без него всё равно понятно.

  • My brother, who lives in Seoul, is an engineer. Мой брат, который живёт в Сеуле, инженер. Бонусный факт: брат у меня один.
  • She is the writer whose book won the prize. Это писательница, чья книга получила приз. Whose показывает, что книга её.
  • The people I work with are kind. Люди, с которыми я работаю, добрые. Who можно убрать: действие делаю я.

Проверка: закройте слова между запятыми. Если предложение понятно, запятые стоят верно.

O'zingizga uchta savol bering

  1. Ma'lumot qo'shimchami? Ha → vergullar, who / which, that yo'q.
  2. Predlog (in, on, at kabi kichik so'z) bormi? Rasmiy → predlog + whom / which. Norasmiy → predlog oxirida.
  3. Qisqartirish mumkinmi? Aniq nisbat → -ing. Majhul (ish kimgadir nisbatan qilinadi) nisbat → -ed. who / which + be tushiriladi.

Ikki gapni birlashtirish

She has three sons. Two of them are doctors. → She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.

Ko'p uchraydigan xatolar

  • The woman to who I spoke was kind. The woman to whom I spoke was kind.
  • He passed the exam, that surprised everyone. He passed the exam, which surprised everyone.
  • She has three sons, two of them are doctors. She has three sons, two of whom are doctors.
  • My brother, that lives in Dubai, is a doctor. My brother, who lives in Dubai, is a doctor.
Vergul yoki predlogdan keyin hech qachon that ishlatmang. who, whom yoki which ishlating.

Oddiy so'zlar bilan

Qo'shimcha ma'lumot ikki xil. Biri qaysi odam yoki narsa ekanini aytadi, gapga u kerak. Ikkinchisi vergullar orasidagi bonus fakt, usiz ham gap tushunarli.

  • My brother, who lives in Seoul, is an engineer. Seulda yashaydigan akam muhandis. Bonus fakt: akam bitta.
  • She is the writer whose book won the prize. U kitobi mukofot olgan yozuvchi. Whose kitob uniki ekanini bildiradi.
  • The people I work with are kind. Men ishlaydigan odamlar mehribon. Who tushib qoladi, chunki ishni men qilaman.

Tekshiruv: vergullar orasidagi so'zlarni yoping. Gap tushunarli qolsa, vergullar to'g'ri.

Real-life use

Advanced relative clauses make writing and formal speaking sound more natural and precise.

Formal writing

"The committee, which met on Monday, approved the plan. The people with whom we worked were helpful."

Giving details about people

"She has three sons, two of whom are doctors."

Commenting

"He passed the exam, which surprised everyone."

IELTS Speaking: describing a place or a person

"I live in a city known for its parks. My teacher, who has taught for twenty years, inspired me."
