Modals: must, have to, should

General info

Must, have to and should talk about obligation and advice: what is necessary, what is forbidden, and what is a good idea.

We use them for

  • Obligation: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Prohibition and not necessary: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Advice: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Words that go with them

rule, law, necessary, forbidden, advice, it is a good idea.

Russian: должен / нельзя / не обязательно / стоит. Uzbek: kerak / mumkin emas / shart emas / yaxshi bo'lardi. English needs three different forms.

Must, have to и should выражают обязанность и совет: что необходимо, что запрещено и что стоит сделать.

Когда используем

  • Обязанность: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Запрет и необязательно: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Совет: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Слова-подсказки

rule, law, necessary, forbidden, advice, it is a good idea.

Русский: должен / нельзя / не обязательно / стоит. Узбекский: kerak / mumkin emas / shart emas / yaxshi bo'lardi. В английском три разные формы.

Must, have to va should majburiyat va maslahatni bildiradi: nima zarur, nima taqiqlangan va nima yaxshi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Majburiyat: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Taqiq va shart emas: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Maslahat: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Yordamchi so'zlar

  1. Modal + base verb: must go, should go. No -s (not musts), no to (not must to go).
  2. must = strong obligation, often the speaker's own opinion or a written rule: I must call my mum. You must wear a seatbelt.
  3. have to = obligation from outside (rules, work, school). With he / she / it use has to. Negative: don't / doesn't have to. Question: Do / Does ... have to ...?
  4. mustn't = forbidden. don't have to = not necessary. They are very different.
  5. should / shouldn't = advice. Question: Should I ...? Short answer: Yes, you should.
  6. Past: must and have to become had to: I had to wake up early yesterday.

mustn't or don't have to? A big difference

FormMeaningExample
mustn'tDon't do it! It is not allowed.You mustn't use your phone in the exam.
don't have toYou can, but it isn't necessary. It's your choice.You don't have to wear a tie. (you can if you want)

must or have to?

Often both are possible. must usually shows what the speaker feels is necessary. have to shows a rule or a situation from outside.

I must call my grandma. (I feel I should) · I have to wear a uniform at work. (it's the company rule)

Negatives and questions, side by side

Giving an opinion with should

We often say I think / I don't think + should: I think you should see a doctor. I don't think you should go out tonight.

must has no past form. For the past use had to / didn't have to: I had to work late yesterday. We didn't have to pay.
  1. Модальный + основная форма: must go, should go. Без -s (не musts), без to (не must to go).
  2. must = сильная обязанность, часто мнение говорящего или письменное правило: I must call my mum. You must wear a seatbelt.
  3. have to = обязанность извне (правила, работа, школа). С he / she / it — has to. Отрицание: don't / doesn't have to. Вопрос: Do / Does ... have to ...?
  4. mustn't = запрещено. don't have to = необязательно. Значения очень разные.
  5. should / shouldn't = совет. Вопрос: Should I ...? Краткий ответ: Yes, you should.
  6. Прошедшее: must и have to становятся had to: I had to wake up early yesterday.

must или have to?

Часто подходят оба. must обычно показывает, что сам говорящий считает нужным. have to — правило или обстоятельства извне.

I must call my grandma. (я чувствую, что надо) · I have to wear a uniform at work. (таково правило компании)

Совет с should

Часто говорим I think / I don't think + should: I think you should see a doctor. I don't think you should go out tonight.

У must нет прошедшей формы. Для прошлого — had to / didn't have to: I had to work late yesterday. We didn't have to pay.
  1. Modal + asosiy shakl: must go, should go. -s yo'q (musts emas), to yo'q (must to go emas).
  2. must = kuchli majburiyat, ko'pincha gapiruvchining fikri yoki yozma qoida: I must call my mum. You must wear a seatbelt.
  3. have to = tashqaridan majburiyat (qoidalar, ish, maktab). he / she / it bilan has to. Inkor: don't / doesn't have to. So'roq: Do / Does ... have to ...?
  4. mustn't = taqiqlangan. don't have to = shart emas. Ma'nolari butunlay boshqa.
  5. should / shouldn't = maslahat. So'roq: Should I ...? Qisqa javob: Yes, you should.
  6. O'tgan zamon: must va have to had to bo'ladi: I had to wake up early yesterday.

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a rule or necessity? Yes → must / have to. If you can skip it → don't have to.
  2. Is it forbidden? Yes → mustn't.
  3. Is it only a good idea? Yes → should / shouldn't.

Mustn't or don't have to?

You mustn't run here. (Don't. It is not allowed.) / You don't have to run. (You can, but it is not necessary.)

Common mistakes

  • She musts go. She must go.
  • You must to wear a helmet. You must wear a helmet.
  • He don't have to work. He doesn't have to work.
  • You mustn't come if you don't want to. You don't have to come if you don't want to.
After must and should the verb (action word) stays in its base form. Never add -s, to or -ing.

In plain words

Think of a traffic light for advice and rules. Must and have to are red: there is no choice. Should is yellow: it is a good idea, but you can decide. Don't have to is green: you are free.

  • You must wear a helmet. This is a strong rule.
  • You should drink more water. This is good advice.
  • You don't have to pay. It is free. You do not need to.

Careful: mustn't means "it is not allowed", but don't have to means "it is not needed".

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это правило или необходимость? Да → must / have to. Можно не делать → don't have to.
  2. Это запрещено? Да → mustn't.
  3. Это просто хорошая идея? Да → should / shouldn't.

Mustn't или don't have to?

You mustn't run here. (Нельзя. Запрещено.) / You don't have to run. (Можно, но не обязательно.)

Частые ошибки

  • She musts go. She must go.
  • You must to wear a helmet. You must wear a helmet.
  • He don't have to work. He doesn't have to work.
  • You mustn't come if you don't want to. You don't have to come if you don't want to.
После must и should глагол (это слово-действие) остаётся в основной форме. Никаких -s, to или -ing.

Простыми словами

Представьте светофор для советов и правил. Must и have to — красный: выбора нет. Should — жёлтый: хорошая идея, но решать вам. Don't have to — зелёный: вы свободны.

  • You must wear a helmet. Нужно надевать шлем. Это строгое правило.
  • You should drink more water. Тебе стоит пить больше воды. Хороший совет.
  • You don't have to pay. Можно не платить. Это бесплатно.

Осторожно: mustn't значит «нельзя», а don't have to значит «не нужно».

Real-life use

We use these words for rules, signs and advice every day.

Rules and signs

"Visitors must sign in. You mustn't park here."

Work and school

"I have to finish this report today. We don't have to come on Friday."

Give advice

"You look tired. You should go to bed early."

IELTS Speaking: giving an opinion

"I think people should walk more, and cities should have more parks. Students shouldn't spend too much time on their phones."

Examples and drill

Must and mustn't

I have to wear a uniform. She has to get up at six. We don't have to pay. Do you have to work on Sunday?

You should drink more water. You shouldn't stay up so late. Should I call him?
