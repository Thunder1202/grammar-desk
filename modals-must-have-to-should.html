Modals: must, have to, should

General info

Must, have to and should talk about obligation and advice: what is necessary, what is forbidden, and what is a good idea.

We use them for

  • Obligation: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Prohibition and not necessary: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Advice: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Words that go with them

rule, law, necessary, forbidden, advice, it is a good idea.

Russian: должен / нельзя / не обязательно / стоит. Uzbek: kerak / mumkin emas / shart emas / yaxshi bo'lardi. English needs three different forms.

Must, have to и should выражают обязанность и совет: что необходимо, что запрещено и что стоит сделать.

Когда используем

  • Обязанность: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Запрет и необязательно: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Совет: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Слова-подсказки

rule, law, necessary, forbidden, advice, it is a good idea.

Русский: должен / нельзя / не обязательно / стоит. Узбекский: kerak / mumkin emas / shart emas / yaxshi bo'lardi. В английском три разные формы.

Must, have to va should majburiyat va maslahatni bildiradi: nima zarur, nima taqiqlangan va nima yaxshi.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Majburiyat: You must wear a seatbelt. I have to wear a uniform.
  • Taqiq va shart emas: You mustn't park here. You don't have to come.
  • Maslahat: You should sleep more. You shouldn't eat so much sugar.

Yordamchi so'zlar

  1. Modal + base verb: must go, should go. No -s (not musts), no to (not must to go).
  2. must = strong obligation, often the speaker's own opinion or a written rule: I must call my mum. You must wear a seatbelt.
  3. have to = obligation from outside (rules, work, school). With he / she / it use has to. Negative: don't / doesn't have to. Question: Do / Does ... have to ...?
  4. mustn't = forbidden. don't have to = not necessary. They are very different.
  5. should / shouldn't = advice. Question: Should I ...? Short answer: Yes, you should.
  6. Past: must and have to become had to: I had to wake up early yesterday.

  3. have to = обязанность извне (правила, работа, школа). С he / she / it — has to. Отрицание: don't / doesn't have to. Вопрос: Do / Does ... have to ...?

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it a rule or necessity? Yes → must / have to. If you can skip it → don't have to.
  2. Is it forbidden? Yes → mustn't.
  3. Is it only a good idea? Yes → should / shouldn't.

Mustn't or don't have to?

You mustn't run here. (Don't. It is forbidden.) / You don't have to run. (You can, but it is not necessary.)

Common mistakes

  • She musts go. She must go.
  • You must to wear a helmet. You must wear a helmet.
  • He don't have to work. He doesn't have to work.
  • You mustn't come if you don't want to. You don't have to come if you don't want to.
After must and should the verb stays in its base form. Never add -s, to or -ing.

Задай себе три вопроса

  1. Это правило или необходимость? Да → must / have to. Можно не делать → don't have to.
  2. Это запрещено? Да → mustn't.
  3. Это просто хорошая идея? Да → should / shouldn't.

Mustn't или don't have to?

You mustn't run here. (Нельзя. Запрещено.) / You don't have to run. (Можно, но не обязательно.)

Частые ошибки

  • She musts go. She must go.
  • You must to wear a helmet. You must wear a helmet.
  • He don't have to work. He doesn't have to work.
  • You mustn't come if you don't want to. You don't have to come if you don't want to.
После must и should глагол остаётся в основной форме. Никаких -s, to или -ing.

Real-life use

We use these words for rules, signs and advice every day.

Rules and signs

"Visitors must sign in. You mustn't park here."

Work and school

"I have to finish this report today. We don't have to come on Friday."

Giving advice

"You look tired. You should go to bed early."

IELTS Speaking: giving an opinion

"I think people should walk more, and cities should have more parks. Students shouldn't spend too much time on their phones."

English only

Examples and drill

Must and mustn't

You must wear a helmet. You mustn't use your phone in the exam.

Have to and don't have to

I have to wear a uniform. She has to get up at six. We don't have to pay. Do you have to work on Sunday?

Should and shouldn't

You should drink more water. You shouldn't stay up so late. Should I call him?

Past

I had to wake up early yesterday.

Drill

1. It's the law: you ___ stop at a red light. (must / should)
must
2. You look tired. You ___ go to bed early. (should / have to)
should
3. Tomorrow is Sunday. I ___ get up early. (mustn't / don't have to)
don't have to
4. You ___ smoke here. It is forbidden. (mustn't / don't have to)
mustn't
5. She ___ wear a uniform at school. (have to / has to)
has to
6. ___ you ___ work on Saturday? (Do ... have to / Must ... to)
Do ... have to
7. Correct it: He don't have to wear a tie.
He doesn't have to wear a tie.
