Future perfect and continuous

General info

These tenses look at the future from a specific future moment. Future continuous = in progress at that moment. Future perfect = finished before that moment.

We use them for

  • Future continuous: action in progress: This time tomorrow I will be flying to Dubai.
  • Future perfect: action finished before a future time: By Friday I will have finished the report.
  • Future perfect continuous: how long up to a future point: By June I will have been working here for five years.

Words that go with them

this time tomorrow, at 8 pm, by, by the time, before, by then, for.

Russian and Uzbek use simple future forms (я буду лететь, я закончу / uchib boraman, tugataman). English shows in progress or finished by the form.

Эти времена смотрят на будущее из конкретного момента в будущем. Future continuous = в процессе в этот момент. Future perfect = закончено до этого момента.

Когда используем

  • Future continuous: действие в процессе: This time tomorrow I will be flying to Dubai.
  • Future perfect: действие закончено до момента в будущем: By Friday I will have finished the report.
  • Future perfect continuous: как долго до точки в будущем: By June I will have been working here for five years.

Слова-подсказки

this time tomorrow, at 8 pm, by, by the time, before, by then, for.

В русском и узбекском простые формы будущего (я буду лететь, я закончу / uchib boraman, tugataman). В английском форма показывает процесс или завершённость.

Bu zamonlar kelajakka kelajakdagi aniq paytdan qaraydi. Future continuous = o'sha paytda davom etayotgan. Future perfect = o'sha paytgacha tugagan.

Qachon ishlatamiz

  • Future continuous: davom etayotgan ish: This time tomorrow I will be flying to Dubai.
  • Future perfect: kelajakdagi paytgacha tugagan ish: By Friday I will have finished the report.
  • Future perfect continuous: kelajakdagi nuqtagacha qancha davom etgani: By June I will have been working here for five years.

Yordamchi so'zlar

  2. Polite questions about plans: Will you be using the car tonight? (Not a request, just asking about your plan.)

Student explanation

Ask yourself three questions

  1. Is it in progress at a future moment? Yes → will be + -ing.
  2. Is it finished before a future moment? Yes → will have + participle. Look for by / by the time.
  3. Do I say how long? Yes → will have been + -ing, with for.

Compare

At 8 tomorrow I'll be studying. (in progress) / By 8 tomorrow I'll have studied. (finished before 8)

Common mistakes

  • By the time you will arrive, I will have cooked. By the time you arrive, I will have cooked.
  • This time tomorrow I will be fly to Dubai. This time tomorrow I will be flying to Dubai.
  • By June I will work here for five years. By June I will have been working here for five years.
  • By 2030 I will have finish university. By 2030 I will have finished university.
After by the time, before and when, use the present simple, never will.

Задай себе три вопроса

  2. Закончено до момента в будущем? Да → will have + третья форма. Ищи by / by the time.
  3. Говорю, как долго? Да → will have been + -ing, с for.

Real-life use

We use these tenses to talk about plans, deadlines and long-term goals.

Polite questions about plans

"Will you be using the car tonight? Will you be coming to the meeting?"

Meeting someone

"At five I'll be waiting outside the station."

Deadlines and goals

"By next year I will have saved enough money. By Friday I'll have finished the project."

IELTS Speaking: talking about your future

"By 2030 I will have graduated, and I hope I will be working as a teacher."
